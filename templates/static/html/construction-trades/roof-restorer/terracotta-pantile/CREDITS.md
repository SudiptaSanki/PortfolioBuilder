## Honesty notes

- Calloway Clay, Ruth Calloway, the yard, the clients (including St Cuthbert's Church, Burgh le Marsh and Glebe Farm), the register entries, prices, batch numbers and the testimonial are fictional and illustrative, created for this template. The footer of every page says so.
- The photographs show the trade — clay tile roofs, a kiln firebox, kiln setting — not the fictional yard, its roofs or its maker. Captions describe literally what each photograph shows, and where a photograph differs from the yard's own practice (the wood-fired kiln, where Calloway's kiln is gas), the caption says so.
- Two downloaded photographs were rejected after inspection and deleted: a roofer fitting profiled metal sheet tiles (not clay pantiles) and a plain beavertail tile roof (not pantiles).
