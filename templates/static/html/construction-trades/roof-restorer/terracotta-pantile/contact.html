Sending a sample tile

  1. 1

    Take the largest fragment you have — ideally a whole tile from a sheltered part of the roof. A handful of frost flakes tells us the roof is failing but not what it was made of.

  2. 2

    Wrap it like crockery, because it is. Newspaper or cloth between fragments, a sturdy box, your name and the building's address inside the lid.

  3. 3

    Photograph the roof before you post anything — the whole slope from the ground, then close-ups. Email the photographs with your name so they meet the parcel in the register.

  4. 4

    Post to: Calloway Clay, Unit 3, Mareham Road Yard, Horncastle, Lincolnshire LN9 6NG. The match fee is £25 — we will write with payment details once the parcel arrives; never send cash.

  5. 5

    What comes back: fired sample tiles to hold against your roof, a written match note (clay, firing, expected colour spread), a price per tile, and the first firing your order could make. If we cannot match it, the samples and the honest reason come back instead, and there is no fee.
