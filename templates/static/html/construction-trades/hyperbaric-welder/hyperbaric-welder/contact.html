Before you write

Three things that speed up any answer: the structure's name or asset reference, the depth at the work face if it is known, and your hard dates — a sailing, a shutdown, a licence renewal. If the answer to all three is “unknown”, write anyway: that is a survey brief, and it is a normal way for a job to start.

Tenders

The certificate pack — diving and coded-welding qualifications at class level with certificates, medical and survival currency, insurance — is supplied with every tender and on request by email. Check it. We would rather lose a job to a checked certificate than win one to an unchecked claim.
