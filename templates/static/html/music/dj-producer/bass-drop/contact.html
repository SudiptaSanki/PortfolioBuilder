What a good brief includes

Two or three reference tracks — not “something like this”, the actual records.

The deadline and what drives it — a release date, a vinyl cut, a tour.

What you already have — a finished song, a vocal stem, a demo, a lyric. Each starting point has a different price shape, and they’re all workable.
