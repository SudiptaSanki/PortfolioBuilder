Tell me what the record has to do.

A sentence about the release, the format you have in mind, and the date the pressing plant wants the lacquer. I reply with a level report and a session date, usually within a working day.

Cutting enquiry

This form is a brief, not a booking: nothing is charged and no date is held until I confirm in writing. Your details are used for this enquiry only.

Before you send

Two things decide most of the session: how long the side is, and how loud the low end needs to be. If you know both, say so and we can skip a round of email.

What to send with the master

  • 24-bit WAV or AIFF, 44.1 or 96 kHz, no clipping
  • 3 dB of peak headroom, or the unmastered mix
  • Track order and which side each track belongs to
  • Any reference records that sound the way you want
  • The label's catalogue number, if there is one

Lead times

Lacquer in three weeks from an approved master, two sides per session. Test pressings back in fourteen days from the plant, not from me. If a release has a fixed date, work backwards from the plant's cut-off, not from the shop date.

Rates

Attended session, two sides: €340. Unattended, two sides: €260. Digital master from the same session: €90. Recut due to a fault in the cut: no charge. Recut after a change of mix: full lacquer price.
