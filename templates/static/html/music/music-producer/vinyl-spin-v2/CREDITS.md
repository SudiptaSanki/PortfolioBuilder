# Credits — Vermeulen Cutting Room (vinyl-spin)

## Persona
Saskia Vermeulen / Vermeulen Cutting Room is a fictional persona created for
this template. No real person's name, face or work is used. (The original
stub carried a real artist's name as its title; that was removed, not kept.)
Clients, records and figures are illustrative; the method, the physics
(side-length bands, inner-groove behaviour, bass narrowing) and the pressing
arithmetic are stated at established-practice level.

## Sound
None, deliberately. Ruling: the honest demonstration of what vinyl does to
music is a record on a turntable; a synthesised imitation of surface noise
would be theatre — the fake warmth the site's own honesty plate argues
against. The stub's hover-triggered plucks and click-to-play beat synth
were removed, not rebuilt. The site contains no audio elements and this
build creates no AudioContext.
