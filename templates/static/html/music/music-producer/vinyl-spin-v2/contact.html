Before you write

What to send

  • a.A link to the mixes — a stream or download link, never attachments. If the record is not mixed yet, say so; production starts earlier than the side sheet.
  • b.The track list with durations, to the second. The side sheet cannot be drafted from “about forty minutes”, and the first honest conversation is the one about your totals.
  • c.The release date you are counting from. The plant queue currently runs ten to fourteen weeks; the date work starts from is the queue's, not the launch party's.
  • d.Your counted audience, if you are self-releasing — the list you can email, the rooms you play, what the last release sold. Not your streaming totals; the log will ask, kindly, and then it will ask again.
The room

Vermeulen Cutting Room is the practice of Saskia Vermeulen, a fictional producer created for this template. The method, the physics and the arithmetic are stated at real practice level; the clients, records and figures are illustrative.

Sound

This site makes no sound, deliberately. The honest demonstration of what vinyl does to music is a record on a turntable; a synthesised imitation of crackle would be the fake warmth this room exists to refuse. The stub's hover sounds were removed, not rebuilt.
