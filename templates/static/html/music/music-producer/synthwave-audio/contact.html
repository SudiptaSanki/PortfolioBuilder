Direct email

studio@renatavoss.example

Replies within two working days. For sync licensing of catalogue tracks, the same address — put the track title and the intended use in the subject line and the quote comes back faster.

What to send

  • The picture or the build — a link is fine; a timing sheet for trailers
  • Two or three reference tracks — what they get right, in a sentence each
  • The deadline — the real one, including who has to approve what, when
  • The use — media, term, territory, and whether exclusivity matters

Booking status

  • Game scores: next full slot March 2026
  • Trailer cues: taken year-round, 5–7 day turnaround
  • Catalogue licences: same-week, usually same-day quotes
