## Sound — the ruling

This is a sound profession, so one bench is permitted under the program's strict pattern
and every condition is met: the booth loop starts only from an explicit Play control;
it is synthesised live (kick sine-drop + filtered noise hats at a printed 124 BPM) and
labelled on the page as NOT a recording of the DJ or anyone; master gain is fixed at
0.12; it self-stops after four bars (≈7.7 s); a Stop control is present; a hearing line
is printed with it. No autoplay, no hover audio anywhere. The stub's original
hover-triggered bass synth and its flashing strobe effect were removed as deal-breakers —
a strobe effect on a website is an accessibility failure, and this site states so in its
footer and terms.

## Pattern note

The single amber dry-brush ribbon under the hero running order is the program's
brush-edge pattern (variant C, accent ribbon), hand-authored SVG in index.html —
genre-fitted to a club sheet (the promoter's highlighter), one per page boundary,
flat fill, no gradient, carrying no text and sitting under no tap target.
