Sheet 02 — The Nights

Case files, with the sheets attached.

Three pieces of work, told the way the booth keeps them: the brief as it arrived, the decisions taken on the night, and the outcome — including the parts that were the room's doing, not mine. No crowd photographs are offered as reviews.

File 01 — The Warm-Up

Hand the room over at the right temperature

Raum 9, Berlin
Sat 14 Jun 2025 · 00:00–01:30

The brief

Warm up for Vela Mar, a Lisbon headliner playing Berlin for the first time. The booker's ask, in writing: "Full floor by 01:30, at her tempo, not yours. She opens at 124 — land her there." The room's own warning came with it: the previous guest warm-up had peaked at 01:00, the floor emptied to the bar during the changeover, and the headliner rebuilt the night from a cold start.

The decisions

The set was planned backwards from the handover. Ceiling first: nothing above 126 BPM and no peak-time records at all — the last three tracks sit at 122, 124 and 126 so the tempo arrives where she starts, not above it. Energy in steps, not a slope: the floor was read every fifteen minutes and the plan's steps were held even when a fuller-feeling record was available. The first hour as played is printed below — start times counted with the same 12-second blend rule the planner on the home page uses.

The outcome

Handover at 01:30, 124 BPM, floor full — the headliner's first record was the room's loudest moment of the night, which is the correct shape of a warm-up: the peak belongs to the person the room paid to see. The booker rebooked the pairing for October before the night had ended.

"She treats warm-up as a craft, not a queue for her own headline. The room was full, at tempo, and quiet enough in the right places for Vela to be the event. That is the whole job, done properly."

— Dana Weiss, booker, Raum 9 — on the night of 14 Jun 2025, File 01

File 02 — The Residency

A season told by its dates

Raum 9, Berlin
Mar – Nov 2025 · 9 dates

The brief

A monthly Saturday at Raum 9 for one season, agreed as nine printed dates before the first one was played — no "resident" title without the run. The room wanted a night that regulars could learn: same slot, same arc, and a record of what changed kept honestly, including the night that did not work.

The outcome

Renewed for 2026 — the only sentence a residency file needs, with the dates above as its evidence. Night 5 stays in the table: a season that prints only its full rooms is a poster, not a record.

File 03 — The Mix

A recorded commission, delivered as a tracklist

Northline Records
Delivered Aug 2025

The brief

A Berlin label's autumn showcase needed a 50-minute-class mix for its mailing list — no streaming upload, no play counts to quote afterwards. The deliverable was specified like an engineer's: a printed tracklist with the arithmetic shown, the master file, and usage terms in writing.

The arithmetic, shown: durations sum to 3,075 s (51:15). Eight tracks make seven blends; at the printed 12-second overlap that is 84 s taken back. 3,075 − 84 = 2,991 s = 49:51 — the delivered master is 49:51 long. The number is checkable against the file, which is the point.

Usage terms, as printed on the delivery note

The label owns the master and may send it to its mailing list and host it on its own site for twelve months. The mix is not uploaded to streaming platforms and no play or download figures are quoted by either side. Track rights remain with their producers; the tracklist above is the credit. A second commission is priced from the same printed line (BK-04), not renegotiated from this one's reception.
