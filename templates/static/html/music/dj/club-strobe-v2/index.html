    Tap tempo

    Tap the pad in time with a track — or with the room. The readout averages your last five intervals; a pause of two seconds resets it. It is a utility: it measures your tapping, nothing else.

    — BPM

    The booth loop — a demonstration, labelled as one

    Four bars of kick and hats at a printed 124 BPM, synthesised in your browser by an oscillator and a noise buffer when you press play. It is not a recording of Sable, or of anyone — it exists so the tempo on this page can be heard, not just read.

    Idle. Nothing plays until you press play — there is no autoplay and no hover audio on this site.

    Hearing: the loop is mastered low (master gain 0.12) and stops itself after four bars. In the booth I wear moulded plugs at −15 dB and play rooms that run a limiter at about 103 dB(A) — the full line is on the terms page.
