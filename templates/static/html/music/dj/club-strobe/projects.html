Clube Lisboa, closing set

Live recording · 1 h 32 · 2025

Six hours played, ninety minutes released. Closing is a separate discipline: the job is to empty a room without making anyone feel asked to leave. The last record is a 1979 ballad at 98 BPM and it worked.

Night Bus 64 (Amara Diallo edit)

Single-sided 7″ · 2025

A rework of Oskar Venn’s track that adds two minutes of drums before the vocal and takes out the synth solo. Made for my own sets, pressed because people kept asking. The B-side is blank, which is a joke about pressing costs that only three people have laughed at.
