How booking works

You send the date, the room and the system. I reply with availability and a fee. If we agree, a 50% deposit holds the date and the rest is due on the night, in cash or by transfer. I do not sign exclusivity clauses that stop me playing in the same city within a month.

For travel, I ask for a train or a flight booked by the promoter, and a hotel within walking distance of the venue. If the set ends at 6 a.m., the hotel is not optional.

What I need to know

The set time and the length, who is playing before and after, and whether the room has a soundsystem that can carry 110 BPM without the bass swallowing the hats. A photograph of the booth is worth more than a paragraph.

If it is a private party, tell me who the guests are and what they actually listen to. I would rather play the right thing for forty people than the wrong thing for two hundred.
