What it sounds like

Warm, patient, and a little bit dusty.

Records first

I buy records on Saturdays at a shop that does not have a website. Roughly half of what I play is vinyl; the rest is unreleased material from people I know, which is the only real advantage a resident has in 2026.

Build, do not peak

The job in the first two hours is not to impress anybody. It is to make the room comfortable enough to stay in. The peak happens once, if it happens at all, and it is better for being late.

Read the room, not the meter

I do not own a decibel meter. I own a notebook with venue names and a line about what the room did at 1 a.m. That notebook is the most useful piece of equipment I carry.
