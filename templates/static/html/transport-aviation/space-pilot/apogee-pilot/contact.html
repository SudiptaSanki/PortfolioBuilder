Before you write

        
Two things decide most enquiries. Whether you have a medical history that will need an examiner's opinion, and whether your experiment has to hold an attitude through the coast. Both change the training and the crew.

        

          
Training
3 days, mandatory, no exceptions

          
Medical
Independent examiner, 2 weeks

          
Campaign slot
6 flights, shared rack

        

      


      

        
What I do not do

        
Sell seats, take a commission from an operator, or clear a passenger medically. If you want to buy a flight, I will tell you which operators I would put my own family in and which I would not, and then you deal with them directly.

      


      

        
What happens next

        
The first reply is either a short list of questions or a plain no. If the answer is yes, the next step is the medical, because everything else — seat, training dates, payload mass — can be moved later, and the medical cannot be argued with at the pad.
