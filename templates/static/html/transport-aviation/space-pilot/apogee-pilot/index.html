The programme

01

Three days of training

Cabin familiarisation, a centrifuge run to 3.5 g, pressure-suit donning in under four minutes, and a full profile rehearsal in the simulator. The day that matters is the second one, when the novelty has gone and the checklist still has to make sense.

3 days · 2 simulator sessions

02

Medical and fit-to-fly

A class 3 equivalent, a cardiology review, and an honest conversation about backs, ears and anxiety. Around one in nine applicants is deferred for something that has nothing to do with courage.

2 weeks · independent examiner

03

Flight day

A 06:10 boarding, a 22-minute count, and nine minutes from engine light to wheels down. The window is 3 min 42 s of weightless time, and the only instruction that matters is to look out of it.

9 min flight · 3 min 42 s weightless
