A-24

Microgravity fluids campaign

Research · 2025 · Flown

Six flights for a university team studying capillary flow in a transparent cell. The payload ran for 214 seconds of the 222-second weightless window, and the three flights that were rescheduled in one week produced the cleanest data because the team had learned the timeline.

My job was the attitude: two of the experiments needed the cell held within 2 ° of a fixed orientation through the coast, which is a hand-flying exercise with no horizon to reference.

A-27

High apogee

Test flight · 2025 · Flown

A vehicle-expansion flight flown to 107.4 km, the highest I have been, with a lighter crew load and a longer burn. The extra 3.2 km bought 46 seconds of weightless time and cost 0.4 g more on entry.

The useful data was thermal: the cabin soak-back after apogee was 4 °C warmer than the model, which changed the suit cooling setting for every flight since.

A-29

First passenger group

Private flight · 2026 · Flown

Four passengers, three days of training, one flight. The two who struggled in the centrifuge were the two who were calmest in the cabin, which has been true often enough to stop being a surprise.

One passenger asked to have the checklist read aloud through the coast. We do that now for everyone; it gives the mind somewhere to be while the body gets used to falling.

A-30

Scrubbed at T−4

Private flight · 2026 · Scrubbed

Upper-level winds were 12 knots above the recovery limit at T−4 minutes, and the balloon data at 12 km had not improved for forty minutes. We stood down. The passengers had a second window three days later and flew it.

The decision took ninety seconds and the argument about it took three days. I would make it again: the recovery zone is not a place to test a margin.
