04 / Enquire

Programmes, not tickets.

This form is for training, preparation and review enquiries — from operators, prospective participants, insurers and spaceports. This site does not sell flight tickets, and no message sent here can book a seat on anything.

Before you write: if you are a prospective participant, say which operator and profile you are booked on and what preparation you have been offered so far. If an operator has told you no preparation is needed, say that too — it changes the conversation.
