## Profile data basis

Representative profiles for a feathered rocketplane class, drawn from published
operator material for the class; the conventions printed on the site (weightlessness
counted MECO → re-entry interface; the 100 km Kármán/FAI and 80.5 km US boundaries)
are the conventions this practice briefs. An operator's own flight manual governs
any real flight.

## Audio

None. No audio files are used: the practice's material is read from profiles,

audio references, canvas HUD and telemetry animation were removed in the rebuild.
