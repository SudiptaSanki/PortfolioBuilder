What we do with a brief

      
We check the alignment against the vertical envelope, the grade and the settlement of the ground, then price the guideway per kilometre from the three most recent comparable routes. Two weeks, fixed fee, one report.


      
What the report contains

      
An alignment drawing at 1:2,000, the vertical envelope and grade check, a settlement assessment where the ground is soft, and a cost per kilometre with the three comparable routes named. Twelve pages, one fee, and no render of a train.


      
Not a fit

      
Routes under a kilometre, anything that needs the guideway to be movable, and projects where the alignment has already been fixed by a civil works contract before the survey. That last one is the expensive mistake, and it is always made before anyone calls us.
