Gap and drag

The on-board superconducting magnets induce a current in the figure-eight coils on the ground; the field they make holds the car up. Lift falls off exponentially with gap, so the car settles where lift equals weight. Move the current, the mass or the tunnel pressure and see where that is.

Drag against speed · this mass, current and pressure total 37.4 kN at 500 km/h

Aerodynamic drag uses CdA 7.5 m² and the tunnel pressure above. Magnetic drag is the EDS penalty that peaks at low speed and disappears as the field is dragged along. Both fall to zero if the car stops, which is why the low-speed drag peak is where the guideway is hardest to cool.

What the guideway has to do

01

Alignment

A maglev guideway is a survey problem before it is a magnetic one. The slab has to hold ±1.5 mm over a 3 m chord along a 40 km curve, in a structure that moves with the ground and expands in the sun. We set the alignment from a monumented control network, then re-survey after every thermal cycle of the concrete.

02

Coils

Figure-eight ground coils do two jobs at once: they levitate and they guide. Each is a closed aluminium loop with a crossover, and its response depends on the vehicle's superconducting magnets, which are never allowed to quench. We have installed 11,200 of them on three lines and replaced 34.

03

Aero

At 500 km/h the air is the dominant loss and the dominant noise. A partially evacuated tube removes most of it, and adds two new problems: the pressure wave the train pushes ahead of itself, and what happens to the gap control when the vehicle passes a tunnel portal. We test both at 1:12 scale before a slab is cast.
