Munich, Germany. Studies run wherever the tunnel is; coast-downs need your test track, tunnels need your portal, and both have been reached by train, which feels correct.

What to send

The train: formation, length, current drag figure and who measured it. The tunnel: length, bore, portal surroundings, the speed you want. The load factor: the real one, not the brochure one.

Confidential programmes are scoped under NDA before any facility time is discussed. If your question is really “is 350 worth it?”, say so plainly — it is the practice's favourite question, and Study File 03 is what the answer looks like.
