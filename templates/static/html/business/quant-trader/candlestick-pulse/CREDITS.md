## Honest replacements (what the stub promised vs. what shipped)

- The stub's "live simulated candlestick chart canvas" and scrolling
  ticker showed invented prices for real instruments (including a
  fabricated "+142% YTD" return). A portfolio for an educator whose
  whole claim is honesty cannot open with fake live data, so both were
  replaced: a static, annotated teaching chart with a fictional
  instrument, labelled as invented.
- The stub's "trade audio feedback" (a beep on a simulated order
  button) fails the program's sound need-test: trading education is
  not a sound profession and no interaction here teaches through
  audio. Removed; the site makes no sound.
- The stub's simulated order button itself was removed: this practice
  sells no trading, simulated or otherwise. Its signature interactions
  are the two things the practice actually teaches — the position-size
  calculator and the candle anatomy explorer.
- template.json's theme ("Bloomberg Dark Terminal") is honoured as a
  sober dark lesson-sheet design, minus the terminal cosplay.
