# Credits — Scandi Wood / Vestergaard Form

Persona: Lars Vestergaard and the workshop "Vestergaard Form" are fictional,
created for this template. The clients, pieces and prices in the text are
invented; the joinery, timbers, finishes and care practice described are real
cabinetmaking. The site footer says so.
