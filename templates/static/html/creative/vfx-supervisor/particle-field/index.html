SEQ 030 · REFERENCE PLATES

Sims are art-directed against the real thing.

Before a hero sim is built, the production shoots — or licenses — practical element plates: real sparks, real spray, real fire. The simulation is then judged against physics that actually happened. These two plates are the kind of reference on the wall of the suite; they are photographs, not renders, and they are captioned as what they are.

SEQ 040 · HOW AN ENGAGEMENT RUNS

Six stages, each with a deliverable you can hold.

STAGE 01

Brief & bid

Boards, script pages and frame ranges are costed per shot, by sim class — the bid is a table, not a day-rate multiplied by hope. Fixed for the agreed shot list.

STAGE 02

Wedges

Scaled tests of every hero behaviour, reviewed in dailies. Three wedge rounds are included in the wedge package; the look is locked here, while changes are still cheap.

STAGE 03

Sim build

Full-scale solvers built on the approved wedge: pyro, FLIP, RBD, grains. Every parameter that matters is logged in the shot's sim sheet, so any version can be rebuilt exactly.

STAGE 04

Cache & farm

Caches are checkpointed every 20 frames and checksummed. A dead node costs minutes, not a night — see the Cinderfall case file for the 03:40 incident report.

STAGE 05

Dailies & notes

Versions posted daily with frame-accurate notes in ftrack. Note turnaround is four working hours during production, one hour in finals week.

STAGE 06

Final & archive

Final caches and EXR plates delivered to spec, then archived for 24 months. A pickup shot in month 20 rebuilds from the sim sheet, not from memory.
