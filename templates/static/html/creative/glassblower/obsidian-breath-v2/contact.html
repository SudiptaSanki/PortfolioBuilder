The direct line

  • Email — studio@obsidianbreath.example
  • Studio — Studio 4, 1240 Industrial Way, Bend, Oregon
  • Visits — by appointment only. The kiln schedule is the studio's calendar, and it is not interrupted; a piece in its anneal does not pause for a viewing.
  • Replies — within two working days, from the maker, not a desk. If the bench is mid-cast, the reply waits for the annealer door to close, and says so.

Before you write

The calendar is printed on the Objects page, and the planner there will refuse a date the glass cannot meet. If your date matters, run it through the planner first — the refusal, when it comes, is the same one you would get by letter, only sooner.

Obsidian Breath

This site is silent — it carries no audio, by choice. Photographs are reference photographs, credited in CREDITS.md in this template's folder.
