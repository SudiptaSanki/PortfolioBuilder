Visiting

Open studio on Thursdays, 16:00–19:00, no booking needed. Any other time is possible if the furnace is not being gathered from; write first and we will tell you when the bench is quiet.

The shop floor is hot, loud and uneven. Closed shoes are required and we will lend you a jacket.

Lead times

  • Reply to an enquiry within three working days.
  • Drawing and glass sample: two to three weeks.
  • Single vessel: one firing, six to ten days in the kiln.
  • Editions and installations: four to twelve weeks, depending on colour changes.

Crating and shipping

Every piece travels in a foam-lined ply case cut to its profile, included in the price. We ship worldwide with DHL Express or a fine-art carrier for installations, fully insured. Buyers outside Iceland are responsible for import duty and VAT.

Care

Hand wash, warm water, no dishwasher and no sudden temperature change. The black will not fade. A vessel that holds water will show a mineral line at the water level; a dilute vinegar soak removes it without touching the glass.
