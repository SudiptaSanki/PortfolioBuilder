# Credits — Matte Lumen (Architectural Lighting Design)

Persona: **Ingrid Halvorsen, Matte Lumen, Copenhagen** — a fictional practice, disclosed in the footer of every page. The projects, clients and figures are realistic worked examples in 2026 prices; the lux model in the scene player is internally consistent and its arithmetic is printed on the page (each circuit's full-level contribution, scaled by level and master).

## Photographs

None — deliberately. This template's portfolio artefacts are the drawings a lighting designer actually hands over: an elevation, a reflected ceiling plan, schedules and tables. Stock interior photography would show other designers' rooms captioned as this practice's work, which is the exact dishonesty the concept ("Light you can specify") is built against. If photographs are added in a later pass, they must be Pexels-licensed, downloaded into `assets/`, captioned literally as room/venue types, and credited in this file.

## Drawings

Both drawings are original hand-coded SVG made for this template:

- `index.html` — Elevation A, living room of the Harbour Apartment: five circuits (cove, pendants, wall washers, floor lamp, shelf strip) drawn as a lit elevation; the glow layers are driven by the scene player.
- `about.html` — Reflected ceiling plan of the same room, simplified, with the five circuits in plan position.

## Sound

None. The sound need-test was applied: lighting design is a visual trade, and no audio teaches anything here that the figures don't teach better. The stub's AudioContext hum — a 300 Hz tone fired on every slider movement, created on page load — was deleted. The stub's background-brightness slider survived in honest form: it is now the master fader of a working scene player, wired to a drawn room with scheduled levels, a colour-temperature model and a printed scene line.
