What happens next

  1. A reply within two working days — with questions if the brief needs them, or survey dates if it doesn't.
  2. The survey (€350) — half a day on site: measure, photograph, note daylight and existing wiring. Credited in full against a design fee.
  3. A fixed fee proposal in writing, against the published fee table. No design work starts, and nothing is billed, before you have signed it.
  4. Design, specification, site days — the six stages on the Specification page, ending with the printed scene schedule in your drawer.
