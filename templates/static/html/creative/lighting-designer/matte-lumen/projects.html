CASE 01 · REGISTER № 02

Nyhavn Townhouse — lit without chasing a single cable

The brief

A family bought a protected townhouse from 1762 on Nyhavn: lime plaster, original cornices, and a conservation condition that no new cable could be chased into the historic fabric. The existing lighting was a ceiling rose per room and a row of halogen downlights cut into a 1990s kitchen. They wanted warm, layered, dimmable light in eight rooms — and the conservation officer's written approval before any of it.

The decisions

Control went wireless (Casambi Bluetooth mesh), so switching and dimming needed no new cable runs at all. Where light had to be new, it was made reversible: miniature track surface-mounted inside window reveals, picture lights hung from the existing picture rail, coves built into new joinery rather than into the building. Every fitting is removable without trace. 2700 K throughout, dim-to-warm in the two living rooms, and the kitchen's downlights replaced by a concealed worktop circuit at 500 lx.

The outcome

Eleven circuits across eight rooms, zero new chases in protected fabric, and approval granted without conditions — the conservation officer's letter notes the scheme as fully reversible. Ambient light in the main living room measures 150 lx at full scene, and the family reports the room they actually live in is the one that was previously too dark to read in after 4 p.m. in winter.

CASE 02 · REGISTER № 03

Fjord Suites — eighteen rooms that light themselves

The brief

A boutique hotel in Aarhus was refurbishing eighteen suites. The old keypads had nine buttons; guests regularly phoned reception to ask how to turn the bathroom light off, and housekeeping had no way to reset a room between guests. The owners added an energy condition: the new scheme had to meter measurably below the old one in its first year.

The decisions

DALI-2 throughout, with five scenes per suite tied to the key-card and the property system: Welcome on entry, Day, Evening, Night, and a Clean scene at full flat light for housekeeping. Bedside plates carry three buttons only, engraved in Danish and English — the research is unambiguous that guests use what they can read in the dark. Vacant suites drop to a setback state automatically, which is where most of the energy saving lives.

The outcome

Metered lighting energy for the first year ran 38% below the previous scheme. Calls to reception about the lighting, logged weekly by the front desk, went from a standing complaint to none recorded in the year after handover. The general manager's testimonial — printed on the Specification page — credits the printed scene schedule: new staff reset rooms from paper, without a controls engineer.

CASE 03 · REGISTER № 04

Collector's Flat — two light levels in one room, one of them capped

The brief

An Østerbro apartment holding forty-three works of art. Six are works on paper, insured under a conservation condition: no more than 50 lx on the surface, ever, and ultraviolet below 75 µW/lm. The same walls host dinners for twelve. The collector's question was precise — can one room be a gallery at 50 lx and a dining room at 180 lx without moving a single picture?

The decisions

The picture circuit is separate from everything else and split by wall: the paper wall and the oil wall are different circuits with different caps, set in the control system where a guest cannot override them. Fittings are narrow-beam (15°) track spots at Ra 97 with UV-free LED sources. Every wall was lux-mapped after aiming, and the map — measured, dated, signed — went to the insurer as part of the policy evidence. A daylight sensor trims the picture circuit on bright afternoons, when the room's north light does the work.

The outcome

The entertaining scene runs both levels at once: 180 lx on the oils, 50 lx on the paper, verified by meter at handover and re-verified at the first annual check. The collection's insurer accepted the lux log without further conditions. The collector's own verdict, at the aiming session: the pictures look better at 50 lx than they did under the old 300 lx wash — conservation and flattery turned out to be the same decision.
