§ 03

How a build runs

STAGE 1

Site visit & feasibility

We walk the plot, shoot the levels, and check access, sun path, snow drift and ledge. Then we tell you plainly if the plot won’t work — some don’t, and it is cheaper to hear it now.

$450 · credited against design
STAGE 2

Design & specification

Plans, sections, a fixed specification and a fixed price. The drawings in §01 are the same set your permit office sees, simplified — what you approve is what gets built.

2–4 weeks · fixed fee
STAGE 3

Groundworks

Piers carried to frost depth, drainage that moves water away from the building, and the water and power runs. The unglamorous third of every build, and the part that decides how long it stands.

1–3 weeks on site
STAGE 4

Frame & weathertight

Wall and roof panels are framed under cover in our workshop, then raised and made weathertight on site in days. Your cabin is never a skeleton standing in the rain for a season.

4–6 weeks workshop · 1–2 on site
STAGE 5

Fit-out & systems

Boards, kitchen, bath, stove, PV, battery and water — installed, tested under load, and labelled so a guest or a grandchild can run them without phoning us.

4–8 weeks
STAGE 6

Handover & first year

You get a manual written for your cabin — settings, shut-offs, service dates — and a visit after the first winter to check levels, seals and systems, included in the price.

Pay 10% · 40% · 40% · 10%
