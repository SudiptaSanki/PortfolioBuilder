What to send for a first conversation

You don’t need any of this to write — but the more of it you have, the more useful the first reply can be.

  • Photos of the plot from its four corners, and one looking at where the cabin might sit.
  • A pin or the address — slope, aspect and tree cover decide half the design.
  • Access: can a truck and a small crane reach the building spot? Any bridges, gates, soft ground?
  • Water & power: is there a well, a spring, a stream? How far is the nearest power pole?
  • The honest deadline: when do you want to sleep in it — and what happens if winter says otherwise?
