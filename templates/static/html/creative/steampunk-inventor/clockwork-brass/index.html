Plate II — the train, live

Set the wheels, see the ratio

Three wheels and an escape wheel. Change the tooth counts or the module and the train re-cuts itself: pitch diameters, centres, the output speed and the beat it would give a pendulum. Nothing here is a picture of a gear — the paths are generated from the numbers.

Plate VI — the path of a commission

From a drawing to a running movement

  1. i

    Brief and sketch

    What the piece must do, where it will stand, and how often someone will wind it. Two weeks, one drawing, one argument about the dial.

  2. ii

    Train design

    Ratios calculated backwards from the hands, then the wheel counts chosen to land on a whole number of teeth without a correction wheel.

  3. iii

    Cut and file

    Wheels cut on the 1890s wheel engine, crossings filed by hand, pinions turned between centres and hardened.

  4. iv

    Depthing and running

    Every pair depthed in the frame, then a fortnight on the test stand with the beat set and the strike adjusted.

  5. v

    Case and handover

    Case finished in shellac, movement cased, and an hour spent teaching the owner how to wind it without hurting it.
