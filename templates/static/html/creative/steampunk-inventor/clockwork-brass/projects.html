MV-61

The Sheffield Longcase

2026

An eight-day longcase for a private house in Ranmoor. Deadbeat escapement, maintaining power, bolt-and-shutter keeping, and a moon phase cut for the latitude of the room it stands in rather than a generic 51°. The dial is silvered, engraved and wax-filled.

MV-58

The Orrery Automaton

2025

Six planets on concentric arbors, driven from one crank through a train of nine wheels and a six-profile cam stack that tilts the ecliptic plane as the planets travel. Runs eleven minutes per wind and repeats the alignment of the outer three every fourth cycle.

Batley bracket clock

2024

A three-train bracket clock of about 1840, water-damaged across the front plate with a replaced escape wheel and a train that had been re-bushed badly enough to run out of centre. Nine weeks: plate repairs, seven new parts, a re-cut escape wheel and a full re-bush.

MV-55

The Lamplighter

2024

A figure that walks four paces, lifts a pole, lights a lamp and returns, driven by a fourteen-cam stack on a single arbor. 220 parts, most of them hand-filed. The figure is carved lime; the mechanism is entirely brass so the two do not fight over humidity.

MV-52

Tower dial, St Mary’s

2023

An astronomical dial added behind the existing clock opening of a parish church: sun, moon, moon phase and a tide ring for the Humber. The moon-phase wheel is cut 29 d 12 h 44 m — the true synodic month — so it drifts a day every 122 years.

Carriage clock, c. 1900

2023
