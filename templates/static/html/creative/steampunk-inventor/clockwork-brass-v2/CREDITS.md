## Sound ruling

**NONE.** No audio is recorded, synthesised or played anywhere on this site, and
the script creates no AudioContext. The original stub's hover-triggered gear-click
audio was deleted in full — hover audio is a deal-breaker in this program, and a
button that plays a fake steam hiss would be exactly the dishonesty this persona
jokes about. The ruling is printed in every page footer.
