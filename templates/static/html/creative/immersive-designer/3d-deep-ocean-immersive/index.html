How this page is built

  1. i

    One scene graph, six stations

    Sky, ocean, fleet and trench are four modules sharing one contract: sea level at y = 0, the sky at y = 180, the seabed at y = −420.

  2. ii

    Depth drives everything

    Fog, ambient light, water colour, particle density and the sound mix all read from one environment curve sampled at the camera.

  3. iii

    Audio is synthesised, not sampled

    Every wave, engine rumble and sonar sweep is generated live with the Web Audio API, then muffled by the same depth the visuals use.
