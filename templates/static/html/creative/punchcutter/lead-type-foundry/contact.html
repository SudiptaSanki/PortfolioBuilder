What happens next

We read the copy you send, answer with a recommendation and one historical reference, then quote. No drawings until the brief is agreed, and no deposit until the test setting is approved.

Can I buy a face outright?

No. Type is licensed for a named use. Exclusive rights are quoted separately and are rarely granted for a retail face.

Do you cut from a scan?

No. We work from drawings and from printed sources we can cite, and we name the source on the specimen. A traced scan cannot be spaced properly.

What files come with a licence?

OpenType outlines, a WOFF2 web build, a PDF specimen, and the character set list. Cast metal is quoted by the fount, weighed and tallied.

Can you match an existing face?

If it is out of copyright and we can examine a good printed source, we will discuss a revival — named as a revival. If it is in copyright, we will point you to the foundry that owns it.

Do you teach punchcutting?

Two places a year on a six-day bench course, usually in April. Ask for the waiting list; it is one page and it moves slowly.

Before you write

The copy, the stock and the press decide the drawing. A brief with all three gets a useful answer in ten days.

What we send

A recommendation, one reference, a lead time and a fixed price. Three historical references if the face is a revival.

What we do not do

No spec work, no free drawing rounds, no licensing a retail face as a template or generator.
