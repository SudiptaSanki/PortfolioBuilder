Ragstone Text

Text serif · regular and italic · 168 matrices

Drawn for a poetry press that sets 10 pt on a 12 pt leading on uncoated stock. The descender is cut short so the lines can sit close without touching; the serifs are sturdy enough to survive a soft packing. It is the face we cast most, and the one we recommend first for a book.

Bellwether Display

Display serif · medium · 94 matrices

A high-contrast face with hairlines thin enough to need a hard packing and a smooth stock. Cut for title pages and broadsides at 24 pt and above; below 18 pt the hairlines break up on anything softer than Zerkall. The capitals are cut slightly narrow so a long title can still be held in one line.

Testament Titling

Titling capitals · regular · 62 matrices

Pilcrow Mono

Monospaced · regular and medium · 88 matrices

The Whitechapel Review, masthead

Bellwether Display, modified · 1,200 copies

A quarterly review wanted a masthead that would hold at 96 pt across a 240 mm page and still print on a rough 90 gsm. We widened the M and thinned the hairlines by two thousandths, cut four new sorts, and pulled 40 proofs on the customer's own stock before the edition ran. The altered punch is held as a private sort.

Reading a specimen

What the numbers on a specimen sheet mean

A fount is counted in sorts, not in files. When we list 168 matrices for Ragstone Text, that is the punches and matrices held for that face: capitals, lower case, small capitals, both figure sets and the punctuation a compositor reaches for. A printed fount in a case is a subset, weighed out to the job.

Sizes are the sizes we cast, not the sizes a file can be scaled to. A 14 pt matrix cast at 72 pt would be a different drawing, and we would cut it differently: sidebearings, serif weight and the depth of the counter all change with the size.

Every retail face is available as a licence for desktop, web and app use, and as cast metal by the fount. The specimen is free; the type is not. Ask for a specimen and we will send a printed sheet on the stock you name, with the matrices listed on the back.
