03 — Commission

A face takes a year, and starts with your reading

Most commissions begin with a page that has to be set: a book, a label, a masthead. We ask for the text, the paper and the press before we sharpen a graver. The drawing follows the printing, not the other way round.

Start a commission →
  1. 01

    Brief and test setting

    Two weeks. You send the copy, stock and press. We return a written recommendation and three historical references, not three sketches.

  2. 02

    Drawing and key letters

    Six weeks. Ten control characters are drawn and proofed as photopolymer pulls before any steel is touched. Two revision rounds included.

  3. 03

    Punch, strike, justify

    Four to six months. Each punch is cut, struck into copper, justified and struck again. Matrices are corrected by hand under a lens.

  4. 04

    Cast, proof, deliver

    Six weeks. Type is cast to 0.918 in, proofed on your stock, and delivered with matrices, a specimen sheet and the digital outline.
