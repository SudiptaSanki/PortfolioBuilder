Part I · The catalogue

What the foundry sells

Three things can be ordered and one cannot. Every entry prints its production state, because a face takes the time it takes — and a catalogue that hides that is selling you a promise instead of type.

The standard page is 180 × 110 mm, set solid. Weight rises with the square of the body — double the point size and each sort weighs about four times as much. Weights are the foundry's own, weighed per hundred sorts on the bench scales.

    Part III · The price shape

    What an order costs, and what moves it

    The rates are printed, so the estimate can be checked. The written quote follows a paper proof; the deposit is half on orders over €500.

    Table R1 — the foundry's rates, 2026

    The tool

    Booking estimator

    The estimate, line by line

      1. 1

        Sorts count moves the price first. Pages times the sorts on a page — the planner above does this sum from the printed tables.

      2. 2

        Point size moves it second. Metal is paid for by weight, and weight rises with the square of the body: a 24 pt sort weighs four 12 pt sorts.

      3. 3

        New punches move it most. A character that does not exist is bought in days at the bench — €420 a day, one to three days a character.

      4. 4

        Carriage is by weight, at cost. A ten-page fount of 12 pt is 60 kg. Type travels as freight, in a crate, not as post — the crate is included, the freight is not hidden in the casting.
