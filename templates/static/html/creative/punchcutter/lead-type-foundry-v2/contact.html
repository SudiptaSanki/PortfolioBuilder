Before you write

The Sorts & Weight Planner will reckon your fount from the printed tables, and the foundry page states the terms — deposit, proof, carriage by weight, and what is hand work. Type metal contains lead; the handling note is printed there too.

The bench

A former stable off the Spaarne, Haarlem. Visits by appointment — write first, and bring the paper you print on.

Colophon

This specimen is set in Fraunces and Source Serif 4 — open-licence faces standing in for the foundry's own on screen. The foundry sells metal; every order is proved on paper before casting.

The fiction, stated

Hendrik Valk and the Valk Foundry are a fictional persona created for this portfolio template. The craft, the tables and the price shapes are true to the trade; no real foundry is depicted.

Sound & photographs

This site is silent — no audio is used, by ruling: a foundry portfolio has no soundtrack. Photographs are reference photographs, credited in CREDITS.md in this template folder; they are not the foundry's own workshop or castings.
