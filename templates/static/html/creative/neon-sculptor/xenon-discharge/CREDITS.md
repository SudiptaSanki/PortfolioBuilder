## Design notes

- Flat colour only. The design uses NO gradients, NO text-shadow or box-shadow
  "glow", and no glassmorphism: on a black ground, the light in the photographs
  does the glowing. Accent colours are the flat colours of the five fills
  (neon orange-red, argon blue, xenon blue-white, krypton green-white,
  helium yellow), used as chips and rules.
- Type pairing: Space Grotesk + Inter. IBM Plex Mono is not used.

## Sound ruling

NONE. A gallery portfolio is silent. No audio is loaded, synthesised or
played anywhere on this site; the ruling is printed in every footer.

## Persona

Maren Ilves / Ilves Discharge Lab is a fictional persona created for this
template, disclosed in every page footer. Master bender "R. Aho" is a
fictional collaborator role, credited per work as the brief's honesty spine
requires (self-bent vs commissioned bends are printed on every plate).
The craft content — gases, colours, loading, burn-in, failure modes — is
real discharge-tube practice; the planner's tables are the studio's stated
planning figures and the tools read them from the printed page.
