Persona: **Danny Reyes, painting as RAZE, Bristol** — a fictional practice, disclosed in the
footer of every page. The walls, clients and figures are realistic worked examples in 2026
prices; the estimator's arithmetic is the rate card printed on the home page, and the probe
re-checks it by hand (10 × 4 m, brick, Band B, coating on → 40.0 m² · 9.0 L · 7.0 days ·
tower scaffold · £7,595–£10,395).

## Sound

None. The sound need-test was applied: mural painting is not a sound profession, and nothing
here is taught better by audio. The stub's spray-can noise synthesis — an AudioContext created
on page load that hissed on every mouse move — was deleted. The stub's promised "interactive
spray wall" was decoration without a job; it was replaced by the wall estimator, a working
tool whose figures come from the published rate card.
