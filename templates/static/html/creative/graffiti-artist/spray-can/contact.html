Tell the studio about the wall

Three things matter at this stage: where it is, whose it is, and roughly how big. Everything else — design, surface, price — comes out of the survey. Currently booking walls from March 2026; two slots are held for council programme work each season.

Write directly

commissions@razewalls.example

Email reaches the same desk as the form. If you already have measurements, a photograph of the wall, or a council programme reference, attach them — they shorten the survey by half.

What happens next

  1. A reply within two working days, with survey dates — not a sales call.
  2. The survey visit: measurements, moisture and tap tests, photographs, and notes you keep either way.
  3. A written figure that names the surface, the access and the paint system, priced from the published rate card.
  4. Design and approvals only after the figure is accepted — the £350 design fee is credited against the painting if the wall goes ahead.

Demonstration form: it checks your answers and confirms them on this page, but sends and stores nothing. For a real enquiry, email commissions@razewalls.example with the reference it gives you.
