One path in — use it early

Report a sign

Owners, neighbours, tenants, history societies, and demolition crews with a conscience all report signs here. You do not need to own a sign to save it; you need to know where it is and what is about to happen to it. The archive restores and records existing signs — for a brand-new sign bent to order, you want a glass bender, and the archive will happily point you at one.

Give your name, so the reply has someone to greet.

What happens next

A reply within two working days. If the sign is threatened, the reply proposes a survey date before it discusses anything else; the survey is £350, fixed, and buys the written condition report described in the index.
