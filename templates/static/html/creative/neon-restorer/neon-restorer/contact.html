Enquiries · bench visits by appointment

Send a photograph of the can and the nameplate

Those two pictures tell me more than a paragraph. The nameplate dates the core and often the shop that made the sign; the can shows whether the face can be saved or has to be rebuilt.

What to send

Photographs: the whole sign, the face close up, the back of the can, the transformer nameplate, and any maker’s mark. Measurements: width, height and depth of the can, and the length of any surviving tube.

If it still lights, a short video helps — flicker and colour tell me a great deal about the gas and the core.

A condition report is quoted at a fixed fee and credited against the restoration if you go ahead. Restoration itself is quoted by the tube, plus parts at cost.

Crating and shipping

Signs travel in a timber crate with the tubes removed and packed separately. I crate in the shop and can arrange freight within the Southeast; further afield, I will work with your carrier and provide the packing specification.

Bench visits

The shop is on Riverside Drive, behind the old garage. Visits are by appointment, Tuesday to Saturday. There is hot glass and lethal voltage on the bench, so children stay behind the yellow line and dogs stay in the office.
