01The frame budget

Sheet B1 · Rev 2026.11

Every scene I ship is planned against this table before any art is made. The costs are my planning figures for standalone, Quest-class hardware at 90 fps. PCVR budgets are set separately per title and never blended with these — a PC number and a standalone number averaged together is a lie with decimals.

The reserve is not padding for my mistakes — it is the headset’s own compositor and OS time, and it is spent whether I plan for it or not. Check the viewport above against this table: 4.8 + 1.2 + 1.5 + 0.9 + 0.5 + 0.8 = 9.7 ms. The graph and the table cannot disagree; they are the same numbers.

    02What contract work costs

    Sheet F1 · Rev 2026.11

    Contract VR development at a day rate of €780. What moves the price is scope and platform count — production days and headset families — not art style: a scene’s cost is its frame time, not its polygon count. Milestones are fixed: 20% on signing · 30% at playable prototype · 30% at vertical slice · 20% at store certification.
