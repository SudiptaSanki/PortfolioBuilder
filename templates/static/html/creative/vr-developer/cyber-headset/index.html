How it gets built

Three rules I do not break

R1

Never move the camera

If the player did not move their head, the world does not move under them. Every cut, every vehicle, every elevator gets a cockpit or a fade. This one rule removes most of the nausea complaints before playtesting starts.

R2

Measure the floor, then trust nothing

Guardian bounds are a suggestion. I sample the head pose for the first thirty seconds and build a floor confidence value; if it wobbles, the game lowers the player’s reach instead of snapping them to an imaginary plane.

R3

Budget latency like a feature

Motion-to-photon is on the sprint board next to the mechanic it serves. A grapple that arrives 12 ms late is a different mechanic, and no amount of polish hides it.
