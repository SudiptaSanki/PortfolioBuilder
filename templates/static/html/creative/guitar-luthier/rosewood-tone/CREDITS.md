A note on honesty: these are stock photographs of lutherie and of guitars.
They show the work — the bench, the finishing room, tops, fretboards, the
wood store — and are captioned on the site for what they show. They are not
portraits of the fictional workshop's numbered instruments, and the luthier
in the photographs is not the fictional Silas Merritt. The site's footer
states the same.

There is deliberately no audio anywhere on this site. A luthier's work is
sound, but any playable recording would be a real instrument misrepresented
as this fictional maker's work; the portfolio shows the making instead.
