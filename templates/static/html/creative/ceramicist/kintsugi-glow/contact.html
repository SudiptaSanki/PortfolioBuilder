Describe the break

An assessment needs four things: what the piece is, how many breaks it has, how big it is, and what happened to it. The form asks for exactly those.

Photographs come second. Once your enquiry arrives, the studio replies and asks for daylight photos — every shard in the frame, a coin beside the piece for scale. The written assessment and fixed quote follow within two working days of the photos.

If a shard is missing entirely, say so in your message. Missing pieces are routine work — they are rebuilt in sabi-urushi — but the studio would rather know before the quote than after.
