## Everything else

- The favicon is a self-drawn inline SVG (the character 金 in gold
  on charcoal). No icon libraries, no third-party CSS or JS.
- No sound is used anywhere in this template, by design.
- The studio, its artist (Hina Mori), its clients and its
  restorations are a fictional persona created for this portfolio
  template, and the site footer says so. The craft facts —
  materials, stages, curing, care — are true to traditional
  kintsugi practice.
