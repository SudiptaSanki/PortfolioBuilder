Ellery Ground / Work

Built work · 2019–2026

Five gardens, and what each one had to prove.

Every project here is in the ground and under a care plan. The figures are from the as-built drawings and the first two years of monitoring, not from a render.

01 / Courtyard

Alder Court

NE Portland · 2022 · 0.18 ha

Twelve townhouses around an asphalt car park. We took out 240 m² of hardstanding, dished the centre into a rain garden, and planted eight Oregon white oaks against the south wall. The courtyard air is 3.8 °C cooler than the street on an August afternoon.

02 / School ground

Larkspur Primary

Gresham · 2024 · 0.6 ha

A 320-pupil primary school with a bare tarmac playground and one shade tree. We added a 180 m² covered outdoor classroom, a native hedgerow along the fence, and a 90 m² curricular garden that the Year 4 classes plant and harvest. Playground surface temperature dropped from 51 °C to 34 °C.

03 / Rooftop

Rainier Terrace

Central Eastside · 2023 · 210 m²

A fifth-floor terrace over a print works, exposed to wind from the river. The brief asked for a garden that could survive a weekend away and a 90 km/h gust. We used 42 cm deep containers, a wind-tolerant palette of sedges and currants, and a 3.2 m² water butt fed from the parapet drain.

04 / Hillside

Willamette Foothills House

West Linn · 2021 · 0.42 ha

A 1-in-4 slope above a salmon creek, with an eroding gully and a lawn that could not be mown safely. We laid out four swales on the contour, replanted the gully with native willow and dogwood, and replaced the lawn with a meadow that is cut once a year.

05 / Pocket park

Moss Street Pocket Park

SE Portland · 2026 · 0.09 ha

A vacant lot between two houses, bought by the neighbourhood association after a decade of fly-tipping. The design is a single meadow bowl, a bench made from a felled street tree, and a path that lets a wheelchair reach the middle. It opened in April and is already used by four households for lunch.
