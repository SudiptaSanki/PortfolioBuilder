Questions we are asked first

What does a garden cost, all in?

Design fees are fixed after the site walk: typically $3,800–$7,500 for a private courtyard, $9,000–$18,000 for a school or community site. Construction is tendered separately and usually lands between $180 and $420 per m² of planted ground, depending on access and hardscape.

How long does it take?

Site walk to planting is usually 4–7 months: 2–3 weeks of survey and testing, 6–10 weeks of design, 2–4 weeks of tender, then the contractor's programme. Planting is best done October–March, so a spring enquiry usually plants the following autumn.

Do you work outside Portland?

Yes, within about 60 km: Gresham, Beaverton, Lake Oswego, West Linn, Oregon City and the Columbia gorge as far as Corbett. Further afield we will say so honestly, because the establishment visits matter more than the drawing.

Can you work with our architect or builder?

Usually, and earlier is better. We need the finished floor level, the drainage strategy and the roof water outlets before the slab is poured. If those are fixed, we can still design the soil and planting but not the levels.

What if we only want a planting plan?

That is a normal commission. We charge a fixed fee for the survey, palette, planting plan and a setting-out visit, with the contractor appointed by you.
