Encomendas · Commissions

Enquire about a panel.

Tell the studio about the wall. You will get drawing questions and a firing slot — in that order — and the price in writing before any deposit.

The order book, November 2026: bespoke panels are booking for March 2027 (8–12 weeks from an approved drawing). Catalogue panels: 4–6 weeks. Restoration singles: 6–10 weeks, because matching takes the firings it takes.

Prefer email?

Write to oficina@inesduarte.example — the same questions apply. Enquiries are answered within three working days, usually with questions about the wall rather than a price, because the price depends on the answers.

What to send

  1. A photograph of the wall, straight-on if you can — and for restoration, a close photograph of one surviving tile.
  2. Width and height in centimetres. Panels are built from 15 cm tiles, so the drawing rounds to the nearest tile.
  3. Where the wall lives — interior or exterior, and whether it gets frost, steam or direct sun.
