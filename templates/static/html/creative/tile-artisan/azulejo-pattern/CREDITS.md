## Pattern motifs — original SVG

Estrela, Flor, Onda and Losango are original geometric/floral repeats drawn in
code for this template, in the azulejo manner. No historical panel, museum
photograph or third-party artwork was copied or traced.

## Fonts — Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License)

- Cormorant Garamond — display
- Archivo — body
- IBM Plex Mono — figures, labels, tombstones

## Sound

None. Tile painting is not a sound profession and no audio teaches anything
here; the owner's sound need-test failed for this template, and the stub's
ambient canvas synth was removed.
