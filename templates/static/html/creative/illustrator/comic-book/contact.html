What to send

A script or a two-page synopsis, the format you are printing (or “digital only”), your deadline, and any hard budget ceiling. If you have reference for the look, send three images you love and one you hate. The one you hate tells me more.

If I am booked, I will name two other inkers and tell you which of them I would use for your particular script.
