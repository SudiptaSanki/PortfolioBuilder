How a page gets made

Four passes, and the third one is where it hurts

  1. 01

    Thumbnails on the script

    Twenty-two pages at 60 mm wide, drawn in blue pencil in one sitting. If a page cannot be read at thumbnail size, no amount of rendering will rescue it.

  2. 02

    Pencils, then a phone call

    Full-size bristol, panels ruled and lettering blocked in first. I send the writer scans before inking so the dialogue can still change without scraping ink.

  3. 03

    Ink with a dead line

    Hunt 102 and a No. 2 sable. Spots go down before the black, because the black always wins the argument about emphasis.

  4. 04

    Scan, flatten, hand off

    600 dpi, cleaned, with flats on a separate layer per spot colour and a lettering layer that stays live text for the translator.
