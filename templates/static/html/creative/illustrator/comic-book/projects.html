Every job, including the small ones

Seventeen titles, and the one I still redraw in my head.

Interiors, covers and lettering for publishers in Mumbai, Bengaluru, London and Portland. Filter by the kind of work; each entry lists what I actually did on it rather than a vague credit.

2026

Bhairavi’s Bastion — issues 1–6

Six 22-page interiors and six covers over eighteen months. The fort needed to read as a character, so I built a physical cardboard model and lit it with a desk lamp for every establishing shot. Two spot colours throughout, which meant planning the black plate before the pencils were finished.

Crater Press132 pp totalInteriors + covers
2025

Salt Winter

Ninety-six pages, scripted and drawn by me. A road book that never leaves the truck cab, so most panels are interiors with a moving horizon. My inker’s wrist hated it; the lettering is deliberately quiet, with no bold except when one brother finally raises his voice on page 71.

Harbour & Ledger96 ppWriter + artist
2026

Monsoon Engine

Fourteen pages in the First Rain anthology. A lift mechanic keeps a flooded tower running with parts she invents. I hand-separated the two spot colours on acetate because the printer was experimenting with a risograph run; the misregistration on page 9 is real and I left it in.

First Rain anthology14 ppArtist + letterer
2024

Chandni Chowk Ghost Club — season two

Eight webcomic episodes at 900 × 1280 px, drawn for the scroll rather than the page. Vertical pacing means the reveal sits at the bottom of the third screen, not the bottom of a page; I built a template for it and then broke it whenever the joke needed air.

Self-published8 × 18 panelsArtist
2024

Kite Season — animated short, 7 minutes

210 storyboard panels and a colour script for a rooftop chase across four buildings. The director asked for the boards to be readable at thumbnail size on a phone, so every panel carries one clear silhouette and no more than two values.

Tidepool Animation210 panelsStoryboard artist
2023

The Last Ration Card

A 64-page non-fiction comic about a family’s paperwork across three decades, written by a journalist who kept every receipt. I drew it in a looser brush style than my serial work and lettered it by hand so the six different bureaucracies each got a slightly different voice.

Ledger Room Books64 ppArtist + letterer
2023

“The Fourth Wall Is a Door”

Six pages for a benefit anthology on housing. A single continuous shot from a doorway to a stairwell, built as one long panel folded across three pages — the gutter falls where the door slams.

Open Rooms anthology6 ppArtist
2022

Night Shift — issues 1–4

My first monthly. A hospital porter who sees the building rearrange itself after 2 a.m. Four 24-page issues at 100 lpi on newsprint, which taught me what a 0.3 mm line actually looks like once it is printed on toilet paper.
