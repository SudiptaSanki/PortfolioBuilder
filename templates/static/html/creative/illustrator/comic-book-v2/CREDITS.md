# Credits — comic-book (Lena Moreau, comic book illustrator)

## Artwork
All artwork on this site is **original SVG drawing created for this
template** — the rooftop splash and its four process passes, the
lighthouse and viaduct case panels, the cover thumbnails and final
cover, and the desk drawing on the About page. No photography is used
anywhere, and no other artist's pages, characters or compositions are
reproduced. The drawn panels are the template's own illustrative
comic pages and are captioned as such on the site.

## Persona
Lena Moreau is a fictional illustrator, disclosed as fictional in the
footer of every page. The books, clients, editors and figures are
illustrative inventions; the page rates, pace table and terms are the
template's working example, true to how comic production is scheduled
and credited in practice.

## Fonts (Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License 1.1)
- **Bangers** — display lettering (masthead, panel headings, cover).
- **Patrick Hand** — caption boxes, labels and hand notes.
- **Source Sans 3** — body text.

## Sound
None. Ruled and printed on the site: a comic is read in silence, so
there is no audio on any page.

## Code
Hand-written HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript for this template. No
frameworks, no libraries, no stock assets.
