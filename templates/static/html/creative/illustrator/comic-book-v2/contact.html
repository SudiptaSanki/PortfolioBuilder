After You Write

What happens next

1 · The arithmetic

Your page count and date are run through the printed pace table — the same one on the home page. If the book fits the slot, the reply says so with the schedule attached. If it doesn't, the reply says that too, with the three options the planner prints.

2 · The conversation

A short call or letter exchange about the book itself — the script's state, the format, who else is on the team and how they will be credited. No pitch theatre; the case files are the pitch.

3 · The booking

Terms drawn from the About page, a schedule both sides sign, and the 50% deposit that promises the slot. From that date, your book is the book — one at a time, in order.
