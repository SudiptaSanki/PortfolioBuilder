Every bend is a decision you can’t unbend.

Havel Tube Works is a one-bench neon workshop. Tomas Havel draws the pattern full-size, bends every letter himself over the ribbon burner, fills, burns in and hangs the piece. No CNC, no LED imitation, no subcontracted bending — the hands on this page are the hands on your sign.

    What moves the price

    bends and metres first — everything else is small print

    A neon price is mostly two numbers: how many bends, and how many metres. After that come the honest extras — electrodes per separate tube unit, transformers, mounting, and installation days. One line surprises people: the breakage allowance. Glass breaks at the bench, usually on the hardest bend of the best letter. On script and complex work the allowance is 7% of the tube line, stated here and agreed before any deposit — never discovered on the invoice.
