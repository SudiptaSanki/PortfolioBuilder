Before a site visit

  • A photograph of the wall, straight on if you can.
  • The width and height you have, in feet or metres.
  • What the wall is made of, and whether it is landmarked.
  • Where the nearest power is, and whether the circuit is switched.
  • Any drawing of the lettering, or the name of the typeface.

Installation and permits

We are a licensed electrical contractor in New York, so we can pull the sign permit and do the electrical work. On a landmarked building the Landmarks Preservation Commission approval is the long part; allow eight to twelve weeks before we bend anything.

Tubes are mounted on fire-rated standoffs with an air gap, on a switched and labelled circuit. The transformer is UL 2161 listed and dead-tested before it is energised.

Cost

Single-line storefront tubes from $1,800. Multi-colour or complex bends to $6,000, before installation. Repairs from $350. Installation quoted after the site visit.
