How a tube is made

  1. BendRibbon burner at 760 °C, glass drawn by eye against a full-size paper pattern.
  2. BombardVacuum, heat and high voltage drive the impurities out of the glass.
  3. FillGas at 6–27 torr, sealed at the electrode, then aged for 24 hours.
  4. InstallTransformer sized to footage, UL-listed, on a fire-rated support.
