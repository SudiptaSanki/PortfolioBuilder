# Credits — Nautical Prestige (Byrne Naval Architecture)

Persona: **Aoife Byrne, Byrne Naval Architecture, Cowes** — a fictional practice, disclosed in the footer of every page. The vessels, clients and figures are realistic worked examples in 2026 prices; the arithmetic (ranges, refit totals, running-cost totals) is internally consistent and checkable on the pages. Photographs are stock photography from Pexels, used illustratively; every caption on the pages describes literally what the photograph shows, and no stock photograph is captioned as a named vessel in the register.

## Sound

None. The sound need-test was applied: a naval architect's profession is not sound, and no audio teaches anything here that the figures don't teach better. The stub's hover-triggered "water chime" (an AudioContext created on page load, played on mouseenter) was deleted, as was its decorative wave canvas — replaced by drawing-layer toggles and a speed/range figure tool that do actual work.

## Drawings

The three vessel profiles are hand-coded SVG line drawings made for this template — schematic profiles in the manner of a general-arrangement sheet, not depictions of real vessels.
