Enquiry noted — reference BNA-2026-000

On the live site this is where your confirmation would appear, with the reference above and a reply promised within two working days. As this is the demonstration form, nothing has been sent — please use the email address opposite for a real enquiry.

Direct

studio@byrnenaval.example

One inbox, read by the designer. If your enquiry is about a boat you are buying, attach the particulars sheet and the last survey — the figures are the conversation.

What to send

  • Length overall and sail or power
  • Where she will actually be sailed
  • Who actually crews her
  • The honest budget, if you have one — it changes the advice, never the welcome
  • Any deadline that is real (a build slot, a season) as opposed to hoped

Fees, again

  • Feasibility package — £7,500 fixed
  • Full design — 6.5% sail / 5.5% power of build contract
  • Audits & representation — £950 / day
  • First call — free, 30 minutes, no obligation either way

Availability

One design slot open from spring 2027. Audits and figure-checks are taken year-round, usually within a month. If we are not the right office, the reply will say who is.
