## Photography (Pexels)

All photography is from Pexels and used under the Pexels licence. Each photo
page was checked individually on 2026-11-20 and showed the "Free" licence at
the time of download. Captions on the site describe literally what each
photograph shows; the photographs show the bench, the materials and historical
leaves the studio's work is modelled on — they are not presented as
photographs of the fictional pieces themselves.

## Type

Cinzel, EB Garamond, UnifrakturMaguntia and Italianno — all via Google Fonts,
all SIL Open Font License 1.1. The specimen alphabet in the gallery is set in
type (UnifrakturMaguntia), not photographed.

## Everything else

- Favicon: drawn for this template (a rubricated G in a gold rule, inline SVG).
- Sound: none, by design. Illumination is not a sound profession, so the
  template carries no audio.
- Eleanor Vane is a fictional persona created for this portfolio template,
  as disclosed in the footer of every page. The craft, materials, processes
  and prices described are true to the trade.
