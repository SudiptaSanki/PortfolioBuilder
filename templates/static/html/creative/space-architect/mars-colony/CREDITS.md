# CREDITS — Solberg Habitats (mars-colony)

Persona disclosure: Maren Solberg and Solberg Habitats are fictional.
The studies, clients, campaigns and testimonial on this site are
invented for this portfolio template. The engineering values are real
published figures, cited in place on the About page (MSL RAD,
ISS ECLSS reporting, Kilopower/KRUSTY, Phoenix/Curiosity soil
chemistry, lander meteorology). No habitat described has been built
for Mars; the one "built" case study is an Earth analogue station.

Sound: none. The stub's hover synth was deleted — habitat design is
not a sound trade and fails the program's sound need-test.
