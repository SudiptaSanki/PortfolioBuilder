Visits

By appointment, Tuesday to Thursday. The bench is small and the optical table cannot be covered, so we do not run open days.

Instruments can be viewed in the studio before a commission is agreed; rooms must be seen on site.

What a site needs

  • A ceiling height of at least 2.8 m for a walk-in room.
  • Ambient light that can be dimmed or blacked out.
  • A floor that can take 60 kg/m² for a suspended instrument.
  • Power for dimmable LED drivers, or a plan for where they go.
  • Access: the largest mirror panel must fit through the door.

Lead times

  • Instrument: 6–10 weeks from drawing sign-off.
  • Room: 12–20 weeks, plus 3–5 nights on site for alignment.
  • Exhibition work: by negotiation, and we will say no if the dates do not allow a proper alignment.

After installation

Every room is handed over with an alignment record and a maintenance note. We return once within the first year to check that the panels have not moved; after that, an annual check is enough.
