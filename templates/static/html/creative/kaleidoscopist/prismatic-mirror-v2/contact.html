The Bench Itself

Visiting, and posting a repair

Visits are by appointment, Tuesday to Friday — write first, and the bench will put the kettle and the test fields out. The Mirror Bench, Unit 4, The Old Tannery Yard, Frome, Somerset. While a mirror set is being aligned the bench is closed to visitors; alignment cannot be hurried by an audience.

Repairs by post: pack the scope as you would pack a camera lens — cell end padded, the instrument unable to move in its box — and include a note with the fault as you see it and the address for its return. The bench writes back with a quote before any work begins, and no repair proceeds without the owner's word. If a scope proves beyond honest repair, it is returned uncharged with the reasons written out in full.
