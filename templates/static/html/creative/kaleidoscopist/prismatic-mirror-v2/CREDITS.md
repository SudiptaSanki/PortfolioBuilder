- The hero kaleidoscope viewer (index.html) is original SVG geometry drawn for
  this template: a two-mirror system at 30°, twelve sectors generated by
  rotation and reflection of one drawn wedge of flat shapes. It is labelled on
  the page as an approximation of looking through a real scope.
