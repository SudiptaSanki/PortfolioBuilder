Why the maths comes first

Kinetic work fails in two ways that have nothing to do with taste: it stops moving, or it moves when it should not. A bearing that is right in July grinds in January; a rotor sized for a courtyard becomes a sail in a funnel between two buildings. So the studio begins every commission with the motion, not the form.

The model is deliberately simple. A pendulum’s period depends on its length and nothing else that matters at this scale:

T = 2π √(L / g)

Set a row of lengths with a constant step and the phases drift at a constant rate. That drift is the piece. The step controls how many standing shapes appear per beat; the count controls how finely the eye reads the wave. Everything else — the bobs, the colours, the mounting — is there to let that arithmetic be seen.

Once the motion is fixed, the structure is sized. Wind pressure for the site, projected area at the worst angle, drag coefficient, then a safety factor of 1.4 on a non-building structure. The mast is checked in bending and fatigue; the anchors are checked in pull-out; the bearings are checked for the load they will actually see, which is usually far less than the catalogue suggests.

Safety, maintenance and the long second act

A public kinetic piece is a machine in a place where people lean. The studio designs for that from the first sketch: no accessible pinch points, nothing that can be climbed, all fasteners tamper-resistant, every moving element either above 2.4 m or guarded.

  • Operating cut-out at 17.4 m/s for wind-driven works, with a controlled park position.
  • Two inspections a year, plus annual dye-penetrant testing on primary welds.
  • Bearing replacement interval logged at 20 years, with the first set of spares supplied at handover.
  • An O&M manual in the client’s language, with a laminated release procedure for staff.

Maintenance is quoted separately and is not optional for outdoor pieces. A work that is not maintained becomes a liability within five years, and the studio would rather decline the commission than see that happen.
