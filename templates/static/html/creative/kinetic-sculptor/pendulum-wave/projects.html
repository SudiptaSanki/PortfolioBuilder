W-14

Phase Field III

2019

For a bank atrium in Zürich, a field that starts the working day. Thirty-two brass bobs on Dyneema, released by a single gate at opening; the beat period was set to 24 seconds so the piece passes through three standing shapes before the first meeting ends.

Client
Beller Bank, Zürich
Setting
Atrium, 11 m void
Motion
hand-released, unpowered
Span / drop
6.40 m / 3.10 m
Elements
32 × 1.8 kg brass
Completed
March 2019
W-27

Rotor Wall

2023

Forty-eight anodised discs on a stainless spine for a Rotterdam office courtyard. Each disc runs on a sealed POM bush; the array moves at 5 km/h and parks itself above 17.4 m/s, which happens about nine days a year on that corner.

Client
Kade 12 development
Setting
Courtyard funnel, coastal
Motion
wind-driven, park position
Span / height
9.80 m / 7.20 m
Elements
48 × 2.1 kg discs
Completed
June 2023
W-33

Drift Line

2025

A floating line at the edge of Copenhagen’s harbour bath, baffled so that boat wake reads as slow breathing rather than slap. Anodes are on a twenty-year renewal schedule and the whole line can be towed to the yard for inspection in one tide.

Client
Havnebade, Copenhagen
Setting
Harbour pool, tidal
Motion
wave-driven, baffled
Length
12.00 m, five rods
Elements
5 × 46 kg floats
Completed
May 2025
W-05

Cascade

2016

The first commission: four stepped arcs in a Basel stairwell, released by a cord at the top of the flight. It has run for ten years on two bearing changes and is the piece that set the studio’s maintenance rule — two visits a year, whether the client asks or not.

Client
Privat, Basel
Setting
Stairwell, 5 floors
Motion
hand-released
Span / drop
3.60 m / 4.20 m
Elements
4 arcs, 18 bobs
Completed
October 2016
W-30

Horizon Study

2024

Ten vertical aluminium fins on a shared shaft for a Lyon media library, geared to turn together through 90°. At rest they read as a solid screen; a light wind opens them into a line. The drive is a single 24 V worm gear with a manual release.

Client
Médiathèque Part-Dieu
Setting
South façade, urban
Motion
geared, 24 V + manual
Span / height
7.00 m / 4.60 m
Elements
10 fins, 62 kg each
Completed
November 2024
W-36

Still Water

2026

A shallow basin in a Bordeaux courtyard, ringed with floats that take their motion from a single paddle wheel under the water. The rings fall out of phase from the centre outward, so the piece reads as a slow pulse rather than a wave.

Client
Cour Mably, Bordeaux
Setting
Basin, 180 mm depth
Motion
paddle wheel, 12 V
Diameter
3.60 m, 3 rings
Elements
36 floats, 316L
Completed
April 2026
