Enquire

Tell me the site first

The object comes second — the survey decides what the site can carry, and sometimes the honest answer is a different piece, or none. Enquiries are answered within three working days, usually with questions about fetch, height and who cuts the grass.

Height above ground, what surrounds it, and who the piece is for will carry the first reply further than a mood board.

This form is a demonstration: it checks your answers and shows a confirmation here, but transmits nothing and stores nothing. The working address is beside it — email reaches the bench directly.

Before you write

The planner and estimator on the Field page will give you the shape of a field and a price before any conversation. The case files include the refusal — read it if your site is a courtyard, a atrium or anywhere the air stands still, because the survey may agree with the building.

Current bench: two garden pendulums in fabrication; the next field slot opens after the spring installations. Inspections of existing pieces are booked to March 2027.

Alting Kinetic Works is the practice of Pieter Alting, a fictional kinetic sculptor. This portfolio is a design template: the practice, clients and commissions are invented — the physics, the wind classes and the arithmetic are real.

Sound: none. This site ships no audio. A real field, heard from where a visitor stands, is mostly wind and bearing, and neither records honestly — the ruling is deliberate, and printed here rather than implied.
