What happens next

  1. A reply within three working days, usually with two questions about your ceiling and your air handling.
  2. A site conversation — video call, or a survey visit at the day rate for complex buildings.
  3. A working 1:10 maquette, fixed fee, credited in full against the commission if you proceed.
  4. A fixed written quote — price, noise figure, service plan — before any fabrication begins.
