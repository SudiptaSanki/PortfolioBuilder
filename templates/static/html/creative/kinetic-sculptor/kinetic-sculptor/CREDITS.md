# Credits — Elias Brandt (Kinetic Sculptor)

Persona: **Elias Brandt, Brandt Kinetic, Sheffield** — a fictional sculptor, disclosed in the
footer of every page. The works, clients and figures are realistic worked examples in 2026
prices; the balance explorer's arithmetic is real (torque = weight × arm; tilt drawn at
40 kg·cm per degree, clamped at the arm's 14° of travel), and the probe re-checks it by hand
(3.0 kg × 96 cm = 288 kg·cm vs 2.0 kg × 144 cm = 288 kg·cm → in balance; fulcrum dragged to
120 cm → 360 vs 240 kg·cm → +120 net → tilt clamped logic and readout asserted).

## Drawings & marks

The balance diagram (Fig. 02) is an original SVG drawn in code for this template — a mobile
arm with vane cluster, sub-beam and dimension lines, re-drawn by the page's own script as the
visitor changes weights and fulcrum. The header's suspended nav (links hanging from a beam
rule on wires of different lengths) is CSS. The favicon is an inline SVG balance mark made
for this template.

## Sound

None. The sound need-test was applied: sculpture is not a sound profession, and where noise
matters to this trade it is stated as measured text (dB(A) at 3 m) rather than performed.
The stub's always-on ambient canvas animation was deleted; on this site, movement happens
only in the balance explorer, only when a visitor starts it, and never under
prefers-reduced-motion.
