Tell me about the opening, not the picture

The picture comes later, and it comes from the building. What I need first is the size of the opening, which way it faces, and what the room needs to do at four in the afternoon.

What happens next

I answer enquiries on Mondays and Thursdays. If the project looks like a fit, the next step is a site visit or a set of measured photographs, then a fee proposal for the design stage.

There is no charge for the first conversation, and no obligation after it.

Lead times

A single domestic window is usually four to six months from approved cartoon. A church or library scheme runs twelve to twenty-four months, most of it the building’s own approval process rather than the glass.

Restoration surveys

For a historic window I produce a condition report with a photograph of every panel, a lead-failure map and a costed schedule of repairs. The report is accepted by several diocesan advisory committees and by the Chicago landmarks commission.
