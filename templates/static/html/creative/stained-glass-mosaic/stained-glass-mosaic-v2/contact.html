Enquiries

Start with the wall as it is.

One contact path, kept deliberately plain: email the studio, or use the form. Either way you reach the bench, not a sales desk — the studio is three people and the person who replies is the person who will draw your cartoon.

Email the studio

maren@solbergglass.example

What to send

The building and where it is; the opening's rough size if you know it (a guess is fine — survey comes later); and a photograph of the wall as it is now. A photograph of the wall in ordinary daylight tells the studio more at this stage than any measurement.

If your building is a church

Say which diocese. Faculty timing differs between dioceses and the studio plans design slots around it — the St Cuthbert's faculty took eleven months, and the case file says so.
