Enquiries

Kamakura, Kanagawa — Japan·Assessment queue open · Bench bookings into spring

Begin with photographs.

Every commission starts the same way: photographs of the blade and its story, read carefully and answered honestly — including when the answer is that a blade needs a smith, an appraiser, or to be left alone.

Write directly

enquiries@aoyama-togishi.jp

Replies within three working days. The workshop keeps bench hours, not office hours; visits are by appointment only.

Photographs to include

  • The whole blade, both sides, on a plain cloth, in daylight — no flash
  • The tang (nakago), both sides, with the peg removed if it comes freely — never forced
  • Close-ups of any rust, chips, cracks or bends, with a coin for scale
  • The scabbard and any papers, certificates or family notes that travel with the blade
  • One line of history: where the blade has been kept, and for how long

What happens next

  • A first reading from the photographs — often the whole answer
  • If warranted, an invitation to send or bring the blade for inspection
  • A written assessment: condition, options, fixed price, queue position
  • No obligation at any stage before the deposit
