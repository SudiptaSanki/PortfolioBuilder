What moves the price

  • Distance. Beyond 60 miles from Norwich: travel at 45p per mile and overnight accommodation at cost.
  • Double-show days. A second performance on the same day is charged at 60% of the first — the get-in is already done.
  • Crew. Fees assume the venue crew listed in each tech sheet. If your venue cannot supply a technician, we bring our own at £180 per day.
  • Blackout. If your space cannot black out and the show needs it, blackout installation is hired and passed on at cost — quoted before contract, never after.

How booking works

  1. Check the sheet

    Measure the show against your room using the explorer above. If it nearly fits, still ask — most near-fits have a known answer.

  2. Pencil the date

    Dates are held for 14 days on a first enquiry, free, while contracts are drawn up.

  3. Contract & 25% deposit

    The tech sheet is attached to the contract as an appendix, so what you read here is what we are obliged to bring.
