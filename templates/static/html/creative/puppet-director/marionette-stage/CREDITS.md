# Credits — marionette-stage (Ellery Puppet Theatre)

Persona: **Ruth Ellery / Ellery Puppet Theatre, Norwich** — fictional,
disclosed as fictional in the footer of every page. All productions,
venues, clients, people and fees on the site are illustrative and true
to the trade (footprints, get-ins, blackout and crew figures are the
real working data a puppet company tours on), but no real show,
company or person is depicted or sold.

## Design & artwork
- Design system, copy and all diagrams are original to this template:
  the hero elevation (Fig. 1), the per-production stage plans drawn by
  the spec explorer, and the four puppet-type diagrams on the Workshop
  page are hand-written inline SVG in the page code. No stock
  illustration, no icon font, no copied artwork.
- Typefaces (Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License): Fraunces, Archivo,
  IBM Plex Mono.

## Sound ruling
**No audio anywhere.** Puppetry's product is the show, not sound, so
the owner's sound need-test fails for this template by design. The
stub's mouse-follow spotlight was deleted; there is no hover audio,
no autoplay, no Web Audio. (The venue-provided PA listed in the tech
sheets is part of the fictional productions' riders, not site audio.)
