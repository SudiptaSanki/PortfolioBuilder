What to send

Venues & festivals

  • Your stage dimensions (width × depth) and clear height
  • Whether you can achieve a full blackout — and if not, say so early; two of our shows don't need one
  • Your dates, and whether a get-in on the show day is possible
  • Capacity and usual audience (family, schools, general)

Schools

  • Year groups and numbers per show (up to 120 on floor seating)
  • Hall size, and when we can get in — 45 minutes is the whole ask
  • Whether you want the 20-minute making workshop after the show

Commissions

  • The script or treatment, and what the puppet must do on stage
  • Character list, sizes you have in mind, and your opening date
  • Who will operate it, and how many performances it must survive

Repairs

  • Photographs of the damage, front and back
  • The puppet's type and, if it is one of ours, its bench-file number
  • Your next performance date — rush repairs are sometimes possible
