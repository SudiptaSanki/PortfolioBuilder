What happens next

  1. A reply inside two working days, opening with the three spike questions: the exact device, the room it lives in, and whose hands operate it.
  2. If the project is real, the spike is scoped at the fixed C$2,400 fee — one platform, one core interaction, two weeks.
  3. The spike report lands in writing: build, adapt or stop, with the measurements behind the verdict. The fee is credited if the build proceeds within 60 days.

Spikes are currently booked three to four weeks out. Audits and rescue work on existing installs are taken between spikes, at the day rate.
