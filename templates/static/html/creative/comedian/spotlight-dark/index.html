What moves the price

  • Travel — included within 60 miles of Manchester; beyond that, rail fare or 45p/mile, agreed before signing.
  • Late finish — sets ending after midnight add £100, except festival late shows where it is already in the fee.
  • Bespoke writing — material written for your company, town or occasion: from £300 a day, credited and agreed in advance.
  • Second set, same night — a second appearance on the same bill adds £150, not a second full fee.
  • Nothing else. No “plus expenses” creep: the quote you sign is the invoice you get.
