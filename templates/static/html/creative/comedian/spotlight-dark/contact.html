Bill № 04 — Bookings

Start a booking

One path, no friction: the form below, or the email beside it — both land with the same office. Every enquiry is answered within two working days, with the fee confirmed against the printed rate card and the date held for seven days while you decide.

Direct & agent

Bookings are handled by Hale & Frost Artists. For promoters already on the circuit, the direct line is faster:

bookings@marcusthorne.example

Agent: Hale & Frost Artists — office@halefrost.example
Diary & contracts: the agent. Everything else: the direct address.

What to send

  • The date — or the two or three you're choosing between.
  • The town and venue — room size if you know it; travel is quoted from this.
  • The format — spec it on the front page if you're unsure which fits.
  • The audience — staff party, club crowd, festival tent; and the rating you need.
  • Corporate? Say so early — the briefing call and kill-list are part of the fee, and diaries fill first in December.

What happens next

Day 0–2: reply with the confirmed fee and the date held. On agreement: a one-page contract, 30% deposit for corporate and festival work. Before the night: billing line, running order and tech confirmed with your venue. On the night: he arrives an hour early and leaves on time — the set, that is. He stays for a drink.
