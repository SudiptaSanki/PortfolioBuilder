Sheet 02 · The planner

Plan the melt before the deposit

Every commission is planned on this sheet before money moves. The three tables below are the practice's own planning tables — the tool reads them directly, so the arithmetic you see here is the arithmetic on your quote.

We plan to the detail, not the collapse. A piece that has lost its face has finished, whatever is still standing — so the lifespan on this sheet is the detail life, and it is the shorter, honest number.

The practice's rule for a short life: a smaller piece is carved last and installed at opening, so its clock starts when the gates open. Refrigeration moves a piece down a band — the verdict names the band you would need. Working law, printed on every sheet: saws stay behind a barrier at 1.5 m, and nobody carves alone.

Sheet 03 · The money

Blocks and temperature move the price

The fee shape, published. What moves the price: blocks and temperature first. A warm week costs more than a big piece — refrigeration, freezer days and a shortened promise all price the weather, not the carving.
