Book a carve · one path, no queue-jumping forms

Give me a date
and a forecast.

Competitions are entered a season ahead. Installations need three weeks for the blocks to grow. Live carving needs a barrier line and a crowd. Everything else is negotiable — start here, or write to hello@korpela-ice.example. Replies within two working days, from the carver, not a studio manager.

Sheet 08 · The enquiry

Put it on the sheet

Demonstration form: nothing is transmitted or stored. It validates and issues a sheet reference so you can see exactly how a booking starts; for a real commission, the email above reaches the same desk.

On the sheet

FC-2027-0000

Your enquiry is written up with this reference. The reply — within two working days — will carry the planner run for your date: carve hours, the band your forecast falls in, and the window I can honestly promise. If the melt table says no, the reply says no, with the arithmetic attached.

What happens next: the planner run comes back first, before any talk of deposits. If the numbers stand, we draw. If they don't, you keep the arithmetic — it is yours, and it will tell you the same thing whoever you ask next.
