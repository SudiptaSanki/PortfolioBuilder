What we need to quote

  • The date and the hours the piece must hold.
  • The room temperature, and whether a door or a heater is nearby.
  • Floor type and load limit, or the venue's technical contact.
  • The widest door and the lift, if there is one.
  • Lighting plan — warm LEDs close to the ice change the schedule.

Lead times

  • Single centrepiece: two weeks' notice, three in December.
  • Bar walls and tables: four to six weeks.
  • Outdoor builds: eight weeks, and weather-dependent.
  • February dates are booked by September.

On the night

We deliver with a drip tray and a subfloor where the venue needs one. Keep candles, halogen lamps and patio heaters at least a metre away. If the piece starts to slump, call the number on the delivery card before you move it.
