What we actually do for a commission

  1. Draw for the room

    We start with the floor plan, the ceiling height and the door width, because ice arrives as a solid object and cannot be tilted.

  2. Freeze and join

    Blocks are frozen over twelve days, then welded with slush and clear water. A welded seam disappears; a glued one shows.

  3. Carve cold

    Carving happens at −10 °C in the shop or in a freezer on site. Warm ice tears instead of cutting.

  4. Deliver and watch

    We deliver in a −18 °C truck, rig onto a drip tray, and stay for the first two hours to catch a structural crack before guests do.

“They arrived at four in the morning, built the bar wall in a freezer truck, and were still standing there at midnight with a torch, fixing a seam nobody else had noticed.”

Marit Solberg, event director, Cedar Lake

Send the date before you send the idea
