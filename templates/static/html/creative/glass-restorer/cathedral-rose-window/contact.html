Visiting the bench

Fabric committees and architects are welcome to see a window in treatment. Please write first: the lifting table is shared, and a panel on it cannot be moved for a visit.

Before a first visit

  • A photograph of the window from inside, taken square-on if possible.
  • The building, the dedication, and any date you have for the glass.
  • Who controls access: the fabric committee, the diocese, the DRAC, or a private owner.
  • Whether the scaffold is already up, and until when.
  • Any earlier report, even one you do not trust.
