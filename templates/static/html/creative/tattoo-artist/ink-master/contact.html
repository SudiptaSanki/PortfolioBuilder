The current state of the book

Books are open. Consultations are running within two weeks of enquiry; first sittings are about five weeks out. Large pieces — backs, sleeves, cover-ups over 15 cm — are taken on a short waitlist so healed stages are never rushed; ask and you will be given the real date, not a hopeful one.

Before you write

The most useful enquiries name the placement, a rough size in centimetres, and the idea in a sentence or two. Reference images help — send links rather than attachments. If you ran the readiness check, its starting point can be carried into the form automatically.

Deposits (£60–£150, off the final sitting) are only taken after a consultation, once a design direction and a fixed price are agreed in writing. Nobody will ask you for money through this page.
