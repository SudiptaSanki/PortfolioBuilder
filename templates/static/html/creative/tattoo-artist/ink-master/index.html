Before you book

What will it take?

Most first enquiries ask the same three things: how long, how many sittings, how much. Answer three questions and the book works it out with the same formula used at a real consultation — the arithmetic is printed beside the answer, and the rate card further down this page is the one it reads from. It gives you an estimate to start the conversation; the fixed price is agreed at consultation, in writing, before any deposit is taken.

One — Where on the body?
Two — Roughly how big?
Three — Which way of working?

The formula: hours = base hours for the size (1 / 2.5 / 5 / 9) × style multiplier (fine line 0.8 · blackwork 1.0 · traditional 1.1 · black & grey 1.4) × placement factor (ribs 1.2, ankle/foot 1.15, chest and back 1.1, thigh 1.05, elsewhere 1.0), rounded to the nearest half hour. Sittings are up to about four hours each. Price = hours × the style's hourly rate, £80 minimum, shown as a band of −10% to +10% because skin, detail and breaks move the real number.

How a piece happens

Five stages, in the book

  1. Consultation

    Thirty minutes, free. We look at the skin, the idea and the space it has to live in, and talk honestly about what will still read well in ten years. If the idea will not work as a tattoo, you will be told here — not after a deposit.

  2. Design & approval

    The drawing is made for your body measurements, shown to you before the sitting, and changed until it is right. Small changes on the day are normal; a new direction means a new drawing and a new date, at no extra design charge.

  3. Deposit & date

    The deposit secures the day and comes off the final sitting. You get the date, the start time and the preparation notes in writing — what to eat, what to wear, what to avoid the night before.

  4. The sittings

    Needles are opened in front of you; the stencil is placed, checked in the mirror, and only then does any ink happen. Breaks whenever you need them. Large work is staged over sittings spaced at least three weeks apart so each layer is judged healed.

  5. Aftercare & touch-up

    You leave with the aftercare sheet and the studio's email for healing questions — answered by Tom, not a bot. One touch-up is free within 12 months where aftercare was followed. The full aftercare instructions are on the studio page.
