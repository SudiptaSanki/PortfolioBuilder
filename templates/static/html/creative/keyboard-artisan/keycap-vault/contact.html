Questions

      
The five I answer most

    

    

      

        Will the cap fit my switch?
        
If it is Cherry MX or a clone with a 4.1 mm cross, yes — I test on three switches from different production years before packing. Kailh Box, Gateron, Outemu and JWK are all fine in my experience, with the caveat that some older Box stems run 0.1 mm wide and crack tight caps. Send one switch and I will fit to it specifically.

      

      

        How long does a commission take?
        
Five to nine weeks from approved sketch. The sculpt is the variable: a blank is four days, a carved motif is two to three weeks with your notes in between. Cure and QC add eleven working days that cannot be shortened.

      

      

        Can I have a full set?
        
Up to 40 caps from one mould, in one colourway per pot. Beyond that the mould wears and the colour drifts, so full 104-key sets go to injection moulders. I will happily make the six caps you actually look at.

      

      

        What if it breaks?
        
Stem fit is warranted for two years. Send a photo and the cap back and I will re-cast from the same mould and pot record. Accidental damage — a drop on a tile floor, a dog — is charged at the re-run rate.

      

      

        Do you ship outside the UK?
        
Yes. EU tracked is £14 and takes four to seven days; the rest of the world is quoted at cost. Customs forms are filled as “handmade resin keycap, £31”, which is the truth and keeps the parcel moving.
