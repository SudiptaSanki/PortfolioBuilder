Batch path

Five weeks, five stages

  1. 01

    Sculpt

    Wax master at 2:1, scanned at 12 µm. Six days, two rounds of your notes.

  2. 02

    Master & mould

    Silicone block mould, platinum cure, cut in four so the skirt casts without flash.

  3. 03

    Cast

    Pressure pot at 40 psi, 18 hours. Two pours per day, one colourway per pot.

  4. 04

    Stem & fit

    Socket reamed to 4.10 mm, trialled on three switches, logged against your board.

  5. 05

    Pack

    Boxed with the sprue, the fit card and a spare stem. Tracked post, £6 in the UK.
