The promises, as facts

Checkable, or not printed
Refund in full until production starts

Group-buy money is a preorder. Cancel any time before the run starts and the full amount returns within 5 working days — no reason needed, no form to argue with.

Delay news within 10 days of us knowing

When a run slips, buyers hear it within 10 days of the shop knowing, with the reason and a revised range. Drop 06's notice went out on day 9; the log is in the case files.

Extras only after every order ships

Leftover stock is sold as extras, labelled as extras, and never before the last group-buy order has left the bench. Counts are printed on the index.

30-day returns on in-stock lines

Unopened in-stock items return within 30 days; you pay the return post, we refund the item in full on arrival. Built-to-order boards are covered by the build terms instead.

Every tuned switch counted

Build sheets list the switch count, the spring weight fitted and the re-measured actuation after tuning. If the sheet and the board disagree, the board comes back at our cost.

The shop

Keycap Vault is the drop sheet of Sorin Switchworks — small-batch keycaps, switches and built boards, run by Ilya Sorin from a bench on SE Morrison Street, Portland, Oregon. Everything sold here moves in numbered drops or from counted extras.

Sound ruling

This site is silent, and so are our product pages. A sound test is a recording of a board you are not buying, in a room you are not in, through a microphone you do not own. We print measurements instead — see Table B1.
