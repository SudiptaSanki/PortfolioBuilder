Contact

One bench, one inbox

The fastest route is email, printed here in the open rather than hidden behind the form:

hello@keycapvault.example

01

Stock questions

Check the index first — it is the count, kept current with the shelf. If a line is not listed, it is not in stock, and an email will only get you the same answer slower.

02

Group-buy orders

Quote your drop number in the subject. Delay and refund positions are the printed ones on the honesty page — they apply to email answers too; nobody gets a better term by asking privately.

03

Builds and bench work

Run the planner first and paste its total into your message. The planner's price is the quoted price; the reply confirms a slot and a turnaround range.

Replies come from the bench, in bench hours — allow two working days. There is no ticket system and no autoresponder; a person reads it, and the person is also the one with the screwdriver.

The shop

Keycap Vault is the drop sheet of Sorin Switchworks — small-batch keycaps, switches and built boards, run by Ilya Sorin from a bench on SE Morrison Street, Portland, Oregon. Everything sold here moves in numbered drops or from counted extras.

Sound ruling

This site is silent, and so are our product pages. A sound test is a recording of a board you are not buying, in a room you are not in, through a microphone you do not own. We print measurements instead — see Table B1.
