01 · Rites

Three rites, and what a family actually has to organise

A ceremony is a sequence, and the sequence has an order that does not change. What changes is the size of the family, the size of the house, and whether the cooking is done at home or brought in. These are the three I am asked for most.

Vivaha · wedding

The full sequence over three to four hours: nichayathartham, muhurtam, kanyadanam, mangalya dharanam, saptapadi and the blessings. Two priests, three assistants, and a mandap that faces east if the site allows it. I will meet the family once before the day, and I ask that the groom's party and the bride's party both be represented at that meeting.

Griha pravesham · house-warming

Vaastu homam, the boiling of milk, the entry with the kalasam, and the first lamp lit in the kitchen. Two hours for a small house, three for a larger one. The family should have the door frame finished and the kitchen floor washed before the priest arrives, and the milk should be raw, not boiled.

Duration
2–3 hours
Hands
1 priest, 1 assistant

Shraddha · the annual rite

Performed on the thithi of the parent, at home or at the river. One and a half hours, with the family seated in the order of seniority and the offerings prepared the previous evening. I do not rush this one, and I ask families to keep the morning free of other work.

The dakshina figures are a guide for families who ask, not a tariff. A family that can give more should; a family that cannot should say so at the first meeting, and the rite is not smaller for it.

On muhurtam I do not fix a muhurtam from a website. The office reads the family's own panchangam and the priest confirms the window by telephone two days before. If your family follows a different panchangam, bring it.

On language The mantras are in Sanskrit; the explanations are in Tamil, and in English if there are children or guests who need it. Nothing is performed in a language the family cannot follow.

On travel Vadipatti, then Madurai, Dindigul, Theni and Chennai. Travel beyond Madurai district is charged at the bus or train fare, not by the kilometre.

    03 · At the temple

    The temple week

    These hours do not move for bookings. If a family needs me during a puja, the office will say so and offer the hour after.

    1. 06:00TiruvananthalDoors open, first lamp, abhishekam
    2. 08:00Kalasanthi pujaArchana for the day's names, prasadam after
    3. 12:00UchikalamSanctum closed for the afternoon
    4. 17:00SayarakshaiEvening lamp, the office receives families
    5. 20:00ArthajamamLast puja, doors close at 20:45

    Every Friday at 18:00 there is a special abhishekam for the Amman, and on the first Sunday of the month the Veda class for boys between seven and twelve meets in the mandap at 07:00. Both are open; neither needs a booking.

    The year Aadi and Purattasi are the busy months for griha pravesham. Karthigai brings the lamps, and Thai the weddings. If you want a Saturday in Thai, speak to the office in Purattasi.

    What the temple asks Nothing for darshan. The hundi goes to the temple's own account, which pays for the lamps and the rice, and is read out at the annual meeting.
