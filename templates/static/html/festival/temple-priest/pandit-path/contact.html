How a booking runs

  1. Write, or come to the office in the evening.
  2. The office calls you back at a time you name.
  3. A first meeting at the house or the veranda, an hour.
  4. The muhurtam is confirmed from the family's panchangam, in writing.
  5. The samagri list goes out ten days before the rite.

The dakshina

What a family gives is a gift, not a fee. The guides in this site exist because families ask and are embarrassed to ask twice. Travel and materials are quoted separately and in writing. Nobody is turned away for the size of the gift, and nobody is charged more for the size of the house.

What to bring to the first meeting

The family's panchangam if you keep one, the names and nakshatrams of the people being blessed, and the date you have in mind. If the rite is a shraddha, bring the thithi and the register entry from the previous year, if you have it.

If the date is close

Say so at the top of the message. The office keeps two evenings a week for families in difficulty, and a shraddha takes priority over a wedding when the thithi cannot move.
