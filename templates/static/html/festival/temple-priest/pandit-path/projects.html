Rites · six in the book

Each rite has an order. The order is the rite.

What follows is what the office actually needs from a family, in the sequence it is needed. Timings are for a household of ordinary size; a large wedding takes longer, and the office will say so before the date is fixed.

Tamil and Sanskrit Vadipatti · Madurai · Dindigul · Theni · Chennai Two priests for a wedding or upanayanam

Vivaha

விவாகம் · wedding

Nichayathartham, muhurtam, kanyadanam, mangalya dharanam, saptapadi and the blessings, in that order and without abbreviation. Two priests and three assistants. The mandap faces east where the site allows it; where it cannot, the office re-plans the seating rather than the rite. The family provides the bride's and groom's clothes, the garlands, and a room where the couple can sit quietly for a few minutes before the muhurtam.

Duration 3–4 hours
Hands 2 priests, 3 assistants
Prep 2 days
Dakshina ₹25,000–45,000

Griha pravesham

கிருக பிரவேசம் · house-warming

Vaastu homam at the threshold, the boiling of milk on the new stove, the entry with the kalasam, and the first lamp in the kitchen. The family should have the door frame finished, the floor washed, and raw milk in a vessel that can go straight onto the fire. If the house is still being painted, wait: the homam is not a blessing for wet walls.

Duration 2–3 hours
Hands 1 priest, 1 assistant
Prep 1 day
Dakshina ₹8,000–15,000

Upanayanam

உபநயனம் · sacred thread

For a boy between seven and twelve, with the family's own guru present if they keep one. Choulam, the thread, the Gayatri, and the first alms. The boy should be able to sit still for three hours; if he cannot, the office will split the ceremony over two mornings rather than rush it. Two priests, because one is speaking and one is watching the fire.

Duration 3 hours
Hands 2 priests, 2 assistants
Prep 2 days
Dakshina ₹12,000–22,000

Shraddha

சிரார்த்தம் · the annual rite

On the thithi of the parent, at home or at the river, with the family seated in order of seniority and the offerings prepared the previous evening. One and a half hours, and the morning kept free of other work. The office will come to the house; families who have moved to Chennai should say so early, because the date cannot move with the train.

Duration 1.5 hours
Hands 1 priest
Prep 1 day
Dakshina ₹3,000–6,000

Satyanarayana puja

சத்யநாராயண பூஜை

Usually on a full moon or a family anniversary, at home, with the story read aloud and prasadam shared afterwards. Two hours. The family supplies the fruit, the rava for the prasadam, and a clean space with a table for the picture. Children are welcome and should not be kept quiet.

Duration 2 hours
Hands 1 priest, 2 assistants
Prep 1 day
Dakshina ₹6,000–12,000

Navagraha shanti homam

நவக்கிரக சாந்தி ஹோமம்

For a family that has been advised to perform it, usually after a birth, a house purchase or a difficult year. One and a half hours, nine grains, nine lamps. The office asks to see the advice in writing, not because it doubts the astrologer, but because the homam differs according to which planet is being appeased.

Duration 1.5 hours
Hands 2 priests, 1 assistant
Prep 1 day
Dakshina ₹9,000–16,000

What the family prepares

Before the priest arrives

Most of the work of a rite is done by the family, and most of the difficulty is that nobody tells them which work. This is the list the office sends with every booking.

  • A clean space, swept and washed, with the lamp placed where the family will sit facing it.
  • The family's own panchangam, if they keep one, and the names and nakshatrams of the people being blessed.
  • Raw milk, unboiled, in a vessel that can go onto the fire.
  • Fruit and flowers bought the previous evening, not the morning of the rite.
  • Seating for the eldest first. The office will arrange the rest.
  • Someone in the family who can say, on the day, where the fire is allowed to be.

The office brings the samagri it owns: the kalasam, the homa vessels, the spoons, the lamp and the cloth for the seat. Everything consumable is bought by the family, and the day book lists the quantities.

What I do not do I do not fix a muhurtam from a website or over the telephone without the family's panchangam. I do not perform a rite for a family that has not met me once, except for a shraddha where the date is fixed by the thithi.

Where I travel Vadipatti and Madurai district as a rule. Dindigul, Theni and Chennai with notice. Beyond that, the family should ask and the office will say honestly whether the travel is possible.

Fees The dakshina is a gift, not a fee. The office quotes travel and materials separately, and gives a written note of both before the date.

The temple year

When the district marries, and when it mourns

The Tamil year sets the work of the office more than any calendar on a telephone. Families who want a particular month should speak to the office a season ahead; the good Saturdays go first.

  1. ĀdiJul–AugGriha pravesham and house work; weddings are avoided
  2. PurattasiSep–OctThe busiest month for new houses and upanayanam
  3. KarthigaiNov–DecLamps, temple festivals, few private rites
  4. ThaiJan–FebWeddings; book by Purattasi for a Saturday
  5. ChithiraiApr–MayThe Madurai festival season; the temple is full

Teaching The Veda class for boys between seven and twelve meets on the first Sunday of the month at 07:00 in the mandap. It is free and it is slow: one verse a month, recited until it is clean.

Records The office keeps a register of every rite since 1994: family, date, thithi and the priest who performed it. Families who have lost their own records can ask.
