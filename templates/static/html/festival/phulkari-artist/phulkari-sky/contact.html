What I need to begin

The names of the people it is for, the date of the wedding if there is one, and a third of the price for the khaddar and silk. I send a photograph of the cloth on the frame before the border is closed.

If the date is close

A bagh cannot be hurried. With less than six months I will suggest a dupatta or a chope instead, which are proper gifts in their own right and not a consolation.
