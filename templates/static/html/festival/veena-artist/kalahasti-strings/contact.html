Contact

Tell me the room, the date and who else is playing

A concierge, a sabha secretary or a host with a living room all get the same reply. I answer in the evening, after practice, which is usually by nine.

A date is confirmed when I write back, not when this form is sent.

Give me the shape of the evening: the hall or the room, who is accompanying, and whether there is a piano tuner nearby, because most halls that can seat two hundred cannot keep a veena in tune for two hours.

A demonstration form. In the live version it reaches sravani@kalahastistrings.in and the reply follows the same evening.

Technical rider

Sound
Two condenser mics on stands, or one DI if the hall has a pickup
Seating
A stool at 45 cm, no armrests
Space
Two metres behind the playing position for the case
Setup
Forty-five minutes before the doors open
Climate
No direct air conditioning on the instrument

Travel and notice

Local
Within 150 km of Srikalahasti included
Further
Train or car at actuals, one night's stay if needed
Sabha
Three weeks' notice
House concert
Two days is often enough
Season
December books by August

What I need from a host

A conversation about the audience before the programme is fixed. A hall of forty people who listen is a better evening than two hundred who do not, and I will choose the ragas accordingly.
