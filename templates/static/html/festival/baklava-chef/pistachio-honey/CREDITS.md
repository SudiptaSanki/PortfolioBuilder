## Sound ruling

**Fails the need-test — no audio anywhere.** A baklava chef's profession
is not sound and no interaction on this site teaches through audio.
The stub carried no audio promise worth keeping; its GSAP drift /
parallax / magnetic-button chrome was decoration without a job and
was deleted, not rebuilt. The footer states the ruling: "This site
makes no sound, by design."

## Stub replacements (honest substitutions)

- The stub's promised festival chrome (template.json tags:
  garland-lanterns, float-stars, scroll-drift, reveal-flip) was pure
  decoration — rising lanterns, twinkling stars, drifting scroll —
  and told a visitor nothing about the trade. It is replaced by the
  signature the trade actually earns: the **Harvest & Tray Explorer**
  (line × harvest state, asserted against the printed Table 1 and
  rate card on the same page) and the **ledger calculator** (harvest
  estimate as a range; order total with deposit). The festival warmth
  is kept where it belongs — in the Eid order book, the celebration
  box and the copy — not in floating ornaments.
- The stub persona ("Ingrid Schmidt", with content about calligraphy
  and lantern crafting that had nothing to do with baklava) is
  replaced by a fictional chef of the trade itself, disclosed as
  fictional in every footer.
- The vendored GSAP/ScrollTrigger files and the four decorative
  stub SVGs (garland, mandala, floats, footer waves) were removed
  with the chrome they powered. The rebuild is vanilla HTML/CSS/JS.

## Fiction & honesty notes

Yasmin Haddad, Forty Layers, the partner orchard and the partner
apiary are fictional. Harvest yields (pistachio alternate bearing,
per-hive honey ranges, ~20% honey moisture keeping standard, kernels
at about half of in-shell weight) are stated at established-practice
level, hedged as ranges. No health claims are made for honey or nuts
anywhere on the site — the About page says why, in print. The
infant-honey line ("not suitable for infants under 12 months") is
standard labelling practice and is printed on the site.
