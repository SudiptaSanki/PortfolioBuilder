Every made-to-measure order starts at 5 weeks — cutting, stitching and two fittings — and the fabric and embroidery weeks above are added to it. Embroidery sets the calendar because it is one pair of hands at one adda frame: a gota border is about 40 hours of work, a zardozi panel about 180, a full jaal about 420. The planner will not shorten those numbers to win an order, and neither will I.

The total includes the choli stitching and both fittings. The advance is 40%, taken only after your delivery date is written on the order card.
