    Every wedding, same spine

    The order of a kalyanam

    1. 01

      Nischayathartham engagement

      Tamarind, coconut and the reading of the lagna patrika. We fix the muhurtham date with the family's panchangam, not with a calendar app. Four hours, usually a Sunday, always too many people in one drawing room.

      T−90 days
    2. 02

      Mallikai & mehendi the house fills

      Jasmine strung at the bride's house from eleven in the morning. I bring the stringers, the cotton thread and the ice boxes. The mehendi artist comes after lunch, when the aunts have finished arguing about lunch.

      T−3 days
    3. 03

      Janavasam the groom arrives

      A short procession, a nadaswaram pair, and a route walked twice on the day of to count the turn at the temple corner. Chairs are set at 5 p.m. and covered in case of rain.

      T−1 evening
    4. 04

      Muhurtham the minute itself

      Kanyadaanam, the koorai saree, the three knots. The muhurtham is eighteen minutes and it is the only part of the wedding that cannot run late. My job is that at 4.36 a.m. the priest, the couple, the photographer and the grandmother are all in the same square metre.

      The fixed point
    5. 05

      Saptapadi & oonjal seven steps

      Seven steps around the fire, then the swing with the garlands. The oonjal is where the family photographs happen, so I keep the swing clear for the first twenty minutes and then let the cousins have it.

      +30 min
    6. 06

      Sadhya the leaf

      Two hundred and fifty leaves, twenty-two items, served in the order the eldest uncle expects. Rice at the centre-left, payasam last, and the pickle never touches the payasam. The kitchen starts at midnight; I taste at 3 a.m.

      +1 hour

    Four things I will not hand to a vendor group chat

    The clock

    One printed run sheet per vendor, on paper, with the muhurtham at its centre and everything else counted backwards from it. Phones die in mandapams.

    The kitchen

    I sit with the caterer and write the leaf order myself. A sadhya is judged on the second serving of rice, not the first.

    The money

    One ledger, every advance dated, every balance due the morning of. Families should be signing the blessings book, not a bill.

    The quiet

    Somewhere in the house there is a room with a fan and a locked door. That is where the bride sits for ten minutes. I stand outside it.
