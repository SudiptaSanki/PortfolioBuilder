# Credits — Pan Drum Gold (steelpan player portfolio)

## Persona

**Marlon De Freitas** is a fictional demonstration persona, disclosed as
fictional in the footer of every page. Harbourlight Steel Orchestra, its
band manager, its tuner, its engagements, fees and register entries are
invented for this template. No real person, band or booking is depicted.
The instrument scholarship is real and was checked against published
steelband sources (pan-mag.com's steel-orchestra guide; Colin Malloy's
steel pan composition guide; the Stockholm Steelband tuning appendices):
low-C tenor C4–E6, double seconds F3–C6, double guitar C♯3–F♯5, six bass
B1–C4; tenor layout in the circle of fifths (lowest notes on the outer
ring, octaves inside, highest at the centre).

## Sound

No audio files. The range explorer's tones are Web Audio synthesis
generated in the visitor's browser (fundamental + tuned partials,
equal temperament A440), started only by tapping a note, and labelled
on the page as demonstrations of pitch and register — never as
recordings of pans.
