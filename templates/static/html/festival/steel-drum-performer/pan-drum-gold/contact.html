What to send

  • The date — or the two or three dates you're choosing between.
  • The place — venue or route, and whether it's indoors or outdoors (outdoors means the cover conversation, early).
  • The format — full band, reduced band, solo, or workshop — and how long you need us playing.
  • The load-in — stage or street, vehicle access, and where six bass drums can stand.
  • The occasion — two lines is enough. It decides the tune list more than anything else you can send.
