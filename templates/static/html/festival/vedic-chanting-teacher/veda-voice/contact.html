Learn with me

Tell me the hour you can keep, and what you have chanted before

I take two new groups a term. The honest question is not whether you have a good voice; it is whether you will sit in the same place at the same hour for twelve weeks.

How it begins

Send the form. I ring you back, usually in the evening, and ask you to hum one note. That is the whole audition, and it tells me more than four years of singing lessons.

If there is a place, you come to the first session with nothing. No book, no recording, no notebook. There is a mat on the floor and a tumbler of water.

Fees
₹6,000 for a twelve-week term in person, ₹8,000 for the ten-week online krama group, ₹4,000 a month for one-to-one. Concessions for students and for anyone who tells me honestly that the fee is the obstacle.
Term dates
January, April, July and October. The Tuesday and Saturday groups start in the first week of each term.
What to bring
A mat, water, and a voice rested enough to hum. Nothing else. Bring a notebook after three months, not before.
Where
My front room, 3rd cross street, Pillaiyarpalayam, Kanchipuram. It holds eight people and a tanpura.
What I will not do
Teach from a book in the first months, sell a recording, or give a certificate. There is no certificate in this tradition.

If you already chant and want one correction, say so. That is a single session, not a term.

Ask for a place

Six fields. The last one is the one I read first.

I have your request

Thank you, —. I ring these back in the evening, after the last group. If you do not hear from me in four days, the telephone is the fault and you should call.
