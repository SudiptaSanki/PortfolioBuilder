How we weigh

The number is only as good as the scale

Everything leaves the grove through one door and one platform scale, and Hafsa writes the weight in the ledger before the box is closed. The sugar reading is taken from three fruit per bunch, pressed on the spot; the average is what you see above. We do not estimate from the palm any more. The one year we did, we were seven tonnes optimistic and sold fruit we did not have.
