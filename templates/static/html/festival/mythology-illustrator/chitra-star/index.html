Commissions

How a painting begins

  1. १
    Your story

    A scene, a text, a memory. Two or three paragraphs is plenty.

  2. २
    Block sketch

    I send a pencil block and a palette strip within three weeks.

  3. ३
    The painting

    Gouache and gold on wasli or canvas. Six to ten weeks.

  4. ४
    Delivery

    Rolled, framed or hand-delivered within India. Scans included.
