Plates & walls

Selected work, 2023 to 2026.

Book plates, private folios, one temple wall and a festival print. Each entry names the pigment, the support and the story it came from.

6 of 6 plates shown

  • Yashoda at the river

    Book plate · 2026 · “The Blue Boy”, Tulika

    Gouache and gold on wasli, 24 × 30 cm. Painted for a picture book retelling of Krishna’s childhood; this is the spread where Yashoda first sees the river at dusk.

  • Krishna of the eight moods

    Festival print · 2025 · Dasara edition of 120

    Eight panels in one frame, one for each rasa. Printed from a painted original on cotton rag; the gold is stamped, not screened, so each print differs slightly.

  • The lamp that would not go out

    Private commission · 2026 · Bengaluru

    A grandmother’s story of a lamp that stayed lit through a flood, painted for her grandchildren. Vermilion field, gold flame, the house small in the corner as she asked.

  • Procession of the small temple

    Temple mural · 2025 · Mandya district

    Six metres of wall in lime plaster, painted over eleven weeks with two students. The village asked for its own two shrines at the centre, which is not the usual order, and is right.

  • The waiting nayika

    Private folio · 2024 · Mysuru

    Karuna, painted in ash blue, for a couple who lost a child. They asked for no moon. The gold line at the horizon is the only ornament.

  • The peacock’s complaint

    Book plate · 2023 · “Why the Peacock Cries”, Pratham

    A monsoon spread for a Kannada folk tale. The peacock was painted from a bird that comes to the studio roof every July, which is why the crest is wrong in the way real birds are.

“We asked for a mural and got a room that changes with the light. At noon the gold disappears; at six it comes back. The children in the village have named every figure.”

Shivanna, temple committeeMandya district, 2025
