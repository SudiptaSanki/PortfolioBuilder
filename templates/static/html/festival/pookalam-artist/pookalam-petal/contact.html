What does it cost

A three-metre pookalam is ₹18,000 including flowers and two hands. Six metres is ₹45,000. A ten-metre wedding floor with 40,000 flowers is ₹1,10,000. Travel outside Thrissur is extra and always quoted first.

What if it rains

An open pookalam cannot survive rain; there is no covering that does not ruin it. I watch the forecast for three days. If we have to abandon, you lose the flowers and nothing else; the advance is returned.

Can I choose the flowers

Yes, within the season. Thumba and chethi are reliable all year; marigold is expensive from March to June; chrysanthemum comes from Ooty and costs what it costs. I will tell you honestly what will look right.

Do you work outside Kerala

Only if there is a local team to pick and a floor I can inspect by video. Flowers do not travel well and I will not lay a pookalam with flowers that have been in a cold box for two days.

The flowers should be cut before dawn.
