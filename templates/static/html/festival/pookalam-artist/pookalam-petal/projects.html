The archive

Every one of these is already gone

Nine floors from the last four seasons, photographed from a ladder before the first guest arrived. The flowers were swept up the same evening. What remains is the count, the palette, and the reason it was laid.

9 of 9 pookalams

Thrikkakara, Thiruvonam

Temple · 6 m · 18,400 flowers · 2026

Laid on the north flagstone from midnight. Four of us, nine hours, the last ring finished as the lamps were lit.

Vadakkechira wedding

Wedding · 4 m · 9,200 flowers · 2026

The bride's family asked for no marigold, which is unusual and, as it turned out, right. Rose petals and thumba with chethi only at the heart.

Grahapravesham, Ollur

Housewarming · 3 m · 5,800 flowers · 2026

Laid on the new verandah before the milk was boiled. The family walked around it seven times and then stood inside it for photographs.

Atham at the studio

Onam · 1.5 m · 1,900 flowers · 2026

The first circle of the season, laid alone on the shed floor with what was left from the previous day's sorting. Nobody commissions Atham; I make it anyway.

Nalukettu, Kunnamkulam

Housewarming · 3.5 m · 7,100 flowers · 2025

Square at the centre because the house is square, with the courtyard open to the sky above it. The only pookalam I have made with corners in it.

Guruvayur, Ekadasi

Temple · 5 m · 12,600 flowers · 2025

For a family that has sponsored the same floor for eleven years. Same palette every time: white, marigold, and the maroon of the temple cloth.

Puthur, a memorial

Memorial · 2.5 m · 3,600 flowers · 2025

Laid for a woman who kept a garden of thumba. Her family asked for one half-ring in red, which is not traditional; it was the right thing.

School Onam, Irinjalakuda

Onam · 3 m · 4,900 flowers · 2025

Ninety children, eight colours, one very patient teacher. I laid the outline at five and they filled it from seven, and every child's ring is slightly wrong.

Kottayam wedding

Wedding · 8 m · 24,900 flowers · 2025

The biggest one I have laid indoors, on the floor of a hall with a marble that showed every petal. The border took two of us three hours alone.
