Finding us

Survey 41/2, Dindori Road, Nashik 422202. Turn off the Nashik–Vinchur road at the second sugar factory and follow the vineyard wall for a kilometre.

Cellar door: Friday to Sunday, 11.00 and 16.00, October to March.

Closed during the harvest, from the last week of February to the end of March.

Getting here

Mumbai, three and a half hours by road. Pune, four. Nashik Road station is thirty kilometres, and there are taxis at the stand; tell them Dindori, not Nashik.

If you are coming from the airport at Ozar, it is forty minutes and the last stretch has no street lights.
