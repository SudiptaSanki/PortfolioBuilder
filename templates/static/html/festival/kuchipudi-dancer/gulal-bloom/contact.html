Tell me the date, the stage and who is in the room

I answer every invitation myself, and I will say no if the stage cannot take the dance — a tarangam on a floor that flexes is a broken ankle, and a hall with a bad acoustic turns a padam into mime.

A solo evening within India starts at ₹1,20,000 plus travel. Workshops start at ₹35,000 a day.

Thank you, friend.

Your invitation is with me. I will reply within three days and ask about the floor before anything else, because it decides whether tarangam is in the programme. If the date is free, I will hold it for a week while the venue answers.

Send a photograph of the stage if you have one. It tells me more than a description.

What a booking needs

A rigid floor. Stone, wood or a properly laid stage. Not a flexing platform, not carpet over tile.

Two hours of get-in. The plate, the pot and the lights are set before the audience arrives, and the sound check is not optional.

A green room with a door. The costume takes twenty minutes to tie and cannot be done in a corridor.

Also available

Workshops for schools, dance schools and teachers — a morning on syllables and stance, an afternoon on a jati the group can perform.

Lecture demonstrations of ninety minutes, for festivals and universities, with the audience counting the tala out loud by the end.

I travel from Hyderabad. Within India, travel and stay are billed at cost, not as a lump sum.
