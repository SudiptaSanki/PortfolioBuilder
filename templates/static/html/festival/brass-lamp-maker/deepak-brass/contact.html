Write to the workshop

Tell me the room and the flame, not the budget first

The useful things are the height of the surface, whether there is a fan, how long the lamp must burn, and which oil will be used. The rest follows. I answer within two working days, usually in the evening after the furnace is off.

Nothing is sent from this page — it is a form to copy into a letter or an email, and the fields check themselves as you go. A real enquiry reaches the workshop at the address on the right.

Where the furnace is

14 Kundarki Road, Peetal Nagri, Moradabad 244001, Uttar Pradesh. The lane is narrow; a car stops at the corner by the pump.

Post goes to the same address, attention Deepak Prajapati. A photograph of the room helps more than a description.

Hours

  • Monday to Friday9 am – 7 pm
  • Saturday9 am – 4 pm
  • Sundayclosed
  • Furnace daysTue & Fri

Visits are welcome, but write first: on a pour day nobody can stop to talk until the metal is in the mould.

What to send

  • The height of the table or shelf
  • Whether a fan moves air over the flame
  • How many hours it must burn unfilled
  • The oil you will actually use
  • A photograph of the room, if you have one
  • The date it is needed, and whether that date can move

Lead times

A single lamp takes three to four weeks. A pair, five. Nine jhoomars took eleven weeks because of the chains. A repair is usually done in a week, unless the seam has to be opened.

I take four commissions at a time. If the book is full, I will say so rather than promise a date I cannot keep.
