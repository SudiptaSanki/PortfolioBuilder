What to send

  • The date — and where it falls in the season, if you know it. Peak dates (Day 1, first weekend) need the longest lead.
  • The venue — address or suburb, and whether we perform indoors, outdoors, or both.
  • The floor & ceiling facts — performance area size, floor surface, and the clear ceiling height. A phone photo of the room helps more than a brochure.
  • The audience — roughly how many, and whether children or noise-sensitive guests are part of it.
  • The occasion — opening, wedding, assembly, festival — and anything the lion should know about it.
