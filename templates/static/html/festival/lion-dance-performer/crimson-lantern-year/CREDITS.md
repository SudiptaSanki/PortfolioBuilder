## Audio

None. By ruling: a lion dance troupe's sound is its product, and a
synthesised drum-and-cymbal loop would misrepresent it. The site is silent.

## Persona

Crimson Lantern Troupe and its coordinator Daniel Teo are fictional,
created for this portfolio template and disclosed as fictional in the
footer of every page. Client names in the casebook are changed.
