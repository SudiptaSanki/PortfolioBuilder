    Cai qing, in plain words

    Cai qing — “plucking the greens” — is the ritual at the heart of most bookings. A lion dance is booked to bring luck and drive off bad fortune; cai qing is how the luck gets delivered to your door:

    1. A lettuce is hung at the entrance, with a red packet tucked inside it.
    2. The lion approaches — sniffing, circling, playing shy. This is the part children watch for.
    3. The lion rears up and “eats” the lettuce: taking in the luck offered with it.
    4. The lion chews, then scatters the leaves over the doorway and the crowd: the luck, spread.

    We bring the lettuces and the scroll ourselves. Firecrackers are not part of our act — most Melbourne venues prohibit them, and the drum does the job.
