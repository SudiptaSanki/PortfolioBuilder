01 The cycle

The taal is a circle, not a bar

Western music counts to four and starts again. Teentaal counts to sixteen, divides into four vibhags of four, and comes home to the same heavy beat each time. Miss the home and the whole thing is a different composition.

सम

Sam — the home

The first matra, and the beat every phrase aims at. Played with the full hand on the bayan. When a soloist and a tabla player arrive on sam together after a long tihai, that is the whole point of the form.

ताली

Tali — the claps

Matras five and thirteen in teentaal, marked by a clap in the traditional recitation. They are not accents you add; they are places the cycle already agrees on.

खाली

Khali — the empty

Matra nine, marked by a wave instead of a clap. The bayan drops out and the dayan plays alone, so the cycle suddenly has a hole in it. Most beginners lose the cycle here, not at sam.

विभाग

Vibhag — the four rooms

Sixteen matras divided into four groups of four. Each group ends on a beat you can feel in the wrist, which is why teentaal is the first cycle taught and the last one mastered.

What I play, and how long it takes
