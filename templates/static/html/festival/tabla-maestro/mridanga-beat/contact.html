02 Questions

Before you write

The same five questions, answered honestly, so the first lesson can be about playing.

Do I need to own a tabla to start?

No. The studio has four drums and they stay at the studio. Between lessons you practice bols on your knees and on a table edge, which is what beginners did for a century before cheap drums existed. If you decide to continue after eight weeks, I will help you choose a pair.

How much practice is enough?

Twenty minutes a day, six days a week, is worth more than two hours on Sunday. The hand learns by repetition with rest, not by volume. If you cannot find twenty minutes, wait until you can; the drum will still be here.

Can I learn online?

Yes, if you already have a basic bol vocabulary and can tune your own bayan. Online does not work for a complete beginner because I cannot see the angle of your wrist, which is the thing that most needs correcting in the first month.

Will I get to play in public?

Eventually, if you want to. Students play at the annual studio baithak in December, in front of about thirty people, most of them other students' families. It is not a competition and there is no prize.

Do you take students under sixteen?

From twelve, with a parent present for the first month. Below that, the drum is usually too big and the lesson too long; a good school music teacher will serve them better than I can.
