02 Recordings

Three things you can actually hear

Recorded live, one or two microphones, no edits except the fades. They are not mastered, which is why they sound like a room.

2019 · LP · 42 min

Ajrada Notebook

Eleven kaidas and four relas in teentaal, played in one take each, in the order they were taught to me. The fourth kaida has a mistake at matra twelve which my guru would have made me repeat.

Pressed on 180 g vinyl, 300 copiesSold out · digital available

2021 · digital · 28 min

Rupak, unaccompanied

Rupak is seven matras with the empty beat first, which makes it the most disorienting cycle for a listener and the most fun to play. Recorded in the teaching room with the fan off.

03 Notation

One kaida, written out

Sixteen matras, four vibhags. The phrase repeats with a small change each cycle — that change is the whole composition. Dashes are rests, not missing bols.
