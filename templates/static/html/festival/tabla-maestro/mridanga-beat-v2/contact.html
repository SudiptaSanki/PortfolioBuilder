One form. I answer it myself.

No manager, no booking desk. Write through the form, or directly to hello@rohankelkar.example. I reply personally, usually within two days — slower in the December festival week, and I will say so in the reply.

What helps me answer well

For lessons: your age or your child's, any playing you have done before, and which tala you are currently stuck on — “none yet” is a perfectly good answer; the first sitting is a conversation and a clap test, and it is free.

For accompaniment: the date, the city, who is leading (vocal, instrument or dance), and the talas you expect. If the date is inside the December festival fortnight, say so early — those cushions are spoken for a season ahead.

For a lecture-demonstration: the audience — school, college or society — and the room. The session needs listeners who can clap, which is to say, any listeners at all.
