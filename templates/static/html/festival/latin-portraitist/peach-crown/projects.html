Faces, in the order they sat

Eleven years of sitters, six of them on this page. Names are changed where the sitter asked; the paintings are not.

Ana, con limón

Oil on masonite · 50 × 62 cm · 2025 · private, Mexico City

Painted in four sittings because she kept laughing at the wrong moment. The lime branch was her condition.

Don Emilio

Oil on masonite · 45 × 56 cm · 2024 · collection of the family

He sat with his hat on his knee and refused to take off his watch. The hummingbird arrived at the third sitting and stayed at the window.

La tormenta

Retablo on tin · 18 × 24 cm · 2023 · collection of the family

A family of three, a hailstorm over the milpa, and a roof that held. The dedication is their own sentence.

María, siete años

Oil on masonite · 40 × 50 cm · 2024 · private, Oaxaca

She asked to be painted with the paper flower from the market, and with the dog, who is in the small one.

El brazo de Ramírez

Retablo on tin · 18 × 24 cm · 2022 · collection of the family

Painted on the bench where the accident happened. The Virgen is a small figure his mother cut from a magazine in 1979.

La milpa larga

Mural, acrylic on concrete · 6 × 2.4 m · 2021 · patio, Xochimilco

A family of three, a corn plant, and the wall of a patio that gets the morning sun. Painted in nine days with two helpers.

Commission a face
