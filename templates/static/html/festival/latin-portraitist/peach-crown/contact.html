Before you write

Three sittings of ninety minutes, a week apart, at the same hour of the day. Wear what you actually wear. Bring a photograph of the person from ten years ago if you have one; it helps me see what the face is holding on to.

Children over six can sit, for about forty minutes at a time. Babies are better painted from photographs, honestly.

The studio has a step at the door and no lift. Tell me if that is a problem and I will come to you.
