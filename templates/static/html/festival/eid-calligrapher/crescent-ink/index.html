The qalam lab

Set the nib before you set the word

A broad reed nib is a flat edge, not a point. Turn it and the stroke swells; drag it along its own edge and the line thins to a hair. Four letters from the studio's practice sheet are drawn here with the same width model I use at the desk.

Four kinds of commission

  1. 01

    Nikkah nama

    The marriage contract, written on a single sheet with the witnesses' lines ruled by hand. I write the couple's names in thuluth and the conditions in Nastaliq, then leave the signature blocks empty for the day itself.

    Eid panel

    One word, one sheet, one colour. Usually عيد مبارك in thuluth with a gold rosette, sized for a hallway wall. The paper is toned with tea before the ink goes down, so the white of the sheet never glares.

A commission, start to finish

Four sittings, one sheet

Every commission begins with a conversation and a sheet of trial strokes. Nothing is inked until you have seen the word on paper and said yes to it.

Start with a brief →
  1. i

    The brief

    We agree on the words, the size, the wall and the date. If the Arabic is a translation, I send it to a scholar I trust before a pen is uncapped.

  2. ii

    The trial sheet

    Three or four drafts in graphite and ink, photographed in daylight and sent to you. You pick one; I keep the others in the drawer with the date on them.

  3. iii

    The writing

    Paper is sized, toned, ruled and written in one sitting, because a second sitting shows in the join. You receive a photograph the same evening.

  4. iv

    The mounting

    The sheet is pressed for a week, then mounted on cotton rag with wheat starch paste. Framing is yours, or I can send it to Rafiq in Koti.
