01 Classes · Bondegatan 42

Six places, one table.

Classes are small on purpose. You will be given materials, a basin and a corner of the table, and roughly two hours of quiet to make one arrangement badly before you make it well.

Ask for a place

Terms run four classes over eight weeks. Autumn places open in August, spring in December. Write with the class you would like and a sentence about your experience — none is perfectly fine.

This is a demonstration of the studio's book; nothing is sent anywhere.

The practical

  • The studio

    Bondegatan 42, rear courtyard
    116 33 Stockholm
    Through the arch, second door on the left. There is no sign; the door is usually propped open on class nights.

  • Times

    Tuesday 18:00–20:30
    Saturday 10:00–12:30
    Four classes per term, 1,600 kr. Trial class 450 kr, deducted if you continue.

  • Getting there

    Medborgarplatsen (Green/Red line), four minutes on foot. Bus 3 and 66 stop on Bondegatan. There is no parking in the courtyard; the garage on Skånegatan charges by the hour.

  • What to bring

    Secateurs if you have them — there are spares. An apron. Nothing else: materials, vessels and kenzan are provided and stay in the studio.

  • What not to bring

    Flowers from a shop. Part of the exercise is choosing from the buckets in the courtyard, which hold what the season actually offers.
Cancellations Classes are paid per term. If you miss one, you may join a class in the other group that week if there is room; the studio keeps six chairs and no more.
Gift places A term can be given as a gift. I send a card with the dates and a note about the first evening; the recipient books their own place.
