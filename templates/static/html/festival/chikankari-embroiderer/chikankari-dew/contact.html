Commission · the studio diary

Tell me the occasion, and the cloth if you know it.

The studio takes commissions in two windows a year, in February and in August, because the karigars' work is planned in months rather than weeks. Write now and you will get a date, an honest lead time and a price before anything is cut.

Lead times

A kurta or shirt takes six to twelve weeks. A dupatta with a jaali border takes twelve to fourteen. A bridal piece is quoted at four to five months, and the studio will only take two in a season.

Price

Embroidery and making, from ₹9,600 for a table runner to ₹64,000 for a full jaali dupatta. Cloth is quoted separately: mulmul from ₹600 a metre, georgette from ₹780, khadi from ₹460.

The studio

14 Aminabad Road, first floor, above the cloth shop. Visitors by appointment on Monday and Thursday afternoons. There is no lift and the stairs are steep; tell me if that matters and I will bring the samples down.

Sizing

For a first commission, send four measurements: shoulder, chest, kurta length and sleeve. If you are local, come and be measured. A piece cut to a guess is a piece that hangs in a cupboard.

Payment

Half on the design sheet, half on delivery. UPI or bank transfer. The deposit covers the cloth and the block if a new one has to be cut, which is why it is not refundable once the block is made.
