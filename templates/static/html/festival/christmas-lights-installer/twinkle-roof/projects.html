Selected installs · 2021–2025

Six roofs, and what each one would not allow.

A roofline is a constraint before it is a design. Some gutters take a clip and some do not; some walls are old lime plaster that a clip will pull off in a week. These are six from the last five seasons, with the run, the bulbs and the thing that made each one awkward.

6 installs

  • Park Street · 58 m fascia · red and gold · 2025

    The bookshop fascia

    Sixty years on the same pavement. The run follows the fascia and the awning edge, but the awning is canvas and cannot take a clip, so the second run is tensioned on a wire between two brackets.

  • Ballygunge · 38 m · warm white · 2025

    The house with the mango tree

    One roofline, two windows and a veranda post. The tree was meant to be lit too, but a branch over the gate touches the 6 A point, and nobody wanted the cable in the foliage.

  • New Town · 42 m, two runs · cool white · 2024

    Parapet and balcony

    A flat roof with no ridge to clip to, so the parapet run uses magnetic clips on the steel railing. The balcony is on its own circuit and its own timer, because the family sits there until midnight.

  • Bow Barracks · 66 m, three circuits · multicolour · 2024

    The courtyard blocks

    Six households, one shared courtyard, three circuits and a rota for who switches on. The council supply could not carry it, so the residents ran a dedicated line from one meter and split the bill.

  • Topsia Road · 34 m · warm white · 2023

    The tyre shop

    A first install for a neighbour's shop, done at cost. The fascia is corrugated sheet, which takes a clip badly, so every second bulb is cable-tied through a drilled hole.

  • Salt Lake · 26 m · multicolour · 2021

    A first Christmas in a new house

    26 m, four colours, one very excited eight-year-old advising on the sequence. The run is short and the design is loud, which is the correct answer for the brief.

Runs are measured along the roofline, not along the wall. Bulb counts include the 15 per cent slack; the cost bands on the season page include install and takedown, not the power.

02

What a good install looks like from the road

Three things separate a professional run from a good attempt. The line is straight — not because the bulbs are evenly spaced, but because the cable is tensioned between clips and the slack is hidden at the ends.

The second is restraint. One colour on the roofline, a second colour inside the windows, nothing on the gate. A house lit in four colours reads as a fairground from thirty metres; the eye cannot find the roof.

The third is the switch-on time. Lights that come on at 18:30 and go off at 23:00 belong to the house. Lights that stay on until two in the morning belong to the street, and the neighbours remember.
