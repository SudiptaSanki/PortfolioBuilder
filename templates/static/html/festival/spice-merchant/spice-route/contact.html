The shop

  • Address

    Shop 214, Bazaar Road, Khari Baoli,
    Chandni Chowk, Delhi 110006
    Second turning after the Katra Neel gate; the green shutter with the brass scale painted on it.

  • Hours

    Tuesday to Sunday, 9 a.m. to 7 p.m.
    Closed Monday, Holi and the last week of June. Grinding begins at 6 a.m. on Thursdays.

  • Telephone

    +91 11 2394 5610 · best between 9 and 11
    sarala@saxenaspice.in

  • Getting here

    Chandni Chowk metro (Yellow Line), Gate 3, then a nine-minute walk. Autos stop at the Fatehpuri Masjid end of the lane; from there it is 200 metres on foot. Parking is not possible in the lane.

  • What to bring

    A list and a cloth bag. A small jar if you want to carry ground spice home; paper pouches are fine for a week, not a season.

Not to scale. The lane floods to the ankle in July; on those days the shop opens at eleven and the sacks come up onto the counter.
