## Sound ruling

FAILS the need-test — no audio anywhere. A chaat counter is not a
sound profession and no interaction here teaches through audio.
The stub promised no sound and added none either; the ruling is
recorded because the test was run, not assumed. The footer states
it on every page: "This site makes no sound."
