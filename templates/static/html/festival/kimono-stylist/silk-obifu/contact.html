Before you write

Four questions I will ask anyway, so you may as well answer them now.

1 · What is the occasion?

A wedding, a tea gathering, a graduation, a festival, a photograph. Each has a different ceiling of formality and a different floor.

2 · What do you already own?

Even a single inherited kimono changes the plan. If you own nothing, that is normal; the studio has a rental wardrobe.

3 · Who else is being dressed?

A party of six needs two dressers and three hours. A party of twelve needs a conversation about the schedule before it needs a price.

4 · Is there a photograph you love?

Send it. It is faster than describing a look, and it tells me which parts of the outfit you are actually seeing.

Fees, plainly

Studio dressing from $120. Wedding parties from $680 including a second dresser and travel inside the metro area. Consultations $180 for two hours. The four-session course is $320 and includes the use of a studio kimono if you do not own one. A deposit of half holds a wedding date; the rest is due on the day.
