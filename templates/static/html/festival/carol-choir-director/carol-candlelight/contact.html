Come to a Wednesday before you decide

We audition in September, but anyone is welcome to sit in on a rehearsal first. Bring nothing except a willingness to read a line of music you have not seen before.

This page does not send anything. It checks the details so you can copy a complete note into an email to the director. Nothing is stored.

Rehearsals

Wednesdays, 7–9 pm, in the parish hall behind the church. We start in September and finish after the service in December. There is tea at the break and a rota for biscuits.

You do not need to attend every week, but the four rehearsals before the service are not optional.

The audition

Fifteen minutes with me and the section leader. One hymn verse of your choice, a short sight-reading exercise, and a few minutes of singing together to check blend.

I am listening for pitch, not volume. If you can hold a line while somebody else sings a different one, you will be fine.

What to prepare

  • A verse of any hymn you know
  • No need for a prepared piece
  • Bring water; the hall is dry
  • Tell me if you read bass clef

Ask the choir to sing

We sing at the Advent service, Epiphany, Tenebrae and the parish’s Christmas Eve mass. We do not take wedding fees, but we do ask for a donation to the music fund.

For funerals, write or call the parish office rather than this page; those requests need to go through the parish first.
