Each year the service changes by two or three carols and one reading. The programmes below are the ones I kept; the pencil marks are still in them.

2025

Nine Lessons and Carols

St Brigid’s · Saturday 13 December · 7.30 pm

Twenty-four singers, six candles on the rail and a broken heating valve that made the pitch rise half a tone by the last carol. We tuned to the organ for the first four and to each other after that. The solo verse of Once in Royal came from the back of the church, unaccompanied, as it should.

  1. 01Once in Royal David’s City — solo, then SAB
  2. 02Adam lay ybounden — Ord
  3. 03O Come, O Come Emmanuel — verses 1 and 3
  4. 04In the Bleak Midwinter — Holst
  5. 05The Cherry Tree Carol — arr. O’Donnell
  6. 06Silent Night — four parts, no organ

2024

Nine Lessons and Carols

St Brigid’s · Saturday 14 December · 7.30 pm

The year we added the Cherry Tree Carol and lost the descant on O Come All Ye Faithful because the second sopranos had all moved house. The congregation sang the descant anyway, from memory, which was better.

  1. 01Once in Royal David’s City — solo, then SAB
  2. 02The Holly and the Ivy — arr. Willcocks
  3. 03O Come, O Come Emmanuel — verses 1 and 3
  4. 04A Spotless Rose — Howells
  5. 05While Shepherds Watched — descant
  6. 06Silent Night — four parts, no organ

2024

Epiphany Carol Service

St Brigid’s · Sunday 7 January · 4 pm

A smaller service, eighteen singers, and the only time the choir sings We Three Kings with the proper alto line. The heating was fixed. The pitch did not rise.

  1. 01Brightest and Best — arr. Vaughan Williams
  2. 02We Three Kings — unaccompanied
  3. 03Bethlehem Down — Warlock
  4. 04The Three Kings — Cornelius, arr. Atkins

2023

Tenebrae

St Brigid’s · Wednesday of Holy Week · 8 pm

The candles are put out one by one and the church gets darker as the choir gets smaller. We finished with four voices in the Lady chapel, no organ, no announcement, and people stayed sitting for a long time afterwards.

  1. 01Lamentations of Jeremiah — Tallis, first set
  2. 02Miserere — Allegri, verses 1–4
  3. 03Drop, Drop, Slow Tears — Gibbons
  4. 04In Manus Tuas — Sheppard

2019

Nine Lessons and Carols

St Brigid’s · Saturday 21 December · 7.30 pm

My first year as director. I programmed too much music and the service ran to an hour and forty minutes. The vicar was kind about it. The choir was not.

  1. 01Once in Royal David’s City — solo, then SAB
  2. 02Ding Dong Merrily on High — arr. Wood
  3. 03In Dulci Jubilo — Pearsall
  4. 04O Little Town of Bethlehem — arr. Vaughan Williams
  5. 05Hark the Herald — descant
  6. 06Silent Night — four parts, no organ
