Selected commissions · 2021–2026

Six panels, and the constraint that shaped each one.

Every commission arrives with one hard limit: a wall that cannot be drilled, a monsoon that will test the grout, a client who wants a family photograph without a photograph. The limit is usually the best part of the brief.

Working with us

Sample

8–14 weeks for a panel under 4 m²
Installation
Studio team, or a supervised local setter

6 panels

  • Anjuna · 42 m² pool floor · vitreous glass · 2025

    The long wave

    Laid to be read through 1.4 m of water, which pulls everything toward blue and eats red first. The palette was tested in a bucket before a tile was cut.

  • Chapel apse, Saligao · 3.6 m² · smalti and gold · 2024

    Peacock for an apse

    The only gold in the building, set in a half-dome that catches light from a single window. The iris is six rows deep so it holds at the far end of the nave.

  • Private house, Assagao · 18 m² · terracotta and marble · 2023

    Courtyard star

    A floor that had to drain. The star points were set on a 1 per cent fall, and the grout is sand-based so water moves through it rather than across it.

  • Boutique hotel, Panjim · 6.2 m² · smalti · 2024

    Façade lotus

    West-facing and four metres above the pavement. Frost-proof smalti, epoxy grout only at the movement joints, and a stainless mesh behind every panel.

  • Dining room, Mumbai · 1.9 m² · smalti · 2026

    Three rivers

    Eleven thousand tesserae at 10 mm, read at arm's length from a dining table. The client asked for the sea at Alibaug; the studio drew the creek instead.

  • Verandah, Fontainhas · 9 m² · marble · 2021

    Verandah eye

    The oldest panel in the studio's book, and the one that established the rule: never lay a floor motif that cannot be walked across without a border.

Panels shown are drawn from the studio's andamento studies, not photographed on site. Sizes and materials are those of the finished commission.

02

What a brief needs to contain

Four things: a dimension, a light, a use and a budget band. Everything else is a conversation the studio is happy to have, but those four decide whether a panel is possible.

Send a drawing at 1:10 if you have one. If not, a photograph with a tape measure in it is enough to start. The studio will come back with an andamento sketch and a tile count before discussing price, because the tile count is the price.
