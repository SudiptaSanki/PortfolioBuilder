Panel brief

What comes back

  • An andamento sketch at 1:10
  • A tile count and a cutting estimate in hours
  • A sample board, 20 cm square, charged at cost
  • A lead time, usually 8–14 weeks

On price

The studio quotes per square metre, by material and tile size. A 10 mm smalti panel costs several times a 20 mm terracotta one, because the tile count quadruples and every tile is cut by hand.

Installation

Within Goa the studio sets the work. Elsewhere the panel travels face down on mesh, with a setter's drawing and a day of supervision included.

Try the tile count first

The andamento studio works out how many tesserae a panel needs before anyone picks up a hammer.
