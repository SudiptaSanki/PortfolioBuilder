Feng Shui · 風水 · Plan Readings

Wind and water, read off your floor plan.

I am Su-Lin Teo. I read floor plans in Singapore — flats, shophouses, small offices — and from October my diary fills with shops getting ready for the Lunar New Year, when the till, the door and the queue get rearranged before the year turns.

A floor plan is a weather map. I read where the wind pools and where the water runs, and then we move furniture — not mountains. A mirror hung facing the front door to bounce the wealth back in mostly bounces back the corridor; I have taken down more of those mirrors than I have hung.

The Reading

Four steps, one plan, no ceremony

1 · Send the plan

The agent's floor plan, the HDB plan, or a measured sketch with the rooms named. A phone photo of a paper plan is enough to start.

2 · The door direction

Measured standing in the doorway, facing out. It goes on the report, and it is the one measurement I will ask you to take twice.

3 · We walk the plan

A call, or a half-day visit. Room by room: what agrees with the grid, what works against it, and what is simply furniture in the wrong place.

4 · The written report

Corrections ranked — free first (move it), then cheap (colour, material). Anything structural is flagged for a surveyor, never dressed up as a feng shui fix.

The Working Materials

Five elements, all of them already in your home

Corrections are made of ordinary things — a plant, a lamp, a rug, a brass bowl. Each element feeds the next: wood feeds fire, fire settles to earth, earth bears metal, metal gathers water, water grows wood. A reading balances what a room already has before it adds anything.

木

Wood

Living plants, timber, tall shapes, books. The growing element — a shelf of books is wood, whatever the chart says.

火

Fire

Light first: lamps, candles, the stove. Red is fire's colour, but a lit lamp does the work better than a red wall.

土

Earth

Ceramic, stone, tile, the square and the low. The Peranakan tile on a shophouse floor is earth under your feet.

金

Metal

Brass, steel, the round and the white. A metal correction is usually already in the kitchen drawer.

水

Water

Glass, dark blue, the sink, the sea beyond the door. Mirrors are water in this system — which is why they are placed carefully, not hopefully.

The Season · 新年

The Lunar New Year diary opens in October

Shops book first. A festival-season refresh is a working visit, not a blessing: we walk the plan with the year's stock in mind — the till out of the doorway's path, the queue given somewhere to stand, the far wall (fame, in the grid) carrying the shop's name and its light, and the centre of the floor kept clear for the crowd that is coming.

Families book differently. Many want a flat read before they sign for it, or before the seventh month, when the Hungry Ghost festival quiets the moving vans and there is time to walk a plan slowly. Both are in the diary by November most years; the planner below will tell you honestly what a reading costs, and the contact form is the way in.

    The Terms, In The Open

    What I will not do
