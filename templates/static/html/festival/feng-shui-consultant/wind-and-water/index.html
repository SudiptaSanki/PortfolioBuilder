八宅 · Eight Mansions

The four directions that suit you

Eight Mansions works from the year you were born and your sex, and gives a life gua — a number from one to nine that belongs to the person, not the house. From it come four directions that support you and four that do not. This is the first half of a reading, and the half you can do yourself.

Born in 1988 — the solar year runs to 3 February, so a January birthday counts as 1987.

A reading uses the whole household, the building's facing and sitting directions, and the year's flying stars. This is one eighth of it.

The reading

Two hours, a floor plan, and a written report

  1. 01

    Before I arrive

    You send the floor plan, the compass bearing of the main door taken from the street, and the year of birth of everyone who sleeps there. I do the Eight Mansions for each person and the flying stars for the year before I set foot in the flat.

  2. 02

    At the door

    I take my own bearing with the luopan, away from the lift shaft and the electrical riser, because both bend a needle. Then we walk the flat in the order you use it: door, living, kitchen, bedrooms, the room you do not use.

  3. 03

    The report

    Twelve to twenty pages, with the plan marked up and every recommendation dated to the year it applies. Written so your contractor can follow it: move this desk, put a light here, do not sleep with your head to the west this year.

  4. 04

    Three months later

    One follow-up call, because a home is never moved in one weekend. If something is not working, I would rather hear it and change the plan than have it sit in a drawer.

What it is, and what it is not

I cannot change your luck. I can change your room.

Feng shui is a traditional practice for siting and arranging buildings. It has rules, a compass, and a long written record. It is not a science, and I do not sell it as one: no reading I give will promise you money, a marriage or a recovery.

What it does well is make people look at their home again — where the light falls, where the door opens, which corner has been quietly making them unhappy for six years. Most of my work is attention, and the compass is how the attention is organised.

I will not tell you to buy a crystal, a frog or a fountain. If a client wants those, they can have them; they are not part of the method and I do not charge for them.

Mei-Ling moved two beds, one desk and a mirror, and told us to stop using the back bedroom as a store. The flat feels larger and we sleep better. She also told us plainly which of our questions feng shui could not answer.
