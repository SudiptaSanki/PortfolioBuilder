Seasons

Evenings this garden has held.

A selection from the ledger, with the numbers that mattered: guests seated, tables joined, and what the weather did.

6 of 6 seasons shown

  • 01

    Haller wedding, second attempt

    June 2025 · 96 guests · whole garden

    The first date was lost to a storm in 2024. This time we laid every table end to end under the chestnuts, moved the ceremony to the gravel court and kept the band under the tent. The bride asked for the same pretzels as the year before; we made them larger.

    96seated, no standing
  • 02

    Brewery night, cask tapped at seven

    September 2025 · 64 guests · north row

    Six tables joined for a Munich software studio. The brass trio played between courses rather than over them. The cask was empty by half past nine, which the cellar has noted as a record for a Thursday.

    64steins chilled: 198
  • 03

    Harvest supper, chestnut menu

    September 2025 · 40 guests · long table

    A single table of forty-two metres, laid with paper and a chestnut menu that ran to five courses. The kitchen roasted the chestnuts in the garden in an iron pan, which smoked out the first two rows and delighted everyone else.

    42 mtable, end to end
  • 04

    Eightieth birthday, four generations

    July 2025 · 28 guests · shade row

    The guest of honour arrived at four and left at ten. We set the table with her mother’s linen, brought from a house in Rosenheim. Her grandchildren served the Kaiserschmarrn and dropped only one pan.

    28seated, 6 hours
  • 05

    Isartaler choir, after the concert

    December 2024 · 52 guests · tent

    The only winter evening I host: the choir sings in the church, then walks over for Glühwein and brotzeit in the tent. Heaters at both ends, lanterns down the middle, and the tent flaps tied shut against the snow.

    52under canvas
  • 06

    Kovač–Sailer, a table of two families

    August 2024 · 74 guests · whole garden

    Two families who had never met, seated in alternating rows so that nobody could talk only to their own side. By the second Maß the rows had mixed themselves. Ana, who now leads our service, was a guest at this one.

    74guests, 9 tables

“I have been to three of these evenings now. The remarkable thing is not the food or the beer. It is that a hundred people who did not know each other end up passing pretzels down a table like a family at Christmas.”

Dr. Eva ReiterCompany evening, September 2025
