My one rule

Snowskin is a fine dessert. It is not a mooncake, and it does not go in my boxes. A mooncake is baked, rested, and cut open under the moon — and 双黄, double yolk, is not greed. Two moons in one cake is balance.

The rest · 回油

3 days

How a cake earns its box

Four jobs, spread over four months

1 · June

Cure the yolks

Duck eggs go into salt in June and stay there about twenty-five days. A yolk is ready when it stands firm and smells of nothing but itself.

2 · July

Cook the paste

Lotus seeds are simmered, sieved and cooked down with oil and sugar, low and slow, until the paste leaves the wok in one sheet. It keeps; it improves.

3 · August

Press and bake

Golden-syrup crust, a thin skin around a heavy heart. Pressed in wooden moulds, baked, egg-washed, baked again for the shine.

4 · Always

Rest three days

回油 — the crust takes back its oil and turns tender. Only then does a cake go into a box, and only then is it worth cutting.
