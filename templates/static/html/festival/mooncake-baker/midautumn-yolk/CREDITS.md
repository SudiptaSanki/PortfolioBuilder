# Credits — Midautumn Yolk

Persona, bakery, orders and people named on these pages are fictional
(disclosed in the footer of every page). Photographs are from Pexels
(free to use under the Pexels licence), downloaded into `assets/`:

- `assets/hero.jpg` — Pexels photo 9863622, traditional Chinese
  mooncakes stacked on a brown surface.
- `assets/rack.jpg` — Pexels photo 28399240, traditional stamped
  mooncakes on a bakery rack in Taipei, Taiwan.
- `assets/yolk.jpg` — Pexels photo 9863717, traditional Chinese
  mooncakes with egg yolk filling on a saucer.
- `assets/plates.jpg` — Pexels photo 9863598, traditional mooncakes
  served on white ceramic plates with a cup of tea.
- `assets/tea.jpg` — Pexels photo 9863692, mooncakes with a white
  teapot and cups.
- `assets/halves.jpg` — Pexels photo 33921276, a hand holding
  traditional Chinese mooncake halves.

Fonts: Noto Serif SC and Noto Sans SC (Google Fonts, SIL Open Font
Licence). Chinese characters used in small doses — 中秋 (Mid-Autumn),
月饼 (mooncake), 莲蓉 (lotus paste), 蛋黄 (egg yolk), 单黄 / 双黄
(single / double yolk), 五仁 (mixed nuts), 回油 (the crust taking back
its oil), 团圆 (reunion).

Sound: none. The need-test fails for a bakery — no audio files, no
synthesis, nothing behind a prompt.
