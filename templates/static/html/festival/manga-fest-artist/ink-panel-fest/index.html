What I clear the month for

Four festivals I do not miss, with the days that actually matter if you are drawing rather than watching.

  • JUL 01–31

    Gion Matsuri

    Kyoto. The whole month, but the drawing is done on the 14th to 16th — yoiyama, when the floats are up and the streets are closed to cars. The 17th procession starts at 9am and is over too fast to sketch.

    24 pages
  • MAY 15

    Aoi Matsuri

    Kyoto. A procession from the Imperial Palace to Shimogamo, in Heian dress, with an ox carriage that sets the pace. The best drawing is the wait before it starts.

    6 pages
  • AUG 16

    Gozan Okuribi

    Kyoto. Five fires on the hills at 8pm, lit within the same few minutes. You get one chance; bring a friend to hold the sketchbook.

    12 pages
  • OCT 22

    Jidai Matsuri

    Kyoto. Two thousand people in costume, walking from the palace to Heian Shrine. Eight periods in one procession, which is a costume reference library on legs.

    8 pages
Method描き方

A page takes four passes, and the first one is standing up.

  1. 01

    Gemba — on site

    Brush pen, B6 sketchbook, no eraser. Forty minutes per spot, then move. I am not drawing a record; I am drawing what I noticed.

  2. 02

    Name — the layout

    Pencil on B4. Panels and balloons only, no faces. If the page does not read as a page at this stage, no amount of tone will save it.

  3. 03

    Ink

    Maru pen for contours, kabura pen for the heavy blacks, brush pen for hair and cloth. White ink for the highlights I forgot to leave.

  4. 04

    Tone and scan

    Traditional tone for the small areas, digital for the big gradients. Scanned at 600 dpi, grey, then converted to bitmap for print.
