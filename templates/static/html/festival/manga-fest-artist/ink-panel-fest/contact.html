What I need to quote

  • The page count you have in mind, and whether it is flexible.
  • The festival or place, with dates. A reportage page cannot be drawn after the fact.
  • The rights you want: print only, digital, or both. They are priced differently.
  • Whether children appear, and whether their guardians have agreed.

How it runs

Sketch from the festival or from your reference, then a name sheet (the layout) for approval. After the name is approved I ink, tone, and letter — changes after that cost pages, so we spend our time at the name stage.

Questions

Do you draw from photographs?

Only from photographs I or the client have the right to use, and never to trace a face without consent. For festival work I would rather be on site.

Can I letter it myself?

Yes, if your letterer is good. I will draw the balloons and leave the copy for them, and I charge the same — the drawing is the work.

Do you take fan work?

No. Not because I disapprove; because I cannot license someone else's characters, and the paperwork is worse than the drawing.

What if the festival is cancelled?

If it is cancelled before the on-site work, you pay only the name sheet. After that, we decide together whether the story works without the event.
