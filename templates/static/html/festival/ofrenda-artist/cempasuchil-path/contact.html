Commissions open in September and close when the season is full, usually by the second week of October. A family altar takes two days to build; a public one takes three or four.

September – OctoberTwo visits beforeMexico City
Names, and the three things they would have reached for first. That is what the altar is built around.

How it runs

  • A reply within two days with the season's remaining dates and a materials estimate.
  • A first visit to see the room and measure the wall, the light and the doorway.
  • A second visit to meet the family and choose the objects together.
  • Build on 28 or 29 October; candles lit that evening and put out on the second of November.

What it costs

  • Family altar, two or three tiers: from MX$6,500, materials included.
  • Five-tier altar: from MX$14,000.
  • Public installation: quoted after the first visit.
  • Workshops: a flat fee, with the school supplying the crates.
