02 · the four sides

Four letters, one pot.

A dreidel is a gambling top with a Hebrew initial on each face: nun, gimel, hey, pe. The game is old and the rules are short. I carve the letters with a 2 mm veining gouge and paint them with milk paint, never a decal.

נ

Nun נישט

Nisht — nothing. You pass the top on and the pot stays where it is.

The quiet face. Half of every game is nun, which is why a child learns patience before arithmetic.

ג

Gimel גאַנץ

Gants — all. You take the whole pot.

I carve gimel deepest because it is the face players run their thumb over while they wait.

ה

Hey האַלב

Halb — half. You take half the pot, rounded up.

The diplomat. It keeps a game from ending too fast, which is useful with cousins.

פ

Pe פּוט אין

Put in — you add one coin or one candy to the pot.

In Israel the fourth letter is pe for po, here, and the dreidel is called a sevivon. I carve both.

On the rack

Nine species, bought as offcuts from a cabinet shop on Springdale Road and a mill outside Lockhart. Six of them are on the rack most weeks.

01

Hard maple

Acer saccharum

Pale, closed grain, takes a letter cleanly. The default for a first dreidel and still the best for a small hand.

Density 705 kg/m³ · finishes cream

02

Black walnut

Juglans nigra

Dark enough that the letters need a pale paint. Heavy, quiet on the table, and it smells of coffee when it is cut.

Density 640 kg/m³ · oiled near-black

03

Texas mesquite

Prosopis glandulosa

Quarried from fence posts and fallen limbs. Hard on the gouge, unpredictable in the grain, and the only wood that smells of the county it came from.

Density 880 kg/m³ · check for cracks

04

Olive

Olea europaea

Brought back in a suitcase from a grove near Zikhron. Figured, heavy, and a little oily; it holds a point longer than anything else on the rack.

Density 990 kg/m³ · limited lots

05

Osage orange

Maclura pomifera

Yellow when cut, honey-brown after a year in the window. So hard that I sharpen twice per dreidel and charge for it.

Density 855 kg/m³ · ages to bronze

06

Cherry

Prunus serotina

Warm, close-grained, darkens with handling. A cherry dreidel used every December will be the colour of a violin by the time a child is ten.

Density 560 kg/m³ · eases beautifully
