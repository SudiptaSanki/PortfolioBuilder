Visit · by appointment

There is no shopfront, only a bench.

The lathe is in a back room behind a print shop on East 6th. Write first and we will find a Saturday morning; bring the wood if you have it, or just bring your hands and try a spin.

Write to the bench

I answer on Sunday evenings and Wednesday mornings. For a repair, tell me the wood and what broke; a photograph helps more than a description.

This is a demonstration of the bench book; nothing is sent anywhere.

Finding it

  • The address

    1204 East 6th Street, rear workshop
    Austin, TX 78702
    The door is in the alley behind the print shop, under the tin awning with the number 2 painted on it.

  • Hours

    By appointment: Saturday 9 a.m. – 1 p.m., Wednesday 5 – 8 p.m.
    No drop-ins during a run — the lathe is loud and the door is locked.

  • Reach me

    kenji@vargaswoodturning.com
    +1 512 555 0184 · calls after 6 p.m.

  • What to bring

    The wood, if it is sentimental — a chair leg, a table edge, a board from a house. Nothing larger than a fence post; the lathe will not take it and I will not cut down a tree for a top.

  • Parking

    Two spaces in the alley, or the lot on Waller Street, four minutes' walk. The 6th Street buses stop at Waller.
A note on the holiday

From September the bench is booked. If you want a dreidel for Hanukkah, write in August. I would rather say no in August than send a rushed top in December; the point of the object is that it lasts.
