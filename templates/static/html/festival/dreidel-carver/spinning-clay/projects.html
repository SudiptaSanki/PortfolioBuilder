Commissions · 2019 to 2026

Six commissions and what each one taught.

Every commission is a different problem: a specific wood, a specific weight, a date that cannot move. These are the ones that changed how I work, with the numbers that mattered.

The commission book

No. 41
Oct–Nov 2024

Two hundred for a museum shop

The shop at a Houston museum wanted a small, affordable wooden dreidel for its Hanukkah table. Two hundred pieces, 60 mm, hard maple, letters in a deep navy milk paint that matches the museum's identity. I turned them in batches of twenty-five, testing every fifth one. Twelve failed the tip test and were set aside; the museum put the twelve in a case labelled “rejects, 2024”, which is the nicest thing a museum has done for me.

Wood Hard maple, 60 mm
Quantity 200 (188 delivered)
Finish Tung oil × 3, navy letters
Lead time 7 weeks
Note All 200 fitted with brass pins

No. 44
July 2025

A chair, made into a top

A woman in Dallas brought a broken walnut dining chair in the back of her car. Her grandfather had sat in it every Friday for forty years; the back legs had split beyond repair. We cut two lengths from the legs and turned four dreidels. Two faces keep the original stain and wear, two are newly cut and oiled, so the top looks like what it is: an old thing made useful again. The remaining wood went back to her in a box, because she could not decide.

Wood Walnut, reclaimed chair
Quantity 4 tops, 66 mm
Finish Oil only, no paint
Lead time 5 weeks
Note Two faces left as worn

No. 47
Sept 2025

Three the size of a soup pot

A theatre company in Dallas needed oversized dreidels it could spin by hand on stage. At 240 mm they are heavy, so the bodies are hollow — 9 mm walls, a lead-free brass weight low in the point, and a maple dowel handle that can take a gloved hand. All three spin for eighteen seconds on a painted stage floor, which was the requirement. The audience sees the letters from the back row.

Wood Maple, 240 mm
Quantity 3, hollow
Weight 1.9 kg each
Lead time 9 weeks
Note Painted letters, 40 mm tall

No. 51
Feb 2026

Nine for a family that loses them

A family in Austin ordered nine dreidels — one for each cousin — from the same board of cherry so the grain would match. They asked for a small initial burned into the base of each, a gift from a grandmother who had died that winter, so every cousin would have the same object and know whose it was. I burned nine initials with a fine tip and got one wrong; that one is mine.

Wood Cherry, 70 mm
Quantity 9, one board
Finish Oil, initials burned
Lead time 6 weeks
Note One kept back, initial wrong

No. 52
Mar 2026

A synagogue's loaner set

A congregation in Austin lends dreidels to children at its Hanukkah party so no child plays without one. They wanted thirty sturdy tops, hard to break, easy to clean, and hard to walk off with. Mesquite, 78 mm, with the congregation's Hebrew name on the base. The first twelve were too heavy for small hands and were re-turned; the final set has a 58 g body and a blunt tip that survives a tile floor.

Wood Texas mesquite, 78 mm
Quantity 30
Finish Hard wax, no paint
Lead time 8 weeks
Note Blunt tip, 58 g

No. 55
Jun 2026

A top that had to be silent

A recording studio ordered one dreidel with no brass pin — a wooden tip instead — for a session where every tick would be picked up by the microphones. It spins for four seconds and sounds like a finger on a table. The engineer sent me the recording. You can hear the wood slow down, which is not something I had thought about before, and which is now the reason I make a silent version on request.

Wood Olive, 74 mm
Quantity 1
Finish Oil, wooden tip
Lead time 3 weeks
Note Silent spin, 4.0 s

How to order

Tell me the date first.

The holiday is fixed and the bench is not fast. Orders for Hanukkah need to be placed by the end of September for a single top, and by the middle of August for a run. Everything else has more room.

Ask about a commission →
  1. 01

    Wood and weight

    Tell me the wood, the size and who will hold it. A child under six wants 60 mm and under 70 g.

  2. 02

    Sample first

    For runs over twenty, I turn one sample and send a photograph before the batch starts. The sample is charged at the single price.

  3. 03

    Deposit

    Half on order, half on despatch. Reclaimed or sentimental wood is quoted separately because it may not survive the lathe.
