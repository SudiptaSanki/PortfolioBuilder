The competition catrina

Comparsa de Xochimilco · 1 November 2025

Daniela had competed for four years and always lost to the same dancer. This year she asked for the high catrina brow, the one that changes the whole architecture of the face, and for a single blue tear, which is not traditional and which she wanted anyway because her mother had died in March.

The brow line took twenty minutes on its own. The tear is the only cool colour on the face, and it worked precisely because it broke the palette.

  • Time 2 h 10 m
  • Pigments 5
  • Result first place

Six faces before sunrise

Film call · Sierra Norte · October 2025

A production shooting a Day of the Dead sequence needed six background faces painted in a village hall with two lamps and no running water. The brief said "traditional", which is not a brief. We agreed on a restrained design: fine marigold rays, two flowers at the temple, no tear.

At four in the morning in a cold hall, a restrained design is also the only one that survives. The first face was on camera at 6:40 and did not need a single touch-up.

  • Time 5 h for six
  • Pigments 4
  • Touch-ups none

The family series

Portrait commission · Barrio de Xochimilco · 2 November 2025

A family of five asked for matching faces for the graveside visit, one design repeated across three generations. We built it around verdigris leaves, which is the colour of the older women's rebozos in the family photographs from the 1970s.

The grandmother refused the full white base and asked for her own skin to show through. We painted the motifs directly on, no base at all. It was the most beautiful face of the five and the only one that was not a calavera in the strict sense.

  • Time 4 h 30 m for five
  • Pigments 3
  • Design one, repeated

Twelve first-timers

Workshop · Casa de Cultura · September 2025

A two-hour workshop where each person painted the face of the person beside them. Most had never held a brush designed for skin. The rule was three motifs and no more, so nobody could hide behind detail.

Two of the twelve now come back every year to help at the comparsa. That is the whole point of teaching it in a room with bad light: the craft has to survive without the perfect conditions.

  • Time 2 h
  • People 12
  • Motif limit 3
