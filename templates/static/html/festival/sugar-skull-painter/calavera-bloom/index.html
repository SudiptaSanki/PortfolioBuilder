The kit

What goes on the face

  1. 01

    Base

    A cosmetic-grade white cream, thinned with a little glycerin so it moves on skin instead of drying in the brush. Two thin passes, never one thick one; the face has to breathe for three hours.

  2. 02

    Colour

    Pressed pigments, activated with a drop of setting liquid. Magenta, marigold, verdigris and ink are the four I mix from; every other shade is one of those with white or black in it.

  3. 03

    Line

    A synthetic brush with a six-millimetre point for the petal edges and a finer one for the rays around the eyes. The line is the part that has to be symmetrical, so it is drawn last, when the base is dry.

Half of the painting is the mirror. The other half is the person deciding, somewhere around the third flower, that they are allowed to be seen.
