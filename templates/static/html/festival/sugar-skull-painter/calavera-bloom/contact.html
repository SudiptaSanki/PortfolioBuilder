How the booking runs

  • I answer within a day with the nearest open date and a price per face.
  • The date is held for three days while you confirm with your group.
  • Two weeks before, we agree the design in a short call or over messages.
  • On the day, come with clean skin and no moisturiser. Everything else is mine.

Good to know

The base survives a procession and a long afternoon, not a swim. Removal is with oil and warm water, and I send the cloth home with you. Under-12s are painted only with a parent in the room, and only with a patch test first.
