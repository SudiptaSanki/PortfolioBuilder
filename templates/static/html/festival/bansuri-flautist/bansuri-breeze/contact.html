Write, and the date is held

A written enquiry holds a date for seven days while we talk. Email works as well as the form — both reach the same desk.

Direct

bookings@nikhilvartak.example

Replies within two working days. Teaching enquiries come to the same address — mark the subject “Lessons” and the January waitlist is checked first.

What to send

  • Concerts: the date (or two), the city and room, the expected audience, and — if you have one — the hour you hope for. The raga follows the hour.
  • Accompaniment: who is singing or playing, their tonic (Sa), the ragas proposed, and rehearsal availability.
  • Sessions: the cue’s length, the scene in one line, your deadline, and whether a score exists or the raga choice is open.
  • Lessons: your age, any instrument you already play, and whether you own a flute yet (buy nothing before the first lesson).
