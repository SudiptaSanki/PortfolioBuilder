## Persona
**Nikhil Vartak** is a fictional demonstration persona created for this
template. His guru (Pandit Shrikant Lele), venues, students, fees and
calendar are invented. The raga scholarship (thaat, prahar timings,
arohana/avarohana, pakad phrases), bansuri facts (tonic naming, hole
layout, sizes, bamboo care) are real and were checked against standard
Hindustani practice. The fiction is disclosed in every page footer.

## Sound ruling
The trade is sound, so a demonstration PASSES the owner's need-test —
in its teaching form only. The "Hear the scale" player synthesises the
selected raga's arohana and avarohana live with Web Audio (sine +
harmonic + filtered breath noise, just-intonation ratios from an E
bansuri's Sa at 329.63 Hz, quiet Sa–Pa drone, master gain 0.22 through
a compressor). It exists only after a deliberate Play tap; it is
labelled on the page as browser synthesis — not a recording and not a
performance. No autoplay, no hover audio, nothing else on the site
makes a sound. The stub's decorative festival audio/garland motion was
removed.
