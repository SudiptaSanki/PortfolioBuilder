Winter weeks · 2025/26

Six weeks,
and what each one is.

The house does not do packages, but the season has a shape and some weeks are better than others for some people. All prices are per person for seven nights, half board, in a double room, and include the ski pass unless it says otherwise.

5–12 December · opening week

The First Tracks

The lifts open on the 5th and the village is still the village. Quiet pistes, early dark, and the lowest rate of the season. Best for people who care more about snow than about apres-ski.

Includes7 nights half board
6-day Ski Arlberg pass
One guided day
€1,340

11–18 January · the empty week

The Quiet Week

After the first week of January the valley empties. Cold, clear, and the best snow of the winter on the north faces. This is the week I would book if it were my own holiday.

Includes7 nights half board
6-day pass
Sauna every evening
€1,690

7–14 February · half-term

Family Half-Term

Two family rooms, ski school at 09:30, and dinner at 18:30 for the children so the adults can eat at the long table afterwards. The busiest week of the year and the one to book first.

Includes7 nights half board
Passes for four
Two half-day lessons each
€1,820

2–9 March · the classic

White Ring Week

Longer days and the 22 km White Ring in condition. I ski it with guests on the Wednesday, start at 09:15, cake at the halfway hut. A timing chip is optional and quietly discouraged.

Includes7 nights half board
6-day pass
Guided White Ring day
€1,640

16–23 March · spring

Sun on the South Face

Firm mornings, soft afternoons, lunch on the terrace at 1,800 m. Ski until four and then sit outside until the sun goes behind the ridge at about 17:50.

Includes7 nights half board
6-day pass
Terrace lunch once
€1,520

6–13 April · closing

The Last Week

Corn snow, half the lifts closed, and the house at its most relaxed. Bring sunglasses rather than goggles. The season ends on the 26th and the stove goes out the day after.

Includes7 nights half board
5-day pass
One snowshoe walk
€1,180

01Before you book

What is in the price, and what is not

Included: the room, breakfast, afternoon cake, four-course dinner, the sauna, the boot room, the visitor tax, and the lift pass where the week says so. Coffee and tea all day; the wine list is priced at what the merchant charges us.

Not included: ski rental (about €38 a day), lessons for anyone not on a package, the guide on off-piste days, massages, and the taxi from Langen am Arlberg if you arrive after the last bus.

Deposit: 30% at booking, the balance on departure. Bank transfer; cards are accepted but the machine is old and slow.

Cancellation: free until 30 days before arrival, 50% until 14 days, full after that unless we can re-let the room — which in February we usually can.

Minimum stay

Two nights, except over Christmas and New Year, when it is seven.

Children

Welcome. Under six in a family room free with two adults; cots and a high chair are here already.

Arrival

Rooms from 15:00. If you arrive earlier, leave the bags and go up the mountain — the lift is three minutes away.

Which week suits you?

Tell me who is coming and what they can ski. I will tell you honestly if the week you want is wrong for your group — February half-term is a wonderful week for a family and a terrible one for a couple who want silence.
