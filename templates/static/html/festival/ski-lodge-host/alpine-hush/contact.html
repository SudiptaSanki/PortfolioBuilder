Getting here

By train: Zürich or Innsbruck to Langen am Arlberg, then the 91 bus to Zug (15 minutes). Tell me the arrival time and I will meet the bus.

By car: A14 to the Arlberg tunnel, exit at Langen, then 12 km up the valley. Winter tyres are compulsory in practice, not just in law.

By air: Zürich 2 h 45, Innsbruck 1 h 30, Munich 3 h. A private transfer from Zürich is about €420 for up to four.

Arrival and departure

Rooms from 15:00 on arrival day. On departure, breakfast and the boot room are yours until 11:00, and you can leave the car in the yard for the day if you want one last morning on the mountain.

01Before you write

Three things worth knowing

The stairs. Nine rooms, three floors, no lift. If anyone in the party cannot manage steep wooden stairs, the house is the wrong choice and I would rather tell you now than at midnight with a suitcase.

The table. Dinner is one sitting at a long table. It is the best thing about the house and the worst thing about it, depending on the person. If you want to eat alone, say so and I will set the small table by the window — but I will ask why.

The weather. We do not guarantee snow, and in a bad December nobody can. What we guarantee is that the house is warm, the food is cooked, and if the lifts are shut Sepp will drive you to a museum in Bregenz.

Response time

Within a day in season. In November, when the house is closed, it may take two — I am usually on a ladder.

Payment

Bank transfer or card. The deposit holds the room; the balance is settled on departure, with the wine list added honestly.
