Nine rooms, one long table,
and a mountain that does
the rest.

Stillhaus sits at the quiet end of Zug, three minutes from the lift and a hundred metres from the noise of Lech. I am the third Brunner to keep it: breakfast at eight, dinner at half past seven, and the front door key on a hook by the stairs.

01The house

Warm wood, cold air, no lobby music.

The house was built in 1978 by my grandfather, who made his money as a carpenter in Bregenz and spent it on a slope above Zug because he liked the morning light. He built nine rooms, a kitchen too small for the business it would do, and a stove in the corner of the dining room that has not gone out in a winter since.

We are not a hotel. There is no reception desk, no minibar, no television in the rooms, and no lift — the stairs are original and steep. What there is: a boot room with a heated rack, a sauna on the lower floor, a small library of climbing and ski books, and a dining room where everybody eats at the same time whether they know each other or not.

Most guests come back. A third of the bookings each winter are people who were here the winter before, and a handful have been coming since my father ran it. That is the whole marketing plan, and it is why we close for five weeks in May.

The rooms

  • 4 doubles south-facing, balcony over the valley, bath.

Rates include breakfast, afternoon cake and dinner. No single supplement in January.

Breakfast

07:30–09:30. Bread from the bakery in Lech, mountain cheese, eggs from a farm in Klösterle, and the coffee machine that my father bought in 1994 and refuses to replace.

Dinner

19:30, one sitting, four courses. Fish on Fridays, Käsespätzle on Tuesdays, and whatever the garden gives in September — there is no menu to order from.

Sauna

16:00–20:00, Finnish, 90 °C, with a cold shower and a window onto the trees. Towels in the basket by the door.

    03The mountain

    What is outside the boot room door.

    Ski area

    Ski Arlberg

    305 km of piste and 87 lifts, linked from Zürs through Lech and Stuben to St. Anton. The Zug lift is 180 m from the door; from the top you can ski to Zürs without taking a bus.

    The classic

    Der Weiße Ring

    22 km, five lifts, 5,500 m of descent, and a timing chip if you want one. Start by 09:30 and you can be back for cake. I have skied it 41 times and it has never once been the same.

    Off-piste

    With a guide, always

    The runs off Rüfikopf and beyond the Zürs saddle are serious and the snowpack is continental. We book guides from the Lech school for guests; nobody leaves the house with a beacon and no plan.

    Not skiing

    The valley, slowly

    Snowshoe to the Formarinsee, the Lech river path to Zug, a morning at the Huber house in Lech, and the church in Stuben that has been rebuilt twice and is still the coldest building in Austria.
