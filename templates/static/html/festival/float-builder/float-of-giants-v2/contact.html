The booking window

Designs close seven months before parade day — for the April corsos, the previous September. Flower-work crews and parade-day crews book later, but not much: flower weeks are claimed by February.

What to tell us

  • The parade — which one, and its date. The regulations come with it.
  • The route, if you know it — its tightest corner and lowest bridge decide more than any sketch.
  • New build or re-skin — and if a re-skin, what is standing in storage.
  • The honest budget shape — Table F1 on the cover page is our whole price list; measure your dream against it first.
