Six facts and we can quote the float.

Length, height, giants, people on the deck, the route, and the date. Everything else is detail we can settle later, but those six decide whether the thing is buildable at all.

The form checks the six facts and prepares an email. Nothing is sent from this page.

What decides the permit

  • Height over 6 m: Class B, route surveyed for cables and branches.
  • Height over 8 m or width over 2.4 m: Class C, special escort.
  • Any passenger on the deck: fire brigade inspection with the load sheet.
  • Generator on board: separate fused circuit and a ventilated box.

What we need from you

  • Photographs of every low point on the route, with something for scale.
  • The gradient of the steepest street, if you know it.
  • Whether there is a weighbridge in your city, or you want the float weighed here.
  • The name of whoever signs for the inspection.

Money

Forty per cent on signing, forty on delivery of the chassis and frame, twenty before the road test. Transport is quoted separately and paid direct to the driver. We do not take a float off the yard until the inspection is signed.
