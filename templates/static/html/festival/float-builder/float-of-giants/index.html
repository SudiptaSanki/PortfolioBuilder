Four things the workshop does

Floats are quoted from a load sheet, not a sketch. Every job below starts with the same four questions: how heavy, how tall, how many people on the deck, and how does it stop.

Workshop capability, Carnival season 2027. Prices are ex-works, before transport and licensing.
WorkWhat it includesLead timeFrom
Chassis and deck Steel ladder frame on a donated or bought truck chassis, deck plated and edged, brake and light check, axle load calculated. 3 to 5 weeks €14,000
Giant puppets Carved EPS head and hands, aluminium spine, silk suit, internal harness. From 2.8 m to 4.2 m, one or two operators. 4 weeks each €4,200
Truss and rigging Welded towers, cross-bracing, guard rails, harness anchor points and a signed load sheet for the fire brigade. 2 weeks €3,800
Skin and finish Silk or stretch lycra over a wire frame, painted with pigment and casein, plus LED strips on a separate circuit. 2 to 3 weeks €2,600
