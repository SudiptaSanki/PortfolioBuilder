What to tell me

Four things, in any order. The rest we can settle on the phone.

Who is in the photograph

Names and ages. A dog counts as a person for scheduling, and gets four minutes.

What worries you

A shy child, a beard, a wheelchair, a loud room, a parent who hates being photographed. All of it changes how I set up.

What you want to do with it

A card, a wall, a gift for a grandparent. It changes the crop, and sometimes the light.

Your dates

Two or three options is ideal. Weekday mornings are quietest and cheapest.

Prices, plainly

Mini $95 · Classic $180 · Family $290 · At Home $420. The session fee covers the time and the edited gallery. Prints from $45 and are ordered separately. Nothing is added at the end of the night.
