03 — HOW IT GOES

Eight minutes with Santa, and the rest is preparation

The photograph is the easy part. Most of the work is making the room quiet enough that a four-year-old stops checking the door.

01 — BEFORE

A phone call, not a form

Ten minutes on the phone tells me more than a booking system: who is shy, who has a costume, who is frightened of beards. You get the room to yourself; nobody else is waiting in it.

02 — THE SITTING

Santa sits down first

He is already in the chair when the child comes in, and he does not stand up or boom. The first frame is always taken from the doorway, before anyone has decided to smile. That is usually the one.

03 — AFTER

Two weeks, then the prints

A gallery of the edited frames, no watermark, no upsell at the end of the night. Prints are ordered from the same page and arrive in a cardboard tube, which children enjoy more than the photograph.

“A child does not need Santa to be real. They need the room to be calm enough to decide for themselves.”
Noah Vargas · session notes, December 2024

04 — BOOK

December fills in October

I open the calendar on the first of October and take a deposit of half to hold a slot. If you have a child who needs a quiet room, say so in the first line and I will find you a weekday morning.
