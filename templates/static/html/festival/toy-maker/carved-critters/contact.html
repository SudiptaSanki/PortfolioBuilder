Everything is made to order in batches of eight. The wait is six weeks, more if the wood is still drying. A hare costs €48 and arrives with a spare set of ears.

Six weeksBatches of 8EU shipping
Over ten is a batch of its own; I will say so honestly.
If it is a gift, say the date and I will wrap it in paper and string, the way the market does.

How it runs

  • I write back within three days with the batch date and the drying time.
  • Payment on dispatch, by bank transfer. No deposit for a single piece.
  • Each animal is wrapped in paper and tied with string; no plastic in the parcel.
  • If it arrives damaged, send a photograph and I will replace it. That has happened twice in thirty years.

Shipping

  • Germany: €5, 2–3 days after dispatch
  • EU: €12, 4–7 days
  • Two pieces or more: postage is on me inside Germany
  • Collection from the workshop on Saturdays, 10 a.m. to 4 p.m.
