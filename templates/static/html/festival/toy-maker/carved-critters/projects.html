The shelf

Six animals,
all in production

Each piece is turned to the same measurements every time, in batches of eight or ten. What changes is the wood, because the wood is whatever the sawmill dried best that year.

Six piecesBatches of 8–10Six weeks' lead time

The hare

Design 1994 · lime · 21 cm

The animal the workshop is built on. Wide body, long separate ears, four wheels on beech axles. The ears are pinned, not glued, so they can be replaced; I keep a drawer of spares for the post.

  • Pieces 6
  • Finish Beeswax
  • Age 1 year and up
  • Price €48

The bear

Design 2007 · beech · 16 cm

Made for smaller hands and a shop in Dresden that wanted nothing that could break off in a mouth. The ears are sanded almost flat and the nose is a shallow dome, not a separate piece.

  • Pieces 5
  • Finish Oil
  • Age 1 year and up
  • Price €42

The fox

Design 2018 · walnut · 24 cm

A commission from a kindergarten. The tail curls on a small brass spring, so it can be pulled by a determined two-year-old without snapping. The spring is the only metal part in any toy here.

  • Pieces 8
  • Finish Oil and wax
  • Age 2 years and up
  • Price €62

The duck

Design 2011 · lime · 19 cm

A low body on four small wheels, with a turned head that sits slightly forward. The beak is stained with plant dye, which fades to a warm peach after a few years of handling. I consider that a feature.

  • Pieces 6
  • Finish Beeswax
  • Age 1 year and up
  • Price €44

The horse

Design 1999 · lime · 23 cm

The tallest animal on the shelf and the one that sells least, because it tips when a child pulls it too fast. I keep it in production anyway; it was the last design my father finished.

  • Pieces 7
  • Finish Beeswax
  • Age 2 years and up
  • Price €58

The goat

Design 2021 · maple · 18 cm

Jointed at the neck and the knees, so it can be stood up or laid down. The most complex piece I make and the one I am least sure about; a toy with five joints is a toy with five places to fail.

  • Pieces 11
  • Finish Oil
  • Age 3 years and up
  • Price €74
