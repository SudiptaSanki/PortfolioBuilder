Before you write

The questions that decide most bookings.

Can the company dance on a stage with carpet?

No. The zapateado needs a hard, hollow surface to sound, and carpet absorbs the heel and grabs the skirt. If the venue cannot provide a wooden platform, we can bring a portable one for an additional $12,000 MXN and an extra hour of setup.

How far do you travel?

Anywhere in Mexico with four weeks' notice. Abroad, we have toured to the United States and Colombia; two months' notice is needed for visas and for flying the skirts as excess baggage. Twelve-metre skirts travel in a crate, not a suitcase.

Do you dance to recorded music?

Only for schools and community dates where the budget cannot carry the musicians. The evening programme is always live: two violins, vihuela, guitarra de golpe and trumpet. A recorded track flattens the half-beat the skirt works against.

Can we book a single dancer for a private party?

Yes, for events within Guadalajara. One dancer, one musician, twenty-five minutes, from $16,000 MXN. It is a different show: smaller skirt, closer audience, more talking between dances.

How do classes work?

Saturday mornings at the studio, six students to a floor. Beginners at nine, intermediate at eleven. The first month is without music and without a skirt; wear a hard-soled shoe and bring water. $1,800 MXN a month.

What deposit do you ask for?

One third on signing, the balance seven days before the performance. If a date is cancelled inside fourteen days, the deposit covers the musicians and the hall; we do not keep it as a penalty beyond that.

Planning the 2027 season now.

July and October fill first. If you are programming a theatre, write before the end of January and we will hold a provisional date for two weeks.
