Stage · selected productions

What the company
puts on a stage.

Two programmes travel: a seventy-minute evening of Jalisco, Veracruz and Nayarit, and a shorter family set for plazas and schools. Both are danced with live music, because a recorded trumpet never gives the skirt the half-beat it needs.

14 dancers · 5 musicians 70 min evening · 35 min family set Stage minimum 8 × 6 m

Evening programme · 2026

Jarabe Tapatío, Teatro Degollado

Our home season, four nights in July. The first dance is the Jarabe with the full twelve-metre skirt, danced by six women and followed by a solo zapateado on a hollow platform laid over the stage. The skirt needs two dressers and a clear wing of three metres, which the Degollado has and most plazas do not.

Festival · 2025

La Bamba at the Cervantino

Guanajuato gave us a stone plaza and a slope, which changes the footwork entirely. The Veracruz set was cut to twenty-two minutes for the outdoor stage and the closing zapateado was moved to the flat apron so the sound would carry. The white skirt came home with a grey hem and never went white again.

Tour · 2025

El Sinaloense in Chicago

Eleven dancers, five musicians, two crates of skirts flown as excess baggage. The Chicago audience was mostly families from Jalisco and Sinaloa, and they sang the verses louder than the trumpet. We added a second remate because the hall would not let us leave.

Fiestas de Octubre · 2024

Los Machetes, Guadalajara

The machete dance is a counting dance: eight strikes, a turn, eight more. We staged it with twelve dancers in two lines, which is twice the usual number and turns the sound into a single instrument. Rehearsals were two weeks longer than any other piece that season.

Guest programme · 2023

Danza de los Viejitos, Michoacán

We were invited to close a Purépecha programme in Pátzcuaro with the old men's dance, which is not ours to own. Four of our dancers learned it from a family in Jarácuaro over three weekends, and we performed it with their permission and their musicians. The masks were borrowed, not copied.

Community · 2026

Niños del Barrio

Sixty children from three neighbourhoods, four Saturdays, one borrowed hall. They dance in skirts the studio has outgrown, cut down and re-hemmed by the mothers. The programme ends with a plaza performance in June that has nothing to do with touring and is the best afternoon of our year.
