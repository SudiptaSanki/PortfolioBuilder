Before you write · 12

Three things the studio always asks

Not bureaucracy. Each one changes the geometry.

01

Which way it faces

A north wall gets a fine line; a west wall gets a pattern that can hold its own against the afternoon sun.

02

How it is made

Tile needs a strap wide enough to hide the grout. Plaster can hold a hairline. Brick has to land on whole bricks.

03

Where the edges are

A pattern that is cut at the edge of a wall is a different pattern from one that finishes in a complete rosette.
