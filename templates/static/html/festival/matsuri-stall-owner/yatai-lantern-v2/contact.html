Write it on the board

One way in, no queue: the form below, or straight to kenji@sato-ya.example. I reply within two days — slower in festival weeks, when the lanterns come first. For a private hire, the useful numbers are the date, the ground, and roughly how many mouths; the prep planner on the front page will show you what one stall can honestly serve.

Before you write · 料金

The hire, priced plainly

From ¥85,000 for 3 hours, including the first 100 portions. Extra portions at menu price (¥600 / ¥700 / ¥400), extra hours ¥15,000 each. Ground must fit a 3.6 m cart and give me one metre of clear space behind the griddles — that metre is not negotiable; it is where the gas lives.

July and September belong to Yamakasa and Hōjōya. Every other week, the board is open.
