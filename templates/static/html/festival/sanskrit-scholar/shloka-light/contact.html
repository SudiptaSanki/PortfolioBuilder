What to include

A useful letter is short and specific. These five things make the difference between a reply and a polite deferral.

The text
Author, title, chapter or verse number if you know it. A photograph of the page is better than a remembered quotation.
The question
One question, stated plainly. If there are three, number them; I will answer them in order or say which I cannot answer.
The script
Devanagari, transliteration, or both. If your text is in another script, say which; I read Devanagari and IAST fluently, and I am slow with Grantha.
The deadline
If there is one, put it in the first line. A real deadline is not rude. A false one wastes the goodwill of both parties.
