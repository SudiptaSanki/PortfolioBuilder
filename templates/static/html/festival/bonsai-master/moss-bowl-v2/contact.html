The bench: Reijerskoop nursery lane, Boskoop, the Netherlands. Visits Thursday to Saturday, by appointment — write first, so the kettle and the right bowls are ready.

Collecting a bowl

Finished bowls are collected from Boskoop, or couriered within the Netherlands packed in moss and straw for €18. A bowl over €500 travels only by collection or by my own van — some things should not be posted.

Commissions

The book opens each year at the repotting weeks (from 22 February) and closes when the bench is full — usually by mid-March. The deposit is 40%, by the formula on the home page.
