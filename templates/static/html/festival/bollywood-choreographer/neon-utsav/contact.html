Book the floor

Tell me the date and the number of dancers. The rest we can work out.

Film, stage and sangeet enquiries all come to the same inbox. I answer on Mondays and Thursdays; if your date is inside two weeks, say so in the first line.

Before you write

Three things help more than a long brief: the date, the number of dancers, and whether the piece is for a lens or a live audience. If you have reference footage, send a link rather than a file.

Rates

Film numbers are quoted per number and include two revision passes. Stage work is quoted per rehearsal week plus a performance fee. Sangeet floors are a flat fee for three sessions and a rehearsal recording for the family.

The room

Rehearsals happen at the Andheri West studio, second floor above the printing press. It holds fourteen dancers comfortably and eighteen if everyone is friendly.
