Every production on this site began with three facts: the date, the songs, and who is dancing. Send those and the reply will come with a planner run and an itemised estimate — the same arithmetic this site prints, applied to your calendar.

What to send

  • The date — of the wedding, the shoot, or the opening night. Everything else is arithmetic.
  • The songs — titles if you have them; “not chosen yet” is also an answer, and choosing them is billable thinking time, said in advance.
  • Who is dancing — a trained troupe, a mixed group, or a family that has never performed. The honest class, please: the planner's rates differ because the floors differ.
  • The floor — where rehearsals can happen, and how many evenings a week the group can honestly give.
