Can I get a bunch today

If you write before eleven, yes. After that the buckets are picked over and what is left is what is left. Call rather than write if it is urgent.

Do you deliver to Ernakulam

Before six in the evening, ₹300. I use a boy with a scooter, not a courier, so the flowers arrive upright and not in a bag.

What flowers will you have

Whatever Broadway gave us: usually marigold, jasmine, tuberose, roses from Thripunithura and chrysanthemum. Orchids only by order. No imported roses, ever.

How far ahead for a wedding

Two months, and a budget. I take four a month and the good Saturdays go first. If you want mogra in June, tell me in March, because the season is short and the price moves.

Do you do funerals

Yes, and at short notice. White, no scent if the family prefers, delivered to the church or the house within two hours anywhere in Fort Kochi. The price is what the flowers cost plus the delivery.
