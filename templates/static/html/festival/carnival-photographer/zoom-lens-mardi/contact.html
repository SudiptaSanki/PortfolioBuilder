Assignments and prints

Tell me which night, and what the desk needs

The season fills by January. If you need a parade covered, say which one and how fast the pictures have to move: a nightly wire is a different job from a magazine essay, and the access is different too.

This page does not send anything. It checks the brief so you can copy a complete one into an email. I answer within two days, sooner during the season.

Travel outside Orleans Parish at cost. Overtime after 2 am is time and a half, because the last parade of the night is the one that hurts.

What I need from you

A press credential or a letter from the outlet, the desk contact on the night, and the filing time in the timezone the desk actually works in.

If you are commissioning photographs of a Black Masking Indian suit, the price includes prints for the person in the frame. That is not negotiable and it is built into the rate.

Turnaround

Same-night wire: a first edit within 90 minutes of the last float, delivered by FTP. Next-morning: 20 frames by 9 am. Essays: three to five days, with captions and a contact sheet.

I do not send unedited cards. Everything is culled, captioned and colour-checked before it leaves the studio.

Studio

Bywater, New Orleans. Visits by appointment, because the darkroom is also the office and there is usually a print in the wash.

Email is best during the season. The phone does not get answered on parade nights.
