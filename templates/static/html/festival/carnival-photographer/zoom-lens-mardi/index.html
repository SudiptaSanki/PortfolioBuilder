How I work

Three rules for a night parade

  1. 01

    Meter for the face, not the float

    A float is designed to be bright. Meter the people in front of it or you will bring home a beautifully exposed dragon and a crowd of black paper cut-outs.

  2. 02

    Stay out of the bead line

    The space between a float and the crowd is where the throws land. Standing in it ruins your frame and somebody’s evening. Shoot from the second row, over shoulders.

  3. 03

    Ask the maskers

    A Black Masking Indian suit is a year of beadwork and a family’s promise. I ask before every frame, I do not photograph children without a parent, and I bring the prints back.
