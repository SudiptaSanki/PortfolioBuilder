Three facts start everything

The krewe, the date and the route. From those, the reply is a block plan and a fixed fee in writing — before you commit to anything, and before any deposit is mentioned.

Write direct

hello@odetteboudreaux.example

Email is answered within two working days — inside one during the season itself. If your parade is this week, say so in the subject line; late enquiries are read first and answered honestly, including when the answer is no.

What to include

  • Krewe or organisation, and your role in it
  • Parade date and route, as published
  • What the photographs are for — programme, press, archive, social
  • One side of the route or both (the second-shooter line)
  • Delivery speed: standard 7 days, or the 48-hour rush

Booking window

The 2027 season diary is open. Two krewe-commission slots remain; single-parade days are taken in diary order. The estimator on The Wall gives the fee shape before you write.
