02The door on Macedonio Alcalá

Tell me the date
and how many glasses.

Bookings are by message, answered by a person, usually within a day. The room seats nine and fills from Thursday onward, so the further ahead you write the better your chance of the night you want.

Getting here

On foot: four minutes from the zócalo, on the east side of the street. Look for the papel shop; we are above it.

Taxi: ask for Macedonio Alcalá 407, not “the mezcal place”. There are eleven of those.

From the airport: 25 minutes by taxi; the last flight from Mexico City lands at 21:40, which is too late for the Thursday table.

After you write

I reply with the night, the price and the door code. The deposit link comes only for private tables and the palenque day. If you do not hear by the next evening, write again — sometimes the message goes to the wrong folder.

Before the first copita

Eat something with fat in it an hour before. The bread on the table is not decoration; mezcal at 47% needs something to land on. Water is always there and always free, and asking for it is not an insult.

Do not wear perfume to a tasting. It sits in the same part of your nose as the agave and it will win. If you smoke, the last cigarette half an hour before is a kindness to the person beside you.

Bring a notebook if you are that sort of person. I will lend you a pencil, badly sharpened.

Accessibility

The room is on the first floor up an original 1930s staircase, 22 steps, no lift. Tell me if that is a problem and we will use the patio downstairs instead.

Languages

Spanish, English, and enough Zapotec to greet the people I buy from properly. The tasting notes are in both languages.
