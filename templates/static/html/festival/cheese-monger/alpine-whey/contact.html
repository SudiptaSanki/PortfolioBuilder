Counter
Thu–Sat, 9–17

Visit · Dorfstrasse 14, Glarus

The counter is four metres from the cave door.

The shop is open three days a week, which is as much as a cave this size needs. Come for the tasting, stay for the tour at eleven on Saturday, and bring a jacket for the cellar. There is no café and no card machine for the tours; the cheese takes cards.

Opening

Thursday, Friday and Saturday, 09:00–17:00. Closed Sunday to Wednesday, when the cave is turned and the wheels are cut for the weekend.

Getting here

Trains from Zurich run twice an hour and take about an hour; the shop is a six-minute walk from Glarus station, on the left past the church. There is parking behind the yard, two spaces, usually full on Saturday.

Tasting

Five wheels at the counter, CHF 12, no booking needed. The cave tour is Saturday at 11:00, CHF 20, limited to twelve people because the corridor is narrow. Book by the form or by phone.

Orders

Restaurants and shops: I deliver within the canton on Tuesdays and to Zurich on Thursdays. Minimum order is one wheel; a wheel is 30–38 kg and I will cut it to your specification.

Raw milk, plainly

Everything here is made from unpasteurised milk. Swiss law allows it, and it is safe when the milk and the cave are clean, but it is not the right cheese for a pregnant woman, a small child or anyone immunosuppressed. Ask me and I will point you to the two pasteurised wheels I keep at the back.
