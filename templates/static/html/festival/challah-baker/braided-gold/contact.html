Order · Friday table

Tell me the table and the week.

Orders close Wednesday at 18:00 for that Friday. I bake Thursday night, braid on Friday morning and hand the loaves over from nine until two. Everything is weighed, nothing is frozen, and the price is the price.

Pickup

5417 avenue du Parc, Mile End. The door is on the street side, between the two windows. Fridays from 9:00 to 14:00; if you are late, call and I will leave the bag in the vestibule.

Delivery

Twelve loaves or more, anywhere on the island, $12 flat. I leave at 08:30 and the route is ordered by neighbourhood: Plateau and Mile End first, then Outremont, then the west side by eleven.

Prices

Friday Six $18 · Round Crown $20 · Turban $20 · Rye and Honey $19 · Za'atar $16 · Water Challah $15. Rolls are $2.50 each or $26 for a dozen. Cash, debit or e-transfer at pickup; an invoice for restaurants.

Holidays

Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur orders close ten days before the holiday. I bake only the shapes on the list in the weeks around them, so a turban ordered on the Tuesday will not happen, however nicely it is asked.

One thing to know

The kitchen handles wheat, eggs and sesame. It is dairy-free and nut-free, but it is a small kitchen in a building with a shared ventilation shaft. If you have a severe allergy, tell me and I will say honestly whether I can bake for you.

Questions

Four things people ask before the first order

How long does it keep?

Three days in linen at room temperature, five if you toast it. Sliced and frozen it is good for a month; thaw it in the paper, not the microwave. The rye loaf keeps a day longer than the egg loaves because it has less sugar and more rye.

Can I get it warm?

The first pickups at nine get loaves that came out at 07:40. By eleven they are at room temperature, which is how bread should be sliced. If you want it warm, ask for a nine o'clock slot and bring a cloth bag.

Do you bake gluten-free?

No. A gluten-free challah needs a different kitchen and a different skill set, and I would rather send you to a baker who does it well than sell you a loaf I am not proud of. The bakery on Saint-Laurent that does the buckwheat one is worth the trip.

What if I need forty loaves?

For a wedding or a shul lunch, write to me four weeks ahead. Forty loaves is three bakes, which means a second mixer and a borrowed oven, and I will only say yes if the week is clear. The bench on the home page will do the arithmetic for you.
