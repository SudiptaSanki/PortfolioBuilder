Asked every season

How far ahead should I book?

For a Saturday between November and February, three to five months. For a weekday or a morning haldi, six weeks is usually enough.

Can you henna a bride who is pregnant?

Yes, with a shorter sitting and no essential oils in the paste. I bring a stool and we work in two halves with a break.

Do you travel outside Rajasthan?

Delhi and Ahmedabad occasionally, when the dates sit together. Anything further needs a conversation about flights and two nights.

What if it rains on a courtyard ceremony?

I carry a backup layout for indoors and will move the seating with your family. Marigold strings survive rain; the floor rangoli does not.

How dark will it come up?

Depends on skin, the season and how long the paste stays on. Palms and soles go darkest. I will not promise a colour, only the mix that gives it the best chance.
