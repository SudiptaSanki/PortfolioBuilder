The mehndi book

Four ceremonies, and the one thing each asked for.

A mehndi is never only a pattern. It is a queue, a temperature, a set of relatives and a bride who has not eaten. These are the four I use to explain what the work is like.

2025AmerBridal

The bride who wanted her grandmother's pattern

Her grandmother had a photograph from 1971 with a paisley palm and a band at the wrist. I redrew it at a smaller scale so it would survive four hours on a hand that had to keep working.
