On the tray

Four ways the pot reaches a room

Every tray comes with the samovar, six spices ground that morning, Pampore saffron, cups, and a cloth for the table. The only thing I ask for is a level surface and ninety minutes.

The year

Winter is the season. The rest is practice.

From October the chinar turns and the first trays go out. By November I am lighting the samovar at seven in the morning and it stays warm until the last guest leaves. December and January are the months people book a table because the house is cold and the pot is the warmest thing in it.

In summer I take fewer tables than anyone would like. The saffron comes in at Pampore in late October, the almonds are shelled in Kupwara in September, and both of those trips are the work behind the cup.
