What happens next

  • A reply within two days with a quote, a rider and the nearest open date.
  • A hold on the date for seven days while you confirm with your krewe or venue.
  • A route walk-through four weeks out, and a section rehearsal the week before.
  • On the day, the line arrives ninety minutes before call time to warm up and check the pitch.
