Repertoire and recordings

Six pieces, four maqams, one room that answers back.

The programme below is what I am playing this season. A concert usually takes four of these and one long taqsim, chosen for the room. Shorter sets drop the suite and keep the songs.

01

Taqsim Rast

The opening piece. It begins on the open C and stays low for two minutes before climbing to the third, so that the half-flat sits in the ear before anything else arrives. The fourth degree is held long enough to hear the stone room answer, which is why I record it in Old Cairo whenever I can.

Rast7:42 · solo2025 · Old CairoLive, one pass

02

Longa Nahawand

A short, bright piece for the end of a set, played at 96 to the quarter. The longa form came to Cairo through the Ottoman courts and never lost its habit of finishing on a joke: the last phrase repeats lower each time until the oud runs out of fingerboard.

Nahawand3:58 · oud, riq, qanun2025 · ZamalekStudio

03

Sama'i Bayati

A ten-beat instrumental in 10/8 with a cello holding the drone and answering the oud's second phrase. The sama'i is a composed form, not an improvisation, which makes it the most honest test of whether a room is listening: there is no moment where the player can hide behind a long note.

Bayati6:10 · oud, cello2024 · AlexandriaConcert

04

Lamma Bada Yatathanna

The Andalusian muwashshah, in a slow 10/8, with the voice carrying the melody and the oud playing only the counterline. I learned it from my father, who learned it from a radio, which is how most of Cairo learned it. We play it in Hijaz and do not hurry the third line.

Hijaz5:20 · voice, oud2023 · CairoWith Nour Saleh

05

Hijaz Kar suite

Three movements built around a modulation that I stole from a Turkish peşrev and have never regretted. The suite is the longest thing in the book and the only piece that needs a rehearsal with percussion. It ends without a cadence, on the fifth, which confuses hosts and pleases audiences.

Hijaz Kar12:30 · oud, frame drum2024 · CairoSuite, 3 movements

06

Saba in slow time

A film cue written for a scene with no dialogue: a woman waits in a car outside a hospital. The director asked for something that would not tell the audience how to feel. I played it once, at half the tempo we had discussed, and that take is the one in the film.

Saba4:05 · solo, to picture2025 · GizaFilm cue

Workshops

Three formats · Cairo and Alexandria

  • Maqam from the hand

    Two and a half hours for up to twelve players of any instrument. We take one maqam apart from its ajnas outward: where the hand sits on the neck, which string carries the drone, and how a modulation is prepared rather than announced. Handouts in Arabic and English, and a drone recording to take home.

    2.5 h · 12 players 9,500 EGP

  • The taqsim lab

    Four online sessions over four weeks, for players who already know one maqam and want to be able to leave it. Each week a recorded phrase is learned by ear, then extended in the session. Maximum six players, because six is the most I can hear at once.

    4 sessions · 6 players 4,200 EGP

  • Teachers' afternoon

    For music teachers who have oud students and no oud themselves. We cover stringing and action, the first six months of right-hand work, and how to hear a quarter tone well enough to correct it. Twice a season, in the Zamalek studio, free for anyone teaching in a public school.

    3 h · 8 places Free · booking needed

On the day

Technical rider · one page

Sound

One DI for the oud, or a single condenser at 40 cm if the room is quiet. No monitor wedge in front of the instrument. I tune to a drone, so a quiet two minutes before doors matters more than a soundcheck.

Stage

An armless chair at the right height, one small table for water and a cloth, and two metres of clear space behind the chair. Warm light from above, not from the front, so the fingerboard is legible from the fifth row.

Travel

The Damascene oud flies in a hard case with the strings slackened, and I prefer to carry it into the cabin. Within Egypt I travel by car with the instrument beside me. Fees inside Cairo include local transport; outside Cairo, travel is added at cost.

A one-page PDF rider, with stage plot, goes out with the booking confirmation. Ask for it even if you have played host to me before; it is updated each season.
