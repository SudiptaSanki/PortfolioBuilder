The course pad

Six courses in Arabic tuning, open string to the sixth fret, with the quarter-tone position at three and a half. Choose a maqam, tap a position, and the page will pluck it. Nothing loads and nothing sounds until you touch the pad: the tone is synthesised here.

How I play

  • Solo taqsim evening

    An hour of unmeasured improvisation that settles into song: Rast, Bayati and Saba, with two muwashshahat and a longa to close. I bring the oud, a small amplifier for halls over 150 seats, and a tuning check two hours before doors.

    70 min plus encore 18,000–24,000 EGP

  • Maqam workshop

    Two and a half hours for up to twelve players, any instrument. We work one maqam from its ajnas outward: where the hand sits, which string carries the drone, and how a modulation is prepared. Handouts in Arabic and English.

    2.5 h · 12 players 9,500 EGP

  • Studio session

    Half a day in a Cairo room, or remote over a guide track. I record to picture with a click only when the edit needs it, and I can supply a second pass of taqsim for the same cue at no extra fee.

    4 h · stems included 6,000 EGP
