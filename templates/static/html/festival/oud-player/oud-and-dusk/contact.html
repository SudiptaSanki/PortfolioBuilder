What the room needs

One armless chair, two metres of clear space behind it, and a warm light from above. A DI for the oud if the room seats more than 150. I bring the instrument, a spare set of strings, and a tuner that I trust more than my ear at nine in the evening.

Teaching

Lessons are Tuesdays and Saturdays in Zamalek, 50 minutes, 700 EGP. I take adults from the first lesson and children from nine. Write with your experience and the maqam you are working on, and I will tell you honestly whether I am the right teacher.

Elsewhere

The recordings go out by link on request. I do not keep a mailing list and I do not post on social media; the season on the home page is the only announcement I make.
