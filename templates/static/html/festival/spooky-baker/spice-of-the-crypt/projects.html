Counter / Cabinet

Six things in the cabinet, and what each one cost.

The cabinet is where the work that is not the daily case lives: menus, commissions, the zine, and the pop-up we do once a year in the cold room.

I

The winter menu

MenuNovember 2026 – February 20279 items

Nine things, four of them dark. Black cocoa brownies, pumpkin rye on Saturdays, cardamom buns on Sundays, bone shortbread year round, and a burnt-sugar cake that exists because my grandmother made one and I finally worked out how. The menu is written on a board behind the counter in chalk, which means the last item is usually crossed out by noon.

Nine items

II

A cake for a funeral

CommissionSeptember 2026120 people

A burnt-sugar cake for the wake of a woman who had taught at the same school for thirty-one years. The family asked for “the one with the dark top” and gave me a photograph of a cake from 1987. We worked out the caramel from the photograph and the flavour from the daughter’s memory; the cake went out in three trays with no decoration except a thin dusting of sugar. It is the commission I am proudest of, and there is no photograph of it on this page.

120 people

III

The midnight market

Market stallFirst Friday, monthlyOakland

A stall at the night market on Telegraph, open from eight until the bread runs out, usually around eleven. We take the bone shortbread and whatever is left from the day, and we sell it at half price because a loaf tomorrow is a loaf wasted. It is the only place to buy the day-old rye, and the only place where I will talk to you about flour for twenty minutes while the queue waits.

Monthly

IV

Wholesale for two cafés

WholesaleSince 20212 accounts

Two cafés in Temescal take bread and shortbread twice a week, on Tuesday and Friday mornings. The arrangement is deliberately small: I will not take a third account unless I can bake it myself, and I cannot bake more than this without a second oven I do not have. The cafés know the delivery is late when the bread is late, and they know why.

Two accounts

V

The recipe zine

PrintNo. 1, 202532 pages

A small, badly bound zine with six recipes from the book, written in grams and percentages, with the failures included in the margins. The first printing was 200 and sold out at the counter in three weeks, mostly to people who wanted the brownie recipe and were too polite to ask. No. 2, on smoke, is at the printer now.

32 pages

VI

Crypt Night

Pop-up31 October 2026Cold room

One night a year the cold room becomes a dining room: four long tables, one menu, no choices, and a waitlist that closes in nine minutes. The menu is always five courses and always includes the bone shortbread. We do not decorate. The room is cold, the plates are warm, and the last course is served at midnight with the lights off.

One night
