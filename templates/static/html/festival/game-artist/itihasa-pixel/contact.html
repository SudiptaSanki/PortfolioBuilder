WHAT I NEED TO QUOTE

  • Screen resolution and tile size. If they are not decided, that is a conversation we can have.
  • The engine, and whether the art pipeline is set up. Godot and Unity are both fine; I do not do 3D.
  • Colour constraint: fixed palette, or free? A fixed palette is cheaper and usually better.
  • How the game reads the art: autotiles, atlas, or individual sprites.

QUESTIONS

Yes, at half rate, one project a year. Send the build, not the pitch deck.

Will you sign an NDA?

After the first call, yes. Not before; I need to know what the game is before I promise not to talk about it.

Can you do a style test?

One paid test sprite, ₹4,000, credited against the job if we go ahead. Unpaid tests are not something I do.

What about the epics?

I will not draw a commissioned game that uses a living religion as a monster set. Everything else, let us talk.
