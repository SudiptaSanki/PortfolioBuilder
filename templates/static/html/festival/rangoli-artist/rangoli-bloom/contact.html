Thank you — I have it

I will reply within two days with either a date, a firm no, or one question. If you have a photograph of the floor, send it in the reply; a photo of the drain and the door swing is worth ten lines of description.

Next: a site visit, at the hour the piece will be seen. I bring a tape and a marble for the slope.

What the season looks like

Rangoli is seasonal work and the season is short. Diwali week is booked by August, and the wedding months by September. If you are reading this in October, a threshold piece is still possible; a fourteen-foot atrium is not.

What it costs

A three-foot threshold is ₹4,500. A six-foot wedding piece is ₹18,000. A ten-foot mandap is ₹42,000. A fourteen-foot atrium, with two assistants and a night's work, is ₹1.4 lakh. Pigment, travel and sweeping up are included; nothing is charged per hour.

The studio is on the first floor with a narrow stair and no lift. If you need me to come to you — for a workshop or a large floor — say so and we will arrange it.
