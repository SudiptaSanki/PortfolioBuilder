Floors · 2019–2025

Nine floors,
none of them still thereEleven pieces from the last six years, with the floor they sat on and the thing that nearly went wrong.

Nothing here exists any more. Three were swept up the same evening, one was rained on, and one lasted four days because nobody would walk on the lobby floor.

2025Wedding

Ashta-dala lotus

A wedding threshold in Kothrud. The family's grandmother asked for eight petals, not twelve, "because eight is a household and twelve is a temple". Drew it in four hours forty, covered it with a bedsheet until the muhurta.

6 ft8-foldrice flour + gulal

2024Public floor

Fourteen feet of lobby

Three assistants, a string grid, nine hours from 11 pm. The marble was polished the week before, so the powder slid: we laid a thin rice-flour key line first and it held. The hotel kept it for four days.

14 ft12-foldovernight

2022Threshold

Ten doors, one lane

Diwali morning, 5 am. Ten doorways on the same lane, each drawn to the width of its own threshold — the smallest a foot and a half, the widest three feet. Finished by nine, before the first scooter.

10 floors1 ½ – 3 ftone morning

2023Public floor

The hospital lobby

A cancer day-care asked for something at the entrance that patients would walk past, not around. No strong gulal — the smell bothers people on treatment — so the palette was rice flour, turmeric and indigo only.

8 ft6-foldno gulal

2024Wedding

Peacock mandap

Ten feet under the seat, drawn the night before and covered. The peacock fan was the groom's mother's request; the eight eyes had to line up with the eight posts of the mandap, which took an hour of measuring before a single line.

10 ft8-foldpeacock fan

2019Teaching

Eight hundred square feet

One square foot per child, one colour per row, no pattern to copy. What appeared was not a rangoli; it was a map of eight hundred decisions. I have never been asked to draw over it, which is the correct outcome.

800 children1 colour each3 hours

2021Threshold

The rainy-day kolam

Four feet, drawn at 5:30 am while it was still drizzling, which is a stupid hour to draw in the rain and the only hour my grandmother ever used. The kolam held for two hours and then the street took it.

4 ft4-fold2 hours

2020Public floor

The temple side entrance

A five-foot piece at a side entrance, drawn off-centre on purpose so the natural line of feet passes it on the left. The priest noticed the offset before he noticed the pattern, which is the highest compliment the piece has had.

5 ft6-foldoff-centre

2023Teaching

The rice-flour workshop

Twelve people, four hours, one ring each. Nobody touches a colour tin until their ring closes. Four of the twelve got a true circle; the other eight learned why a line of powder drifts, which is the actual lesson.

12 people4 hours1 ring each
