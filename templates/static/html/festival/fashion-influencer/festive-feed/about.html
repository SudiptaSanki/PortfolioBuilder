A wardrobe, not a set

My mother kept her sarees in a steel trunk with a lock, and the first thing I did when I moved to Hyderabad was buy my own trunk for four cotton sarees. Festive wear was a different country: heavy, expensive, and mostly photographed on women who had been lent the clothes for an afternoon.

The first paid styling job was a Diwali shoot for a jewellery shop in 2017, ₹4,000 for a day and a set of photographs I still do not like. The client wanted the necklace to be the hero and the model to be a mannequin; I argued for a woman who looked like she had put the necklace on herself, and won one of the fourteen frames.

“The outfit is not the story. The story is how she got ready, what she borrowed, and what she decided not to wear.”

How the studio works

Every client sends three numbers: the budget, the date, and the number of events in one week. From those I build a look, usually from shops in Hyderabad with honest return policies, and I do the first fitting in the client's own home, because a lehenga behaves differently in a bedroom than in a studio.

I photograph on a phone for most of the day and on a borrowed camera for the final set. The lighting is a window and a bedsheet; the columns between the photos are my mother's and my aunt's opinions, which are more useful than any mood board.

What I will not do

  • 01
    Say a price I have not checked. If a shop raises it between the reel and the shoot, I publish the correction.
  • 02
    Borrow an heirloom and call it mine. Anything borrowed is credited in the caption, including the necklace and the grandmother.
  • 03
    Edit a body into a clothes hanger. The points of the fitting are the fit, the hem and the pinning, not the waist.
  • 04
    Take a brand brief that needs a lie. If the fabric pills or the colour transfers, the campaign does not happen.

Nine seasons, briefly

  1. 2017

    First shoot

    A Diwali campaign for a jewellery shop in Secunderabad, ₹4,000, one usable frame and a long argument about necklaces.

  2. 2019

    The price posts

    Started writing the cost of every piece in the caption. Two shops stopped sending samples; four started.

  3. 2022

    Fittings, not shoots

    Added a paid fitting service for women who had bought something and could not make it sit right. It is now half the work.

  4. 2026

    214 fittings

    Nine seasons, a studio in Banjara Hills and a waiting list for October that starts in July.
