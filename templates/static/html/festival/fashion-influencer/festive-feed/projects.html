Campaigns · 2023–2026

Six briefs,
six budgets.

Brand work pays for the fittings, so I choose it carefully. Every campaign below names what was delivered and what it cost to make, because a campaign that only works with a borrowed set is not a campaign; it is a photograph.

Hyderabad & Bengaluru Jewellery, handloom, footwear 3–5 deliverables per brief
01

Jewellery · Diwali 2026

Kundan, on a real neck

A Hyderabad jeweller wanted a Diwali set that did not look like a catalogue. We shot on the owner's daughter, in her own saree, in the shop after closing. The brief asked for the necklace in every frame; the compromise was three full looks and four close crops, one of which they used for the print window.

Deliverables
7 stills, 2 reels
Team
3 people
Days
2
02

Handloom · Pongal 2025

Nine yards, one weaver

A weaver's cooperative in Pochampally asked for a campaign that showed the loom, not just the saree. We shot in the workshop at six in the morning, with the warp threads still on the frame, and paid the weaver to stop work for three hours. The cooperative sold fourteen sarees in the first week, which is a good week.

Deliverables
9 stills, 1 film
Team
4 people
Days
3
03

Footwear · Wedding season 2025

The shoe that survives a sangeet

A Jaipur footwear label wanted proof that their juttis could be danced in. We filmed a real sangeet rehearsal in Hyderabad: four women, one choreographer, three hours, no shoes changed. Two pairs came back scuffed, which the brand used in the caption and sold out the style.

Deliverables
6 stills, 3 reels
Team
3 people
Days
1
04

Wedding editorial · 2024

The heirloom issue

A magazine asked for a bridal editorial with clothes from three families rather than three shops. We photographed the real owners in their own lehengas, with their own jewellery, and wrote the provenance of each piece. The cover was a 1970s Kanjivaram, reworn with a new blouse, and the caption said so.

Deliverables
12 pages
Team
6 people
Days
4
05

Home & food · Diwali 2023

Five homes, five budgets

A five-part Diwali series for a homeware brand: the same puja table styled in a one-bedroom flat, a joint family house, a chef's kitchen, a hostel room and a terrace. Each episode had a budget under ₹5,000, and the brand's own products had to earn their place in the frame.

Deliverables
5 films
Team
3 people
Days
5
06

Retail · 2026

The honest rack

A multi-brand store in Jubilee Hills let me shoot their festive rack with the price tags visible and a caption for every piece on why it is priced where it is. Three labels refused; eleven agreed. The store said the tags sold more than the styling did, which I choose to believe.

Deliverables
14 stills, 1 reel
Team
2 people
Days
1

Tell me what the clothes have to do.

Dance, sit through a puja, survive a car journey, photograph under yellow hotel light. The brief is easier when it starts with the evening rather than the mood board.
