Before you send

The things that decide a yes or a no.

Do you accept gifted product instead of a fee?

No for campaigns. A shoot costs a photographer, a second pair of hands and a day of my time; a gifted saree does not cover any of that. I do accept product for honest review posts, clearly marked, but those are not campaigns and I keep the right to say the fabric pills.

Can you guarantee a certain reach?

No, and I will not pretend to. I can tell you what the last three campaigns did and how the audience responded, and I can make the work good. Anyone who guarantees a number is selling you a number, not a campaign.

Who styles the models?

I do, with one assistant on set. If the brand supplies a stylist, I am happy to direct instead of style, but that changes the fee. What I will not do is let a fit go out unpinned because there was no one to pin it.

Are fittings only in Hyderabad?

In person, yes. I do video fittings for clients elsewhere: you send measurements and photographs in good light, we do a forty-minute call, and I send back the alterations written down. It costs ₹2,500 and saves a lot of tailoring mistakes.

Do you work with plus-size and older clients?

Constantly, and it is the part of the work I care most about. The studio keeps a tailor who can recut a blouse properly, and I will say when a shop's cut is the problem rather than the body. If a brand will only lend sample sizes, I will tell them the campaign is not for me.

What does a brief need?

The occasion, the date, where the images will be used, who will be in them, the deliverables, the budget and the usage period. Six lines is enough. A mood board is welcome but not a substitute for the budget.

One honest note beats ten mood boards.

If the date is inside three weeks, say so in the first line. If the budget is fixed, put the number in. Both save us a week.
