## Sound ruling
**NONE.** A photographer's site is silent; no audio is used anywhere, the
script contains no audio code and never creates an AudioContext, and the
ruling is printed in every footer. The stub's ambient festival audio ideas
fail the program's sound need-test for a still-photography practice.

## Frame & CSS spec (Frame Diversity + CSS-Layer directives)
- **Frame F7 — spec-as-hero:** one full-bleed fireworks frame; the exposure
  settings that made it (f/11 · 4.0 s · ISO 100) are laid over it at poster
  scale as the headline; the proposition sits small beneath. Inside, sections
  open with their technical plate (Plate 01–05, F1–F3, A1–A4, C1) before
  their prose — the settings-table logic of the firing schedule / shot log.
- **CSS spec:** narrow paperwork container (960px) under the full-bleed
  heroes; settings **tables** instead of cards (no card grids in the
  paperwork zone); **no scroll-reveal, no hover-lift**; the one restrained
  effect is the cross-document view transition (with the program's
  InvalidStateError abort guard); sharp corners throughout; type pairing
  Archivo (display + numerals) / Work Sans (text) — IBM Plex Mono not used;
  tabular figures from the faces' own lining figures (`tnum`).
- **Dry-brush edge:** deliberately NOT used — this template's honesty
  language is the printed schedule and the ruled table; a torn edge would be
  decoration against the concept. (The pattern remains available to the
  set's maker/festival templates whose briefs call for it.)

## Tools
The Exposure & Show Planner parses Tables T1 (show structure), S1 (settings
by burst class) and T2 (frame cadence) from the DOM; the Booking Estimator
parses Table F1 (fees) from the DOM. Tool and page cannot disagree.
Hand-check: full show = 120/10 + 300/12 + 210/15 + 270/10 + 120/20
= 12 + 25 + 14 + 27 + 6 = **84 frames**. Estimator default =
€1,150 + €190×2 + €260 = **€1,790**.
