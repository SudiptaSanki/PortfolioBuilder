Before the sitting

I cast the chart by hand the day before and look at it without you in the room, so that the first thing I say to your face is not a calculation. If the chart does not describe you, say so early. That is useful information, not rudeness.

Questions

I don't know my birth time. Can you still read?

Partly. Without a time there is no lagna and no house structure, so the reading is limited to the Moon and the dashas. I will tell you what is missing rather than guess.

Do you record the sitting?

Only with your permission. I keep handwritten notes; you get a one-page summary. If you ask me to delete a recording, I do, that day.

Can you read my partner's chart without them?

No. I will read your chart and talk about your side of the question. A chart belongs to the person it describes.

Do you wear a rudraksha or recommend one?

I wear what my mother gave me. I do not sell anything and I do not prescribe stones. If a remedy costs more than a book, it is not the remedy I practise.
