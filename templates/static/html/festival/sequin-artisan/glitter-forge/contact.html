Tell me what the cloth has to do

Write with the occasion, the date and roughly how the piece will be seen. I answer every enquiry myself, usually within three days, and I will say no quickly if the date cannot hold — that is kinder than a promise.

No deposit is taken here. If I can take the work, the next step is a cloth sample and a written date.

Received, ji.

Your enquiry is on the bench. I will read it myself and reply within three working days — sooner if the date is close. If you have photographs of the cloth or of an old piece to match, send them in the reply and I will look at those first.

What to send with an enquiry

Daylight photographs of the cloth, taken flat. A reference image if you have one, and a note of which part of it you actually want. The date the piece must be finished — not the date of the event, because cloth needs a week at the end for finishing and packing.
