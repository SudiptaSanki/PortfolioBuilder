Madhubani · Mithila · Bihar

A wedding written on a wall, in lampblack and turmeric.

I am Sunaina Jha, and I paint in Madhubani, the town in Mithila that gives this painting its name. My year turns on two seasons: the wedding months, when a bride's room waits for its kohbar, and Madhushravani, the fifteen rainy days when the snake goddess is told her stories and I paint them panel by panel. Everything here is drawn freehand with a bamboo pen — no pencil first, no print after.

What the motifs mean

A Madhubani painting is read, the way a letter is read. These are the words I use most, in plain language.

The fish — machhli

Fish mean a house full of life: children, grain, good luck that keeps moving. A pair of fish on a wedding wall wishes the couple a life that never runs dry.

The peacock — mor

The peacock stands for beauty that also guards. His feather goes near doors and cradles, where a house wants both pride and protection at once.

The lotus — kamal

The lotus is the heart of the kohbar. It grows out of pond mud and opens clean, so it carries the wish that love stays pure inside an ordinary, muddy life.

The sun and moon — suraj, chanda

Painted in the top corners of a wedding wall, the sun and moon are witnesses. They promise the marriage will be seen, remembered, and kept in the open.

Ordering a painting

1. You write to me with the occasion — a wedding, a house-warming, a festival — and the date it must be ready.

2. We agree the subject on a short call or by letter: which motifs belong to your family's story, and which wall or paper they will live on.

3. I write the price and the start date on an order card before any money moves. The advance is one third; the rest is paid when you see the finished piece.

4. The painting is made in the open. I send a photograph at the outline stage and one at the first colour, so there are no surprises at the end.

Prices follow one printed rate card — by size, style and surface — with no bargaining built into them. The full card and a planner that works the figures out for you are on the Work & Commissions page. Wedding walls for this season are booked from March; panels on paper can usually start within a fortnight.
