Contact

Write to me the way you would write to a neighbour — plainly, with the date in it.

One form, one email address, and no other door to knock on. Tell me the occasion and the date it must be ready, and I will answer with a start date and a price from the printed card — or with the honest news that the date cannot be met, and the ready piece that can.
