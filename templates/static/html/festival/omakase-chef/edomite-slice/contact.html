The price of the menu is set by the day's buying and confirmed when we telephone. It has never been above ¥30,000.

Two things asked of every guest: no strong perfume, and arrive on time. A scent in a ten-seat room flattens the nose for everyone, and a late seat makes the rice wait.

If you are late by more than fifteen minutes, telephone. We will hold the seat, but the first courses may be served together.
