LOT032VISITS

The season

The still room runs from March to July, starting before dawn and finishing when the last charge is done. Visitors are welcome in the morning, but the morning is also when everyone is working.

Rose season
March to April, best in the first three weeks
Jasmine
June to July, picked at night
Visiting hours
6:00 to 9:00, during the season only
Samples
One 1 ml sample per flower, posted within two weeks
Stock
Minimum six bottles per lot for shops
Languages
Hindi, English, and enough Urdu for the ledger
If the lot is sold out: it is gone until the next season. The still room does not blend a new lot to imitate an old one, which is disappointing in November and the reason the bottles are worth keeping.

Before you write · 11

Three things to tell us

01

The flower

Rose, jasmine, kewra, mitti or the house blend. If you do not know, describe the smell you remember.

02

The season

Which lot you want, if you know it. A lot is a harvest; it cannot be repeated exactly, only approached.

03

The skin

Attar sits close and warms slowly. Tell us where it will be worn and whether you want it to announce itself or stay near.
