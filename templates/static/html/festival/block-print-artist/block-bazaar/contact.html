What we will not do

Polyester, anything wider than 110 cm, reactive dyes, or a copy of another workshop's block. If a brief needs a colour that only a screen printer can reach, we will say so at the quoting stage rather than after the cloth is cut.

Monsoon adds a week, sometimes two. Indigo does not dry in the rain, and no amount of deadline changes that.

One thing to know about colour

Natural dye shifts. Two batches of madder, boiled on different days, will not match exactly, and the same vat gives a deeper blue in December than in May. We keep a cutting from every batch so a reorder can be matched to the nearest shade, but an exact match across seasons is not something an honest printer promises.

If that is a problem for your product, say so now. It is usually better to print everything in one run, even if it means storing the cloth, than to reorder in six months and explain the difference to a customer.
