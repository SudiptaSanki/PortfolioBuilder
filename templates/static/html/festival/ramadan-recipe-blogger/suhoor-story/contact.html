Ask me about the dough. I am less good at email.

Recipe questions get an answer, usually within a few days. I read everything, including the corrections, and I have changed three recipes because a reader in another city was right and I was not.

What I answer

Recipe trouble, substitutions, timings, and anything about feeding people who are difficult. I do not do sponsored posts, I do not review products, and I am not able to answer medical or dietary questions beyond what is written on the recipe.

The Friday letter

One email a week during Ramadan: what I cooked, what failed, and the shopping list. It stops the day after Eid and starts again the following year. About 4,100 people get it.

Using the recipes

Cook them, print them, put them in your own notebook, teach them to your daughter. Credit the site if you publish, and please do not paste the whole method into a group chat without the weights — the weights are the recipe.
