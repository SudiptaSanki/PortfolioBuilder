Programmes, with the schedule written out

A course is a defined block of days with fixed review points, not an open-ended series of massages. Below is what each one contains, what it asks of your week, and who should not begin it.

Reading these Durations are therapy days. Arrival, rest and the final review sit outside them. Fees are quoted after the assessment, never before it.

Six courses currently running

Panchakarma is the first two; the rest are kayachikitsa programmes that run alongside your physician's care.

Karkidaka Panchakarma

21 therapy days
Season Varsha, mid-July to mid-August Rhythm Daily, 6.30–12.30 Room Two patients a day

The full course, timed to the monsoon because that is when the body is soft and the season holds you indoors anyway. Five days of oleation and steam, one day of therapeutic purgation, then alternate oil and decoction enemas for a fortnight, with a strict diet of karkidaka kanji and no travel.

What the three weeks contain

Preparation

  • Snehapana with medicated ghee, graded over 5 days
  • Abhyanga and swedana each morning
  • Diet sheet from day one, cooked in the sala kitchen

Main therapy

  • Virechana on day 6, supervised through the afternoon
  • Anuvasana and niruha basti on alternate days, days 7–20
  • Rest for two hours after each session, phone off

Review points

  • Day 5: readiness for purgation
  • Day 10: tolerance and sleep
  • Day 21: discharge plan and six-week review date

Not suitable for

  • Pregnancy or the first six weeks after birth
  • Active infection or fever
  • Recent heart attack, stroke or surgery in the last three months
  • Uncontrolled blood pressure without your physician's clearance

Snehana–Swedana short course

7 therapy days
Season Any, except peak heat Rhythm Daily, 7–10 Room Three patients a day

For patients who cannot give three weeks, and for those preparing for a longer course later in the year. Oleation and steam only: no purgation, no enemas. It loosens stiff joints and settles a dry, restless body, and it is honest about being a beginning rather than a cure.

What the week contains

Therapy

  • Abhyanga with sesame or ksheerabala oil, 45 minutes
  • Bashpa sweda in the wooden steam box, 12 minutes
  • Kativasti or janu vasti where the joint asks for it

Review points

  • Day 3: oil selection and heat tolerance
  • Day 7: what continues at home, and what does not

Not suitable for

  • Fever, active skin infection, open wounds
  • Pregnancy
  • Bleeding disorders or blood thinners without clearance

Fee

Quoted after assessment; therapy, oil and diet included. Accommodation is not.

Kitibha care — scaly, itching skin

16 weeks
Format Weekly therapy, daily medicine Alongside Your dermatologist Review Weeks 4, 8, 16

Supportive management of chronic scaly skin conditions, run with your dermatologist rather than in place of them. Takradhara once a week, medicated ghee internally, and a diet that removes the four things most patients already suspect: curd at night, too much salt, fried food, and a late supper.

What the sixteen weeks contain

Weekly

  • Takradhara, 40 minutes, buttermilk medicated with nimbadi
  • Review of itching score and sleep, recorded in your file

Daily

  • Medicated ghee, dose adjusted at each review
  • Ointment only as prescribed by your dermatologist

What we measure

  • Itch score (0–10) at intake, week 8, week 16
  • Area involved, photographed with consent, kept in your file only
  • Sleep and school or work days lost

Not suitable for

  • Acute infected eczema needing antibiotics
  • Pregnancy without obstetric clearance

Prameha lifestyle programme

12 weeks
Format Fortnightly visits, daily routine Alongside Your diabetologist Review Weeks 0, 6, 12

A diet-and-routine programme for people with pre-diabetes or type 2 diabetes whose medication is already managed elsewhere. Udvartana — dry powder massage — twice a week, a measured food plan built from what the household actually cooks, and a walking schedule that starts at the distance you can already do.

What the twelve weeks contain

At the sala

  • Udvartana twice weekly, 30 minutes
  • Food plan revised at each fortnightly visit
  • Weight, waist and blood pressure recorded every visit

At home

  • Barley or millet in place of white rice at one meal
  • Walking 25–40 minutes daily, after the evening meal
  • Sleep before 11, no day sleep

Held by your physician

  • HbA1c at 0, 6 and 12 weeks, ordered by them
  • No change to insulin or tablets from this sala

Not suitable for

  • Type 1 diabetes
  • Active foot ulcer or recent amputation
  • Pregnancy

Artava kshaya — perimenopausal care

10 weeks
Format Weekly therapy, daily medicine Alongside Your gynaecologist Review Weeks 5, 10

For irregular cycles, disturbed sleep and heat through the chest in the years before menopause. Shirodhara once a week, a cooling diet, and a written sleep routine that is less romantic than it sounds: same hour, dark room, no phone after ten.

What the ten weeks contain

Therapy

  • Shirodhara, 35 minutes, once a week
  • Abhyanga with a cooling oil in the fortnight before the expected date

Recorded

  • Cycle dates, hot flushes per day, sleep hours
  • Bone and thyroid reports you already hold

Not suitable for

  • Postmenopausal bleeding not yet investigated
  • Suspected pregnancy

Referral

Any abnormal bleeding is sent for gynaecological assessment before treatment begins.

Post-viral fatigue

8 weeks
Format Fortnightly visits Main therapy Rasayana, graded activity Review Weeks 4, 8

For the flat, unrefreshed months that follow a bad viral illness. The programme is deliberately unambitious: sleep, food, one restorative formulation, and activity that stays below the level that causes a crash the next day. No stimulants, no heroic exercise.

What the eight weeks contain

At the sala

  • Abhyanga once a fortnight, light pressure
  • Rasayana reviewed at each visit

At home

  • Activity log, with a hard ceiling for the day
  • One warm meal more than you think you need
  • Bed by 10.30, no exceptions for two weeks

Stopped if

  • Fever returns, or breathlessness at rest
  • Any chest pain — go to a hospital, not here

Not suitable for

  • Unexplained weight loss not yet investigated
  • Active infection

Programme records are anonymised and published with written consent. What is listed above are the review points we hold ourselves to, not promised results; two patients with the same diagnosis rarely travel at the same speed.

Therapy room Two wooden tables, one steam box, a copper vessel for the oils. The room is emptied and washed between patients; the schedule leaves time for it.

A day in the therapy room

During a panchakarma course the timetable does not move. Patients are woken, fed, treated and rested in the same order every day, because the body reads the repetition as safety.

  • 6.00
    Oils warmed, tables prepared
    Sesame base for vata, ksheerabala for joints, coconut for pitta.
  • 6.45
    First abhyanga
    45 minutes, two hands, in the direction of the hair. No phone in the room.
  • 8.00
    Steam, then rest
    Twelve minutes in the box, then twenty minutes flat on the cot.
  • 9.30
    Kanji, and the second round
    Karkidaka kanji with a spoon of ghee. Therapy resumes at ten.
