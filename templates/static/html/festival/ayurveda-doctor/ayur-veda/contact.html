Book a first consultation

Send the form or call the desk between eight and twelve. A first consultation is seventy-five minutes and is held only by Dr. Anjali, so the wait for a new patient is usually eight to twelve days.

Or call +91 487 244 0198. The desk answers, not a machine.

After you send

  1. The desk calls the number you gave, usually the same afternoon.
  2. You are offered two times for the coming fortnight and told what to bring.
  3. The file is opened before you arrive, with the notes you sent already in it.

If you do not hear within two working days, call the desk. Mail sometimes sits in spam, and a missed call is not a judgement.

This is not an emergency service. For chest pain, breathlessness, bleeding, a fever above 103°F or a suspected stroke, go to Jubilee Mission or Aswini Hospital, or call 108.
Before you come Bring what you already have. We do not repeat tests that were done last month unless the reading depends on it.

What to bring, and what to leave in the car

A list of every medicine you take, including anything for blood pressure, thyroid, diabetes or sleep, with the dose. Recent blood work, scan reports and discharge summaries, even if they are years old — the timeline matters more than the latest number. For women, the dates of the last three cycles.

Eat something light an hour before. Coffee, a heavy breakfast and a long argument all change the pulse, and the pulse is the first thing read. Come with someone if you would like a second pair of ears; there is space for one person in the room.

Leave the children with someone if you can. A first consultation is a long conversation and the room is small.
