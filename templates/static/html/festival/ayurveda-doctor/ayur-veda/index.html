      How a first visit runs

      Nothing is prescribed on the first day except food and hours. Treatment begins once the reading is clear and you have agreed to it.

      1. Nadi pariksha

        Pulse is read at both wrists, under three fingers, before you say a word about why you came. It is the part of the examination that decides the rest.

        10 minutes

      2. History

        Sleep, bowels, appetite, work hours, menstrual history, what you eat in a week and when. Bring recent blood work and any scan reports you already have.

        25 minutes

      3. Examination

        Tongue, eyes, skin, nails, abdomen. Blood pressure and weight are recorded at every visit and kept in your file.

        15 minutes

      4. The written plan

        Food, timing, one or two formulations, and the season's adjustment. You leave with the sheet in hand and a review date.

        20 minutes

      5. Pharmacy

        Decoctions are prepared in the room behind the courtyard. A three-day supply is dispensed at the desk, with the method written on the bottle.

        10 minutes
