Before you write

The questions people ask

Can I get a piece before Hanukkah?

If you write before 6 November, yes. Between then and the first night on 4 December I can usually manage a mezuzah or a small repair, because those take days rather than weeks. A menorah ordered in late November cannot be raised honestly in time, and I will not pretend otherwise to take the order.

Do you make kiddush cups for a wedding?

Often, and they are my favourite commissions because they get used every week afterwards. A pair takes about four weeks. Engraving the Hebrew date of the wedding is included; a full verse costs extra, because setting Hebrew type by hand is slow.

Is the silver really recycled?

About four fifths of it, from a refiner in Gliwice. It is assayed to 925 and stamped with the Polish standard mark, exactly like new metal. If you want virgin silver, say so at the start; it costs about fifteen per cent more and I will not argue with you about it.

Can you repair a piece that is not yours?

Yes, if it is solid silver. Send photographs of the whole object and of the damage, plus the base or the inside of the foot so I can see any marks. Silver plate is a different matter — I will tell you what can and cannot be done, and often the honest answer is to leave it alone.

How do I look after it?

Use it. Silver that is used needs less cleaning than silver that is stored. Wash in warm water with a little mild soap, dry with a soft cloth, and keep it away from rubber bands and felt, which contain sulphur and will turn it black. Do not use a silver dip on a piece with a matte finish; it will leave streaks in the brush marks.

Can I visit the workshop?

Yes, Tuesday to Friday, eleven to five, by appointment. The stairs are steep and there is no lift, which I am sorry about. Bring the object you want mended or the drawing you have in mind; we will sit at the bench and talk it through.
