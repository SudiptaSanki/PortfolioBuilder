Walk booking

What is included

  • A licensed guide for the whole walk
  • Chai at the mid-point stop
  • Any boat crossing the route needs
  • A short reading list sent afterwards, if you want one

Payment and cancellation

Cash at the meeting point, or UPI in advance. Cancel up to 24 hours before and there is no charge; after that the fee is half. If it rains hard enough to cancel, I will say so the evening before.

Meeting point

Assi Ghat, at the wooden boat landing, ten minutes before the start. There is a chai stall and a wall to sit on. If you are late, call; the walk will wait ten minutes and no longer.
