What a gongfu table actually is

器

The vessel is small on purpose

A hundred millimetres of water, not a litre. Small volume means the leaf gives up its flavour in seconds rather than minutes, so you can take eight different readings of it before it dies. A big pot gives one reading, and by the time you pour the second cup it has already gone bitter.

水

Water is most of the drink

Tea is 98 per cent water. I use filtered water with a little mineral left in it, because distilled water makes tea taste hollow and hard water makes it taste like a swimming pool. Green tea at 80 °C, oolong at 95, aged puerh at a full boil.

時

Time is counted in seconds

The first infusion is a rinse for anything roasted or aged. Then ten seconds, twelve, fifteen, twenty, and so on, each one poured off completely. The leaf is not steeped; it is visited, briefly, eight times, and the last visit is usually the sweetest.
