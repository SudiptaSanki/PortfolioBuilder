Order

Tell me your night

One form for everything: a few dozen for your table, a tray for a crowd, a lesson, or the full event fry. Or skip the form and write to shani@latkelantern.example — it reaches the same kitchen.

What happens next

  1. 1. You send the form and get a reference number on this page, right away.
  2. 2. I write back within a day — usually faster in November — with your time slot and your total.
  3. 3. Pickup from 4 pm on your night, or delivery within 3 miles. Pay on pickup, card or cash.
  4. 4. No deposit under four dozen. Over that, half holds the night; events are half on booking.

Deadlines, honestly

Nights 1–3 close Sunday, Nov 29 — the first weekend is when everyone's cousins are in town. Nights 4–8 take orders five days ahead. Lessons are Sundays in November and fill by mid-October most years; this year two dates are still open.

Event frying starts at $450 and goes by crowd and distance — tell me the room and the number and I'll give you one figure, not a range, in my reply.

No account, no deposit under four dozen, no mailing list. Your address is used for your order and nothing else.
