Give me the route before the fee.

Charts are booked per project, usually eight months out. Send the date, the route, the band size and the tempo you can hold; I will walk the route before anyone signs anything.

Ask about a date

I answer on Mondays and Thursdays. If the parade is inside four months, say so in the first line; the answer may still be yes, but the calendar decides before the fee does.

  • What to send

    The date, a route map or a link to one, the band size, the number of ranks you can field, and the tempo your drumline can hold on the day.

  • Getting here

    Metro A Line to Memorial Park, three minutes on foot. Street parking on Raymond is metered until 6 p.m.; the lot on Union is free after 5.
What a booking includes A route walk at parade hour, the drill book in 11×17 and PDF, cadence scores, and two site visits. Extra rehearsals are quoted separately.
Cancellations If a parade is cancelled before the route walk, no fee is due. After the chart is delivered, the fee is payable in full; the chart is yours and can be walked another year.
