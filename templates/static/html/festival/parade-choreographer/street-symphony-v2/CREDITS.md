## Sound ruling

**None.** Parade music belongs to the bands; a choreographer of movement has
nothing honest to play you, and a synthesised parade would be exactly the
decoration this rebuild removes. The stub's ambient festival audio ideas were
not carried over. The ruling is printed in every footer.
