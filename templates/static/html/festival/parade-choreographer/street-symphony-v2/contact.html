Book a parade

Enquiry · one path, no maze

One frictionless path: write, and the first reply contains a date for the route walk or an honest reason why the calendar cannot hold your parade.

Email, visible and copyable:

hola@ruizmovimiento.example

A useful first message contains four things: the parade and its city; the date; the route, if one exists, or the streets you hope for; and roughly how many blocks — bands, dance groups, floats — you expect. I will do the arithmetic from there.

Lead time: route walks are booked 10 weeks out. Inside 10 weeks I will still answer, and the answer may be a recommendation of a colleague rather than a booking — a parade designed in a hurry is a bunching waiting for its street.
