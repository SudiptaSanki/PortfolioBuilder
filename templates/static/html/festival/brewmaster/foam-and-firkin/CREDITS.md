# Credits & rulings — foam-and-firkin ("The Copper Firkin")

## Persona & fiction
Sam Whitaker and The Copper Firkin brewpub (Hebden Bridge) are fictional,
created for this template and disclosed as fictional in the footer of
every page. The Calderdale Ale Appreciation Society and its secretary
are likewise fictional. No real pub, brewery, brewer or beer is depicted
or named. The brewing facts are real: a firkin is 9 imperial gallons =
72 pints; a UK barrel is 288 pints; ABV is computed with the standard
approximation (OG − FG) × 131.25; cask ale is live beer, cellared at
11–13 °C, vented, tapped, and sold inside a short window once tapped.

## Sound ruling — FAILS the need-test: no audio anywhere
A brewer-publican's profession is not sound, and no interaction on this
site teaches through audio. The stub's festival decoration is not a
reason to add noise. There is no `<audio>` element, no Web Audio, no
autoplay of anything. The ruling is stated in every footer.

## Stub promises — honest replacements
The stub promised "garland-bunting, float-leaves, scroll-cascade"
decoration and generic celebration copy under a borrowed persona.
Those were decoration without a job, and have been replaced by the
instruments the trade actually runs on: a Brew Sheet Explorer whose
figures are asserted against the printed brew table on the same page,
and a cellar calculator that runs the stillage arithmetic in the open.
The festival itself survives where it belongs — as a worked record
(the October festival, Record 02) with its losses printed. The stub's
vendored GSAP files and decorative SVGs were removed; the build is
framework-free static HTML/CSS/JS, which is all this portfolio needs.
