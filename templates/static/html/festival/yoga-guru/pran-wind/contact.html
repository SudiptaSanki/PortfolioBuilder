I have never done yoga. Which class

Tuesday, 06:30. Come ten minutes early and tell me at the door. You will spend most of the class lying down and that is correct.

Do I need my own mat

No. There are eight mats, four bolsters and a shelf of blankets. Wash your hands before you touch the blankets; that is the only rule I enforce.

Is there a changing room

A curtained corner and a bathroom. Women change first, then men, then everyone pretends it is not an issue. It is not.

Can I come just once

Yes, and I would rather you came once than bought a month and vanished. Drop-in is ₹500. If you return, we will talk about a month.

Do you teach online

Two students at a time, by video, on Thursdays. It is a poor substitute for a room with windows but better than nothing when you are travelling.
