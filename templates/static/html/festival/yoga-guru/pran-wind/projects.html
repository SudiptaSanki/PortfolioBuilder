Programmes · 2026 – 27

Everything is taught in this room, by me.

No franchise, no assistant leading the room while I sit at the front. Two classes a day, four courses a year, one retreat, and one training. If a programme is full, it is full; I do not add mats.

How to choose

If you are new, come to a Tuesday class and nothing else. Do that for a month. If you are returning after years away, come to the Tuesday class and tell me before we start, and I will give you a simpler shape for the standing work.

The trainings are not a path to teaching here. They are for people who want to teach somewhere else, properly, and who can take being told that their instructions are too long.

“You will not be given a sequence to memorise. You will be given a question to ask your own body, and four months to ask it.” Training handbook, page 1
