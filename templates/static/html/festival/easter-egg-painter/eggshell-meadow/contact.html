Answers before you ask

The questions that come up in almost every letter.

How long does one egg take?

Three evenings for a hen or goose egg with a traditional motif. A new design takes longer because the first version is usually wrong. Wedding pairs need three weeks from the first bath, including the ten days of drying.

Can you copy an egg I saw in a museum?

I can write a new egg in the same grammar, but not a copy of a museum piece. Those designs belong to a village and a period, and a copy without that context is decoration. Bring me the photo and we will find the part that is yours.

Do you ship abroad?

Yes, packed in a wooden box with the egg suspended in wool. Shipping to the EU takes six to ten days and costs about €18; to North America, three to four weeks and about $38. I do not ship ostrich shells outside Ukraine.

What if it breaks in the post?

Send a photograph of the box and the shell. I will write another one in the next list at no charge, though the dyes may not match exactly. In eleven years, four eggs have arrived broken.

Do you teach complete beginners?

Yes. The Saturday workshop is built for people who have never held a kistka. Four to a bench, four hours, one egg each. You will not finish a complex pattern, and that is deliberate.

The spring list closes when the pots run out.

Madder is the first to go, usually by the end of April. If you need a red egg for a May wedding, write before the thaw.
