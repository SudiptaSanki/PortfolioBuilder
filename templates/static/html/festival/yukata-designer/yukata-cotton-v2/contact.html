Write to the studio

One address, one form, and I answer both myself — usually within two days, a little slower in June. If you have run the planner, send me its numbers: your height, your cloth, your obi and your date. That is the whole first letter, already written.

The studio

A machiya off Kawabata-dori, Demachiyanagi, Kyoto. Visits are by appointment — the cutting table is not a shop floor, and a fitting deserves a closed door. Open Tuesday to Saturday, 10:00–18:00.

Before you write

Made-to-measure for a festival closes when the sewing queue passes your date — 21 days at the moment, double in June. Ready sizes leave in 3 days all season. The planner on the home page shows the queue honestly; believe it.
