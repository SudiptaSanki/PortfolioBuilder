## Fiction disclosure
Yui Hoshino and her studio Hoshino Yukata are fictional, created for this template; her clients (Okamoto-ya inn, the Sakyo ward Bon Odori circle, the Ueda family) and their testimonials are invented. The craft is real: the tanmono bolt, the cutting table, the dye methods, Gion Matsuri and the prices are true to the trade. No real person's name, face or work is used; the photographs show cloth, dyeing and festival streets, not the fictional designer.
