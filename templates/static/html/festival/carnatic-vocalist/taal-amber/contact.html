Studio & base

Mylapore, Chennai. Lessons Tuesday to Saturday; concerts by date, anywhere the season calls. Outstation engagements are quoted with travel and a rest day included — see “what moves the price” on the home page.

What helps an enquiry

  • The date, the venue, and the programme length.
  • Whether accompanists are engaged by you or by Nikhil.
  • Any recording or broadcast plans — they change the quote.
  • For lessons: your current level, honestly stated. Beginners are welcome; that is what Level 1 is for.
