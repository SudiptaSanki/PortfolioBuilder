## Sound — the tala player
**Need-test: PASSED.** The trade is sound, and the Carnatic tala cycles are teachable data: the player exists so a listener can learn to follow a cycle — pick Adi (8), Rupaka (6), Misra Chapu (7) or Khanda Chapu (5), and the chart lights each akshara as a synthesised stroke sounds, sam landing with extra weight.

Honesty rules kept:
- All strokes are **synthesised live with Web Audio** (four stylised mridangam-family voices: bass, slap, tick, ring) and are labelled on the page as demonstrations of cycle structure — **not recordings of a real mridangam**.
- The tala structures are the true anga divisions: Adi 4+2+2, Rupaka 2+4, Misra Chapu 3+4, Khanda Chapu 2+3. The syllables are the sollukattu as taught in class.
- AudioContext is created **only after a deliberate Play tap**; there is a visible Stop; playback also stops when the tab is hidden. No autoplay, no hover audio, anywhere on the site.
