What I need from you

Not a formal brief. Just enough that my answer can be useful rather than polite.

  1. The date, the start time, and whether it is fixed.
  2. The kind of occasion, and which parts you expect sung rather than spoken.
  3. The room: size, whether it has a piano or organ, and whether there is amplification.
  4. Whether a choir is expected, and whether it already exists.
  5. Any family melody or setting that matters to you — even a phone recording of someone humming it.

Fees, plainly

A single service in the Pittsburgh area is $450, including one planning call and a rehearsal with any local singers. Weddings and memorials begin at $650. Workshops are $300 for a two-hour session plus travel outside Allegheny County. I do not charge a congregation for the first conversation, and I will say no if the date does not work rather than quote a number that makes it work.

Visiting first

If you are considering a wedding or a memorial and have not heard the choir, come to a Friday service. Sit at the back. Stay for the oneg and introduce yourself afterwards — I will be the person near the kitchen door, eating something I should not.
