Programmes · 2026

Six ways in. All of them measured.

Every programme starts with a measurement and ends with one. What happens in between depends on why you came: a recital, a race, a bad year, or a resting rate that has been creeping up since you turned forty.

Ninety minutes. Resting rate, breath hold, exhale length, a short history, and a first attempt at coherent breathing. You leave with a two-minute daily practice and a baseline on paper.

What I will ask you

Why now. What you have already tried. Whether a doctor has ever told you to be careful with your breathing. Whether you can sit still for six minutes without checking your phone, and if not, we start there.

I will also ask what you expect to change. Most people say sleep or anxiety. Those are reasonable things to want, and they are not the same as a promise I can make.
