Do I need to be flexible or fit

No. You sit in a chair or on a mat with your back supported. Nobody is asked to touch their toes, and most of the first session is conversation and quiet counting.

I have asthma. Can I come

Yes, with your inhaler beside you and your physician informed. We will not do long holds. If your asthma is unstable at the moment, wait until it is not.

Is this the same as meditation

No. Meditation is attention training with the breath as an anchor. This is deliberate manipulation of rate and ratio. They do different things and people often need both; I teach the second.

Can I do it online

Yes, Thursdays, two slots. You need a chair, a quiet room and a camera at chest height so I can see your ribs. The measurement is less precise but the teaching works.

Will it fix my anxiety

It may help you settle in the moment, and it can give you a sense of control over your own state. It is not a treatment for an anxiety disorder. If that is what you are dealing with, bring your psychiatrist or therapist into the conversation; I will happily work alongside them.
