Before you write

What people ask before booking.

Do you travel outside Rio?

Yes, anywhere in Brazil with three weeks' notice, and abroad with two months. A backpiece travels in a hard crate that counts as a piece of luggage; the client pays the excess. For a solo show I bring one costume and a small case of feathers for the encore.

Can you dance on a small stage?

The solo set needs a floor of 4 × 3 m and a ceiling of 3.5 m if the backpiece is worn. For smaller rooms I dance a lighter costume with a headdress only. Tell me the ceiling height — it decides which piece travels.

How long does a costume take?

A headdress takes two to three weeks. A backpiece takes six to eight, plus a fitting. In November and December the atelier is full, so the real answer is: book in September if you need it for carnival.

Are the feathers real?

Ostrich, rooster and pheasant, all from licensed suppliers, dyed in-house. No wild bird feathers and no plastic imitations in the pieces I sign. If you want a vegan costume, I can build with synthetic plumes, but I will tell you honestly that it will not move the same way.

Do you teach complete beginners?

The workshop assumes no sewing experience. You will leave with a headdress you built yourself, a burn from the glue gun, and a realistic idea of how long a costume takes. All materials are included.

What deposit do you ask for?

Half on the commission, the rest on delivery. For shows, 30 per cent holds the date. If you cancel a show inside seven days, the deposit covers the dancers and the transport; beyond that we can move the date once.
