Build the kit.
Then weigh it.

A backpiece that looks light on the drawing board can ruin a parade by the second pass. Set the piece, the feathers and the coverage — the atelier returns the count, the weight, the sewing hours and the cost in reais.

All feathers are ostrich, rooster or pheasant from licensed suppliers; the atelier dyes them in-house. Sequins are hand-sewn, not glued — glue fails under stage lights.

What a passista carries

The costume is a machine with a weight limit. A backpiece over three kilos changes how you turn; heels over eleven centimetres change how you land. These are the numbers I sew to.
