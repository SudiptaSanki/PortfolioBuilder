Your note is in the book

I answer on Sunday and Wednesday. If December is full I will say so straight away and offer the second week of January, which is quieter and better for a first sitting anyway.

What to bring: your hands, and the question. Nothing else is needed.

Before you come

The stairs are steep and there is no lift. The room has two chairs and a north window; the fan is loud between eleven and two. If you need a ground-floor sitting, say so and we will use the spice shop's back room.

What a sitting includes

Ten minutes on the question, twenty on the two hands, ten on the comparison, five on the note. Tea is offered. Silence is allowed; some people want the reading and not the conversation, and that is a perfectly good way to use the hour.

If you are in distress, this is not the room. Please speak to a doctor or a counsellor first; I will happily be the second thing you do that week.
