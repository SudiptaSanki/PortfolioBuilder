## Sound ruling

**Fails the need-test — no audio anywhere.** Goldsmithing is not a
sound profession and no interaction here teaches through audio. The
stub's festival chrome carried no audio worth keeping; nothing was
added. The ruling is stated in every page footer.

## What was removed from the stub, and why

- The stub persona ("Swara Mahajan", with fashion-styling copy pasted
  onto a goldsmith template) and all festival decoration — marigold
  garland/toran SVGs, floating-flower canvas art, kash-grass footer,
  mandala art — pure decoration with no job in a goldsmith's
  portfolio. Files deleted: `assets/floats.svg`, `flowers.svg`,
  `footer.svg`, `garland.svg`, `kash.svg`, `mandala.svg`.
- The vendored GSAP + ScrollTrigger (`vendor/`) — the rebuild is
  vanilla HTML/CSS/JS; no library is loaded.
- The stub's promised effects (garland, float-marigold, scroll
  cascade) were honestly replaced by the two instruments the trade
  actually earns: the **Bench Calculator** (itemised metal + making +
  stones from the printed rate card) and the **Weight & Measure
  Explorer** (g ↔ troy oz ↔ pennyweight ↔ tola, karat → fineness),
  both asserted against the printed tables on the same page by the
  touch probe.

## The numbers

The rate card is the fictional practice's own printed card, derived
from one basis (fine gold ₹9,600/g) divided by karat fraction, so it
is internally consistent by construction: 22k ₹8,800 · 18k ₹7,200 ·
14k ₹5,600 · 9k ₹3,600 per gram. Conversion factors are the standard
ones (1 troy oz = 31.1035 g; 1 dwt = 1.55517 g; 1 tola = 11.6638 g).
Case-entry figures are the card's arithmetic worked in the open.
