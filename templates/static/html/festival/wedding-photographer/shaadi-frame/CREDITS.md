## Sound ruling — FAILS the need-test: no audio anywhere
A wedding photographer's profession is not sound, and no interaction on
this site teaches through audio. The studio's films are delivered
artefacts — files handed to couples — not site content: nothing is
embedded, played or autoplayed here. The ruling is stated in every footer.

## Honest replacements (stub promises → what shipped)
- The stub's drifting petals, spinning mandalas, scroll hero-fade,
  magnetic buttons and GSAP character splits were decoration; they were
  deleted, not rebuilt. In their place: the **Coverage Planner**, which
  does the practice's real arithmetic (Table 1 hours × 45–60 edited
  frames/hour; Table 2 prices + printed add-on rates) in the open.

  silent.
- The mobile menu is a "Run sheet" panel fitted to the concept:
  animated, aria-expanded, Esc closes, focus is managed in and out,
  links ≥52px.
