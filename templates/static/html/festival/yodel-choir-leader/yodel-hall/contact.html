Do you use microphones?

No. A Naturjodel through a PA loses the room, and the room is half the instrument. For a very large hall the choir will bring a small wooden platform instead.

Can we request a specific number?

Yes, from the repertoire list. Anything marked hard needs four weeks' notice. The Fasnacht programme is fixed and not available outside February.

Is there a minimum notice for a booking?

Four weeks is comfortable. Two is sometimes possible if the altos are in the valley. December is usually gone by the end of October.

I have never yodelled. Is that a problem?

It is the normal starting point. Most new singers spend their first month on the break without a single note of repertoire. The open Tuesday is designed for exactly this.

Where does the money go?

Travel, the schoolhouse rent, sheet music and a small honorarium for Maria. The choir is an association, not a business, and the accounts are read aloud at the January meeting.
