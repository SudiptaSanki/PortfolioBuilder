What the choir actually sings

Nine numbers make up the working book this winter, between them about twenty-two minutes of music. Four are wordless Naturjodel, two are Jodellieder with verses, three are solo calls. Filter by form to see the shape of an evening.

Durations are concert timings, measured at rehearsal with a stopwatch and rounded to five seconds. Levels describe the break, not the tune: a number marked ··· asks for an octave leap at speed.

II Programmes

Two evenings, currently touring

Both run about fifty minutes with a spoken introduction to each number. The choir travels with twelve to eighteen singers depending on the month, and always with the same three-part core.

Winterlicht

December–January · 50 min · 18 singers

  1. Der Alphornruf — solo call, from the back of the hall
  2. Abend am Bach
  3. Wenn d'Sunne über d'Berge geit
  4. S'Älplerli — with the audience on the verse
  5. Zäuerli in G
  6. Brienzersee
  7. Nachtjodel — lights down, no introduction

Fasnacht, before dawn

February · 25 min · 12 singers, costume

  1. Gemsjäger — outdoors, at the station steps
  2. S'Älplerli
  3. Zäuerli in G — faster than the concert tempo
  4. Ranz des Vaches — as the parade turns the corner
  5. Abend am Bach — the quiet one, to finish
III Booking

How a booking works

Weddings, village evenings, hotel concerts and the occasional corporate winter dinner. The choir does not sing at events where it would be background music for a speech, and it does not amplify: a yodel through a PA loses the thing that makes it worth hearing.

  • Ask early. Four to six weeks is comfortable. December is usually full by the end of October.
  • Say where and how many. A church, a hall and a barn need different programmes. Outdoors needs a wall behind the singers and no wind.
  • Agree the length. Twenty-five minutes or fifty. The choir will not stretch a twenty-five into forty, because the repertoire does not.

Fees go to the choir's travel fund, the schoolhouse rent, and a small honorarium for the leader. They are on the contact page with the rest of the practical details.
