One way in

WRITE TO ME.

Singers, listening members and guest choirs all come through the same door: this form, or the email beside it. Tell me which row you think you are — or that you have no idea, which is the most common and most honest answer. I reply within the week, usually the same evening as a rehearsal.

Thank you.

Your message is on the hall table. Your reference is YH-2027-0000 — quote it if you write again before I reply. Audition evenings for the Herisau season are Tuesday 2 and Friday 5 February 2027; open rehearsals run every Tuesday and Friday until then, and the back row is always free.
