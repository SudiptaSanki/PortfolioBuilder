## Sound

The pitch bench on the front page generates plain reference tones in the
browser with Web Audio at the moment a visitor taps a button (chest voice
E3 ≈ 165 Hz, head voice E4 ≈ 330 Hz, and the flip between them; peak gain
0.12, every tone self-stopping inside two seconds). No audio files are
used or shipped, and nothing sounds before a tap.

## Everything else

All layout, strips, tables and copy are original to this template. The
craft facts are the real facts of the trade they describe: the chest/head
register break, rehearsal structure by voice part, the Jodlerfest jury's
five marks (sound culture, intonation, rhythm, dynamics, expression) and
its classification from Klasse 1 to Klasse 4. The Eidgenössisches
Jodlerfest in Zug took place in June 2023; the club, its leader, its
Herisau target and its season figures are the fiction built around them.
