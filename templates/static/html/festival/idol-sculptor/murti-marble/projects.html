Note Prices

Why we do not quote from a photograph

Two murtis of the same height can differ by a third in cost. The block is one reason, the detail is another, and the third is the face: some faces take a week, and some take a month and a half.

A 36-inch marble Ganesha in a straightforward finish is a different job from a 36-inch Ganesha with an inlaid halo, a carved mouse and a jewellery set cut to a jeweller's drawing. We quote after the drawing is agreed, and the drawing is included in the price.

If you have a budget, tell us the budget before the drawing. It is easier to design a murti for a number than to reduce one after it is drawn, and we would rather carve a simpler figure well than a complicated one badly.
