Questions

Asked across the bench

How do I know the murti will fit?

Send the height of the plinth or shelf, and the width and depth of the space. We make a cardboard profile at full size for murtis above 30 inches; you can stand it in the room before the stone is cut. It costs nothing and prevents the most common mistake.

Can I see it while it is being carved?

Yes, twice: after the rough-out, when the figure has its mass, and after the detail, before polishing. Photographs at the same two stages if you cannot come. We do not encourage visits during the first weeks of roughing, when the figure looks like rubble and clients worry.

What if I do not like the face?

The drawing is approved before carving, and the face is cut from that drawing. If the finished face is not right, we will recut it once at no charge — but a recut costs the figure some depth, so we would rather spend the time on the drawing.

Do you handle installation?

In Jaipur and within 300 km, yes: a mason, a crane if needed, and a carver on site for the placing. Beyond that we crate the murti and work with an installer you nominate; we send a lifting plan with the crate.

Is a bronze murti made here?

The wax model is carved here. The casting is done by a family in Swamimalai we have worked with since 2011, using lost-wax. The bronze is not a copy of the marble work: the model is made separately for casting, and the finish is patinated, not polished.
