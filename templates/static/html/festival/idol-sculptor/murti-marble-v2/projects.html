1 · Temple Ganesh — Pune

Ganesh · 150 cm · Makrana First White · Satin polish · ₹4,36,725 · 18 weeks carving

The ask

A housing-society temple committee wanted a five-foot seated Ganesh installed before Ganesh Chaturthi. They came in January, having already been promised six weeks by a machine-assisted shop across town.

The work

I told them eighteen weeks and showed them why: a 1,755 kg block, chosen at Makrana in February after eleven blocks failed the ring test or carried a vein across the face line. Roughing out took four weeks alone. The modak in the lower left hand was carved last, in the final fortnight, as tradition in my family orders it.

The outcome

Crated in July, trucked to Pune, installed three weeks before the festival. The committee's priest performed the consecration — that rite is the temple's, never the sculptor's, and my part ended, as it should, at a well-made stone.

“He told us eighteen weeks when another shop said six. We chose the eighteen — and the murti was installed three weeks before the festival, with the face our old photos promised.”

— Secretary, housing-society temple committee, Pune

2 · Home-shrine Lakshmi — Ahmedabad

Lakshmi · 45 cm · Ambaji White · Painted detail · ₹71,690 · 4 weeks carving

The ask

A family in Ahmedabad was building a teak shrine into a new flat and wanted a Lakshmi in place for Diwali. They ordered in June — early, which meant the figure could be fitted between temple commissions.

The work

Ambaji white, chosen for its warm tone against teak. Three weeks of carving, then a fourth week of painting: the sari border, the lotus, and the coins in the lower hands picked out in pigment and a little gold, as the family asked.

The outcome

Delivered by car in a small crate in September, a month before Diwali. The family's grandmother inspected the face with a magnifier — the correct test at this size — and passed it.

“My mother checks the face every morning when she lights the lamp. She says the goddess looks calm, not startled. From her, that is the whole review.”

— The family's daughter, Ahmedabad

3 · Replacement Krishna — Indore

Krishna · 90 cm · Makrana Veined · Satin polish · ₹1,46,040 · 8 weeks carving

The ask

A community hall's Krishna, forty years old, had cracked across the base when it was moved for painting. The hall secretary asked the question every sculptor hears: can you repair it?

The work

The honest answer was no. A structural crack can be pinned and filled so it looks mended, but the stone will not carry weight through it again, and a 90 cm figure stands on its base. I carved a replacement instead, working from the hall's photographs and the old figure's measurements, so the congregation kept the face it knew. The veining in the new block was steered into the base and the peacock feather.

The outcome

The new Krishna was installed in eight weeks. The old figure was not discarded — it stands in the hall's garden, with a small plaque giving its years. That was the secretary's idea, and it was the right one.

“He could have sold us a repair that would fail. He sold us the truth and a new Krishna instead, and found a place of honour for the old one.”

— Secretary, community hall, Indore
