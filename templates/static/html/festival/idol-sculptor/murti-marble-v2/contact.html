Before you write

For a replacement: photographs of the old murti — front, side, and the damage — plus its height. That is enough for a first answer.

For a festival date: check the planner's ready date against it. If the stone says no, I will say no too — and offer the smaller figure or the shelf instead.

Deposit: a commission is booked with the stone cost only. The carving weeks are paid in stages, and the balance when the crate is sealed.
