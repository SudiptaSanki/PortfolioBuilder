Admissions

Put your name in the register.

Trial classes are on the first Friday of each month at five in the evening. No audition, no costume, no commitment. Children from seven; adults welcome, though the beginners' class moves at a child's pace and adults often prefer the Wednesday abhinaya session.

Bring water, a notebook, and a willingness to be in the back row. Wear something loose enough to sit cross-legged in; a dupatta that stays put is worth more than new dance clothes.

Is there an age limit?

Children from seven. There is no upper limit for the open class, though students over forty usually begin with the Wednesday abhinaya session, which is gentler on the knees.

Do we need to buy a costume?

Not until the second year, and not from the school. The tailor on Sayyaji Rao Road has made costumes for four generations of students and will not overcharge you.

How long until an arangetram?

At least six years of steady practice, usually eight. It is not promised at admission and it is not a graduation; it is a first full performance, and it should happen when it is ready.

Can adults join the children's class?

They can, and some do, but it is not the best fit. The Wednesday abhinaya class is mixed-age and assumes no prior training.

Do you teach online?

No. The school has never recorded a class and does not intend to. If you are outside Mysuru, the honest answer is to find a teacher in your city and come to the annual workshop.
