The method

Three things we do
before anyone dances

01

Stand still for eight counts

Every class opens with the sama stithi. Feet together, spine tall, eyes level, eight counts. Most beginners cannot do it without shifting. That is the first lesson, not a warm-up.

02

Say the tala out loud

Before the feet move, the mouth does. Ta-ka-dhi-mi, dha-dhin-dhin-dha. A student who cannot recite the cycle will not be able to keep it once the ankles are tired.

03

Watch one person, not the mirror

There are no mirrors in the hall. A dancer learns by watching a body in front of her and by being watched. It is slower, and it is the only way to learn to hold an audience.

Trial classes run on the first Friday of each month, at five in the evening. Wear something you can sit on the floor in. Bring water and no expectations about your knees.

The hall is a ten-minute walk from Mysuru's Gokulam main road, upstairs from the old printing press. Look for the brass bell on the door.
