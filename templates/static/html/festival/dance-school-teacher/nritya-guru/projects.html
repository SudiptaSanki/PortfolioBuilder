Repertoire & productions

What the hall has been making.

Six items from the last three years: two productions, two arangetrams, one outreach tour and one festival commission. Filter by kind. All of them were rehearsed on the same wooden floor.

Kind
ProductionNov 2026

Kaveri, the river that is also a mother

A new varnam in raga Kalyani, composed for the school by R. S. Sheshadri, tracing the Kaveri from Talakaveri to the delta. Six dancers, live mridangam and violin, forty-eight minutes without an interval. The piece was built around one question: how do you dance a river without pretending to be water?

Choreography
V. Rao
Music
R. S. Sheshadri
Premiere
Rangayana, Mysuru
ArangetramAug 2026

Ananya Hegde's arangetram

Nine years of training, two hours on the stage at the Jaganmohan Palace hall, and a varnam in Bhairavi that her grandmother requested. The mridangam was played by her uncle, which was not planned and was the best part of the evening.

Student
Ananya Hegde, 17
Years trained
9
Accompanists
4, live
OutreachJul 2026

Six schools, one fortnight

Senior students took a forty-minute programme to government schools in Hunsur taluk: two pieces, an explanation of the tala with the children clapping, and a question nobody expected — “why do you stamp only one foot?” Six schools, 1,100 children, no stage, mostly a courtyard and a tree.

FestivalSep 2025

Dasara procession, twelve dancers

Twelve senior students on the wooden float behind the palace band, performing a five-minute nritta piece twice along the route. The floor of the float was new and slippery; we rehearsed on wet wood for a week. Nobody fell, though one ankle was lost to history.

12

ProductionFeb 2026

Two rows, one breath

A chamber piece for eight dancers built entirely on the idea of a corridor: two facing rows, hands meeting and parting, one dancer walking between them. The music is only nattuvangam and voice — no mridangam, no violin. It is the quietest thing the school has made.

Dancers
8
Music
nattuvangam & voice
Venue
School hall, 90 seats
ArangetramJun 2025

The Nair sisters, together

Two arangetrams on one evening, which the school rarely allows. Sisters, four years apart, trained in the same hall, performing separate solos and one duet their mother asked for. The duet was the weakest piece technically and the only one anyone remembers.

Students
Meera & Tara Nair

21 years
