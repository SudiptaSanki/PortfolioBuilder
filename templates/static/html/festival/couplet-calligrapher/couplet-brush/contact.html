Commission · the studio

Send the words, the wall and the date.

A commission goes faster if you have a sher in mind and a photograph of the wall. If you have neither, write anyway: I will suggest three couplets and we can start from the space. The studio answers on Sundays and Wednesdays, and every message is read by me, not by an assistant.

The studio

Studio 4, Bazaar-e-Qalam, Hyderabad 500002. First floor, above the paper shop, by appointment. Bring the wall measurements and a photograph if you have them; bring the sher written out if you are unsure of the spelling.

Lead times

A standard panel takes six to ten weeks. Illumination adds two to three. Wedding season, October to February, fills first; a date inside six weeks is possible only if the studio is quiet.

Prices

From ₹9,500 for a small plain panel to ₹96,000 for a twelve-panel portfolio. Price depends on the size, the paper and the border, not on the number of words. The bench on the front page will give you an estimate before you write.

Shipping

Panels travel flat, in a wooden crate, by courier. I will not roll a written panel. International shipping is quoted at cost and takes about a week to Europe or the Gulf.

Payment

Half on the proof, half on delivery. If the panel is not what we agreed, I will rewrite it; that has happened twice and both times it was my fault.
