Tell us about the day.

A feast, a wedding, a procession or a concert. Sepp answers on Monday evenings after rehearsal; in July and August it can take a week, because the band is somewhere on a meadow.

J

Booking enquiry

The date, the place and the rough number of listeners are enough to start. If it is a wedding, mention whether the band plays in the church, at the marquee, or both.

K

What a booking includes

The band is a volunteer association, so a booking is a fee for the association rather than a wage. The fee pays for music, insurance, the hall, and the annual repair of the bass drum.

Standard engagement
Up to three hours, one break, full band
Travel
Included within 30 km; a per-kilometre rate beyond that
Programme
Agreed two weeks ahead, one special request learned if time allows
Deposit
None. A confirmed date is kept on trust and a handshake
Weather
Outdoor engagements are played in rain unless there is lightning
Uniform
Dark green, brass buttons. The band arrives in it
Short notice: a funeral or a sudden feast can often be covered with a small ensemble at a few days' notice. Call the band office rather than writing; the phone is answered by whoever is nearest the hall.

L

Before you write

Three details that change the answer more than anything else.

01

Inside or out

An outdoor programme is written for wind and distance: more middle register, fewer subtle dynamics. A church programme can be quiet.

02

The walk

If the band has to march, the programme has to be playable while walking, and the tubas need a trailer or very strong legs.

03

The one request

There is always one piece the family expects. Name it, and it will be rehearsed properly rather than attempted on the night.
