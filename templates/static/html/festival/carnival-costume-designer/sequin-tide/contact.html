Book a fitting

Tell me who has to dance in it

The useful details are the event, the surface the dancer will walk on, how long the costume must stay on, and whether anyone has to climb onto a float in it. The design follows from those, not the other way round.

This page does not send anything. It checks the brief so you can copy a complete one into an email or bring it to the atelier. I answer within three days, except during carnival.

Where to come

Sequin Tide, first floor, Rua 31 de Janeiro, Fontainhas, Panjim 403001. The door is blue, between the bakery and the house with the yellow balcony.

Ring the bell twice. The machine is loud and the stairs are narrow, so please do not bring a group to a first fitting.

What to bring to a fitting

  • The shoes, or the exact heel height
  • A photograph of the venue floor
  • The dancer’s measurements, if taken recently
  • Any reference pictures, and who made them
  • The undergarments the costume will be worn over

Lead times and cost

A single ball gown takes four to six weeks and starts around ₹48,000, depending on the sequin size and coverage. A float costume is six to nine weeks. A troupe of forty needs eleven weeks and a deposit.

The bench on the home page gives a close estimate before you write. It is not a quote, but it is not a fantasy either.
