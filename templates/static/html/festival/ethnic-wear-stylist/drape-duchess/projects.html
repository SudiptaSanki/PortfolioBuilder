Lookbook · 2027

Eight drapes,
eight evenings.

Each look lists the drape, the fabric and the number of pins it took. The colour on the figure is the fabric, not a mood board; the notes are what I would tell you in the fitting room.

Wedding01

The Monday morning muhurtham

Kanjeevaram in deep wine, Nivi drape, seven pleats. Three pins, all at the shoulder. The border is heavy enough that the drape holds itself through the ceremony.

Wedding02

The Coorgi, with no dupatta

Handloom cotton in bottle green, pleats at the back, waist band instead of a tuck. Five pins. The cleanest front of any drape here, and the one I recommend in June heat.

Reception03

Organza, opened like a shawl

Pale rose organza with the pallu unpinned at the hip so it falls open. Four pins at the shoulder, all hidden under the border. It photographs wide, which is the point at a reception.

Reception04

Banarasi with the pallu to the front

A seedha pallu for a bride who wants the brocade visible in every photograph. Six pleats at the right hip, four pins. The blouse was cut twice to make this work, and it was worth it.

Sangeet05

Gold chiffon, pinned for dancing

Chiffon in old gold, seedha pallu, and a brooch rather than a safety pin at the shoulder. Two extra pins at the waist, because there will be dancing and nobody wants to think about their clothes.

Sangeet06

A lehenga set for the cousins' dance

Not a saree at all: a pre-stitched lehenga with the dupatta pinned at both shoulders like a soft cape. Four pins, no tuck. It is the most forgiving thing in the studio for a night on your feet.

Festival07

A pooja drape that survives the floor

Handloom cotton in deep green, pleats low and loose, pallu pinned flat at the left shoulder. Two pins. You will be sitting cross-legged and standing up repeatedly; this is the drape for that.

Festival08

Diwali at home, in silk cotton

An old silk cotton in pomegranate, five pleats, pallu over the right shoulder. Two pins and a bindi. Some evenings do not need a stylist, only someone to hold the pleats straight.

No looks of that occasion in the public book. Write to me and I will send private examples that fit the function.
