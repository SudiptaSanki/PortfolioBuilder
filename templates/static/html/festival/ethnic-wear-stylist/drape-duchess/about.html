The stylist · Bandra since 2013

I dress bodies,
not hangers.

I am Rukmini Iyer. I have tied sarees on about nineteen hundred people, and the thing I have learned is that most advice about draping is written for a mannequin that does not have to eat dinner in it.

I trained as a costume designer at NIFT, which taught me how clothes are cut but not how they behave on a person who is nervous. That came later, in a bridal shop in Dadar where I spent two years watching women cry in fitting rooms because a blouse did not close.

In 2013 I rented a room off Pali Hill with a mirror, a steamer and a borrowed dress form. The first month I draped sarees for free at a friend's salon, on the condition that I could photograph the result and ask questions afterwards. Forty fittings in, I understood that the problem was almost never the saree.

Since then the work has settled into three kinds. Bridal fittings, mostly in the six weeks before a wedding, when the blouse is still being altered. Lookbook and editorial work for a handful of labels who want the drape to look like it happened by accident. And wardrobe edits for women who own fourteen sarees and wear two, because nobody ever showed them how to tie the other twelve.

A good drape is not the one that looks best when you stand still. It is the one that is still there after the second song.

The studio is one room and one assistant, Meena, who has been with me since 2018 and can pin a pallu in the time it takes me to find my glasses. We do not sell sarees and we take no commission from shops. If your fabric is wrong for what you want, I will tell you before you spend money on a blouse.

  • i

    The body first. If the drape requires you to hold your stomach in, it is not finished.

  • ii

    Pins are a diagnosis. More than six means something else is wrong.

  • iii

    Photographs last. The camera is shown the drape that already works, not the other way round.

  • iv

    No surprises on the day. Everything is tried with the jewellery and the shoes.

A note on bodies

Every drape on this site can be tied on any body

The pleat count changes, the pallu width changes, and sometimes the fabric has to change. Nothing about a drape is reserved for a particular size, and I will not pretend otherwise to sell a fitting.
