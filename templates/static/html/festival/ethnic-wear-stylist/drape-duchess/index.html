The classic. Seven pleats, each about four fingers wide, tucked at the centre front and pinned only where the fabric insists.

Pin count is a real thing. If a drape needs more than six, the fabric is wrong for the style or the blouse is too loose — both are fixable before the day.

What a fitting is

Ninety minutes,
and you will sit down

A fitting is not a fashion show. You will walk, sit on the floor, raise your arms, and eat a biscuit. Anything that fails, we fix before you leave.

  1. 01

    Bring everything

    The saree, the blouse, the petticoat, the jewellery you intend to wear, and the shoes. Half of a bad drape is a missing piece.

  2. 02

    We try the standard first

    Nine times in ten, the classic Nivi is right. We only depart from it when your body or the fabric asks us to.

  3. 03

    Then we make it survive

    Sitting, stairs, dancing, a plate of food. I pin and re-pin until you can do all four without thinking about your clothes.

  4. 04

    You leave with the map

    A photograph of every pin, and a card showing the pleat count and order, so anyone can put you back together on the day.
