What happens next

  1. 1You write with a date and a rough head count. Rough is fine — the reckoning forgives.
  2. 2I answer within two days with the reckoning in writing: ears, meal, firewood, hours of fire, and the price. The same table as the planner on the home page.
  3. 3We walk the ground — your field, yard or hall — or talk it through by phone if the field is far.
  4. 4A third of the price holds the date. The rest is due the week of the feast. If the crop fails, we eat the store, and I tell you the day I know.
