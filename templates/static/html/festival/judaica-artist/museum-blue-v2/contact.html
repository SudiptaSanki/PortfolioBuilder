The terms, printed

Deposit: half the price confirms a commission; the balance is due when the piece is ready, before posting.

Time: the bench weeks printed on the gallery page are the promise — 3 to 8 weeks by piece — and postage is on top of that.

Engraving: names and dates in Hebrew or English are included in the price. Wording for synagogue pieces goes to your rabbi first, at no charge and with no hurry.

Returns: stock pieces return within 30 days, unused. Commissioned pieces with names cut into them are yours — which is why the wording is confirmed in writing before the graver touches metal.

How an order goes

  1. You write — form or email — with the piece and the date.
  2. The bench replies with a ready date, or an honest no and the shelf alternative.
  3. Deposit confirms; engraving wording is agreed in writing.
  4. The piece is made, photographed for you before posting, and sent with its care card.
