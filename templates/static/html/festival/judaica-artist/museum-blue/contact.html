Questions

Before the first note

How long does a piece take?

A mezuzah is four to six weeks; a kiddush set ten to fourteen; a seder plate about eight weeks; synagogue silver four to seven months. The clock starts when the drawing is approved, not when the first email arrives.

Can you write the scroll?

No. The scrolls in my mezuzot and tefillin are written by a sofer, Yitzhak Halevi, and his name and the date are on the certificate. I will fit a scroll you already own if it is the right size, but I will not vouch for its kashrut.

Do you ship abroad?

Yes, insured, by courier. Buyers pay duty. Silver crosses borders easily; wood does not, so a challah board may need a phytosanitary certificate depending on the country.

Will a silver piece tarnish?

Yes. Sterling silver tarnishes, especially in Jerusalem's air. A piece used weekly needs polishing twice a year with a cloth, not a dip. I send a cloth with every piece and will re-polish anything I made, at no charge, if you bring it to the studio.

Do you repair old Judaica?

Sometimes, if the piece is worth it and the repair will not erase the maker's work. I will not re-silver a worn piece to make it look new; the wear is the history. Send photographs first.
