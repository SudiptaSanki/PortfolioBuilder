Note Prices

How a price is built

Silver is weighed, the work is counted in days, and the two are added. There is no separate design fee: the drawing is part of the work, and you see it before the silver is cut.

A mezuzah case is about 90 g of silver and four days of piercing; a seder plate is 430 g and three weeks. The difference in price is mostly the difference in days, not the difference in metal.

Silver is priced at the day's rate plus a small margin for wastage. If the rate moves more than ten per cent between the quote and the order, I will tell you before starting; otherwise the quote holds for sixty days.
