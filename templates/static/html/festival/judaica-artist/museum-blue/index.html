03 Commissions

Three things people ask me to make

Most commissions come from a family event: a wedding, a bar mitzvah, a house that has just been bought. I take eight a year and keep two slots for synagogues.

I

A mezuzah for a new house

The case, pierced and lined, with the family's word or initials. The scroll is written by a sofer I have worked with for years; I do not write it myself, and I say so.

The studio is one room with a north window

You are welcome to visit, but write first: the bench is small and the torch is hot. Mornings are for sawing, afternoons for finishing, and Fridays the studio closes at one.

Bring the object you already have if you want something to sit beside it. Bring the room's measurements if it is for a wall. Bring nothing if you only want to look, which is also a good reason to come.
