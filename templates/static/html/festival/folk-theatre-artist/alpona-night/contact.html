Fee and travel

A night is quoted as one figure for the full company, agreed before the date. Nights within sixty kilometres of Santipur include travel; beyond that, transport and the troupe's meals are added at cost.

Half the fee confirms a date. If a committee cancels inside seven days, the advance is not returned, because the company has turned other bookings away.

What the stage needs

  • A raised platform, 20 by 16 feet or larger
  • Two power points and a generator if the ground is unlit
  • A room to change, and water for twelve people
  • An hour and a half to set the flats and lamps
