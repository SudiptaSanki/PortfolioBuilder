Repertoire

Eight palas, and the roles I keep returning to

Each production below is in the current book: rehearsed, costumed, and ready for a village ground or a festival stage. Running times are for the full version; the ninety-minute festival cut is noted where it works.

8 productions
4 jatra · 3 pala · 1 kirtan

Showing all eight productions.

  1. 01 Pala First played 2019

    Chaitanya pala

    Nimai, then the sutradhar in the second half

    The boy who takes the name and the following that comes with it. Played without a miracle on stage: the transformation happens in the crowd's faces, and we light the audience during the kirtan rather than the actor.

    Running time
    3 h 40
    Company
    9 on stage
    Festival cut
    95 min, two songs
    Stage
    Wide, two wings
  2. 02 Jatra First played 2004

    Behula's lament

    Behula; later Chand sadagar in the revival

    The longest-running production in the book and the one that taught me how far a lament can carry in an open field. The snake is a length of blue cloth worked by two backstage hands, and it has never once failed to frighten the front row.

    Running time
    4 h 10
    Company
    11 on stage
    Festival cut
    Not advised
    Stage
    Deep, riverbank set
  3. 03 Jatra First played 2011

    Yugal milan

    Krishna; the gopi in the comic interlude

    A Radha–Krishna pala built around the meeting in the grove, with the comic subplot of the two cowherds given more room than the script originally allowed. The audience asks for the boat song every night, and we have learned to place it late.

    Running time
    4 h
    Company
    12 on stage
    Festival cut
    100 min
    Stage
    Grove flat, three entrances
  4. 04 Jatra First played 2016

    Savitri–Satyavan

    Yama, and the sutradhar

    The best villain part I have. Yama is played quietly, standing still at the edge of the lamp light while the argument happens around him. Schools book this one for the last week of January, and the students argue about the ending on the way home.

    Running time
    3 h 30
    Company
    10 on stage
    Festival cut
    90 min
    Stage
    Forest flat, single entrance
  5. 05 Pala First played 2021

    The clay horse

    The potter, and his son in the dream scene

    A new pala written with the company during the closed season, about a potter in a village that is being widened into a highway. The only production in the book with no king in it, and the only one where the backdrop is unpainted clay.

    Running time
    2 h 50
    Company
    7 on stage
    Festival cut
    Written at 80 min
    Stage
    Small, courtyard
  6. 06 Jatra First played 2013

    Harishchandra's market

    Harishchandra

    The truth-keeping king, played as a man who cannot stop explaining himself. It is the most argumentative jatra we carry, and the one that divides a village committee most reliably when they are choosing a date.

    Running time
    3 h 55
    Company
    11 on stage
    Festival cut
    Not advised
    Stage
    Market set, two levels
  7. 07 Kirtan First played 2018

    Night kirtan

    Lead voice, with four singers and harmonium

    Not theatre, but the form the troupe came out of. Two hours of kirtan for a wedding eve, a housewarming or the opening of a festival, with the audience answering the refrain. We can lead a congregation of any size from a single microphone.

    Running time
    2 h
    Company
    5 singers
    Festival cut
    45 min
    Stage
    Any raised platform
  8. 08 Pala First played 2022

    A winter Ramayana

    Ravana, and the sutradhar

    Three episodes from the Aranya kanda, played as a single night. The set is built from bamboo and white cloth, and the snow that falls in the last scene is rice husk dropped from the lighting bar.

    Running time
    3 h 20
    Company
    13 on stage
    Festival cut
    100 min
    Stage
    Wide, bamboo frame

Which pala fits your night

Tell me the occasion, the size of the ground and whether there is a generator. I will suggest one of the eight rather than the one that is easiest to stage.
