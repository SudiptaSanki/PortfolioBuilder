How a commission runs

  1. You send the room, the light and the date.
  2. A visit, or a video call with the measurements.
  3. A crease-pattern sketch and a paper maquette, 2–3 weeks.
  4. Approval, then a 40 per cent deposit and the sheet order.
  5. Folding, boxing, delivery and installation.

Lead times and prices

A single exhibition piece is ¥180,000–480,000 depending on sheet size and complexity. A wall or installation is quoted after the visit, usually ¥60,000–90,000 a square metre. Editions are priced per piece and boxed. Workshops are ¥4,500, materials included, paid on the evening.

Paper and light

Tell me the light before the colour. White washi in direct sun yellows in a season; a north-facing room will keep it for a decade. For a dark room I use a warmer paper and a stronger crease, so the folds read as shadow rather than line.

Shipping

Folded works travel in a rigid box with a paper cradle, and the wings are braced. Within Japan, a courier; abroad, EMS with the piece declared as art. I do not ship wet-folded curves without a crate, and I will say so if the budget cannot carry one.
