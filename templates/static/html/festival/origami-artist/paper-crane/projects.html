Folds · 2019 to 2026

Six pieces, and the sheet each one needed.

Every work here is one sheet unless the note says otherwise. The crease count is the number of folds that remain in the finished piece; the sheet size is what went onto the bench.

Exhibitions · Kyoto, Tokyo, Osaka No cuts, no glue Washi, tant, kozo

Senbazuru, a thousand and one

2026 · Kyoto · group show

A stairwell in Nakagyo, 96 threads and 1,001 cranes from 6 cm sheets, all cut from the same run of washi so that the flock reads as one material rather than a collection. The extra crane is the one the legend asks for.

1,001 cranes · 6 × 6 cm sheets · 16 creases each · installed over two days

Folded room

2025 · Osaka · commission

A 4 by 6 metre wall for a hotel lobby, folded from 240 tant sheets joined along their creases so the seams fall inside the pattern. The repeat is a diamond that reads as cloth from three metres and as paper from thirty centimetres.

240 sheets · 50 × 50 cm · 24 creases each · 6 weeks

Water

2024 · Tokyo · solo

Six fish and a wave wet-folded from 90 g kozo so the curves hold without wire. The wave is one 90 cm sheet and took nineteen attempts; the nineteenth is the one that hung.

7 pieces · 90 × 90 cm wave · wet-folded · 5 months

Eight petals

2023 · Kyoto · edition

An edition of twelve flowers from 24 cm tant, each folded from the same crease pattern and each slightly different because the paper has a direction. Sold in a box with the pattern printed on the lid, which is the only place the pattern is published.

Edition of 12 · 24 × 24 cm · 28 creases · 2023

Three birds, one room

2022 · Kyoto · installation

Three pieces at 1.6 metres, folded from joined sheets because a single sheet that size will not hold a crease. Hung at eye level so the folds catch the gallery light; the shadows are half the work.

3 pieces · 1.6 m span · 3 joined sheets each · 9 weeks

Crane, edition

2021 · Kyoto · edition of 20

The first piece I sold as an edition: 20 cranes from 30 cm tant, on a painted plinth, boxed with a folded card. It paid for the studio's second lamp.

20 pieces · 30 × 30 cm · 32 creases · 2021
