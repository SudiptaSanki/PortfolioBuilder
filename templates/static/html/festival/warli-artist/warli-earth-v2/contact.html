Commission

Tell me about your wall

One wall at a time — that is the whole of my system. Write to me here, or by email at sunita.bhoye@example.in. I answer within three days, sooner in the quiet months, slower when the harvest walls are on.

Before you write

What helps me answer well

The wall itself. Its width and height, what it is made of, and whether it sees damp in the monsoon. If you have used the planner, you already have your count of figures, days and price — the same numbers I will work from.

The date, if there is one. A wedding date does not move, and I will not pretend a wall can be hurried. If your date is too close for the wall you want, I will say so plainly and tell you what could still be done — a second painter, a smaller wall, or a cloth hanging for the day itself and the wall after.

The wait. Wedding walls for the season book early, and at harvest time the godowns and courtyards of this district keep me fully. When you write, I will tell you the honest wait before I take any advance — the advance is half the price, only after I have seen the wall and we have agreed the scene, and the rest is paid when the last figure is dry.

Paper and cloth travel by post, packed flat or rolled, anywhere in India. Walls, I paint where they stand — Palghar and the neighbouring districts, further if the wall is worth the journey and we talk first.
