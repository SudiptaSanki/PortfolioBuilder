# Credits — Warli Earth

**Photographs: none.** This template uses no photographs at all. The
paintings are the media, and they are drawings, not photos.

**The paintings and figures are hand-drawn SVG made for this site.**
Every Warli figure — the tarpa dancers, the tarpa player, the drummer,
trees, suns, birds, grain sheaves, the peacock, the fish — was drawn in
code for this template, in the Warli manner: two triangles and a circle
make a person; white on geru (red ochre) is the tradition's palette.
The running figure band that frames every page is built from the same
drawn figures. They are original drawings made in respect of a living
tradition; they are not copies of any artist's work, and no real
person's likeness appears anywhere on the site.

**Type:** Kalam and Mukta, Google Fonts (SIL Open Font Licence).

**Persona:** Sunita Bhoye is fictional, and the site footer says so on
every page. The Warli tradition she describes is real: it belongs to
the Warli people of Palghar district and the North Konkan, Maharashtra.
Motifs are named as the tradition names them — the tarpa dance, the
dev chauk and lagna chauk, Palaghat, Kansari the goddess of the grain,
Devicha Dongar, the hill of the goddess. Traditional practice is stated
as traditional (cow-dung and mud plaster, geru wash, rice-paste white,
a chewed bamboo stick for a brush); the one modern substitution — a
lasting white pigment for paper and cloth that must travel — is stated
openly on the About page, not passed off as rice paste.

**Sound:** none. The sound need-test fails for a painter — no audio
files, no synthesis, nothing behind a prompt.

**Code:** hand-written HTML/CSS/JS, no framework, no libraries. The
stub's decorative assets (garland lights, fireflies) and its unused
GSAP vendor files were removed in the rebuild.
