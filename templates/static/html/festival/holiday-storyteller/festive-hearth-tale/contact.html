What happens next

  1. I reply with two or three nights

    Each with a set length and a fee, so you can compare rather than negotiate blind.

  2. We talk for fifteen minutes

    Phone or video, at your convenience. This is where I ask about the room and the youngest person in it.

  3. I hold the date for five days

    A deposit of half the fee confirms it. The rest is due on the night, by cheque or e-transfer.

Cancellation

Full refund of the deposit up to fourteen days before the date. Inside fourteen days the deposit becomes credit for a set within twelve months. If I cancel, you get everything back and I will help find another teller.

Two things that make a booking easier

Send the room, not the idea. A photograph of the space is worth more than a paragraph. I need to see where the audience sits, where the table can go, and whether there is a wall behind me that the candlelight can work against.

Tell me the youngest age. Everything else in the set adjusts around it: the length, the vocabulary, whether the Judith story is in the room, and whether the props go on the table or on the floor. It is the single most useful fact you can give me.
