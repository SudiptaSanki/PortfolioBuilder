  • NoticeSix months for a new loan; three for an existing borrower

  • TravelMuseum courier, borrower pays
  • Environment50 ± 5 % RH, 20 ± 2 °C, 50 lux max

If you are offering an object, do not send it. Send photographs and whatever history you have, and we will tell you honestly whether it belongs here, in another collection, or with your family.

What happens next

Three ordinary weeks.

  • 01

    The register is checked

    Ira searches the accession book, the loan history and the condition file before replying. If the object is not ours, she will say so and suggest where else to ask.

  • 02

    You get a plain answer

    Yes with conditions, no with a reason, or a request for one more piece of information. No holding reply that means nothing.

  • 03

    The paperwork starts

    Facilities report, insurance, crate specification, courier and a condition check on the day. The object travels only when all five are in place.

    • Or simply come and look.

    The galleries are open Tuesday to Sunday, ten to six. Thursday is free for everyone, which is the busiest and the best day to see who actually uses a museum.
