Commissions & appointments

Tell me what it is for, not what it looks like.

The studio takes six commissions at a time. Write with the occasion, the hand it is for, and anything it must not be. If you have a stone, say so; if you do not, I have a drawer.

Engraving is charged at £4 a letter after the first twelve. Chains and cords are extra; I make them in silver or buy in waxed cotton. Shipping within the UK is £12 signed-for; abroad, at cost.

Can you work from a photograph of an old piece?

Usually. Send clear images of the front, the back and any hallmark. If the piece is fragile, bring it in rather than posting it; I will not take responsibility for a courier.

Do you make engagement rings?

Yes, but not with a diamond. I set cabochons and old-cut stones. If you want a brilliant, I will send you to a colleague who does that better.

What about human hair?

I make the compartment. You place the hair yourself, in the studio, with tweezers. I do not cut, wash or set hair — it is not mine to handle.

How long is the wait?

Four to nine weeks from the approved drawing, depending on setting and engraving. Around November it is longer. I will tell you before you pay.

Can I visit without commissioning?

Yes. The studio is open by appointment Tuesday to Saturday. Come and see the stones; there is no obligation and the tea is real.
