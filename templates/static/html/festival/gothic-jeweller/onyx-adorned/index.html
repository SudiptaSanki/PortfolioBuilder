The house style

Three rules at this bench

I

The stone sets the scale

I do not cut a stone to fit a design. The design is drawn around the stone that exists, including its flaws. A garnet with a feather in it stays a garnet with a feather in it.

II

Nothing shines that shouldn't

Blackened silver, matte gold, cabochons rather than brilliants. A jewel for mourning should catch the light once, not send it back in pieces across a room.

III

The back is finished

Nobody sees the inside of a ring except the person wearing it. It is filed, sanded and polished anyway, because the hand knows what the eye does not.
