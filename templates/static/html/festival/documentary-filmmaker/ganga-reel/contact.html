Screening Screeners & invitations

Screeners go out on a password, not a public link.

Festivals, film clubs and classrooms are all welcome. Tell me the venue, the date and who the audience is, and I will send the right file — DCP, ProRes, or a watermarked link.

What travels

DCP (2K and 4K), ProRes 422 HQ, and a 720p watermarked screener. Subtitles in English are burned in for the screener only; the DCP carries a separate subtitle track.

Costs

Community screenings and classrooms pay nothing. Festivals pay a standard screening fee, which is waived for the first Indian showing of a new film. I do not charge for a Q&A, but I do ask for travel.

Not for

No uploads to streaming platforms, no derivative edits, no use of the footage in advertising. The people in these films agreed to a film, not to a campaign.
