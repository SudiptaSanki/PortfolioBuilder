Practice Consent, on camera

The first thing I shoot on any location is not the location. It is the conversation in which the people in it tell me what I may not film.

That conversation is written down, in a notebook, in the language it was held in. It has stopped me filming a funeral, a fight and a wedding, and each time the film was better for the gap.
