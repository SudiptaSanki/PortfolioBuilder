Six reels, one river, and a lot of waiting.

Each film below lists what it was shot on, how long it runs, and where it has been shown. Screeners are available for festivals and programmers on request; nothing is online in full.

Ghat

Four mornings on the same steps in Varanasi, cut without commentary. The camera never leaves the ghat; the light does all the arguing.

201972 min16 mm · B&W

The Ferryman's Arithmetic

A ferry operator counts his day out loud — fares, diesel, the mood of the river — until the counting becomes a kind of prayer.

202241 minDigital · Colour

Kal, Today

In the edit: three generations of a family who cremate on the same ghat, filmed across four years and two floods. First cut expected in spring.

202684 min16 mm + FX6

Low Water

A twenty-minute short about a boat stranded by a shifting channel, and the three men who argue about whose fault the river is.

202021 minDigital

Night Ferry

Eleven minutes, one crossing, no dialogue. The sound was recorded from the far bank, which is why the engine arrives before the boat does.

201811 min16 mm push

Six Feet of River

The first film: a measuring rod, a hydrologist, and the question of how much water a river needs before anyone will call it a river.

201718 minDigital · Student

Screenings and awards

Ghat — DMZ Docs, MIFF, Film Southasia. Special mention at Film Southasia, 2019. The Ferryman's Arithmetic — Rotterdam (Bright Future), 2023; Jury prize, Dharamshala, 2023. Kal, Today — in production, no festival submissions yet.
