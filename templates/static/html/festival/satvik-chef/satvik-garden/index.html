No onion. No garlic. No hurry. A garden thali in Mysuru.

Bed 02The thali

Six preparations, one plate.

A satvik thali is built in six parts: a grain, a dal, two vegetables, a chutney or rasam, and a sweet. The parts do not change; the vegetables do.

01 · Grain

Rice or rotli

Sona masuri rice, washed three times and cooked until the grains stand. In the north of the state, a soft rotli instead.

Local · always
02 · Dal

Toor or moong

Tempered with mustard, curry leaf and asafoetida. No onion, no garlic; the asafoetida does the work they would do.

Ghee · optional
03 · Palya

A dry vegetable

The season's bean, gourd or greens, cut small, cooked fast, finished with fresh coconut.

From the garden
04 · Kootu

Vegetable & dal

A softer dish: vegetable and dal cooked together, finished with pepper and a spoon of ghee.

Ghee · optional
05 · Chutney

Fresh, ground today

Coconut, coriander or tomato, ground fresh with green chilli, lime and a little jaggery.

Ground to order
06 · Sweet

Payasa

Jaggery, cardamom and reduced milk, or a coconut milk version for those who avoid dairy.

Jaggery · not sugar

        Bed 04The year

        The year, on four plates.

        Mysuru does not have four equal seasons, but the market has four moods. These are the plates that belong to each.

        Nov – Feb

        Cool & clear

        Beans, greens and the first sweet potato. Ghee comes back into the kitchen after the heat.

        avarekai · beans · greens · sweet potato · lime
        Mar – May

        Hot & dry

        Mango, raw banana and ash gourd. Buttermilk at every meal; the payasa gets lighter.

        raw mango · banana · ash gourd · cucumber · coconut
        Jun – Sep

        Monsoon

        Drumstick, yam and horse gram. The kitchen smells of pepper and the floor never quite dries.

        drumstick · yam · horse gram · ivy gourd · pepper
        Oct – Nov

        Second spring

        Pumpkin, snake gourd and the festival season. The sweet gets a little larger.

        pumpkin · snake gourd · beans · jaggery · cardamom
