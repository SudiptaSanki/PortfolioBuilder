Can I bring a group of ten

The bench is twelve stools and no separate room. For ten or more I close the counter on a Monday and cook the Friday menu; write at least three weeks ahead.

Is there anything for a vegan

Three plates: shiitake bao, corn and water chestnut potsticker, and the mushroom xiao long made with agar instead of pork stock. Say so in the form.

Do you teach the fold

Saturday at three, two people at a time, ninety minutes, ₹1,800 each. You leave with a box of what you folded and the recipe by weight, not by cup.

Where do I park

Nowhere useful. Come by metro to Central, walk down Rabindra Sarani and turn left at the blue awning. The lane is closed to cars after eight in the morning.

The lane at six

“You will smell the bamboo before you see the awning. Follow it past the flower sellers and the man with the key-cutting machine.”

The counter sits between a paper-goods shop and a shop that sells nothing but buttons. Both open later than us. If the blue awning is down, we are closed, and no amount of knocking will change it.
