Dumpling chef · third generation · Kolkata

Eighteen folds, one seam, nothing hidden. Mei-Lin Chen · 12 seats, Sun Yat Sen Street

I am Mei-Lin. My grandfather carried a dough scraper and a steamer lid out of Hankou in 1938 and set them down two lanes from here. I still fold the way he taught: skin in the left palm, filling pressed flat with the back of the spoon, thumb holding the seam while the right hand pleats toward it.

Fold 03

Four moves, in this order

Nothing here is a secret and nothing here is fast. The dough is made twice a day because it does not survive the afternoon. The filling is chopped by hand because a machine turns chive into water.

01 / Dough

Flour and water

00 flour, water at fifty-five per cent, a pinch of salt. Knead until smooth, rest forty minutes, then roll the skins thin at the edge and thicker at the heart.

40 min rest
02 / Filling

Chop, do not grind

Shoulder chopped to the size of rice, chive folded in at the end with sesame oil so it stays bright. Salt goes in last, ten minutes before folding.

20 min ahead
03 / Fold

Pleat to the thumb

Skin in the left palm, filling pressed flat. The right thumb and forefinger push a pleat toward the left thumb, and the seam closes itself as you go.

9 sec each
04 / Steam

Eight minutes, lid down

Water at a hard boil, dumplings not touching, one sheet of baking paper between tiers. Lift the lid once at the end, and only once.

8 min
