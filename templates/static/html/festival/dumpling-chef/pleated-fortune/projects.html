Ten plates, ten ways to close a seam

How to read this

Each plate is filed by its fold, not by its filling, because the fold decides how it cooks. A crescent sits on its belly and steams; a pouch stands and holds sauce; an ingot is pinched at both ends so it can be fried flat.
