지난 시즌 · Last season

Five looks, and the reason for each.

Colour is decided in daylight, at the window, with the client standing up. These are the five that came out of last season and the small problem each one solved.

Doljanchi · 14 months

First birthday, pale pink and a chewed goreum

The mother wanted pink and the grandmother wanted yellow. We put the yellow in the jeogori, where it catches the light, and kept the chima pale so the child would not disappear into the colour in photographs.

The goreum was made short and stitched double, because the first thing a fourteen-month-old does with a ribbon is put it in her mouth.

  • Pieces3: jeogori, chima, goreum
  • SilkGyeongju, spring dye lot
  • Fittings1, with the child present
  • Rental3 pieces, 2 days

Wedding · Samcheonggak

Red chima in a hall with warm bulbs

The bride's family wanted the traditional red. Under the hall's lamps the first silk turned orange in photographs, so we changed the dye lot rather than the colour: same red, one shade cooler, and it held on camera.

The hwarot went over it for the ceremony and came off for dinner. The jeogori underneath had to work alone, which is why it is jade — the one colour that reads as dark in a photograph without going black.

  • Pieces4: chima, jeogori, hwarot, goreum
  • SilkGyeongju, cool red lot
  • Fittings2, second with shoes
  • Made or rentedMade, 3 weeks

Palace day · Changdeokgung

Mist chima, cut for stairs

A full-length chima on a stone stair is a hazard. This one is cut 6 cm above the instep, pleated tighter so it does not catch the wind on the ridge, and worn with flat shoes that were part of the fitting.

The client walked the route in the atelier twice before the date. That is the whole fitting: walk, turn, sit on a low step, and see what the silk does.

  • Pieces3, and shoes
  • Chima lengthInstep minus 6 cm
  • Fittings1, with walking
  • RentalDay rental, back by 19:00

Daily · 2024

A hanbok for a Tuesday

A graphic designer who wanted to wear hanbok to work without arriving in costume. We narrowed the sleeve, shortened the jeogori by 3 cm, and used a matte silk that does not shine under office light.

The goreum is the only ornament. She has worn it eleven times and it has been cleaned twice.

  • Pieces2: jeogori, chima
  • SilkMatte, charcoal / grey
  • Fittings2, made to measure
  • Made2.1 million won

Family · 2025

Mother and daughter, matched but not identical

Matching sets are usually a mistake: the same colour on a 40-year-old and a 6-year-old reads as a costume. We swapped the proportions instead — plum chima with an ivory top for the mother, rose chima with an ivory top for the child, so the two look related across a room.

The child's set was cut from the hem pile of the mother's wedding chima, two years on. Nobody has noticed unless we say it.

  • Pieces6 across two people
  • SilkPlum, rose, ivory
  • Fittings1 together
  • Made or rentedMade, both
