Come with the shoes you will wear.

A fitting is forty minutes and it is free. Bring the shoes, and if the hanbok is for a child, bring the child — a set fitted to a sleeping baby will not survive the first hour of a party.

Request a fitting

I answer Tuesday and Thursday evenings. There is one fitting room and it is a paper screen, so tell me if you need privacy for changing.

No deposit for a fitting. Rental is paid on the day of collection.

Rental and made

A palace-day rental is 120,000 won for two pieces, 160,000 with a jacket, collected the evening before and returned by 19:00. A doljanchi set is 190,000 for three pieces. A wedding set rents from 480,000, or is made from 1.4 million.

Cleaning is included. If a piece comes back with a stain that will not lift, the cleaning cost is charged at the counter and I will tell you before you leave.

Before you come

What do I wear to a fitting?

A thin top and leggings. The chima wraps the chest, so a padded jacket will give a false measurement. Shoes matter more than anything else.

Can you fit someone pregnant?

Yes. The chima band sits above the bump and the jeogori is cut longer. Tell me the month at booking so I can pull the right bolts before you arrive.

Do you have plus sizes?

The chima is a wrapped skirt, so it fits a wide range. Jeogori are cut to measure beyond a 77. I keep extended shoulder patterns and will say honestly if a rental cannot be altered in time.

Is there hair and make-up?

No. I work with two people nearby for weddings and will give you their numbers. For a palace rental, most clients do their own hair and it looks better for it.

What if it rains?

Silk and rain do not mix. Palace rentals can be moved once at no cost if the forecast is bad. Bring an umbrella and wear the flat shoes.
