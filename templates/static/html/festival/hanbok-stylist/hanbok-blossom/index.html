맞춤 · The fitting

Three weeks, two visits

A rental hanbok still has to sit on your shoulders. The atelier measures eleven points, and the second fitting happens with the shoes you will wear.

  1. 1

    Measure

    Shoulder to shoulder, back length, chima length from waist to instep, and the width of the rib cage at three points. Twenty minutes.

  2. 2

    Choose

    Bolts come off the shelf and go against your face in daylight, never under the atelier's warm bulbs. This is where most first choices change.

  3. 3

    Fit

    Seven days before the date. The jeogori is pinned at the shoulder seam and the chima is marked at the hem; both are finished in two days.

  4. 4

    Dress on the day

    I come for weddings and doljanchi. For a palace rental the piece is collected the evening before and returned by 19:00.
