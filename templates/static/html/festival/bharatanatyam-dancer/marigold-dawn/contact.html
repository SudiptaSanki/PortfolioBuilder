Send the date. Get a programme back.

Every enquiry is answered in writing within three working days — with the date held for fourteen days, a proposed running order, and the fee taken from the published card, not invented for the occasion.

What to send

  1. The date and the city — December-season dates go first; holds last fourteen days.
  2. The venue — stage width and depth, and the floor surface. Barefoot dancing makes the floor a booking question, not a detail.
  3. The audience — how many, and who: connoisseurs, students, a company's guests. It changes the programme, not the standard.
  4. Orchestra or recorded — the live four add a flat ₹60,000 and about a month of diary coordination.
