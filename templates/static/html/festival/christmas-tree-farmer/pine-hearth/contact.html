Visit · Cabot, Vermont

Up the hill, past the mailbox

No booking is needed to cut a tree. Write ahead only for wreaths, a group, or if you want to be sure about a particular height.

Finding us

From Montpelier take Route 2 west to Cabot, turn up Creamery Road at the yellow house, and keep going past the mailbox with the pine cutout. The road turns to dirt after the second cattle guard. We plough it, but not early.

Payment
Cash or card at the barn. No deposit for a tree; wreaths over $50 can be reserved.

What to bring

Boots, gloves, and a tape measure if you have one. We lend saws, twine and a sled for the walk back. A blanket under the tree in the car saves the upholstery.

Dogs and children

Both welcome. The dog here is old and will ignore yours. Children should stay on this side of the pond in Plot 3 — the ice is never as thick as it looks in December.

If you cannot come up

We deliver within twenty miles of Cabot on Thursdays in December, for $25, and we will not deliver a tree taller than the door you describe. Wreaths go by post anywhere in the state.
