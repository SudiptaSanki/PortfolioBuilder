Send the date before the sketch.

The atelier is on the second floor above a shoe repair. There is no shopfront and no walk-in rail; everything begins with a note, a date and a conversation.

Write to the workroom

I answer on weekday mornings, before the machines start. If a production opens within six weeks, say so in the first line — the answer may still be yes, but the calendar decides.

This is a demonstration of the workroom book; nothing is sent anywhere.

  • What to send

    The date, the venue, the designer's sketches or reference, and the performer's rough height and build. Measurements are taken here, not mailed.

    Broad Street Line to Tasker–Morris, two blocks east. Bus 29 stops at Passyunk and Morris. Street parking is metered until 10 p.m.
Please noteMeasurements are taken in the workroom or on site, never by photograph. Nothing is cut until a toile is approved, and the toile is charged with the build.
