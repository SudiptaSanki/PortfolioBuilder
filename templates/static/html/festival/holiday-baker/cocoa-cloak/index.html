Kitchen instrument

Batch scaler

My cocoa cake, scaled by tin and by batch. Weights are in grams, the way a scale reads them. Bake times follow the tin, not the batch.

The bake
20 cm
1

Tin area scales the ingredients; batches multiply them. A 24 cm tin holds about a third more batter than a 20 cm one, and takes ten minutes longer.

IngredientWeight

Wheat flour210 g

Butter, soft180 g

Dark cocoa60 g

Sugar200 g

Eggs3

Sour cream120 g

Total batter1 kg

Bake plan · 1 tin

  1. 0:00Butter and sugar, 6 minutes until pale
  2. 0:06Eggs one at a time, then cocoa and flour
  3. 0:20Into the tin, level the top with a spoon
  4. 0:26Oven at 170 °C, 48 minutes
  5. 1:14Cool in the tin, then cloak with chocolate

One 20 cm tin, one batch. Cloak it while the chocolate is still moving.
