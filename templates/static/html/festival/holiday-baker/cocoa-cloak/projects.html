Orders

What went out of the kitchen this December.

Six orders from the 2025 book: what was baked, how many, and the one detail that made each of them particular.

6 of 6 orders shown

  • Hotel Sanders, Christmas lunch

    Hotel · 40 cakes · 24 December

    Forty small cocoa cloaks, all the same size, boxed in fours. The kitchen asked for them unsliced so they could plate them warm.

  • Møller family, Christmas Eve

    Cake · 1 stollen · collected 23 December

    A stollen with marzipan, wrapped in paper and tied with red string. The note asked for it to be left a week, which they did not manage.

  • Tins for a bookshop

    Tins · 120 tins · 12 December

    A hundred and twenty tins for a shop on Blågårdsgade, half gingerbread hearts and half pepper nuts, each with a small printed card.

  • Stollen class, six at the table

    Class · 6 people · 5 December

    Six people, one long table, four hours and a lot of butter. Two of them had never baked with yeast before and both took a stollen home.

  • A cake for a ninety-first birthday

    Cake · 1 large · 20 December

    Ordered by a daughter who asked for “the one from the TV advert, but better”. No candles; the family brought their own, from 1958.

  • Paper and string for a hotel

    Hotel · 80 boxes · 18 December

    Eighty boxed slices for a Christmas market stand, each wrapped in paper with a wax seal. The hotel wanted no plastic; the paper cost more, and they paid it.

“I have ordered the same cake for eleven Decembers. The year my mother was ill, Rosa brought it to the hospital herself, in paper, with the string still on.”

Ingrid MøllerChristmas Eve order, 2024
