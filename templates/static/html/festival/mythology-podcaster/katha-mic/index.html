    How an episode is made

    Record the telling. Then earn the right to explain it.

    1. 01Find the keeper

      The teller decides the version we record. I do not merge variants into one tidy plot.

    2. 02Sit with the tape

      Every recording is transcribed in its own language first, then translated with a named collaborator.

    3. 03Give the sources back

      Tellers receive the final cut, a transcript and half of the episode's licensing fee.
