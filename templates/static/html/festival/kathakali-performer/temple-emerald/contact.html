What the company brings

  • Nine performers and musicians, including two singers
  • Chenda, maddalam, chengila and elathalam
  • The full make-up kit and both crowns
  • The lamp, its oil and the curtain cloth

What the committee provides

  • A green room with a door and running water, five hours before curtain
  • A level stage, at least 6 × 5 metres, covered if the season is wet
  • Two meals and tea for nine people
  • Local transport between the station and the venue

A note on the stage

Kathakali needs less than most forms and more than people expect. There must be space to walk a full circle without touching the audience, and a place to hang a curtain at waist height. A proscenium works, but the night reads best in a courtyard where the lamp is the only light on the face.
