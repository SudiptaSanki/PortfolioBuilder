Questions the shop is asked

Short answers, so that the phone call can be about the person and not about the policy.

Can you deliver at an exact time?

I can promise a two-hour window, not a minute. The rider has eleven other bunches. If the moment is exact — a proposal, a surprise at a restaurant — telephone and we will arrange it separately.

Do you deliver outside Kolkata?

Not flowers. Cut roses do not travel well by courier, and a bunch that arrives tired is worse than a phone call. For another city, I can suggest a florist I trust in Delhi and one in Bengaluru.

What if nobody is home?

The rider will call. If there is a guard or a neighbour who can take the bucket, we will leave it with them and photograph it. If not, the bunch comes back and we try again the next day for half the delivery charge.

Can I choose the exact roses?

Yes, if they are in the bucket. The builder on the shop page shows what is in stock this week, and the shop will tell you honestly if a variety is finished for the day.

Do you do funeral flowers?

Yes, and quietly. White roses and tuberose, delivered without a card if the family prefers. Telephone rather than use the form.

Can I bring back the box?

Please. The cardboard carry-boxes with the reservoir are reused, and ₹40 comes off the next bunch. The buckets go back to the market with the van.
