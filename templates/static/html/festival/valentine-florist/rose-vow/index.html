03The
fourteenth

One day, in four parts

Valentine's Day is about eleven hundred bunches and a great deal of water. This is how it goes, every year.

04:00
Mullick Ghat

Two of us at the market with a van and a torch, checking head size and stem length by hand. The good reds are gone by five.

09:00
The shop

Stems cut at an angle, lower leaves stripped, buckets refilled. The front table becomes a tying bench and stays one until ten at night.

14:00
Cut-off

The last order we can promise for the evening. After that, whatever is in the bucket, and a honest answer about whether it is enough.

22:00
Doors shut

Floor swept, water changed for the morning, and the ledger balanced. Every year one order is a proposal, and every year I worry about it.
