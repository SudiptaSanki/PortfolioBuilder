Fitting, in the shop

Half an hour, both hands, no charge. Come with the outfit if you have it — daylight at the counter is the only honest light for a colour match. The shop is busy between four and seven, so mornings are better for a fitting.

Fitting, by post

We send a printed paper tape and a short video showing where to measure. You send back the number and three photographs of the hand with the tape on. If the fit is wrong, the first re-sizing is free; you pay the courier one way.

How to find the shop

Come from the Charminar side, past the Mecca Masjid, and turn into Laad Bazaar at the police outpost. Gali no. 4 is the fourth lane on the left; the shop has a green shutter, no signboard, and three rings painted on the door frame.

By metro, get off at Charminar and walk ten minutes. By car, park at the Charminar parking and walk in — the lane is too narrow for anything wider than a scooter, and the bangle sellers will not move their carts for you.

“If the shutter is half down, we are at the flame. Knock.”

The counter closes at half past eight, but a fitting that started at eight will finish. Nobody is turned away mid-measurement.
