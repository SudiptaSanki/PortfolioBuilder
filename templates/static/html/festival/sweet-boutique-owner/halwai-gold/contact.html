What things cost

A nine box works out between ₹340 and ₹760 depending on what you choose, plus ₹60 for the box and ribbon. A half tray of mixed mithai is ₹3,200–₹4,600. Gold leaf is ₹180 a box and must be asked for; on a hot day it will not survive a long car ride.

Delivery inside Delhi is ₹250 for up to 5 kg, ₹600 for 10 kg, and a measured rate beyond that. Wedding trays go by arrangement with a person who stays to set them.

Asked at the counter

How long does it keep?

Kaju katli and barfi, four days in a cool room. Laddoo, three. Sohan papdi and chikki, two weeks. Rose sandesh is a same-day sweet; do not buy it for tomorrow.

Can you deliver outside Delhi?

Not ourselves. We pack for a courier in a sealed box with a cold pack, and only for kaju katli, barfi and chikki. Laddoo and jamun do not travel well and I will say so.

Do you make sugar-free mithai?

Two: a kaju roll and a date-and-nut barfi, both made on Sundays. They are not sugar-free in the medical sense, so if someone is diabetic, ask their doctor about the portion first.

Is the silver leaf real silver?

Yes, edible silver from Jaipur. It has no taste and no nutritional value; it is there because that is how kaju katli is presented.

Can I choose the pieces myself?

At the counter, always. For forty boxes the kitchen will ask you for a fixed composition so the packing line stays honest — usually three sweets, divided evenly.
