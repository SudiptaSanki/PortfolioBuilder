The kitchen

Three things we will not change

The shop is small and the kitchen is smaller. What comes out of it is decided by four people, not a menu board.

  1. 01

    Ghee, not vanaspati

    Sixty kilos a week from a co-operative in Alwar. It costs nearly twice what the trade uses and it is the whole difference in a laddoo.

  2. 02

    Made in the morning

    Nothing is held overnight except sohan papdi and chikki. Kaju katli is cut at 7:00 and sold by 19:00 or given away.

  3. 03

    A box is packed in front of you

    You choose the pieces, we weigh them on the brass scale, and the box is tied at the counter. If you cannot see it, it does not go in.
