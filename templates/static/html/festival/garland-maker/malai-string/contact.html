The stall, plainly

Stall 14, East Masi Street flower market, Madurai. Open every morning from 4 a.m.; the stringing bench is at the back, past the marigold rows.

Email: selvi@malaistring.example

Order by 4 a.m. for the same morning's flowers — that is when I leave for the market, and the jasmine goes to the names already on the list.

Big orders (more than six hours of stringing) close at 8 p.m. the evening before. Weddings and temple festivals: a week ahead is comfortable, two days is possible, the same morning is a conversation.

Unsure of lengths? The planner on the home page works the whole order out from the rate card.
