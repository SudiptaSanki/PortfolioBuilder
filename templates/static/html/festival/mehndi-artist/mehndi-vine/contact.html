Before you write

Bridal sittings sit two days before the wedding. If your wedding is on a Saturday, we sit on Thursday. A Friday sitting gives you rust, not maroon — the planner on the home page shows the same rule.

Prices are the rate card, nothing added: palms ₹900 · wrist ₹1,800 · elbow bridal ₹3,600 · elbow + feet ₹4,800 · sangeet guests ₹250 each. Travel inside Jaipur is included; the paste, cones and aftercare oil are mine to bring.

A deposit of ₹500 holds a bridal date, adjusted against the sitting. Festival mornings (Teej, Eid, Karva Chauth) are first come, first drawn — write early.

Natural henna only. If anyone in the family has been promised “black” or “instant” henna by another artist, read the note on the about page before their sitting, not after.

The studio table
