## Drawings

The hand outline, the hand-map markers, the four-pass pattern diagram and
all paisley / peacock / vine / mandala motifs are original inline SVG drawn
for this template. No stock art, no copied design: the motifs are the
traditional vocabulary of the trade (paisley, peacock, vine, mandala),
drawn fresh here.

## Sound

None. The sound need-test fails for this trade on a portfolio page:
a mehndi sitting is quiet work, and no audio teaches anything here that
the four-pass diagram does not show better.
