Before you write

The service is small and deliberately so: a studio in Alwarpet, one coach, and a waiting list that moves slowly. I would rather tell you honestly that the wait is six weeks than take a referral I cannot hold.

If someone is at risk

Do not use this form. Call 104 for the Tamil Nadu health helpline, Tele-MANAS on 14416, or the nearest emergency department. I do not provide crisis cover.

How long is the wait?

Usually three to six weeks for an individual first session. School programmes are booked a term ahead. The February training has six places left at the time of writing.

Do you work with children under twelve?

Rarely, and only with a parent present and a counsellor involved. The form of the work suits adolescents and adults better; for younger children I will usually suggest a play therapist.

Can sessions happen at school?

Yes, for groups and for individual work where the school provides a quiet room and a safeguarding adult. I do not run individual sessions in a room with a window onto a corridor.

What if the person does not speak Tamil or English?

I work in Malayalam and some Hindi as well. For other languages we would need an interpreter, and I will say if that is not workable.

Do you write reports?

Only with the client's written consent, and only what is needed: attendance, the shape of the work, and any concern that should reach the referrer. No session content.
