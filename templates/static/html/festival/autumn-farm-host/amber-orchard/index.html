01The year
  1. Dec – Feb

    Pruning

    Every tree is opened up by hand so light reaches the lower branches. The pruned wood becomes the winter fire.

  2. Mar – Apr

    Blossom

    Nine days of white on the slope. We keep two hives at the bottom of Plot 2 and bring in a third from the village.

  3. May – Jul

    Thinning

    We take off two of every three young apples so the ones left grow heavy. It is the least romantic and most important work of the year.

  4. Aug – Nov

    Picking

    Six varieties, six weeks apart at the edges. Fruit is picked into cloth-lined crates and graded on the same evening.

02A stay

Three rooms, one long table

The house is the old packing house, extended twice. There are three rooms, each with a window onto the upper terrace, and one bathroom shared between two. Breakfast is at seven, because that is when the pickers eat; dinner is whatever the day produced.

We do not run a hotel. There is no television, the mobile signal is one bar on the veranda, and the nearest shop is a twenty-minute walk down. What there is: a wood stove, a small library of hill books, and permission to walk anywhere on the farm.
