Harvests

Eight crops, and what becomes of them

The orchard sells fruit, but a farm that sells only perfect fruit loses half its income to a hailstorm. Everything below is grown here, and everything in the second half is made in the shed from what the market did not take.

Crop register

Order of ripening, as it usually runs. A warm August moves everything two weeks earlier; a wet September moves the late varieties later.

Crop Ready Typical yield Leaves the farm as Note
Royal Delicious Weeks 35 – 38 9 – 12 tonnes Fresh boxes, mandi The backbone of the farm. Picks over three passes, not one.
Red Delicious Weeks 37 – 40 3 – 4 tonnes Fresh boxes Colours early, eats later. We pick on sugar, not on colour.
Golden Delicious Weeks 37 – 41 4 – 5 tonnes Fresh, cider, drying Bruises if you look at it. The best of the six in October.
Granny Smith Weeks 40 – 44 3 – 4 tonnes Fresh, chutney Holds its shape in cooking. The chutney is built around it.
Ambri Weeks 42 – 45 1 – 2 tonnes Fresh, gifts Kashmiri variety, fussy at this altitude. Worth the fuss.
Cox's Orange Pippin Weeks 37 – 39 600 kg Fresh, farm table Twenty-two trees on the home terrace. Never enough of it.
Plum, Santa Rosa Weeks 28 – 31 400 kg Fresh, jam Two rows along the lower wall. The wasps get a third.
Walnut, local seedling Weeks 38 – 41 250 kg shelled Shelled kernels, oil Nine old trees. Cracked by hand in the shed through November.

From the shed

Made from what the market did not take

Every jar and bottle is made here in small batches, between grading and dinner. We sell at the farm gate and at two shops in Mandi; we do not ship outside Himachal.

  • Cider vinegar

    ₹320 · 500 ml

    Pressed from windfalls in October and left on the skins until March. Sharp, cloudy, and nothing like the clear kind.

  • Granny Smith chutney

    ₹260 · 350 g

    Green apples, jaggery, mustard seed and a long afternoon of stirring. Sharp with pork, better with paratha.

  • Dried apple rings

    ₹180 · 150 g

    Sulphur-free, dried on the roof in the last hot week of October. Brown, chewy, and gone by December.

  • Walnut oil

    ₹640 · 250 ml

    Cold pressed from nine old trees, in the one week the press is free. Bitter fresh, rounder after a month.

  • Hill honey

    ₹480 · 500 g

    From two hives below Plot 2, plus a third that arrives each spring. Wildflower, dark, and it crystallises in January.

Buying fruit

Three ways to get the apples

  1. A box, delivered. Five kilos of mixed varieties, graded but not waxed, delivered in Mandi and Shimla for ₹700 including the box. Order in the first week of the variety you want.
  2. At the farm gate. Whatever is in the shed that day, at ₹90 a kilo. Bring your own bag; we have run out of paper ones every October since 2014.
  3. The Karsog mandi. Our lots go to the Tuesday and Friday markets through September and October. Ask for the Rana lot; the commission agent will know.
