The road

Karsog is 120 km from Shimla by the Narkanda road, three hours in a car and four by bus. From Mandi it is 80 km and slower.

The last two kilometres are a concrete farm track. Any car can do it in dry weather; in snow, call first and we will meet you at the road.

Bring

  • A torch, because the path to the lower terrace has no light
  • Warm layers even in May; the evenings drop to eight degrees
  • Shoes you do not mind losing to mud
  • Cash, since the nearest cash machine is in Karsog

Or just call

The landline rings in the kitchen. If nobody answers, we are outside, and we will call back in the evening.
