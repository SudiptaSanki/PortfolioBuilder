Tell me the building, the date and the room.

Most commissions are for a lobby, a shop window or a table. The size decides everything else, and the date decides whether it is possible at all: the workshop builds September to December and stops when the dough stops behaving.

The form checks the details and prepares an email. Nothing is sent from this page.

What survives a lobby

  • 8 mm walls, an interior wall over 30 cm, and a base board that cannot flex.
  • Isomalt windows are beautiful and will slump next to a radiator. Say where the heat is.
  • Six weeks is realistic in a cool lobby. Four weeks is realistic in a warm one.
  • Ship it upright, strapped, in a box bigger than the base by 10 cm on every side.

Money

Table cottages from 3,800 kr. Manors from 9,500 kr. Lobby pieces from 18,000 kr. The price includes the drawing, the dough, the icing, the base and the repair kit. Delivery is by arrangement and I would rather bring it myself.

If it collapses

Every build goes out with spare icing and a repair sheet. If a wall fails within the first week, message me a photograph and I will tell you what to do; if it is my joint that failed, I will come and fix it, or replace the build if the date allows.
