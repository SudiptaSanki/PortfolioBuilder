What to send

The pages around the plate, not the whole manuscript. A paragraph on the tone. Any reference you love, and one you hate — the second is more useful.

Questions

Do you work digitally?

No. Plates are drawn on paper, scanned at 600 dpi, and cleaned in Photoshop. Lettering and colour separations may be digital; the drawing is not.

Can I buy an original?

Sometimes. Originals for published work are held for a year after publication, then sold through the studio. Prices start at ₹30,000 for a small plate.

Do you take student work or collaborations?

I do one unpaid collaboration a year, usually with a writer who has never been published. Send the writing, not a proposal about the writing.

What about AI-generated reference?

I will not trace it, and I will not draw over it. If reference is generated, say so, and I will work from photographs or from life instead.
