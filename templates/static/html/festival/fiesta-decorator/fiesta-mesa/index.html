What I dress, and how far ahead to ask

  • Weddings and quinceañeras

    A single long table or a room of them, with a runner, a flower line down the centre and place settings that do not crowd the plates. I quote per table and bring my own linen.

    Six weeks' notice
    From MXN 5,800 per table

  • Ofrendas de Día de Muertos

    A table for the dead, built in two tiers with cempasúchil, pan de muerto, salt, water and the photographs the family brings. I build it on the 31st of October and take it down on the 3rd.

    Four weeks' notice
    From MXN 9,200 per ofrenda

  • Baptisms, birthdays and small rooms

    Shorter tables and tighter budgets. Papel picado, a runner, two flower clusters and the good glasses borrowed from my aunt's cupboard. The version most families actually book.

    Two weeks' notice
    From MXN 2,400 per table
