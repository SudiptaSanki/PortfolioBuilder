What I need from the venue

  • The table and chairs, already in place, or a loading door and thirty minutes.
  • One working socket within ten metres, for the iron and the lights.
  • Permission to tape a paper pattern to the floor during the dry run.
  • A place to put six buckets of flowers out of the sun.

Cancellation

Deposit back in full up to twenty-one days before the event. Inside twenty-one days the deposit becomes credit for a date within a year. If I cancel, you get the deposit back and the name of another decorator who can take the date.
