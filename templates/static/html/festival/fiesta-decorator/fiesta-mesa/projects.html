Boda Fuentes & Rojas

A courtyard wedding for 140 in Chautla, in June, with the ceremony at five and dinner at eight. Fourteen long tables of ten, dressed in jade cotton with rosa mexicano runners and a single line of marigolds down each centre. The couple asked for no flowers taller than a wine glass so the room could see across it.

The hard part was the light: the courtyard has a pergola and the sun drops fast at seven. We built two hours early and moved every candle twice.

Hacienda de Chautla, Puebla

Ofrenda for the Museo Amparo

A two-tier ofrenda in the museum courtyard for Día de Muertos, built with the museum's education team and a list of names from the staff's own families. The lower tier carries water, salt, fruit and pan de muerto; the upper tier carries photographs and the things the dead liked: cigarettes, a bottle of mezcal, a deck of cards, a toy car.

We use real cempasúchil, not paper, and we build the arch first so the flowers can be wired while they are still cold from the market. The museum keeps the ofrenda up for four days, which means the flowers have to be conditioned to open slowly.

Venue
Museo Amparo courtyard, Puebla
Years
2022, 2023, 2024, 2025
Structure
Two tiers, 2.4 m wide, 1.9 m tall
Cempasúchil
140 bunches, wired by hand
Candles
48, replaced daily
Build
Four people, eleven hours

Quinceañera Itzel

Twenty-two places in a family courtyard in Cholula, arranged in a U so the waltz could happen in the middle. The tables were dressed in cream linen with cempasúchil and a papel picado curtain cut with Itzel's name in the centre flag, which took Ana most of a day and is the detail everyone photographs.

We kept the centrepieces below the eyeline for the video, and put the candles on the outer edge of each table so nobody's sleeve went into a flame during the waltz. The courtyard had one socket, which ran the lights and nothing else.

Venue
Family courtyard, Cholula
Date
September 2025
Guests
22, three tables in a U
Papel picado
18 metres, name cut by hand
Cempasúchil
28 bunches
Build
Two people, five hours
