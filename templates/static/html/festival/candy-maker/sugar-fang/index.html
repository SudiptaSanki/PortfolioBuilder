Boil & batch

Sugar is a material with stages, and every stage has a temperature and a use. Choose what you want to make and how much, and this works out the ingredients and the exact point to pull the pot.

A candy thermometer that reads two degrees wrong will ruin a batch. Test yours in boiling water first: it should read 100°C at sea level, less if you are up a hill.

Marshmallow, 2 kg

Pull the syrup at 118°C, pour it into the bloomed gelatin in a thin stream with the mixer running, then whip for twelve minutes until it holds a ribbon.

June Vance, who cooks here

I started this in a rented kitchen in 2019 with one copper pot and a thermometer my mother used for jam. The first fangs were a disaster — too much citric acid, they took the skin off my tongue — and I sold them anyway, to a man who came back the next week and asked for four boxes.

We still make everything by hand because a machine cannot tell when the caramel has gone far enough. You can. It smells like the last thirty seconds before burning, and you have to be standing there for it.
