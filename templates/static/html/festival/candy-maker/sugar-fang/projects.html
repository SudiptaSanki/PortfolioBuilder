Allergen labels are on every box and are written by hand at the counter. If you have a serious allergy, telephone before you come: the kitchen handles peanuts, tree nuts, wheat, milk, egg and gelatine in the same room, and we will not pretend otherwise.

What changes with the year

September to October

Fangs every day, pumpkin fudge, and the skeleton. The counter is open seven days and the queue is the queue.

November to December

Caramels in a bigger box, peppermint bark made to order, and the mixed box becomes a gift box with a ribbon tied by whoever is free.

January

Closed. The pot is descaled, the labels are reprinted, and June does not answer the telephone.

February to August

Wednesday to Sunday, the standing eight, and whatever experiment survived the winter. Last year that was a smoked-salt honeycomb that people still ask for.

Wholesale, and the rules for it

We supply four shops and one restaurant, and that is the limit of what the pot can do. Minimum order is six kilos, mixed. We deliver within twenty miles of Salem on Mondays, and you will get a sheet with the batch numbers and the dates because your customers will ask.
