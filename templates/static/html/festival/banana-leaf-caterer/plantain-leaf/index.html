    SERVICE

    How a sadya runs

    The same order at every size. It is the only way to get hot rice in front of eight hundred people without a queue.

    1. 04.00 The kitchen lights Coconuts grated by hand until seven. Sambar and rasam on the long fire; the payasam last, so it is still warm at noon.
    2. 09.30 The leaves go down A banana leaf per guest, tip to the left, rinsed and dried. Salt, pickle and chips first, so nobody sits in front of an empty leaf.
    3. 10.15 Curries, in order Thoran, olan, avial, kootu, pachadi, kichadi, erissery, kalan, inji curry — each from its own vessel, one spoon per guest.
    4. 11.00 Rice and the pouring Two servings of rice. Parippu and ghee, then sambar, then rasam, then moru. The leaf is replaced between rice and payasam.
    5. 11.40 Payasam Palada first, ada second, both poured at the table. A second leaf, folded, for anyone who wants to take the banana home.
    6. 12.30 Clearing Leaves to the compost pit at the kitchen, vessels counted, leftovers weighed and recorded. The van leaves for the next venue by one.
