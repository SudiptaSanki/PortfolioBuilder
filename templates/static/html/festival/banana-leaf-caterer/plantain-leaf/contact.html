What the rate includes

Leaves, serving and clearing, all vessels, the serving crew and their travel within 30 km of Palakkad town. Beyond that, ₹18 per km both ways. The deposit is 30 per cent, refundable up to fourteen days before the date, half up to seven, none after.

Where we cook and serve

Palakkad district without a surcharge; Thrissur and Coimbatore within 75 km. Pollachi weddings are cooked at the second kitchen and travel no further than 40 km. We do not take sadyas north of Ottapalam or south of Chalakudy.

A tasting is not a sadya

We do not run tasting sessions. A sadya is a hundred vessels cooking at once and a single spoon on a plate proves nothing. Instead, on a booking above three hundred guests, we invite two people from the family to the kitchen on a sadya morning to eat from the serving vessels before the van leaves. That is the real thing, at the real scale.
