Leaves include the six per cent spare and the second leaf for the payasam. Guests are counted from the leaf tally at the venue, not from the booking, which is why the two never quite match.

The two that went wrong

In 2023 a van broke down on the Pollachi road with four hundred leaves and every vessel of sambar aboard. The second van was at another venue and could not be pulled. Guests sat for fifty minutes; the sambar arrived at 12.40 and was good, but the timing was ours to lose.

Since then the vans do not travel in convoy on the same road, the sambar is split across two vehicles on any sadya above six hundred, and the serving supervisor carries a list of what is on which van. It is a dull fix. It has held for three seasons.

The rain at Ottapalam

The temple annadanam in June 2026 was served in rain that came an hour early. The pandal held, but the leaf line flooded at the entrance and two counters had to be moved inside the mandapam mid-service. Nobody went without; four hundred people ate standing.

What changed: the kitchen now carries forty metres of extra tarpaulin and a portable drain mat on every monsoon booking, whether the forecast asks for it or not. The cost is ₹1,800 a job and it is not itemised.
