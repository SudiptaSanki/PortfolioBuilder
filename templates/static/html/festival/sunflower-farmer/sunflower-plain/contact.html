Come for the light, not for the flowers.

The gate is open for two weeks in the year, from six in the morning until ten. Keep to the cut paths. A picked head is a lost head, and the plant will not make seed from it.

Tell us you are coming

No booking is needed for a family or a group under twelve. This is so we know who to expect, and so that a group of forty does not arrive at a gate built for a tractor.

Getting here

Nandi Road, Chikkaballapur 562101
Karnataka

From Bengaluru: the airport road to Devanahalli, then the highway north to Chikkaballapur, then four kilometres on the Nandi road. After the tank, on the left, the yellow gate.

By bus: any Chikkaballapur-bound bus from Majestic, then an auto from the stand — ask for the sunflower farm past the tank, and the driver will know it in December.

What to bring

Shoes you do not mind ruining — the red soil does not come out. Water. A hat by eight o'clock. Cash, if you want oil, because the card machine depends on a signal that depends on the weather.
