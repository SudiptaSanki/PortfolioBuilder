Prints and walks · spring 2026

One print, or one morning

Prints are made to order and take two weeks. Walks run for eleven days in spring only, and I take two people at a time because the path is narrow and the point is quiet.

The practical part

I answer on Sunday evenings, Kyoto time (UTC+9). If you are writing from Europe or America, expect the reply while you sleep.

Email
mio@hanami-film.jp
Studio
Kamigyō, Kyoto. Printing room visits by appointment, and only when the fan is working.
Prints
Editions of twelve, fibre paper, selenium toned. Two weeks from order. Shipped rolled in a tube, or flat between boards inside Japan.
Walks
28 March – 8 April 2027. Two people, three hours, from 05:45. ¥12,000 per person, tea included.
Payment
Bank transfer in Japan, or Wise from abroad. Prints are paid before they are made; walks on the morning.

If it rains

Rain is good for petals on stone and bad for cameras. If the forecast is heavy, I will write the night before and we will move the walk to the next dry morning, or refund it.

If the bloom is early

It usually is. I watch the Kyoto records from 1 March and write to everyone booked if the peak shifts by more than two days. No one has ever complained about the trees.
