Kyoto · the last week of March

For eleven days the city is the colour of the inside of a shell.

I photograph that week on film, mostly at dawn, mostly within walking distance of my flat in Kamigyō. The tree does the work. I stand where the light is still blue and wait for the first petals to move.

Fourteen springs in Kyoto. Thirty-eight rolls a season, developed at a lab in Fushimi, printed by hand at home.
