Commission · 2025

The stairwell lantern, Zamalek

A hotel on the Nile asked for one body to fill a stairwell that had been dark since the building was converted. At 1.4 m it needed a crane to hang and nine bulbs to light evenly; the glass was cut in bands of amber and deep red so the stair reads warm from the third floor. It is cleaned twice a year by the same electrician.

Mosque commission · 2024

Al-Aqmar entrance pair

The pair at the entrance of a small mosque near Al-Aqmar: green glass with cream centres, because the committee asked for the colour of the tilework rather than the usual red. Eight sides, four tiers, mounted on brass brackets cut to the stone. The bodies were lifted into place with a rope and two men on the roof.

Film props · 2023

For a set dressed as 1943

A production company needed four lanterns that looked forty years older than they were. We made them with an unpainted seam, a slightly wrong door and glass cut with a wider blade, then aged the brass with vinegar and salt. The director asked for one to flicker; we wired a dimmer into the base and hid it in a matchbox nailed under the table.

4 medium
Finish

Coptic Christmas · 2025

Five for a doorway in Fustat

In December the same bench makes small red lanterns for Coptic Christmas. This set of five went to a church doorway in Fustat: simple four-sided bodies, no door, a candle cup inside, hung at eye height so the children can reach them. The priest asked that they be easy to replace, so we made six.

Repair · 2026

A 1974 body, re-glazed

A customer brought a fanous her father bought the year she was born. Two panes were gone, the dome was dented, and the red she remembered was no longer made. We matched the old glass from a box of offcuts kept since the 1980s and left the dent, because she asked us to. The new pane is marked on the inside with the year.
