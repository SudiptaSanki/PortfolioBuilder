Questions from the counter

What customers ask before they reach for a wallet.

When should I order for Ramadan?

By the middle of Sha'ban for small and medium bodies; a month earlier for large or commissioned pieces. The shop stops taking new season orders ten days before the month, because the last days are for finishing what is already on the bench.

Do the lanterns come with a bulb or a candle?

Small bodies come with a candle cup and one beeswax candle. Medium and large are wired for an E14 bulb with a 1.5 m cloth cable and a switch on the cord; a bulb is included. If you want a candle socket in a large body, say so — it can be done, but the light will be uneven.

Can the glass be replaced if it breaks?

Yes, and it usually can be matched. We keep offcuts from every batch and a box of older cathedral glass going back to the 1980s. Send the body or bring it to the shop; a single pane takes two to ten days depending on the colour.

Is the finish safe outdoors?

Aged and polished brass are fine under a balcony or an awning. Painted tin is the better choice for a doorway that takes rain, though the paint will dull after two or three winters. None of the finishes are suitable for full exposure to salt air.

Can I visit the workshop?

Yes, Saturday to Thursday, ten to seven. The stairs are narrow and the bench is not a museum, so keep children at arm's length from the shears. During Ramadan we work until midnight and visitors are welcome until ten.

Do you export to museums?

We have sent bodies to two museum shops in Europe, crated and declared as decorative metalwork. The paperwork takes about three weeks; the crate is made by the carpenter two doors down and is charged at cost.

The bench is busiest in Sha'ban.
