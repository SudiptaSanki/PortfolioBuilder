If you are inviting me

A baithak needs forty people and a floor to sit on, not a stage. If the room is bigger, I will still sing unamplified unless it is over two hundred, and then I need one good microphone and someone who knows how to place it.

  • Fee₹25,000 for a baithak in Maharashtra, plus travel outside it. No fee for a student-run or temple concert; ask.
  • What I bringMy own tanpura and a spare electronic one. I need a tabla player; I work with Nikhil Joshi, and will happily work with yours if he is good.
  • SoundOne condenser on the voice at about a metre, one on the tanpura. No reverb, no compression on the voice. Forty minutes to check the room.
  • LengthNinety minutes with a break, or seventy without. I will not sing for three hours and I will not pretend to be sorry about it.

If you want to learn

Six private students, one hour a week, in Kothrud, on weekday mornings before nine. I do not teach over video anymore; I tried it for eighteen months and something important did not survive the screen.

  • Before the first lessonYou need a tanpura, a quiet hour a day, and the patience to sing one note for a month. A harmonium is useful after the first year, not before.
  • Fee₹4,000 a month, paid at the start of the month. Two students a year pay nothing; tell me if that is you and why.
  • Waiting listThere are four people on it and places open in ones. If you write in November you are realistically looking at the following monsoon.
