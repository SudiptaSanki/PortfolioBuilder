How a commission runs

  1. A visit to the site, or the yard, with the committee's convenor.
  2. Three drawings and a written quantity sheet within three weeks.
  3. A 30 per cent advance holds the season slot; the frame is cut in June.
  4. Site build starts eleven days before Puja, or fourteen for a site over 70 ft.
  5. Handover with the electrical certificate, the fire-retardant invoice and a walk-through of the exits.

What it costs

Frame, cloth, panels, light and labour run between ₹1,050 and ₹1,650 a square foot of frontage times height, depending on the material. A 60 × 24 ft shola pandal is around ₹18 lakh; the same footprint in terracotta is around ₹21 lakh. Fire retardant, the electrical certificate and the idol are separate.

The season

We take twelve large commissions and about twenty small mandaps. Bookings close in March because the yard can only cut and stand up so many frames before the monsoon. A committee that comes to us in July is usually asking us to repair someone else's work, and we will say so.
