Selected work · 2019 to 2026

Six pandals, and what each one taught the yard.

An archive is not a portfolio of photographs. What matters to a committee is the frame, the material, the number of hands and the week it was finished. Those are the facts below.

Nabapally Sarbojanin

Baranagar · 2026 · Terracotta

A 74-foot frontage built as three bays so that the crowd moves through the centre and past the two side shrines instead of stopping at the steps. The tile wall is 4,200 baked panels, each wired to a cane backing so that a broken tile can be swapped in five minutes. The lesson was the bays: a wide pandal does not need a wide crowd in front of it.

Size 74 × 48 × 30 ftBuild 26 daysHands 41Material terracotta, cane

Dum Dum Aparna Sangha

Dum Dum · 2025 · Jute and mirror

The committee wanted a pandal that could be seen from the main road, so we went low and wide instead of tall. Four bands of jute with 1,900 mirror dots, and the whole skin replaced in three days after a storm took the north corner. Cheap to repair was the brief; it became the design.

Size 86 × 56 × 22 ftBuild 21 daysHands 34

Sinthee Milan Sangha

Sinthee · 2025 · Bamboo and shola

A neighbourhood mandap of 52 feet, built in nineteen days with a team of twenty-four. The scallop count on the shola panels is the detail the committee still mentions: four hundred and ten scallops, cut by hand in the loft above the yard. The frame came back to us in 2026 and is being re-dressed.

Size 52 × 34 × 26 ftBuild 19 daysHands 24

Belgharia Tarun Dal

Belgharia · 2024 · Paper and cane

The year the paper ran short. We built three scalloped layers instead of five, and the committee's younger members spent a Sunday cutting the fourth. It rained on Saptami and the outer layer was replaced overnight, which is the argument for paper: it is the only skin a yard can remake in a night.

Size 64 × 42 × 28 ftBuild 22 daysHands 30

Kamarhati Ananda Sangha

Kamarhati · 2024 · Neon LED

Black cloth over a cane frame and 640 metres of LED, all of it dimmable in four zones. On the busiest evening the committee turned the outline down to a third and the crowd slowed instead of pushing. Since then we specify dimmers on every light job, even when the committee has not asked.

Size 58 × 38 × 27 ftBuild 17 daysHands 26

Sodepur Utsav Committee

Sodepur · 2023 · Terracotta, two shrines

The first time we built a pandal with the pratima in a side shrine and the centre kept open for the crowd. It changes everything: the queue does not stop in front of the idol, and the committee's volunteers stop arguing about the rope line. We have drawn the bays that way ever since.

Size 70 × 46 × 29 ftBuild 24 daysHands 37Award Baranagar municipal, 2024
