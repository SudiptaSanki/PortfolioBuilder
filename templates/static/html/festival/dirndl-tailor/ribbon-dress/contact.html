FragenBefore the fitting

What people ask before they come

Six answers, so that the first appointment can be about the dirndl and not about the process.

Do I need to know what I want?

No. Most people arrive with a colour and a date. Bring a photograph of something you like, and one of something you hate — the second is more useful.

Can you copy a dirndl I already own?

Yes, if it fits or if I can see how it was cut. I will trace the pattern and adjust it rather than unpicking the original, unless the original is the only record.

How much does it cost?

Cotton or linen from €680, silk from €1,450, blouse from €180, apron from €140. Cloth, lining, ribbon and fittings are included. The full list is on the atelier page.

Can I lose weight before the wedding?

You can, and the side seams are cut with 3 cm to allow for it. Tell me at the first fitting and we will leave the seam wider on purpose.

Do you make lederhosen?

No. Leather is a different trade and there is a good maker two streets away; I will send you to him.

Is the blouse included?

No. A blouse is a separate garment and a separate fitting, because it changes the neckline of the bodice. From €180, in cotton or silk.

Bring the shoes, and wear the bra

Both change the length and the line more than a centimetre on a tape measure. The kettle will be on.
