Commission bench

Every ring starts as a drawing.

What the bench is saying

A 6 mm band with a half-moon crest is the workshop's most ordered ring, and the easiest to wear. The silver is 8.4 g; the crescent is cut from the same plate as the band, so there is no solder line at the horn.

  • Band wall 1.4 mm — enough for daily wear
  • Crest plate 1.2 mm — supports the stone without a bezel cup
  • Assay and hallmark included

Allow three weeks in silver, six in gold or platinum. Sizing after the fact is possible within one size; past that the crest has to be reset.

The method

Forged, not printed.

I buy sterling sheet and wire from the assay office in Karaköy, forge the band over a mandrel, cut the crescent with a jeweller's saw and set the stone by hand. A ring passes through eleven steps and three annealings before it is polished. Nothing is cast unless it has to be, and nothing leaves without a hallmark.

Inside the workshop
01
Drawing at 1:1 on graph paper, with the ring size and the stone measured.
02
Band forged over a steel mandrel, soldered with hard silver solder.
03
Crescent cut from sheet with a 0.3 mm saw blade, filed and burnished.
04
Setting, then three annealings, then a final polish on the wheel.
05
Assay and hallmark, then a week on the bench to settle before it is boxed.
