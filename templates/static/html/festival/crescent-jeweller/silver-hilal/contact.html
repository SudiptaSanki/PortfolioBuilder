Visit & commission · Zincirli Han

Come up the stairs; the door does not close.

The workshop is open on weekdays and Saturday morning, and you do not need an appointment to look at the drawings. If you want a ring made, come with a finger to measure and a stone if you have one. The bench on the front page will give you the price before you climb the stairs.

The workshop

Atölye 12, Zincirli Han, Kapalıçarşı. Enter from the gate on Kalpakçılar Caddesi, cross the courtyard, first floor, the door with the brass crescent. Weekdays 10:00–18:00, Saturday 10:00–14:00, closed Sunday.

Sizing

Measured on the brass gauge in a minute. If you are not in Istanbul, measure a ring that fits across the inside in millimetres and divide by 3.1416; add one size for a band over 8 mm.

Lead times

Three weeks in silver, six in gold or platinum. Wedding season, May to September, adds a fortnight. A drawing comes first and nothing is cut until you approve it.

Shipping

Insured courier worldwide, €25 to Europe, €40 elsewhere, at cost. Silver goes in a cotton pouch inside a wooden box; gold and platinum are sent with the assay certificate.

Payment

A third with the order, the balance on delivery. Bank transfer in euros or lira, or cash in the han. The deposit covers the metal, which is why it is not refundable once the sheet is cut.
