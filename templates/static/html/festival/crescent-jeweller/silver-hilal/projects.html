What is not in the catalogue

Engagement rings, signet rings and anything with a customer's own stone. Those are drawn individually, because a stone has its own proportions and a setting that ignores them is a setting that will fail. Bring the stone to the han, or send it by insured post, and I will draw for it without charge.

Repairs are taken for pieces made here, and for other people's silver when the repair is simple. I will not re-set a stone into a mounting I have not seen.

A note on price

The silver price moves; the labour does not.

A piece is priced from the grams, the hours and the stone, and the grams follow the London fix. When silver moves, the price moves with it, which is why the catalogue is quoted weekly and why a quote is held for fourteen days. The labour is the same whether silver is cheap or dear, and it is the larger part of the price.
