Before you come

Wear something loose and warm; the room is kept cool. Eat lightly an hour before. Bring nothing and no one — this hour is intentionally unaccompanied, unless you are a carer.

Can I bring a friend or a carer?

Yes, if you need one. A carer may sit in the room. For a general companion, it is better to come alone; the session is quiet and their presence changes it.

Is it safe during pregnancy?

Generally yes after the first trimester, with your doctor's agreement. I do not place bowls on the abdomen. Tell me in the form and we will plan the placement together.

What if I fall asleep?

That is common and fine. I let the bowls finish and sit until you wake; you will not be rushed out.

Do you record anything?

No audio, no video, no photographs. I keep a written session log with initials, dates and what was placed, which you may read at any time.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancel or move with more than 24 hours' notice at no cost. Inside 24 hours I ask for half the fee, because the hour cannot be re-offered. Illness is always an exception.
