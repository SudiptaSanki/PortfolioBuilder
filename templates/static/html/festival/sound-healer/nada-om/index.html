01 — The overtone room

Seven bowls, one long decay

Each bowl is struck once and left to ring. The instrument is not the bowl; it is the space between the strike and the silence. Choose a bowl, set a length, then strike when you are ready — nothing plays before that.

Sound is off. It begins only when you strike.

02 — How an hour goes

Nothing is asked of you but arrival

  1. i

    The conversation

    Ten minutes, sitting. What brought you, what you would like to leave with, and whether sound is the right instrument for it. Some people should be sent to a doctor first, and I say so.

  2. ii

    The listening

    You lie down, fully clothed, on a cotton mat. Bowls are placed on and beside the body and struck in a sequence built for your state, not from a recording. Silence is part of the score.

  3. iii

    The landing

    Fifteen minutes of quiet and water, then a short note on what to expect for the next two nights — vivid dreams and heavy sleep are common and not a sign of anything wrong.
