A short film about the last houseboat carpenter in Srinagar, scored for solo santoor and the sound of the lake. The music was recorded before the film was cut, which is why it breathes the way it does.

Film festivals2024

How to listen. These recordings were made in rooms, not studios. Headphones will give you the bridge noise, the breath and the lake; speakers will give you the body of the instrument. Both are correct. If you only have ninety seconds, listen to the last minute of Bhairavi on Chinar, where the santoor is allowed to ring until it stops by itself.
