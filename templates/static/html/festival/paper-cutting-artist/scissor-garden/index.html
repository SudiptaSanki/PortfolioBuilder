01 — What the sheet is

A ceremony held
together by cuts.

Sanjhi began in the temple courtyards of Braj, and it has always been an art of absence. You begin with a full sheet of handmade paper and remove everything that is not the picture. What remains is a stencil so fine that a whole courtyard can be drawn through it.

On the night of the festival the stencil is floated on the temple tank. Coloured powder is sifted through the gaps, the paper is lifted away, and the image stays on the water for an hour — a rangoli the water itself holds. In the older form, a lamp is set behind the wet sheet and the figure glows through the lace.

My family has cut these sheets since my grandfather set up a stall near the Potra Kund in 1948. He cut Krishna’s flute from a folded square with a pair of tailor’s scissors and sold it for four annas. The scissors are on the wall behind me. They still cut.

The cut

One continuous line is worth ten short ones. If the blade lifts, the eye feels it.

The paper

Handmade rag paper, 90 gsm, sized with starch. Mill paper tears along the grain and ruins a week’s work.

02 — Cut studio

Fold. Cut. Unfold.

A sanjhi sheet is never drawn first. It is folded into a wedge, cut once, and opened into a symmetry. Try it below: choose the fold, then click the paper to remove a piece. Everything you cut is repeated around the circle, exactly as it would be under the blade.

04 — Teaching

The fold is easy.
The patience is not.

On the first Sunday of every month I open the workshop to six people. You will leave with one small sheet, one pair of scissors, and a fold you can repeat at home. No experience needed; left-handed cutters welcome, we work on both sides of the table.

Ask about the next session
  1. 01

    Fold

    Halve, halve, halve again until the wedge is eight layers. Crease with the thumb, never the nail.

  2. 02

    Hold

    Scissors in the right hand, paper turned by the left. The blade stays still; the sheet does the travelling.

  3. 03

    Cut inward

    Open the paper at the rim, close the cut before you reach the centre. The last bridge holds the sheet together.

  4. 04

    Open

    Unfold slowly, on a clean cloth. This is the only part that cannot be taught.
