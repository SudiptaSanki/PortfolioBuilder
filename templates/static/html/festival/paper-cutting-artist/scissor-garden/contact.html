Before you write

Questions that come up

How long does a commission take?

A small sheet is usually ready in three weeks. A temple or wedding sheet of 45–60 cm takes six to eight weeks, and joined sheets longer, because the paper has to rest between each stage. If your date is fixed, tell me at the start; I would rather decline than rush the cutting.

Can you copy a family stencil?

Yes, and it is my favourite kind of work. Send a clear photograph of the old sheet against a plain background, with a ruler in the frame. If the original is torn I can often still read the fold from the surviving quarter and reconstruct it.

Do you cut names or lettering?

Devanagari, yes, and Latin letters in a simpler cut. Lettering cannot be folded, so it is cut flat and joined into the sheet; the joint is made at a border, never through the middle of a word.

Will the sheet survive being floated?

A properly sized sheet will hold colour for years if it is dried flat and kept away from damp. I test every commission sheet on water once before it leaves the workshop. If it fails the test, I cut it again.

Can I visit without commissioning anything?

Yes. Come on a weekday morning, bring nothing, and stay for a cup of tea. If the table is free I will show you the fold. Please write first so I know to keep the hour.
