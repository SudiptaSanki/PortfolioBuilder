The seven

What goes on the cloth

Every item has a job beyond decoration. If I cannot explain the job at the table, it does not come out of the crate.

  1. 01

    Sabzeh sprouted wheat

    Grown from 120 g of wheat on wet cotton, started 18 days before the equinox and turned toward the window every morning. Rebirth, and the only item on the table that will not keep.

  2. 02

    Samanu wheat-germ pudding

    Cooked for six hours with a copper paddle, no sugar added beyond the wheat's own. Sweetness earned by patience. I make it in a 4 kg batch and give the extra to neighbours.

  3. 03

    Senjed dried oleaster

    Picked from a tree in Karaj in late summer, still on the branch when it can be. Small, dusty orange, faintly date-like. Love, and the only item guests always ask about.

  4. 04

    Seer garlic

    One whole bulb, unpeeled, from Hamedan. Medicine before it is ornament. I set it on a small saucer so the papery skin does not mark the cloth.

  5. 05

    Seeb apple

    Two red apples, stem up, polished with a cloth and not with oil. Beauty and health, and the only thing on the table a child is allowed to take before the salutation.

  6. 06

    Serkeh vinegar

    Aged grape vinegar, at least four years, in a glass bottle with the year written on the label in pencil. Patience, age, and the sharp note that keeps the sweets honest.

  7. 07

    Somaq sumac

    Ground sumac from the Zagros foothills, heaped in a shallow dish and drawn into a small cone with a card. Sunrise, and the colour the whole table is built around.

    How a table gets built

    Three weeks, three heights, one morning

    1. Week −3

      Wheat in the dark

      Wheat goes onto wet cotton in a shallow dish and stays in the cool pantry for four days, then to the north window. By day eighteen it is 22 cm and must be turned twice a day or it leans.

    2. Week −1

      The cloth and the copper

      Linen is washed and pressed, copper is polished with lemon and salt, and every dish is chosen for the height it will make. A table with seven plates at one level is a table with no music in it.

    3. Equinox morning

      Dressing, in order

      The cloth first, then the mirror with its back to the wall, then the seven from heaviest to lightest. The last thing lit is the candles, and the first thing removed is the goldfish bowl.
