What a table costs

A haft-sin starts at 18 million toman for a shelf, 34 million for a dining table with the full seven and the copper, and is quoted per room for events. The number includes the cloth, the dishes, the sprouts grown here and the dressing on the day; it does not include a mirror that belongs to your family.

If the budget is smaller, say so. Half my tables are built around one good piece the client already owns, and the rest is borrowed from the studio.

Asked every Esfand

Can I book after 3 Esfand?

Yes, but not with sprouts grown for you. After that date I use shop-grown sabzeh, which is shorter and leans. I will say which one you are getting before you pay.

Do you lend the copper and the cloth?

The seven dishes, the copper and the linen are studio stock, included in the price. The mirror, the candles and anything sentimental are yours to keep afterwards.

Can the table be vegetarian, or nut-free?

The seven have no meat and no nuts. Senjed is a dried fruit, not a nut, but I keep it in a sealed dish if anyone at the table is unsure.

What happens on Sizdah Bedar?

I collect the sabzeh and the cloth on the 12th or 13th, whichever suits the house. Sprouts go to a friend's garden in Lavasan; the cloth is washed and stored.

Do you work outside Tehran?

Kashan, Isfahan and the Alborz towns, with travel and one night's stay added. Anything further needs a conversation about who carries the mirror.

If you are not sure what you have, send a photograph.

A phone picture of the shelf with a shoe or a book in it for scale is more useful than a floor plan. I answer with two layout options and the height of each.
