Commissions

Tell me the site. The array comes after the wind.

Anything over a thousand diyas needs eight weeks, because the clay is thrown and dried before it is fired. Smaller works can be made in three. Write with the place, the date and the number of people you expect.

Lead times

  • Under 300 diyas — three weeks
  • 300 to 1,000 — five weeks
  • 1,000 to 2,500 — eight weeks
  • Over 2,500 — ten weeks and a site visit

What I need

The ground surface, the direction of the prevailing evening wind, and whether the public will walk through the work or only around it. Photographs of the site at the same hour are more useful than a brief.

Costs

Quoted per work, not per diya: the clay, oil, wicks, shelter, labour, and the fire watch are all in the number. Public festivals are quoted at cost plus a studio fee; commercial and private commissions carry the full rate.
