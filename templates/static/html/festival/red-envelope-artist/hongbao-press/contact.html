A commission brief

If the envelopes are needed inside two weeks, say so in the first line — that decides whether I can take it at all.

A die is cut only after the proof is approved. No deposit before that.

What a run costs

NT$ 38 a piece at 500; NT$ 34 at 1,000; NT$ 30 at 3,000 and NT$ 26 from 10,000. A new die is NT$ 6,800, cut once and kept on the wall — a repeat order two years later pays only for paper and foil.

Folding, gluing and counting are included in the piece price. Shipping in Taiwan is NT$ 900 a carton, about 5,000 envelopes; overseas goes by the client's courier and I pack it flat.

Before you write

Can I use my own artwork?

Yes, as vector, with strokes at 0.3 pt or heavier. I will redraw hairline detail if needed, and I will tell you if a design cannot hold at this size.

What if I need fewer than 500?

I print a short run of 200 for a private order at a higher piece price, but the die still costs the same. For a wedding, the smaller run is usually the right one.

Can the envelope be a different colour?

Red is dyed through at 120 gsm. I also keep a cream and a deep blue stock for private editions; anything else is a paper order with a four-week lead time.

Do you print names inside each envelope?

Only as a second pass, and only in batches of a hundred under one name. Individually named envelopes are hand-stamped here, at NT$ 12 each.

Can I visit before commissioning?

Yes — Thursday afternoon, when the press is running. Bring the artwork on paper if you have it; a screen at 100% is not the same as a sheet in your hand.
