How a plate becomes a box

Three passes, no shortcuts

Letterpress is pressure, not printing. The foil sits between a heated die and the paper, and what makes the line sharp is the platen closing flat — a half-second too long and the paper embosses through.

  1. 01

    Cut

    Magnesium die, etched from the vector proof and mounted on a maple block. Two days at the cutter in Sanchong.

  2. 02

    Make ready

    Packing under the platen until the impression is even left to right. This is the slow hour, and it is the hour that decides the edition.

  3. 03

    Run

    Foil changed every 400 sheets, paper fed by hand. A thousand envelopes is ten lifts and one long afternoon.
