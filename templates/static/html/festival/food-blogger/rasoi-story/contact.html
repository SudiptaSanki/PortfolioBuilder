Letter received

Thank you for writing. If this is a correction, I will test it against the recipe before I change anything — that takes a week or two, and you will hear either way.

If the pot is on the stove right now, write “today” in the subject of the reply and I will answer before the evening.

How to write a useful letter

Tell me which recipe number, what you changed, and what happened. “The dal was sour” is a start; “I used jarred tamarind paste, 30 g, and it was sour” is a letter I can answer.

About the letter

One recipe and one note, on Thursday, to about seven thousand people. No photographs, no discount codes, no “five things you must cook this week”. You can leave at any time and I will not write to ask why.

The kitchen is on the second floor with no lift. If you are coming to collect a zine, come between ten and twelve on a Sunday and ring twice.
