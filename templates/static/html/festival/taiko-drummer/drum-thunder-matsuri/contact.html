What to send

  • The date — or the month, if the programme is not fixed yet.
  • The venue and city — and whether the stage is outdoors.
  • Stage size, if you know it: the full set needs 8 × 5 m, the half set 5 × 4 m.
  • Audience size and setting — festival field, hall, school gym.
  • Any noise limit the venue works to, in dB if they give you one.

The short version of the terms

30% deposit confirms a date. Festival season books four months ahead. Outdoor quotes include the rain plan in writing. Full terms are on the drums & player page.
