The calendar · 2027

Five weeks
that are not a course.

Each retreat has a shape, not a syllabus. We keep the same timings every day, add one afternoon of teaching, and leave the rest alone. You can join any of them without having done the one before.

8 – 14 February · six nights

Winter silence

The coldest, clearest week of the year. Silence is held from the evening bell until after breakfast, and again from eight at night. There is one teaching each afternoon on sitting with discomfort, and no conversation is required at any meal.

For
Anyone who has sat before, even badly
Practice
Two sits and one slow asana a day

6 – 10 March · four nights

For people who want the basics done properly: how to stand, how to breathe, how to fold without hurting a knee. Two teaching sessions a day, plenty of props, and a written sequence to take home at the end.

3 – 9 April · six nights

The walking week

A retreat with boots on. Four long walks from the gate — to the old temple, to the ridge village, to the water tank above Kasar Devi, and one full-day walk to a meadow with lunch carried in a cloth. Afternoons are for stretching and sleep.

For

18 – 24 July · six nights

Monsoon sitting

Rain on a tin roof for six days, and nothing to do about it. The programme shrinks to two sits and one restorative session; the rest of the day is reading, listening, and watching the cloud come up the valley. Bring a book you have been avoiding.

For

12 – 18 September · six nights

The autumn reset

Light food, early nights, and a practice that gets longer as the week goes on. Kamla cooks without wheat, sugar, or fried things for the first four days; on the fifth there is kheer, and everyone notices. Not a fast, and not a cleanse — a pause in the ordinary.

Anyone who has been running too long

Children over twelve can join the walking week. The silence retreats are for adults only, and we ask that you keep your phone in the office safe for the six days — not because it is forbidden, but because the silence does not survive a notification.

What is in the price

Everything except getting here.

All retreat rates include your room, both meals, tea twice a day, all practices and teaching, and the use of the library and the walking maps. They do not include travel to Almora, taxis from the bus stand, massages (we do not offer them), or anything you buy in the village.

A deposit of ₹6,000 holds a room; the balance is paid on arrival, in cash or by transfer. If you cancel more than three weeks before, the deposit is returned in full. Inside three weeks it becomes a credit for a later stay, valid for a year. If we cancel — a landslide, a death in the family — everything is returned.

  • Room

    Single occupancy, hot water twice a day, quilts and a hot-water bottle in winter.

  • Food

    Two vegetarian meals and two teas, cooked by Kamla on wood and gas. Dietary needs met if told at booking.

  • Practice

    All sessions, mats, blankets, bolsters, chairs, and a written sequence to take home.

  • Not included

    Travel, taxis, laundry, alcohol (not served), and the small shop in the village that sells wool.

Rooms are limited

Eight rooms, six places held for each retreat.

We keep two rooms free for people who need to come at short notice — a bereavement, a diagnosis, a week that has gone wrong. If that is you, write anyway and say so.
