Book the band

A date, a room, and the names of three tunes.

That is enough to begin. I answer on Mondays and Thursdays, and I will tell you honestly if the quartet is already out that night.

Enquiry

Wedding, concert or dance night. Include the date and the town, and whether there is a tune that must be played.

What it costs

Fees depend on the players, the travel and the length of the night. These are the ranges I quote most often, in euros, before travel.

We travel with our own microphones and a small amplifier; the room needs one power socket and a table for the cimbalom. No backing tracks, no playlist during the break — the accordionist will play quietly instead.

If the date is free and the room is kind, I will hold it for a week while you decide.
