Klezmer clarinet · Vienna & the road

A clarinet that laughs, then grieves, in the same breath.

I play the old wedding repertoire — dobriden, freylekhs, a slow doina before the meal — with a band of four and a preference for rooms with wooden floors.

The programme

An evening, in order

A wedding set runs about three hours, with the doina placed where the room can hold it. Times are honest; every band stretches.

  1. 00:00

    Dobriden

    Welcome tune, played at the door while the guests come in from the cold.

    8 min
  2. 00:12

    Slow doina

    Unmetered, rubato, the clarinet alone with the cimbalom holding a drone.

    6 min
  3. 00:24

    Freylekhs, first set

    Four dances in a chain, each a little faster, until the floor gives up.

    22 min
  4. 01:05

    Table tunes

    Quiet klezmer and Yiddish song while the food is out. The band eats too.

    14 min
  5. 01:40

    Bulgar and sirba

    The second set. Brass joins; the tempos get unreasonable.

    26 min
  6. 02:30

    Goodnight, then one more

    A lullaby, a sher, and the tune the family asks for every time.

    12 min

Studio bench

Freygish phrase bench

Three scales the repertoire leans on, one phrase each. Choose a mode, set the tempo, add an ornament and hear the shape. Nothing plays until you press play.
