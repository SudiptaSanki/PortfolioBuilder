Finding us

Kallanai post, off the Thanjavur–Kumbakonam road

By bus

Route 14 from Thanjavur old bus stand, four times a day. Get down at Kallanai post and walk two hundred metres along the canal, past the water board tank. The house is the one with the red gate and the kolam outside it.

By train

Thanjavur junction is 28 km away and receives the Cholan express from Chennai. Tell us the arrival and we will arrange an auto for ₹450; there is no taxi stand at this end of the road.

By air

Tiruchirappalli is the nearest airport, 60 km and about ninety minutes by road. Chennai is further but has more flights; the drive is five hours and the last stretch is the slow one.

Bring

  • A torch, because the yard light is one bulb
  • Cotton clothes; the delta is humid even in January
  • Shoes you can leave at the step
  • Nothing sweet from a shop. There is a pot on the fire.

Leave behind

  • Bottled water; we boil ours and it is fine
  • Expectations of wi-fi
  • Loud music after nine; the village is up at five

Or telephone

The landline is in the front room and it does ring.
