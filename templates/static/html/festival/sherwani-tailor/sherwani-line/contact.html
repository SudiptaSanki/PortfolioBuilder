Before you write

Questions that come up

Can you finish in less than eight weeks?

No, and I would rather lose the order than promise it. The canvas chest alone needs four days of hand padding to settle, and the cloth has to hang between the second and final fittings or the hem will drop after the wedding. If you are late, the honest options are a ready-made garment from a shop I can name, or a different date.

Do you make garments for women?

I cut sherwanis cut for women, and I do about four a year. The pattern changes more than the measurements: a shorter shoulder slope, a different armhole, and a longer placket. Write with what you have in mind and we will talk before any deposit.

Can I supply my own cloth?

Yes, and about a third of clients do. Bring it before the toile so we can judge whether it will hold a collar. If it will not, I will say so and show you why on a test piece — and we will not charge for the opinion.

What if I lose or gain weight before the wedding?

Tell us at the second fitting and we will leave inlays at the side seams and the centre back. A sherwani can usually move two inches either way without recutting. Beyond that, it is a new garment, and we would discuss it honestly rather than pretend.

Do you ship outside Lucknow?

Yes, by insured courier in a rigid box, after the final fitting. Many clients fly in for the measure and the final, and do the middle two by video. The garment travels better than the client does.

Why is there a waiting list?

Because Iqbal cuts every pattern himself and I pad every chest. The list opens in February and August, twelve names each time, and weddings in the family go first. If your date is fixed and the list is closed, write anyway; people change their plans and the book moves.
