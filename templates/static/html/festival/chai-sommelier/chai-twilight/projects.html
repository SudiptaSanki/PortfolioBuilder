The room seats four tables, but the work travels: weddings, restaurant kitchens, a bookshop, and one very long Durga Puja shift. Each list is what actually went into the cup.

A wedding, 120 guests

Dec 2025

Salt Lake · 4 hours · two counters

The brief was “chai like my grandmother made, but for a hundred and twenty people”. We built two identical counters so the queue never formed, and kept the masala in a cloth bag rather than loose.

  • Assam CTC base, boiled with milk120 cups an hour
  • Kesar cardamom, separate pot40 cups an hour
  • No-sugar pot, marked green18 cups
  • Khari biscuits and jaggery240 pieces

Kitchen training, 18 cooks

Sep 2025

Park Street restaurant · 3 mornings

Three mornings on the line, teaching a kitchen to brew the same cup at 8 pm as at noon. We weighed everything, then took the scales away and made them do it by eye until it matched.

  • Standard cup for service4 g / 180 ml / 3 min
  • Batch brew for 2070 g / 3 L
  • Milk temperature72°C, never boiled
  • Holding time before it turns25 minutes

Durga Puja stall

Oct 2025

Jorasanko lane · 9 hours · 800 cups

One stall, two burners, and a queue that did not stop from the evening aarti to midnight. The only cup on offer was the masala; the second burner was for hot water to keep up.

  • House masala, boiled800 cups
  • Leaf used3.2 kg
  • Milk used190 litres
  • Longest wait in the queue11 minutes

Chai and poetry

Aug 2024

Bookshop, College Street · 30 people

Three cups across an evening of reading, chosen so the tea did not compete with the voices. All three were light, unaccompanied, and served in glass so people could see the colour.

  • Nilgiri Korakundah, 90°C2.5 g / 180 ml
  • Kangra orthodox, second flush3 g / 180 ml
  • Darjeeling autumn flush3 g / 200 ml

A menu pairing, six courses

Mar 2024

Ballygunge · chef’s table · 16 seats

Six courses, six cups, including a smoked Nilgiri with the fish and a cold-brewed Kangra with the dessert. The hardest pairing was the second course; the easiest was the last.

  • First flush, 80°Cwith the jhol
