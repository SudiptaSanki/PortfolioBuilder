What a tasting includes

  • Five cups, 45–60 minutes
  • Leaf weighed at the table
  • Water and khari biscuits
  • Notes to take away, not a certificate
  • ₹600 a person, paid at the table

For larger bookings

A wedding counter needs six weeks and a kitchen I can inspect. A restaurant training runs three mornings and costs ₹18,000. A festival stall is priced by the day, and I bring my own burners.

I do not do corporate “chai bars” with a menu of twenty syrups. Three cups done properly are better than twenty done badly.
