Questions from the order book

Before you write

The same six questions every December, answered once so the email can be about the parcel.

Do you wrap on the spot?

For one or two parcels, yes, if the attic is open and the paper is in stock. Anything over ten needs to be booked; the table has to be cleared and the rolls brought down from the shelf.

Can I bring my own paper?

Yes, if it is not plastic. I will tell you honestly if it will not fold — thin recycled paper tears at the corner and there is no trick that fixes it.

Will you wrap something odd-shaped?

That is most of the fun. A hat, a lamp, a cello bow, a set of keys with a story. Send a photograph and the longest dimension.

Do you deliver?

By bicycle inside the Gürtel, €9. Outside Vienna, the parcel goes by post or courier at cost, and I pack it so the wrapping survives the journey — usually a second, plain outer box.

Can you match a colour exactly?

From a fabric swatch or a paint chip, in daylight, yes — within the twelve ribbons I stock. If it has to be exact, I will order a custom dye lot, which takes three weeks and 100 metres.

Is the paper recyclable?

Kraft and tissue, yes. The printed paper is recyclable but not compostable because of the ink. Ribbon is not recyclable and should be kept; I will take it back and reuse it.

Forty parcels a week is the honest limit

Beyond that the corners go soft. If the order is bigger, I will say so and give you a date I can actually hold.
