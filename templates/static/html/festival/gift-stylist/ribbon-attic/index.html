05 / Orders

How a job is booked

  1. Send the object and the date

    Photographs, size in centimetres and the day it has to be ready. For hampers, the count and the delivery address.

  2. Approve a sample

    One parcel, wrapped, photographed in daylight and under office light. Nothing is cut in quantity until you have held it.

  3. Pay half to start

    Materials are bought before the run. The balance is due on collection, by transfer or card in the attic.
