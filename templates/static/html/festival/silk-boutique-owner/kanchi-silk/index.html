The loom

A sari is a record of the loom that made it

Kanchipuram saris are woven in three pieces — body, border and pallu — and joined by interlocking the warp threads. That join is the korvai, and you can feel it with a finger.

My mother opened this shop with eleven saris and a wooden cupboard. She bought from four families in Pillaiyarpalayam and sold to women who came in on the bus from Chengalpattu. The cupboard is still here, behind the counter, and the fourth family still weaves for us.

A Kanchipuram sari is heavier than it looks. Three-ply mulberry silk in the warp, three in the weft, and zari that is silver wire drawn and gilded — not the polyester thread that shines like gold and weighs nothing. Lift a real zari sari and your wrist knows before your eye does.

The korvai

The border is woven separately and joined to the body by interlocking the warps, so there is no seam and no stitch, just a line you can feel. A weaver does this with three shuttles and a great deal of patience; a power loom imitates it with a printed band. If a shop cannot show you the join, it is not a korvai.

What the motifs mean

The annapakshi is a swan, the mayil kann a peacock's eye, the thazhampoo the screw-pine flower, and the yali a temple guardian. The vanasingaram pallu is a whole forest, and it takes the longest. None of this is decoration for its own sake; the motifs came off temple walls before they came onto cloth.

Buying one

Bought at a counter, not a checkout

There is no cart on this site. A sari is chosen standing up, in daylight, with someone who will tell you when it does not suit you.

01

Sit down

Come to the shop, or ask for a video call at eleven in the morning when the light through the front door is best. I will show you six saris, not sixty.

02

Take it to the door

Every sari goes to the doorway and is held against the light. Zari that is not real will look flat there; real zari keeps changing as you turn it.

03

Leave it for the tailor

Blouse stitching takes four days, or you take the piece with you. Fall and pico are done in the shop, included in the price.

A wedding sari is not the most expensive one in the shop. It is the one the grandmother approves of.
