01

A burro for a quinceañera

Family order · Iztapalapa · April 2026

The mother wanted the burro her own mother had at her fifteenth birthday, rebuilt from a photograph: same cobalt body, same gold fringe at the neck, same slightly too-long ears. We worked from a print that had been folded in a wallet for thirty years.

The ears were the whole argument. In the photograph they stand up; in paper they need a wire core to stand at all, and I do not use wire. We built them from four layers of paste around a reed and they held.

  • Size 90 cm
  • Fringes 2,400
  • Lead time 12 days
  • Survived one party

02

Five hundred stars

Film production · Mexico City · November 2025

A production designer needed five hundred star piñatas for a party scene: two hundred full size for the foreground, three hundred smaller and hollow for the background, all in the same three colours. The scene shot for two days.

I built them in nineteen days with two helpers. The smaller ones had no candy door at all, which felt wrong to make and was the only way to hit the count.

  • Units 500
  • Sizes 30 / 18 cm
  • Lead time 19 days
  • Survived the shoot

03

Pineapple for a market stall

Comisión · Mercado de Jamaica · February 2026

A fruit stall wanted a piñata in its own image to hang over the counter during the feria: a pineapple a metre tall, with the crown built from forty separate paper leaves. It was not going to be broken, only looked at.

Without the promise of a swing, the shell did not need to fail gracefully, so the paste went on heavier and the fringe tighter. It hung for eleven weeks and came back to the workshop for a repair at the crown.

  • Size 1 m
  • Leaves 40
  • Lead time 16 days
  • Survived eleven weeks

04

Forty children, blunt scissors

Workshop · Primary school · October 2025

A primary school in Iztapalapa asked for a morning of cartonería with two classes. Forty children, forty paper armatures prepared the day before, blunt scissors, and one bucket of paste between four tables.

Every child made a small star. Half of them put the fringe on upside down, which is why the workshop now starts with a demonstration of the bottom row and a story about my grandfather's Judas figures.

  • Children 40
  • Armatures 40, prepared
  • Duration 3 hours
  • Fringe upside down about half
