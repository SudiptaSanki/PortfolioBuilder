Reading the north-east

Kitchen in the north-east

Direction
North-east · Ishanya
Verdict
Warned against

The kitchen is in the north-east.

The north-east is the lightest corner and the one the texts give to water and prayer. A kitchen puts fire in it, on the wall that takes the morning sun.

What the texts say

Agni belongs in the south-east. The north-east is Ishanya, given to water, in every version of the mandala I have read, north or south.

The practical reason

A kitchen in the north-east heats the coolest corner of the house in the morning and pushes the flue into the room the family uses first. It also puts water and fire next to each other at the point where the sump usually sits.

If it is already built

Do not demolish a kitchen to satisfy a text. Move the cooking plate to the south-east corner of the room if the gas line allows, keep the sink on the north or east wall, and treat the rest as a kitchen that happens to be in the wrong room.

    The order of work

    Measured first, then read

    1. 01

      The plan as built

      Not the brochure plan. The one with the columns on it, and a tape measure for the two things drawings lie about: the kitchen window and the height of the sump.

    2. 02

      Sun, wind, water, weight

      Which wall takes the afternoon sun, where the breeze comes from in April, which corner drains, and where the heavy structure already is. This is architecture, and it is not negotiable.

    3. 03

      The mandala, on top

      The nine zones over the measured plan. Where the two disagree, I say so and give both readings rather than forcing the drawing to fit the text.

    4. 04

      The three changes worth making

      A report of four pages with three changes ranked by cost. Not thirty remedies, not a yantra, not a copper strip in the doorway.

      The kitchen could not move

      A north-east kitchen in a rented flat with a gas line that could not be re-routed. The reading was three changes under ₹12,000: the plate to the south-east corner of the same room, the sink to the east wall, and the drinking water out of the toilet wall. No demolition.

    • 2025 · house · Jayanagar

      The plot faced the wrong way

      A 30 × 40 plot with the road to the west and the owner set on an east entry. The entry moved to the north-west corner of the west wall, which is allowed, and the puja went to the north-east inside. Cheaper than the compound wall the family had planned to break.

    • 2024 · clinic · Indiranagar

      A clinic is not a house

      The owner wanted the consultation room in the south-west because a consultant had said so. The south-west is the hottest corner at four in the afternoon, when the room is full. We put the waiting area there and the consultation in the north-east, and the owner stopped needing the air conditioning at five.
