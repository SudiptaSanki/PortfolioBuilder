Send a plan

Send the plan, the address, and the room you are worried about

A reading takes a week from the plan and one site visit. If the plan is still on paper, the visit can wait and the reading comes first.

What you get

A four-page report: the plan marked with the nine zones, the placements graded, the three changes in order of what they cost, and a page of what not to bother changing.

If the plan is fine, the report says so in one page and the fee is the visit only. That happens about a third of the time.

Fee
₹8,000 for a flat, ₹14,000 for a house or a site, including one site visit in Bengaluru and a second visit after the changes.
Without a visit
₹5,000 for a plan-only reading, from a PDF and the site address. The solar path for the latitude is part of the report.
Turnaround
Seven days, or three if the plan is on paper and the site does not need a visit.
What to send
The plan as a PDF or a photograph, the facing of the plot, the floor level, and the rooms that worry you. If it is a flat, the floor number and which wall is shared.
What I will not do
Promise health, money or marriage, sell a remedy, or read a plan to explain an illness. If that is what you are looking for, I am the wrong consultant.

Plans are read by me, not by an assistant. Nothing you send is shared or published.
