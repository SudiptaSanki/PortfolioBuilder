Sheet 03 · Readings · 2022–2025

Readings

Twelve plans, and what was actually changed

Each entry gives the one thing the family asked about, the answer they got, and what it cost. Three of these I advised against changing anything at all.

2025 · Flat2BHK, 980 sq ft

The kitchen that could not move

Whitefield, Bengaluru · rented

A north-east kitchen with a gas line that could not be re-routed in a rented flat. The reading was three changes under ₹12,000: the cooking plate to the south-east corner of the same room, the sink to the east wall, and the drinking water out of the toilet wall.

3 changes₹11,400No demolitiontenant's flat

2025 · House30 × 40 plot

The plot faced the wrong way

Jayanagar, Bengaluru · new build

The road was to the west and the owner was set on an east entry. The entry moved to the north-west corner of the west wall, which the texts allow, and the puja went to the north-east inside. A change of two metres instead of the compound wall the family had planned to break.

Entry moved2 m along the wallSaved₹1.4 lakh of wall

2024 · Clinic1,600 sq ft

A clinic is not a house

Indiranagar, Bengaluru

The owner wanted the consultation room in the south-west because another consultant had said so. The south-west is the hottest corner at four in the afternoon, when the room is full. We put the waiting area there and the consultation in the north-east; the owner stopped needing the air conditioning at five.

2 rooms swappedno structural workBilldown 18% in month one

2024 · Flat3BHK, 1,420 sq ft

Nothing needed changing

HSR Layout, Bengaluru

A family that had been told by a website their master bedroom was in the wrong corner. It was in the south-west. The kitchen was south-east. The puja room was north-east. The report said so, listed the two allowed-not-ideal placements, and charged for the visit only.

0 changesrecommendedFeevisit only

2024 · School1.2 acres

The classroom block and the afternoon sun

Mysuru · primary school

The trustees wanted the classrooms in the north-east because a donor had asked for it. The north-east was the assembly ground. Putting classrooms there would have put 400 children on the hottest concrete on the site. The classrooms stayed on the east with deep verandahs, and the ground stayed open.

Layout keptverandah deepened 0.6 mCost₹2.8 lakh

2024 · Flat2BHK, 1,050 sq ft

The toilet behind the puja wall

Hebbal, Bengaluru

The puja shelf was on the wall the second bathroom shared. Moving the shelf to the opposite wall cost a carpenter's morning and a new drilling point. That was the entire reading: one wall, one shelf, ₹3,500.

1 change₹3,500Shelfmoved 2.4 m

2023 · House40 × 60 plot

The staircase was in the centre

Banashankari, Bengaluru

A stair in the middle of the plan, which the texts warn against and which also blocked the only cross-ventilation path in the house. Moving it to the south-west cost money but fixed a ventilation problem the family had been living with for nine years. The vastu reading and the architect's reading agreed, which is rare.

Stair movedto the south-westCost₹2.6 lakh

2023 · Office4,000 sq ft

The founder's cabin in the north-east

Koramangala, Bengaluru

The founder wanted the north-east cabin, which is the corner the texts give to prayer and water. In an office the north-east is also the best light and the corner with no afternoon glare. We gave it to the design team and put the founder in the south-west, facing north. Both readings were satisfied, and the design team got the window.

Cabins swapped2 roomsCost₹40,000

2023 · HouseVillage plot

The sump on the high side

Kanakapura taluk · farmhouse

The sump was planned on the north-east, on the high side of the slope, which is the right corner and the wrong hydraulics: the pump would have run against a fall. We kept the tank in the north-east and moved the sump to the north, two metres downhill. The texts allow both corners; the water only works in one.

1 changesump to the northSaveda pump upgrade

2022 · Flat1BHK, 620 sq ft

One room, four jobs

BTM Layout, Bengaluru

A studio flat where the same room was kitchen, dining, study and bedroom. The reading was about sequence rather than zone: cook on the south-east plate, eat at the west window, work facing north, sleep with the head south. Nothing moved except the furniture, and the flat became noticeably easier to live in.

0 rooms movedfurniture onlyCost₹8,000

2022 · Temple hall3,000 sq ft

The hall that was already right

Basavanagudi, Bengaluru

A community hall built in 1978 with the kitchen in the south-east, the entry east and the water in the north-east. The trust wanted a reading before a renovation. The reading said: do not change the plan, spend the money on the drainage and the ramp. They did.

1 changedrainage and a rampPlanleft alone

2022 · Restaurant2,200 sq ft

The kitchen door faced the dining room

Jayanagar, Bengaluru

The kitchen was in the south-east, correctly, but its door opened straight into the dining room so every plate crossed the room the guests were sitting in. The texts say nothing about doors between the kitchen and the room it serves. We moved the door 1.8 m along the wall.

1 changedoor moved 1.8 mCost₹22,000
