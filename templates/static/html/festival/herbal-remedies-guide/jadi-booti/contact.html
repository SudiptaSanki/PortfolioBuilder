What to bring

  • Any medicines or supplements you take, in the box if you can.
  • A list of what you eat in a normal week — not the good week.
  • Your last blood report, if you have one in the last year.
  • A question you actually want answered.

What I can help with

Sleep and anxiety that has not settled, digestion, the tail of a cold, menstrual cramps, the heat rash of May, a child's appetite. Small, ordinary things, at the level where a plant and a routine can move the needle.

I cannot help with: anything needing a diagnosis, an acute injury, a fever in an infant, chest pain, or a condition that is already being treated with a prescription I have not seen.

Questions

Do you see children?

From age five, with a parent present, and only for simple things. An infant with a fever goes to a paediatrician, not to me.

Can I buy without a consultation?

The shelf mixes are available at the dispensary, but for anything beyond a single herb I would rather talk first. The jar is the smallest part of the work.

Do you do video calls?

For follow-ups, yes. Not for a first visit; I need to see how you sit and move, and that does not survive a camera.

Is anything here tested?

The plants are not tested in a lab; they are weighed, tasted, and recorded. Any preparation that needs a lab test has one done at a Pune lab, and the certificate is in the folder by the door.

If you are pregnant, nursing, under 16, on blood thinners, blood-pressure medicine, thyroid medicine or sedatives, tell me before you take anything from this site. A kadha is a small dose of a drug, not a cup of tea.
