04 · How I work

Three rules that have not changed

They come from theatre, where the audience is close enough to notice everything, and they survive the move to a festival stage.

01

Design the dark

A good lighting design is mostly a decision about what stays unlit. The audience should look where the story is, and darkness is how you point.

02

Sound is a cue, not a carpet

Music under everything flattens a room. I would rather have eight precise sounds in an evening than a playlist that never stops.

03

Nobody gets hurt

Strobe warnings at the door, no flashes above 10 Hz, hearing protection for the crew, and cable ramps on every walkway. The show is not worth an injury.
