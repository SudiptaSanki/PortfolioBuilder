The season

Sixty-two utsavs. Twelve written up.

One page per festival I would tell a new committee about: the footfall, the number of volunteers it took, and the thing that went wrong badly enough to change the sheet.

October 2025Bathukamma

The stack that eleven girls built

Sri Laxmi Nivas, phase 2 · Kukatpally

Six hundred and forty people in a courtyard that has held four hundred. Two sittings, the second at 20:15, and the flower stack moved to the middle so the second sitting could see it. The songs ran to the cut-off exactly, which is what a 75-minute cultural block with a hard stop is for.

640neighbours38volunteers224 kgflowers composted

September 2025Vinayaka Chavithi

One gate, two immersions

Sai Enclave · Miyapur

The lane has a single 1.8 m gate and a transformer at the corner. The murti left at 21:10 with forty people and the second batch at 21:50 with twenty, instead of the procession everyone wanted. The police constable at the gate said it was the first year he had not been called at midnight.

380neighbours15volunteers21:10first immersion

January 2025Sankranti

Six metres from the transformer

Bhagyanagar Colony · KPHB

Nine hundred people for bhogi, which is the one festival where the fire is the attraction. The fire NOC took eleven days and the clearance was measured with a tape before the wood arrived, not after. Two sand buckets and a 9-litre water can at each end, and one volunteer whose only job was the fire.

900neighbours26volunteers6.0 mfire clearance

October 2024Deepavali

Six hundred diyas, two lighters

Sri Laxmi Nivas, phase 2 · Kukatpally

The lamp row went along the compound wall instead of across the courtyard, so the queue for prasadam never crossed it. Two people with long lighters lit the row in eleven minutes; the children were given the last fifty so they could say they had done some. Cracker window 20:00 to 21:40, and the terrace edge had an adult on it.

520neighbours600diyas0complaints

December 2024Carol evening

The brass band under the water tank

All Saints layout · Balanagar

The school brass band arrived with nine players and one chart, and the courtyard could not hold the sound of a snare without the wall sending it back. We moved them under the water tank, which broke the reflection, and put the choir on the stage. Twenty minutes of rain at the ninth lesson, and two hundred people moved into the stairwell without being asked twice.

October 2024Bathukamma

The year the stack fell at 18:10

Sai Enclave · Miyapur

Built at five in the evening by whoever was free, it leaned and went over before the first song. Nobody was hurt, the flowers were still usable, and we rebuilt it flat in twenty minutes. Since then the stack is built at eleven in the morning by a named group, and the sheet says so in the first line rather than the fourth.

September 2024Vinayaka Chavithi

Eleven hundred, and a queue that did not stop

Bhagyanagar Colony · KPHB

The street joined in and the footfall went past a thousand for the first time. Prasadam ran out at 20:50 with three hundred people still in the queue, which is the worst thing that can happen at a festival and the reason every sheet since has a five-kilo buffer and a second counter position marked on the plan.

January 2024Sankranti

Kites, and a terrace with no parapet

Sri Laxmi Nivas, phase 2 · Kukatpally

The kite line was the thing the children came for and the thing the committee worried about. We strung a rope at 1.1 m across the terrace edge, put the youngest on the lower terrace, and the man who owns the shop below kept his shutter open in case a kite came down with a child after it. Nobody fell. The rope stayed up for a week.

300neighbours1.1 msafety rope0falls

November 2023Deepavali

The cracker window was the whole job

Sai Enclave · Miyapur

Four hundred and seventy people, and a lane where three families buy crackers by the sack. We published the 20:00 to 22:00 window on the sheet and in the WhatsApp group, put an adult at each end of the lane, and had two buckets of sand. Two boys tested the rule at 19:40; their father took the box away himself, which is better than a committee doing it.

470neighbours20:00window opened2sand buckets

December 2023Carol evening

Forty minutes of rain and a basement

Bhagyanagar Colony · KPHB

The forecast said no rain and it rained at 19:20. Three hundred and forty people moved to the parking basement in eleven minutes because the alternative had been measured the week before: 220 chairs, one ramp, two lights. The choir sang under the water pipes and the acoustics were better than the courtyard.

340neighbours11 minto move220chairs below

October 2023Bathukamma

The first compost tie-up

KPHB phase 5 · Kukatpally

Two hundred and eighty people and a hundred kilos of flowers that used to go into the corner tank. The GHMC compost point at the third cross took them in nine sacks the next morning, and the society secretary has asked for the same arrangement every year since. It cost nothing and it is the change the neighbours mention most.

280neighbours98 kgcomposted9sacks

September 2023Vinayaka Chavithi

The generator failed during the aarti

Sai Enclave · Miyapur

Two hundred and sixty people, the aarti at 20:05, and the hired generator cut out for eight seconds. Eight seconds is a long time in a courtyard. Since then the aarti is on house power with the sound on a separate line, and the caretaker's changeover time is written on the sheet before the pandal goes up.

What I have not written up
