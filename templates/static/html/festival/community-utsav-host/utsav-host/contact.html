Ask for a date

Tell me the colony, the date and what usually goes wrong

I take four to six utsavs a year outside the three colonies I work with. The seasons that fill first are Bathukamma in September and Vinayaka Chavithi in August.

What happens next

I reply within two days, usually in the evening after a site visit. If the date is possible, we meet at the venue at the hour of the event, and I come with the sheet from last year and the corrections in the margin.

Two dates a season I keep open for a colony that has never done it before, at half the fee. Say so in the message if that is you.

Fee
₹6,000 per colony utsav, paid into the committee ledger. Travel within 12 km is in the fee; beyond that, auto fare.
Area
Kukatpally, KPHB, Miyapur, Balanagar and Nizampet. Other parts of Hyderabad if the venue has held an utsav before.
What I need from you
The footfall last year, the gate width, whether the colony has a treasurer, and the date the murti or the stack arrives.
Already on the board
10 Oct — Bathukamma, Sri Laxmi Nivas. 26 Oct — Vinayaka Chavithi, Sai Enclave. 13 Jan — Sankranti, Bhagyanagar (on hold).

If it is the day before the utsav and something has gone wrong, call. Do not use the form.
