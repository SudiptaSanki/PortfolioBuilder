Book a Meeting

One form. Then a walk around your ground.

Tell me about your society and the festival you want to run. I reply within three working days — usually with two questions and a date for a first meeting at your ground, because a festival is planned standing on the field, not sitting at a desk.

In the book

Your note is recorded.

Reference:

Keep the reference — quote it in any follow-up and I will find your page in the book at once.

Or write directly

Email: utsav@mitalisengupta.in

Committee room: Lake Gardens, Kolkata 700045 — Sundays, 11 am, April to September. Outside those months, write first; the book travels with me.

What happens next

Within three working days: a reply with two questions — usually about your ground and your last budget.

Then: a one-hour meeting at your ground, free. We walk the field, count the households honestly, and run the worksheet with your numbers.

Then: a written plan with the budget shape and the calendar — yours to take to your committee whether or not you hire me.

One honest limit

I run one five-day utsav per festival week. Durga Puja 2027 is my own para's, as always; Saraswati Puja and Diwali dates for client societies are open, and I take them in the order the book receives them.
