The Lead Story · Durga Puja 2027

Five days in October, seventeen years of practice, one open book.

I am the person Lake Gardens calls when Durga Puja is six months away and nobody has booked the ground. I host this para's puja — the pandal, the stalls, the programme, the bhog, the money — and my whole method fits on one board at the gate: who is on duty, what things cost, and where every rupee went. If your society wants a festival that runs like this, the worksheet further down this page is where we start.

Mitali Sengupta · Host & Secretary · Lake Gardens, Kolkata

The Programme

Five evenings, ten acts

  • Shashthi · Sat 16 OctBodhon at dusk; the children's sit-and-draw The idol is woken, then forty children draw her from the front row.
  • Saptami · Sun 17 OctKola bou snan; para theatre — “The Rota Book” Our own comedy about ourselves. It sells out every year.
  • Ashtami · Mon 18 OctAnjali in three batches; dhunuchi dance at night Pushpanjali at 9, 10 and 11 so nobody is crushed at the gate.
  • Nabami · Tue 19 OctSandhi puja at the join; the big cultural evening Four acts: the dance school, the para band, two guest singers.
  • Dashami · Wed 20 OctSindur khela, accounts at the gate, visarjan The full accounts go up on the board before the idol leaves.

Noticeboard

Notices & deadlines

  • Stall list for 2027 opens 1 June. Last year's stallholders get first refusal until 15 June; after that, the row is filled in order.
  • Sponsor board: six of eight slots signed. Two left. The board goes to print on 1 September and does not change after that date.
  • The rota book needs 34 names by 31 August. If your name is not in the book, you are not helping — you are visiting. Visitors are welcome; they just don't get a duty badge.
  • Loudspeaker hours: 6 in the morning to 10 at night, as licensed. No exceptions, not even for anybody's favourite song. That is the licence, and it is also how we stay welcome in our own para.
  • Committee meetings run ninety minutes. A meeting that runs past ninety minutes is a rota nobody wrote down. We write the rota instead.
