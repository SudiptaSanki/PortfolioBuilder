Yaman · early evening

The pakad of Yaman: N R G M̃ P, M G R S — the tivra Ma is what makes it Yaman and not something else.

Early evening · 19:00

Yaman

The first raga of the night, and the one every student is given first. It is calm and wide, and it forgives a beginner.

Aroha
S R G M̃ P D N Ṡ
Avaroha
Ṡ N D P M̃ G R S
Pakad
N R G M̃ P, M G R S
Vadi / samvadi
G / N
Taraf tuned to
N, R, G, M̃, P, D
Do not play
shuddha Ma, komal Ga

The raised fourth is the whole raga. Play it in the ascent and touch it lightly coming down; a Yaman without the tivra Ma is a Bhupali with extra notes.

What to listen for

In the pakad, the N is a grace note and the G is held. If you can hear the G before the M̃, you have the raga.
