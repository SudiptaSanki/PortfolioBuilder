Listening room

Twelve evenings, and the hour each one began

A concert list is a list of rooms. I have kept the hour beside each one, because the hour is half of what was played.

November 2025Baithak

Puriya Dhanashri at sunset

Assi Ghat, Varanasi · 18:10

Forty seats in a house with a courtyard. Fifty minutes of alap, then a gat in Jhaptal with tabla and no amplification. The lamps were lit at the aroha, which was not planned; it had simply got dark.

40seats78 minset1raga

October 2025Temple

Bhairav before sunrise

Kashi Vishwanath precinct, Varanasi · 05:40

Played unamplified in the courtyard as the light came up. Twenty minutes in, the first bell started and I stopped, because the raga belongs to the moment before the temple wakes and the temple had woken.

05:40start34 minplayed0microphones

September 2025Festival

Bageshri, after the last train

Kolkata · 23:20

A late slot that was actually late. Four hundred people, half of them waiting for the headliner, and by the second avartan the hall had settled. The organiser asked for twenty minutes; I played fifty and he did not stop me.

400people50 minset23:20start

August 2025Studio

Marwa, one take

A studio in Pune · two microphones, no edits

An attempt to record what a raga sounds like when nobody is watching. One Neumann on the tabli, one on the neck, no compression, and the wolf tone at the fifth fret left in because removing it would remove the instrument.

1take2microphones26 minmaster

July 2025Baithak

Yaman for people who had never heard it

A flat in Bengaluru · 19:30

Thirty people in a living room, most of whom had never sat through a raga. I played Yaman and explained nothing until the end, then answered questions for an hour. Two of them now come to baithaks regularly.

30people45 minset1 hquestions

March 2025Festival

The slot at two in the afternoon

A folk festival in Jaipur · 14:00

They asked for Malkauns at two in the afternoon. I played Bhimpalasi instead, which belongs to the afternoon, and explained why from the stage. Half the audience stayed; the other half had come for the name on the poster.

14:00slotBhimpalasiplayed40 minset

February 2025Temple

Malkauns at the ghat

Dashashwamedh Ghat, Varanasi · 00:40

After the aarti, when the ghat empties and the boats are tied. Two hundred people stayed, sitting on the steps. The raga has no Re and no Pa, and at that hour on those steps that is not an absence.

200stayed00:40start52 minset

December 2024Baithak

Two ragas, one hour, no interval

A house in Lucknow · 17:30

Marwa, and then Puriya as the light went. Two ragas that share a scale and nothing else, played back to back in a room of thirty. The retuning of eleven taraf between them was ten minutes and the room listened to it.

2ragas70 minmusic10 mintuning

November 2024Studio

Bageshri, with tanpura only

A studio in Varanasi · no tabla

A recording of the alap alone, twenty-eight minutes, with the tanpura player in the room rather than on a loop. It is the least commercial thing I have made and the one people write to me about.

28 minalap0tabla1tanpura

February 2024Festival

A stage, a sound system and a compromise

A music festival in Chennai · 20:00

A big stage with a system tuned for a rock band. We spent two hours getting the jawari to survive the PA, and it never fully did. The audience enjoyed it; I could hear what was missing all evening.

2 hsoundcheck1,200people45 minset

October 2023Temple

The festival of the goddess, at midnight

A village near Chandauli · 23:50

A temple courtyard with a generator and one bulb. No sound system, three hundred people on the ground, and a raga I had not planned because the drummers had come from another tradition. We found a common taal and played for an hour.

300people0amplification60 minset

March 2023Baithak

The first baithak in this house

My front room, Varanasi · 18:30

Eighteen people, borrowed chairs, my guru's photograph on the wall. I played Yaman and made four mistakes I could hear. Two of the people in that room still come, eleven years later.

18people40 minset4mistakes
