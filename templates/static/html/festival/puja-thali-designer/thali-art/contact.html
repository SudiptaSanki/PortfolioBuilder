Commission enquiry

What happens next

  • A reply within three working days
  • A paper-circle drawing at true size for approval
  • A fifty per cent advance before the metal is cut
  • The balance on collection or before courier

If you visit

The bench is at the back of the shop, past the display cases. Mornings are best: the raisers start at ten, and you can see a disc go into the fire. Bring the old plate if you are replacing one.

Delivery

Hand delivery in Jaipur, packed in cotton and a wooden frame. Elsewhere in India by insured courier. We do not ship ashtadhatu plates without insurance.
