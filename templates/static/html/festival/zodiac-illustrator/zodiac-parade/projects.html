NBOn editions

Why the runs are small

A risograph stencil lasts about two hundred impressions before it starts to break down, so an edition is not a marketing decision. When a poster sells out, it is reprinted with a new stencil, and the second printing is marked as such on the back — a small pencil II, then III. The plates are kept, the stencil is not.

Posters are numbered and signed in pencil. Sets are not signed, because signing across a fold ruins the fold. Hongbao are not signed at all; they are for spending, not collecting.
