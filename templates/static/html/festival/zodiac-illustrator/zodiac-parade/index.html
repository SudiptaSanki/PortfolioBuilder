The parade line

Twelve animals in order, drawn as one set for the 2025 banner and reused every year since. Each is a single-weight line drawing, because a line survives being blown up to twelve metres and a gradient does not.

How a banner gets made

From a sketch to twelve metres of street

  1. 01

    Draw at the size of a hand

    A banner is not drawn at twelve metres. It is drawn at ten centimetres and scanned at 1200 dpi, because a line that looks confident at small size is the only kind that survives being enlarged.

  2. 02

    Separate the inks

    Two plates: fluorescent orange and teal. Everything that should be dark is printed twice, once in each, and the overprint makes a third colour that is nearly black but not quite. The registration marks are part of the design.

  3. 03

    Print, then stand back

    The riso prints the small editions; the parade banner goes to a large-format shop in Ubi and is printed on scrim, which is mesh-reinforced and survives a monsoon. I still proof it at A3 first.
