The pavilion at evening

PL. 01

A pavilion of the kind that never existed exactly, built from three I have drawn in Lahore and one in Agra. The sky took eleven washes; the gold on the finial is burnished twice.

Wasli18 × 24 cm2025Lapis · vermilion · gold

In a private collection, Lahore

Portrait of a boy with a falcon

PL. 02

Commissioned by a family in Islamabad from photographs of a grandfather at sixteen. The falcon is from a different photograph; the hood is the one detail I was asked not to change.

Night garden with lamp

PL. 04

The only page I have painted almost entirely in indigo. The lamp is gold on a base of orpiment, and in a dark room it catches the light from across the room, which is the effect I wanted.

Shamsa for a wedding

A illuminated rosette made for a marriage in 2023, with the couple's names in the centre band. Shamsa work is geometry first: if the eighth circle is a millimetre out, the whole page leans.
