Questions

Before the first note

How long does a miniature really take?

The painting is eight to eighteen weeks depending on size and detail. The wasli needs three weeks before that, and the finished page rests face-down under a board for six weeks before it is framed. From a confirmed sketch to a framed page, count four to six months.

Can you copy an existing miniature?

I will copy a historical folio for study, and I do, but I will not sell a copy as an original. If you want a painting in the manner of a particular school — Akbar-period, Kishangarh, late Mughal — that is a different thing, and I am glad to discuss it.

Do you work from photographs?

For portraits and buildings, yes. A photograph gives me a likeness and a proportion; it does not give me the person. I ask for three or four photographs from different days, and one long conversation.

How should the page be framed and hung?

Behind glass with a wide mount, out of direct sun, in a room that is not a kitchen. Ultraviolet-filtering glass is worth the money. Do not hang a miniature above a radiator; the wasli will move and the gold will lift.

Do you ship outside Pakistan?

Yes, framed or rolled in a tube, by courier with insurance. Buyers pay duty. I photograph the page before packing and send the photographs with the tracking number.
