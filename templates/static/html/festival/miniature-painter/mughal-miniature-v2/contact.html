What happens next

1. I reply within three days, with questions about the scene.

2. I draw the tarh — the drawing — and send it for your approval. Nothing is painted before you have seen the drawing.

3. Half the price books the folio; the other half is due when it is in your hands.

4. Folios travel flat between boards, by insured post within India. Never rolled.
