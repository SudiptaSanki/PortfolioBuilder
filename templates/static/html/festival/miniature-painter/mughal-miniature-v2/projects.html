Three folios, three errands

A commission is an errand someone trusts you with. Here are three, told the way I would tell them at the bench — what was asked, what the painting took, and where the folio lives now. The photographs show stages of work in the studio, not the finished pages.

The Wedding Procession — Jaipur, 2025

Format
Court folio, 30 × 42 cm
Figures
11, plus one elephant
Pigment
Lapis, vermilion, malachite, gold details
Time
14 weeks
Price
₹1,62,500

The ask Aditi and Rohan Chaturvedi married in Jaipur with two hundred guests and no painting — only phone photographs, and a grandmother who remembered the evening better than any camera. They asked for the baraat as a court folio: the procession, the elephant, the exact moment the garland exchange went wrong.

The painting Eleven faces from eleven photographs, none of them taken from the same side. The drawing alone took three weeks — likeness at this size is a matter of a millimetre, and a millimetre wrong is a stranger. The elephant, the family agreed, came easiest.

The outcome The folio hangs in the couple's home in Jaipur, framed behind museum glass. The grandmother has been named its keeper, and corrects visitors who misidentify the cousins.

A Page for Ghalib — Delhi, 2024

Format
Standard folio, 21 × 30 cm
Figures
1 — the poet at his desk
Pigment
Lampblack, indigo, gold details
Time
6 weeks
Price
₹67,000

The ask A Delhi publisher was bringing out a new selection of Ghalib's verses and wanted a frontispiece painted in the manner of the poet's own century — not an illustration of a poem, but the room the poems were written in: the desk, the lamp, the hookah, Delhi beyond the lattice.

The painting One figure only, which makes it harder, not easier — there is nowhere for a weak passage to hide. The books on the desk are titled in a hand small enough that the printers wrote to ask what it said. It says the names of his books.

The outcome The folio was photographed for the frontispiece and the original is held by the publisher. The book is in its second printing; the painting is not for sale, and the publisher has stopped asking.

The Companion Folio — Lucknow, 2024

Format
Small folio, 15 × 21 cm
Figures
3
Pigment
Earth pigments, indigo, no gold
Time
4 weeks
Price
₹38,500

The ask The Rizvi family brought me their grandmother's folio — a garden scene, painted perhaps a century ago, its corner eaten by damp and silverfish. They asked if I could repair it. I could not, and said so: repair belongs to conservators, and a painter's hand on an old page is a second damage.

The painting Instead I painted a companion: the same garden, copied figure for figure from what remained, the eaten corner left honestly empty in the copy too — a white space where the peacock used to be, because inventing him would have been a lie.

The outcome The original now rests in an acid-free box, as a conservator in the State Museum advised. The companion hangs where the original hung. The family says the empty corner is the part they show guests first.
