# Credits — mughal-miniature

Persona: Meher Kidwai, a fictional miniature painter of Lucknow, invented for
this template. The folios, clients, prices and exhibition history are invented;
the craft (wasli, pigment-grinding, brush-making, gold work, conservation) is
described as it is truly practised. No historical manuscript artwork is
reproduced anywhere on this site — the works are described in words and
tombstone data only.
