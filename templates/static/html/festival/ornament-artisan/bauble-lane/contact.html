What we will not do

Print a company logo on a bauble. Copy a design from another workshop. Promise a colour we have not sampled. Send a commission before it is paid for, because the furnace does not run on trust alone.

Visiting

The lane in December

Lauscha is four hours from Berlin by train and one from Sonneberg. The shop is open from ten, the furnace is working until early afternoon, and the Christmas market in the square runs on the first three weekends of December. There is one hotel and it is full by October.

If you want to watch the blowing, write first. The floor is hot, the dog is enthusiastic, and we can only take four people at a time.
