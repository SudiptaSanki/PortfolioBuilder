Packing and post

How it arrives in one piece

Each bauble sits in a moulded pulp tray, the tray in a card box, the box in a second box with paper between them. We post from Lauscha on Tuesdays and Fridays. Within Germany it takes two days; the rest of Europe, four; the United States, about ten and occasionally three weeks if customs is busy.

If something arrives broken, send a photograph within seven days and we will replace it in the next firing at no cost. We have replaced 41 pieces this year out of about 2,300 sent.
