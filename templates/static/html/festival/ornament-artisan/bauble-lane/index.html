01 · The making

Five pairs of hands before it hangs

Each stage is done by a different person in the workshop, and none of them can do the next one well. That is not tradition for its own sake; it is how the glass survives.

  1. 01

    Gather

    Clear glass on the pipe, rolled in coloured frit while it is still soft, then brought back to shape at the furnace mouth.

  2. 02

    Blow

    Into a wooden mould for the round ones, free-blown for the onions and drops. The wall is 1.2 mm; thinner and it breaks in the post.

  3. 03

    Silver

    A little silver nitrate and sugar water inside, warmed until the mirror forms. This is the step that makes a bauble look lit from inside.

  4. 04

    Paint

    Gold lines and dots by hand, one at a time, on a turntable. A steady hand does 60 an hour; a tired hand does 20 and ruins 5.

  5. 05

    Cap

    Brass or silvered steel, pressed onto the neck with a little glue and a lot of habit. The loop is tested with a 3 kg weight before the piece is packed.

03 · Commissions

Sets, collections and the shop at the front

We take commissions for sets of ten or more: a family's initials, a particular red, a shape that matches the ones your grandmother had. The order book for 2027 opens in February and fills by June, which sounds grander than it is: there are three of us.

The shop at the front of the workshop is open from the first week of November until Christmas Eve, six days a week. In July it is a room with a table in it.

Ask about a commission

What a commission includes

  • A drawing and a glass sample posted before anything is blown
  • One colour trial at no cost, then a fixed price per piece
  • Ten per cent spare, packed separately, because glass breaks
  • A card naming the three people who made them

Minimum order ten pieces. We do not print logos, and we do not sell wholesale to hotels.
