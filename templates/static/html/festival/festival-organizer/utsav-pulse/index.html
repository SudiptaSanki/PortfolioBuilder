The order of decisions

    Six desks

    Every festival I run has the same six desks, and one command post

    1. 01

      Gate

      Two people a gate whose only job is counting the inflow in ten-minute blocks, on a paper tally. A tap counter lies when the crowd is tight.

    2. 02

      Crowd

      Density marshals on the two narrowest lines of the site, with a rope and a hold signal. They do not direct traffic; they stop it.

    3. 03

      Ritual

      The priest’s time is fixed. Everything else moves around it, and the procession route is agreed with the temple before the stage is booked.

    4. 04

      Water and food

      Two tanks at the gate, one at the medical post, and the prasadam queue on the far side from the exit so it cannot meet the inflow.

    5. 05

      Medical

      A doctor, two paramedics and an ambulance with a route agreed with traffic police. The lost-child desk sits beside it, not at the gate.

    6. 06

      Sweep

      Forty sweepers on a two-hour rotation from 22:00, with the waste separated at the bin and not at the yard. The road is handed back clean.
