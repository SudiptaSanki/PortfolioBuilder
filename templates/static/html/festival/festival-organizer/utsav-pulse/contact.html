Brief us

Give me the site, the footfall you actually had, and the ritual that cannot move

I take six operations a year. The brief is read before the site walk, and the site walk is what decides whether I take the job.

What happens after the form

I reply within three days and ask for last year’s footfall number, the gate dimensions and the permission status. Then a site walk at the hour of the event, with the committee and whoever runs the sound.

The proposal is three pages: the density arithmetic, the gate and marshal plan, and the cost. No deck.

Fee
₹1,20,000 for a one-day operation up to 40,000 footfall, including the site walk, permissions envelope and debrief. Multi-day and larger sites quoted after the walk.
Not included
Marshals, barricade, water tanks, toilets and medical are hired directly by the committee on my specification, so the invoices are visible to everyone.
Lead time
Ten weeks for a large temple day. Four weeks is possible with a site that has run before and a committee that has the permissions started.
Seasons
Panguni (March) briefs close in November. Margazhi (December) by September. Aadi (July) by April.
Area
Chennai and within 200 km. Madurai and Coimbatore with travel and two site visits.

If the festival is inside three weeks, call. I will tell you honestly whether the plan can be rescued.
