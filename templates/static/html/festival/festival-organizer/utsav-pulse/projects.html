Festivals

Twelve operations, with the numbers that made them work

Footfall is the day’s total through the site, not the number in the newspaper. Gates and marshals are what was actually staffed, not what was requested.

March 2025Temple

Arupathimoovar procession

Mylapore, Chennai · 63 idols, 11 streets

The largest day of the year and the one with the least room: a temple compound, four lanes and a one-way loop agreed with traffic police. The plan holds 45% of the day standing at once and splits the crowd into two ritual windows.

1.2 lakhfootfall4gates96marshals0holds

July 2025Civic · waterfront

Aadi Perukku

Besant Nagar beach · tide at 19:40

A waterfront evening built around a tide table. The dry line sits 30 m from the water, the exits face inland, and the whole site closes to inflow for the twenty minutes around high tide. Two people on the water line all evening with a rope.

40,000evening6gates48marshals19:40tide

Dec 2024 — Jan 2025Music · 14 venues

Margazhi sabha season

Mylapore and T. Nagar · 22 nights

Not a crowd problem but a timetable: fourteen venues, 9,000 seats a night, green rooms, parking and a 21:30 turn-around between concerts. The operation is a radio channel, four drivers and a shared list of who is on stage when.

9,000seats a night14venues22nights21:30turn-around

June 2024Temple

Kumbhabhishekam

Madurai · consecration, three days

A consecration draws people who have not been to the temple in years, and they arrive early. The site opened at 04:00 with two gates and a water point, and the queue was managed in the courtyard rather than the street, which kept the bus route clear.

62,000three days3gates54marshals04:00first gate

August 2024Music · beach

Marina evening concert

Marina Beach · 18,000 standing

An open beach has no gates, which is the problem: people arrive from every direction. We built a perimeter with 400 m of barricade and three entrances, and accepted that the fourth side was the sea. Low frequency from the stage carried to the residential line, so the sound was aimed north and cut at 21:30.

18,000standing3entrances400 mbarricade21:30sound off

January 2024Civic

Chennai Sangamam

Seven venues across the city · nine days

A city festival is seven small operations sharing one command post. Each venue had its own gate count and its own volunteer captain, and the command post called every venue at 18:00 and 21:00 for a two-minute report. Two venues were closed for weather on day six.

7venues9days1command post2weather closures

February 2024Food

Mylapore food street

North Mada Street · 40 stalls, two evenings

Forty stalls on a street with a temple at one end. The queue is the product and the hazard: we set each stall’s service line parallel to the street so the crowd could not form a wall, and put the waste separation at the exit rather than the middle.

26,000two evenings40stalls2gates1.4 twaste

September 2023Temple

Panguni car festival hold

Mylapore · the hold that worked

The one gate hold I have had to call in Mylapore. At 19:12 the loop was 5.8 persons per m² at the second corner. Gate two closed for eleven minutes, the announcement was read from the stage, and the crowd thinned to 4.1 before it reopened. The log runs to two pages.

5.8/m² at hold11 minclosed4.1after0injuries

August 2023Civic

Independence Day flag walk

Marina to Fort St George · 6 km

A moving operation: 12,000 people walking six kilometres with three water points and a sweep vehicle behind the last walker. The road closure is agreed in fifteen-minute slices with traffic police, and the lead vehicle sets a pace of 3.5 km/h that the crowd can hold.

12,000walkers6 kmroute3water points3.5km/h

December 2023Music · indoor

A sabha hall at 1,100 seats

T. Nagar · a hall that holds 800

The committee sold 1,100 tickets for a hall with 800 seats. We ran two sittings and an overflow screen in the courtyard, and refunded a hundred tickets rather than let the aisle fill. The fire officer’s signature on the seating plan is what decided it.

1,100tickets800seats2sittings100refunds

January 2023Food

Pongal harvest market

Guindy · 60 farmers, one day

A market, not a festival, until sixty thousand people arrived. The site had been planned for 20,000 and the gates were two. We opened a third gate in a service lane by 11:00 and stopped vehicles at the outer ring; the water ran out at 13:00 and was the lesson that changed my standing order on tanks.

60,000one day3gates (2 planned)13:00water out1lesson

March 2022Civic · first big one

The festival after the gap

Mylapore · 30,000, first season back

The first large gathering after two years of nothing. Half the volunteers were new, the crowd had forgotten how to queue, and the police wanted a plan with twice the usual detail. We wrote it as a rehearsal script: every fifteen minutes, who speaks, who opens, who holds.

30,000footfall4gates64marshals18new volunteers

The numbers I do not publish

The two festivals that went badly and the one where the committee overruled my density figure, which is in the debrief and not on a website. If you are deciding whether to hire me, ask for those three; the way a plan fails is more useful than the way it worked.
