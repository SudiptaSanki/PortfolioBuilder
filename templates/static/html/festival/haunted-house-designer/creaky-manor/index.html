Built & running

    The Ashby Hall Cellars

    Ashby-de-la-Zouch · 9 rooms · October 2025

    Built into the real cellars of a stately home: low vaults, one very convincing rat, and a final room that is simply a longer corridor than anyone expects. Ran 26 nights without a stoppage.

    Glasgow · 6 rooms · December 2024

III

How a haunt gets built

Four stages, in this order. The safety plan is written at stage two, not stapled on at the end.

room 03 · method
  1. 1

    Walk the space

    Two hours, a torch and a notebook. Where the floor slopes, where the fire exits are, where the queue can stand without blocking a road.

  2. 2

    Draw the route

    Plan, elevation and a one-page safety plan. Scares are marked as numbers on the plan, never as “a thing that happens here”.

  3. 3

    Build and rehearse

    Sets and mechanisms first, actors second. A scare that needs the actor to be brave is a scare that will fail on a wet Tuesday.

  4. 4

    Run and watch

    I am in the queue for the first three nights, counting how long people take and where they stop talking.
