Visit · 11

The yard is open

Visitors are welcome Tuesday to Saturday, 9:00 to 17:00. The shop needs no appointment; a demonstration at the bench does, because it means stopping work.

  • AddressCamino al Panteón, San Martín Tilcajete, Oaxaca, Mexico
  • Getting thereAbout 30 minutes by car from Oaxaca de Juárez, on the road to Ocotlán. Local buses from the Central de Abastos stop at the village entrance.
  • Best timeMorning, when the machete and chisel work is underway. Afternoon is painting, which is quieter.
  • ClosedSundays, Mondays, and the week of Día de Muertos.
  • LanguagesSpanish, English, and enough Zapotec to greet the neighbours.
Before Día de Muertos: the workshop stops taking visits in the last week of October. The village fills, the yard is full of pieces for the fair, and nobody has time to be polite about it.

Before you write · 12

Three useful things to include

Not required, but each one saves a round of emails.

01

The room

A photograph, or a sentence about the light and the wall colour. A dark indigo piece and a pale bone piece behave very differently in a white lobby.

02

The date

When the piece is needed, and whether that date is fixed. Drying cannot be hurried, but a smaller animal can sometimes be fitted in.

03

The animal

Jaguar, deer, rabbit, iguana, armadillo, or something that does not exist yet. The last category is the most interesting and takes the longest to draw.
