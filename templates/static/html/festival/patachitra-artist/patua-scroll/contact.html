How a commission runs

You write with the story and the wall. I reply with the panel count, the length and a price. A third is paid to buy the cloth and start; the rest when the pat is dry and photographed for your approval. Nothing is shipped until you have seen it unrolled.

If you are buying a gift

Say so, and I will paint the dedication on the bottom border in Bengali and English, and send the scroll in a cloth sleeve rather than the travel tube.
