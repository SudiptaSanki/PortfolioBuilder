Graphic novels · wordless sequences · riso zines

Every story I draw is a question of how long a single panel is allowed to sit still.

I draw about water, women who work on it, and the small negotiations of a Kerala afternoon. Ink wash for the weather, dry nib for the talk, one spot colour when something has to be remembered.

Instrument 01

Page pacing bench

A page is read at the speed you build into it. Set the panel count, gutter and dialogue load, then watch the page and its reading rhythm redraw. Numbers follow the bench method I use for Kayal: a little over one second to enter a panel, roughly a third of a second per word of caption.

Four values, one page. Nothing is saved.
