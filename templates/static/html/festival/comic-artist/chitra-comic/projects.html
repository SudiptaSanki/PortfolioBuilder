Standing order for shops and libraries

Each new title ships in a bundle of ten with a riso proof sheet signed and numbered. Shops in India pay trade terms of 40% and keep the proof. Libraries and reading rooms pay cost plus postage; I would rather the books be read than shelved.

Foreign orders go through the shop, which handles customs. Wholesale requests, translation rights and workshop invitations are all handled by letter — see the contact page for the address.
