i

A minute of practice

Count the breath, not the thoughts

Choose a pattern and sit with it for a minute. With the counting done for you, there is less to hold in your head and more room to notice what the breath is actually doing. If you feel light-headed, stop; breath work is not a competition.

ii

What I teach

Three ways to begin

Most people arrive believing they cannot meditate because their mind wanders. The wandering is not the problem; it is the practice. What changes is how quickly you notice, and how kindly.

01

One-to-one

Sixty minutes, in the shala or by video. We start with what your days are actually like, then find a practice that fits inside them. Six sessions is the usual arc; one is enough to see whether this is for you.

₹2,400 a session · ₹12,000 for six · Tuesdays and Thursdays

02

The six-week course

Small groups of eight, in the shala on Saturday mornings. Weeks one to three are attention and posture; weeks four to six are working with difficulty, sleep and the days when nothing happens. There is home practice, about fifteen minutes a day.

₹9,600 · next course 9 January 2027 · eight places

03

For workplaces

Four sessions of forty minutes, run over a month for teams of up to twenty. No incense, no bells, no slides about mindfulness. Practical instruction for people who will do this at a desk with their eyes open.

₹38,000 for four sessions · in Rishikesh or online
