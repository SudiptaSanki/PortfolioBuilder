Programmes · 2027

Small rooms,
long practice.

Everything below runs in the Tapovan shala unless it says otherwise. Places are limited by the size of the room rather than by marketing, and the prices include tea, handouts and nothing else.

  1. 9 January 2027six Saturdays · 7–8:30am

    Foundations, six weeks

    Attention, posture and the ordinary breath. Weeks four to six move into difficulty: restlessness, sleep, and the days when the practice feels pointless. Home practice is fifteen minutes a day, and I will ask you about it.

  2. 6 February 2027four Saturdays · 5–7pm

    Yoga nidra, four weeks

    The systematic rotation of awareness, taught properly rather than as a long nap. Useful for people who cannot sit upright for long, and for anyone whose sleep has gone thin. Bring a blanket and a willingness to lie on the floor.

    ₹6,40010 places · 6 leftShala
  3. 13–15 March 2027Friday evening to Sunday noon

    Silent weekend

    Two nights at a small guesthouse above Kaudiyala, with silence from Friday supper to Sunday breakfast. Sitting, walking, and one interview a day. This is the hardest thing on the list for most people, and the one they remember.

    ₹14,50012 places · 5 leftKaudiyala
  4. 2–6 November 2027five nights

    Five-day sit

    A longer, quieter version of the weekend, with more sitting and less instruction. It is intended for people who already have a practice and want to see what happens when it is given room. Interview required.

    ₹26,00010 places · openKaudiyala

    Attention at work

    Forty minutes, online or in your office, for up to twenty people. Eyes open, at the desk, no cushions. Three practical practices and the reasoning behind them, plus a short recording for the days I am not there.

    The open sit

Before you book

Read this if you are new to it

None of these programmes require you to be flexible, vegetarian, religious or calm. They do require you to arrive on time and to tell me if something is not working. If you are being treated for a mental health condition, write to me first; some practices are better avoided, and I would rather have that conversation than take your money.
