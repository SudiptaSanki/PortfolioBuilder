Episodes · 2021–2026

Seventeen episodes,
six buildings

Each episode is about one address and one sound. The building is always real; the names are never. Warnings are given at the top of each file and repeated here, because a surprise is not the same thing as a story.

S4 · 09

The third-floor landing has a second set of feet

Ballygunge · November 2026

A woman counts eleven steps up her staircase and twelve coming down. She is not frightened at first; she is annoyed, because she has counted them since she was a child. Then she records it on her phone, and the recording has two sets of footsteps for eleven seconds.

I left her phone recording exactly as it arrived — no clean-up, no denoise. The point is that you can hear the ceiling fan in it, and the fan is off.

41:20 14 Nov 2026 Sudden sound · one scream
S4 · 08

The lift at 4:10

Salt Lake · October 2026

An office block with one working lift and a watchman who will not ride it after four. For most of the episode the explanation is mechanical, and the watchman is a reasonable man with a roster. Then he plays me a voice note he made in 2019, and the episode changes.

36:48 31 Oct 2026 No sudden sound
S4 · 07

The tap in the thakur dalan

Shyambazar · September 2026

A brass tap in a courtyard that runs for three minutes at 2 a.m., recorded over four nights with the family's permission. Their dog is on the track, which is why the fourth night is unusable and also why the episode ends the way it does.

44:02 26 Sep 2026 Dog barking · water
S3 · 06

A room that is not on the plan

Gariahat · March 2026

A flat above a shop, with a door in the corridor that opens onto a room the building plan does not show. The tenant has never opened it. The landlord has a story about it that he tells differently each time — I recorded him three times over two months and put all three versions in the episode.

48:15 27 Mar 2026 Discussion of a death
S3 · 04

The monsoon in someone else's house

Dhakuria · July 2025

Recorded entirely in one night of rain, in a house I was house-sitting. Every sound in the episode is the rain, the roof, and one thing that walked across the terrace at 3:40 a.m. and was too light to be a cat.

29:50 25 Jul 2025 Rain throughout
S1 · 01

My mother's landing

Shyambazar · October 2021

The first episode, recorded on a phone in my mother's house because I did not yet own a microphone. She tells the story of the landing at the top of the stairs where her grandmother used to sit, and then she says something at the end that I did not expect and did not cut.

22:10 29 Oct 2021 None
