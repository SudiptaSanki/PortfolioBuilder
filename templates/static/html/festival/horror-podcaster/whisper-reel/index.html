03 / How it is made

Record the room first

Every episode opens with eight seconds of nothing — the room's own tone. If I cut it, the story feels pasted on. The tone goes under everything at about minus fourteen.

One impossible thing

A story earns its ending with one detail that cannot be explained and is never explained. Two of them and it becomes a monster film. I cut the second one in the edit, every time.

No soundtrack

No strings, no drones over the voice. What you hear is the room, the object, and the person telling it. If the story needs music to be frightening, the story is not frightening.

I listened to the landing episode on a train and took my headphones off at the second set of feet. Then I put them back on. That is the whole review.
