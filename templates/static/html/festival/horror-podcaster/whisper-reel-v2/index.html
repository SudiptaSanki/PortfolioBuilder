Case file Nº 03 · Season three now releasing

Stories for after midnight.

I’m Maren Holloway. I write, voice and produce Whisper Reel from a coat closet in Sleepy Hollow, New York — and every October I take the show out of the closet and put it on a stage for the village’s Halloween season, in front of four hundred people who came to be frightened politely. The rest of the year it is just you, me, and the silence between sentences.

The bench

Hear the craft, not the episodes

The episodes live on the podcast apps. What I can show you here is the workbench: three cues, synthesised in your browser at the moment you tap — nothing on this page makes a sound on its own, and every cue stops inside two seconds. This is exactly how the show is built: small sounds, placed carefully.

The sound-design bench

Tap a cue. Headphones recommended; a quiet room assumed. Peak level is capped at the same ceiling I mix to for the bench demos — these are sketches of cues, not the finished design.

A jump scare is a sentence that gave up. If the line isn’t frightening read flat, at a kitchen table, at noon — no sting will save it.
— Maren Holloway, from her own edit notes, Season Two
