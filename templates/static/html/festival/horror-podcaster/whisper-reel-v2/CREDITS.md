## Sound

The sound-design bench on the Episodes page synthesises its three cues
live in the browser with Web Audio at the moment a visitor taps a cue
button (filtered noise for room tone, a swept sawtooth for the door
creak, a dissonant cluster for the cut sting). No audio files are used
or shipped. Nothing plays automatically: the single AudioContext is
created only on a tap, the master gain is capped at 0.12, and every
cue self-stops within 2 seconds.
