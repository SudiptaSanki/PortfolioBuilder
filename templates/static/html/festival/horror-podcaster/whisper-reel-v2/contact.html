File 04 · Correspondence

Write to the show.

One inbox, one form, one person reading both. Sponsors get the rate card and the slot ledger — the same ones printed on the Episodes page. Programmers get the live show’s terms. Everybody gets an answer.

The address

maren@whisperreelpodcast.example

Replies come within three working days. In October, allow five — the show is on stage, weekly, and the closet is dark for other reasons.

What to say

Sponsors: the season and placement you want, and how many episodes. The planner will already have told you the price — quote it back at me; I honour the card.

Festivals & venues: your dates and your room’s seat count. The live show needs a quiet house, one door’s worth of stage, and an audience willing to sit in the dark.

Podcasters: collaborations are considered twice a year, in January and June. Send the show, not a pitch deck.

The form

Filed.

Your letter is in the case file. Keep this reference — it is how Maren will find it:

WR-2026-0000

A reply will come to the email you gave, within three working days (five in October). Nothing about this form leaves your browser — it is a template demonstration.
