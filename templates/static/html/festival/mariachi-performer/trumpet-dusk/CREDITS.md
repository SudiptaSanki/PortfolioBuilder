# Credits — Trumpet Dusk (Mariachi Solano)

Persona: **Rafael Duarte** and **Mariachi Solano** are fictional, created for this
template and disclosed as fictional in every page footer. No real person, group
or venue is depicted. The repertoire named is traditional and standard mariachi
repertoire (titles only — no lyrics reproduced).

## Sound ruling — no audio, by design

Mariachi is voices, strings and brass in a room. Its sound cannot be
synthesised honestly in a browser, and the person booking a mariachi is not a
student looking for a practice loop — so the sound need-test **fails** for this
template and the site ships **no audio of any kind**: no samples, no
synthesised backing, no autoplay, nothing on hover. The interactive centre of
the site is the Songbook Explorer and the Serenata Programme Builder — timed
data, not fake sound. The ruling is also stated in the footer of every page.
