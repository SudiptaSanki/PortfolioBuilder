    What moves the price: lineup size (trío → 6-piece → full 9), hours beyond the package, travel past 40 miles (billed at $0.70/mile round trip), peak dates — Mother’s Day weekend, 12 December and 1 November — at +25%, and bookings inside 14 days at +$100. A 30% deposit holds the date; the balance is due on the day. These are starting prices for standard bookings, confirmed in writing before any deposit is taken.
