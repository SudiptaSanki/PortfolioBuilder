What to send

Four things make an answer fast:

  • The date — and whether it can move.
  • The venue & city — indoors or out, and the room size.
  • The moment that matters — an entrance, a waltz, a 6 a.m. door.
  • Your songs — build a serenata on the songbook page and name them here.
