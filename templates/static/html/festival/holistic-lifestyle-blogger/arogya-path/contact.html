The five questions I get most

Can you suggest a routine for me?
Not individually. The seasonal routine in each issue is the general version; a practitioner who can see you is the right person for the specific one.
Do you take guest essays?
Rarely, and never about a product. Send a pitch of three sentences, not a finished piece.
Why no photographs?
Because stock photographs in a food essay are a kind of lie, and mine are not good enough to be worth the honesty.
Where are the sources?
At the end of each essay in the archive. The ones I rejected are listed too.
Can I republish an essay?
Yes for a school or a non-profit, with the credit and a link. Ask for anything commercial.
