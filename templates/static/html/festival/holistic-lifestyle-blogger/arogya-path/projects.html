Reading list

  • Charaka SamhitaAny scholarly translation. Read the parts on food and season; skip the parts on surgery, which are not my subject.
  • Ayurvedic Cooking for WesternersAmadea Morningstar. Useful recipes, dated framing. The chapter on digestion is the one I return to.
  • Salt, Fat, Acid, HeatSamin Nosrat. Not Ayurvedic at all, and the most useful book on cooking I own.
  • The Yoga Sutras of PatanjaliRead for the ethics, not for the promises. Two translations disagreed enough to teach me how to read old texts.
