Tell me about the wall.

A panel is made for a room, and the room decides more than the figure does. Send me a photograph of the wall, the light it gets, and roughly what you want to spend, and I will tell you honestly whether it is a panel, a print, or nothing at all.

How it works

  1. A conversationUsually by telephone, once, about the room and the figure. No charge.
  2. A drawingA pencil sketch on paper at the size of the board, posted to you. Two rounds of changes are included; after that we are designing, not adjusting.
  3. A third downThe board is ordered when the deposit arrives. The balance is due on completion, before crating.
  4. Nine to fourteen weeksDepending on size. Nine for a 12 × 16, fourteen for a 30 × 40. The gesso sets the pace, not me.
  5. A fortnight on the rackEvery panel hangs in the studio for two weeks before framing, because a board that will crack should crack here.

Packing and hanging

Crated in plywood with foam blocks, never in bubble wrap alone — the gesso lifts if the board is allowed to flex. Within India we use a specialist art courier and insure the full value. Abroad, we crate to a specification you can send your own shipper.

Hang it out of direct sun and away from a bathroom wall. Dust with a dry, soft brush, never a cloth. Do not polish the gold; it is not meant to be shiny like brass, and polish takes the lacquer with it.
