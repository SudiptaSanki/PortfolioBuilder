Restoration

Panels come back to us after thirty or forty years with the gesso lifting at the edges, gold gone green from sulphur, or a figure that has been wiped with a wet cloth one time too many. We lift the failed gesso, re-pipe the missing ornament, and re-gild only that area, matching the wear of the rest.

We do not repaint faces. If the face has gone, the board becomes a border and a background, and we will tell you that before we begin rather than after.

Restoration is quoted after the panel has been seen, not photographed. Bring it in, or send it with a carnet and we will write to you with a price and a reason.

Workshops and teaching

July workshop

Five days, twelve places, no experience needed. Gesso on the first day, gold on the third, and you leave with a small board you have gilded yourself. ₹18,000, materials included.

The year course

Four students, one day a week for eight months, from chalk to a finished twelve-by-sixteen panel. It is the way this craft has always been taught, and it is not quick. ₹1,20,000, materials extra.

For schools

A half-day demonstration for art students, with gesso, foil and a board that gets ruined on purpose so they can see what the material does. We ask for the cost of materials and nothing else.
