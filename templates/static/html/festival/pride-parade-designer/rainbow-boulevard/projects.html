Built work · 2019–2025

Nine things
that went down a streetEach one with the constraint that shaped it, because the constraint is the design. Filter by what was built.

62 floats in total; nine that taught the shop something. Every drawing below is mine, made after the build, from the measurements on the truck.

2025Float 58

The unbolting crown

The Kanteerava signal is 5.2 m. The group wanted a crown that read as a crown from 40 m. So the top 1.1 m comes off in two panels, pinned with four bolts, and goes back on in ninety seconds on the other side.

9.4 × 2.5 m16 build days24 riders

2025Contingent

Sangama walking block

Eighteen people, one banner, one whistle. The whole design is a spacing diagram: four ranks of four plus two at the back who close the gap when the rank ahead gets swallowed by the crowd.

18 walkers3 rehearsalsno vehicle

2024Float 52

Twenty-six metres

A banner too wide for the road, so it was sewn in six panels and carried at a shallow arc by forty people who rehearsed the dip at every traffic signal. The cloth is now in a legal aid office.

26 m cloth40 carriers2 rehearsals

2023Float 49

Princess Street cart

Five and a half metres of carriageway and a 5.4 m turn. The answer was to stop designing a float and design a cart: 3.5 m long, two people on the shafts, canopy of stitched bedsheets, and it turned.

3.5 × 1.4 mhand-pulled9 build days

2022Float 44

The tempo-van cart

Two towns, no workshop, no truck. The cart breaks into seven pieces that fit a rented tempo van, and goes back together in fifty minutes with a spanner and four people. Both marches were firsts.

3.0 × 1.3 m7 parts50 min assembly

2023Float 47

The quiet float

Deck at half a metre with a 1:8 ramp at the rear, a rail at 1.1 m, no amplification, and a shaded half. Six wheelchair users rode it for the full 2.6 km instead of waiting at the side with a steward.

1:8 rampno sound6 riders

2024Costume

Forty degrees

120 wearers, cotton voile at 120 g, no synthetic lining, a peaked cap instead of a wig, and a sash that doubles as a flag. Washed and dried overnight in a hotel bathroom; still wearable the next day.

120 wearers6 sizes1 sash = 1 flag

2024Staging

Two stages, one generator

The speeches never finish before the march arrives. So the second stage faces the dispersal point and runs quiet: a 4 kW generator, two speakers at 78 dB, and a schedule that hands over when the first float appears.

2 stages96 dB cap4 kW

2021Kit

The hinge kit

Two hinge plates, twelve M12 bolts, a spanner, a hacksaw and a crate. It has saved four floats at four different turns, and it is the cheapest thing in this portfolio.

2 plates12 bolts1 crate
