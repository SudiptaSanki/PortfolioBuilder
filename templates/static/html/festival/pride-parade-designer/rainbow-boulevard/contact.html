Thank you. I read briefs on Monday and Thursday mornings and answer within three working days. If your date is inside eight weeks, write “urgent” in the subject of a follow-up email and I will look that day.

Next step: a site visit, on the route, at march hour. Bring whoever signs the permission letter.

Before you write

Three things decide whether a float is possible: the date, the narrowest point on the route, and how many people have to stand on it. Everything else is negotiation.

If you have never done this

Write anyway, with whatever you know. First marches are half the shop's work, and the first thing I will send you is a one-page list of the permissions you need and the order to ask for them. It is free and it has saved more parades than any float.

Access: the shed has a level entrance and a wide door. If you need an interpreter for the site visit, say so and I will arrange one.
