Booking

Tell me the date and the moment you cannot lose

I take twelve to fifteen shoots a year. The dates that go first are the ones where the festival itself has a fixed hour: sandhi puja, the usha arghya, the maha deepam.

How a booking runs

Send the form. I reply within two days with two questions and a recce time. The recce is an hour at the venue at the hour of the event, and it is free; it is also where half the technical problems get solved before the day.

The quote comes after the recce, because a festival in a courtyard and the same festival on a ghat are not the same job.

A day
₹28,000 for one camera and edit on a festival day, including the recce and one revision. Second camera ₹9,000. Travel outside Kolkata at actuals.
Delivery
First cut in ten days, final in three weeks. Footage handed over on a drive you provide, or two drives I buy and bill at cost.
The seasons
Durga Puja booked by June. Chhath by September. Karthigai and Onam by August.
What I need
The date, the venue, whether there is power at the spot, and the one moment that must be in the film.
What I will not do
Direct a ritual, put up lights in a crowd, or promise the film before I have seen the venue.

If the festival is within 72 hours, call rather than write.
