CALL SHEET · 01 · DURGA PUJA

Durga Puja · 4-minute film

2 camera bodies, dusk into night, 35 minutes of footage for a four-minute cut

TAKE
02

    The technical trap

      Call 16:40 · card offload at 23:30 · backup on two drives before sleep

      What I bring

      Two bags, and a plan for when one fails

      • A

        Body and glass

        Two bodies that match, 24–70 f/2.8, 70–200 f/2.8, a 35 mm f/1.4 for the ghat before light, and a 14 mm for the lane that has no room.

      • B

        Sound

        Two lavs, one shotgun with a softie, and a recorder that runs from the moment we arrive. The dhaak is the soundtrack; the ambient is what makes the cut breathe.

      • C

        Power

        Six batteries per body, two power banks, and a charger with an inverter lead because the pandal supply will drop when the lights come on.

      • D

        Weather

        Two rain covers, three microfiber cloths, a dry bag for the ghat, and a filter I am willing to scratch with sindoor.
