Films

Twelve cuts, and what each one cost to get

Every film here was shot in conditions nobody would choose: a lane with no light, a ghat at 3 a.m., a crowd that does not stop for a camera. The runtime is the finished cut, not the footage.

Durga Puja · 20254:12

Sandhi puja, Bagbazar

Camera and edit · commissioned by Bagbazar Sarbojanin

Two bodies, one on the courtyard floor and one on the balcony opposite. The last ninety seconds carry no picture of the priest at all — only the crowd, because that is where the noise of the bell comes from.

Durga Puja · 202411:20

Nineteen years of one man’s pandal

Camera, sound and edit · self-commissioned

A single-subject record of the puja secretary, from the first bamboo delivery in July to the last bag of straw in October. Fourteen interview minutes, shot across three visits, and the festival as he sees it from the ladder.

Chhath · 202411:40

Four ghats, one sunrise

Camera and edit · commissioned by the Sinha family, Patna

From the 03:30 walk to the usha arghya at 04:40, then the quiet hour after. The family has bathed at the same ghat for four generations; the film is meant for the fifth, who was born in Toronto.

Chhath · 20230:58

The offering, vertical

Camera and edit · for a community account

A vertical cut made from the same footage, shot separately at 4K 50. Two hands, one lamp, the river. It has been watched more than anything else I have made, which says something about where festival films now live.

Ganesh visarjan · 20243:38

The lane is too narrow

Camera and edit · commissioned by Lalbaug mandal

An idol that needs eleven men to turn a corner, filmed from two positions: one walking backwards, one standing at the corner for the forty minutes it takes. The film cuts between them and never once moves the camera itself.

Ganesh visarjan · 20236:05

After the water

Camera and edit · self-commissioned

What happens from 23:30: the trucks, the sand, the men sitting on the tailgate, the flower petals coming back in on the next wave. A film about the ten minutes after the loudest moment of the year.

Onam · 20255:20

Pookalam at six

Camera and edit · commissioned by Thrissur Pooram foundation

The flower carpet is laid in flat dawn light, which is the hardest light there is. Knee height, one hand in frame for scale, and the sound of the courtyard waking up underneath it.

Onam · 20242:44

Pulikali, the paint and the drum

Camera and edit · commissioned by a troupe from Swaraj Round

Charcoal and turmeric on a body, then the same body in the street. Shot close with a 55 mm because the paint is the point; the drummers are in the same frame for the last thirty seconds.

Onam · 20238:10

The sadya for four hundred

Camera, sound and edit · commissioned by a temple committee

One serving line, four hundred leaves, ninety minutes. Filmed overhead and at leaf height with a single camera, because the kitchen could not take a second person in it.

Karthigai · 20257:30

The flame on the hill

Camera and edit · commissioned by a Tiruvannamalai trust

Ten thousand lamps at the gate, then the maha deepam at 18:00 shot on a 200 mm from the eastern path. Two exposures of the same minute: one for the flame, one for the hill, and the crowd sound joining them.

Karthigai · 20244:50

Girivalam, 14 km

Camera · edit by Meghna Rao

One body, one 35 mm, walking backwards for the first two hours and then alongside. The cut is built on footsteps and the sound of the hill at night, not on music.

Durga Puja · 202216:40

Five days in a lane

Camera and edit · family archive

The first long-form film I made: Saptami to Dashami in a single lane off Bidhan Sarani, with the same six people in front of the camera throughout. Grainy, overlong, and still the one I send people to watch first.

What is not here

Client work I cannot show, two weddings I would rather forget, and the years of footage that sits on drives waiting for someone to pay for an edit. If you want to see the raw logs — the ungraded five-minute takes with the mistakes in them — ask; it is a better way to judge a camera operator than a reel.
