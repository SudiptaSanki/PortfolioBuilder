Based in Pune. Travels where the festivals are — Kolkata for Puja, Madurai for Pongal, the Hooghly bank for Chhath. Travel is charged at cost and agreed before booking, never after.

What to include

The festival, the place, the first day's date — and who holds the organiser's pen: the person who can say yes to conditions, no-film zones and dates. Four lines is plenty.

When to write

The autumn season — Ganesh, Durga Puja, Diwali — books out by August, and visarjan dates go first. If your festival is sooner than the planner allows, you will be told that plainly rather than booked hopefully. Run the planner first →
