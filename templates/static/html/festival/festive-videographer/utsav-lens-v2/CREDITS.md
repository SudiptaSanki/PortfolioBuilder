## Design record
- **Frame: F14 IMMERSIVE DARK GALLERY** — near-black ground throughout; festivals hung as
  large frames in rooms with oversized section words; the paperwork (tables, planner,
  estimator, fees, terms) sits in one deliberately quieter dim-ruled zone, the "office door".
- CSS spec (per the CSS-layer diversity addendum): full-bleed gallery container (text measure
  1240px); borderless frames with hairline caption rules — no shadow cards, no hover-lift;
  effects vocabulary limited to dim-to-lit opacity reveals (gallery lights) and the one pair of
  opposing-direction rows (Night Processions vs Dawn Rituals — a genuine contrast, one pair only).
- **Ground level only:** the practice is the videographer inside the festival. No drone
  content anywhere (the stub's drone project was removed); the scoreboard prints "0 drone flights".
- **Sound ruling: NONE.** Festival sound belongs to the festival; the site's films are the
  organisers' property and are not embedded. The stub's ambient audio behaviour was removed.
  The ruling is printed in the footer of every page.
- The dry-brush edge was considered and not used: the gallery's ruled hairlines are this
  template's honesty language, and a torn edge would fight the dark-gallery frame.
