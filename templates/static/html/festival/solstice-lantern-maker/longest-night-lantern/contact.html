After this date I stop taking solstice orders, however kind the letter. The bench has one pair of hands.

You asked about lanterns. I will reply to your email within two bench days with making days, a collection date, and the deposit figure — worked from the same printed tables as the planner, not from a mood.

If the solstice book has closed by the time your letter lands, the reply will say so plainly and offer the January bench instead. Keep the reference; quote it and I can find your letter in the pile in seconds.
