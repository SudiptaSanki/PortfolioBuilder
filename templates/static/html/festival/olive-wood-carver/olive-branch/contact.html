Small pieces — spoons, pendants, mezuzot — are often already in the finish room and can be sent within two weeks. Bowls and boards are cut to order, and large pieces are cut only when the right blank is dry.

Can you use wood from my own tree?

If it is olive and dry, sometimes. Send a photograph of a cut end and tell me how long it has been down. Green wood will crack, and I will not pretend otherwise.

What does shipping cost?

From Bethlehem, a bowl of 25 cm to Europe is usually $28–$40 tracked; to North America, $38–$55. Exact cost is quoted with the piece, and I do not mark it up.

Do you take returns?

Yes, within 30 days, undamaged, for a full refund of the piece. Return shipping is yours unless the piece was faulty. A piece that has cracked in the first year is repaired or replaced at my cost.

Can I visit before ordering?

Yes. The workshop is open Tuesday to Saturday, nine to four, off Star Street. Write first if there are more than four of you.

Do you make nativity sets?

I make them in the autumn only, from offcuts, and they sell out by December. If you want one for this year, write in September.
