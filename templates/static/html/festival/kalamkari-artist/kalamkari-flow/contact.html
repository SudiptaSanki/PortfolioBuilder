Send me the measure of the wall, not a photograph of a room

I need three things to say yes properly: how big the cloth can be, where it will hang, and what you want it to hold. Everything else we can decide by post.

This puts you in the conversation, not in the queue.

I answer by post or email within four days. If I take the work, a third of the price buys the cloth and the rest is paid when it is washed and dry.

The waiting list

Eleven weeks from the sketch, not from this message. A panel takes six to eight weeks; a sari nine to twelve; a temple hanging is agreed separately and takes as long as it takes.

Two saris a year and no more, because a sari is drawn standing at the frame and my back is older than it was.

What to send

Measure
Width and height of the wall space, in metres
Light
Whether the wall takes morning or evening sun
Motif
A photograph of anything you have seen and liked
Date
An occasion, or a month, not a promise

Care, honestly

Wash in cold water with a little soap nut, dry in shade, and never wring it. The first three washes will bleed a little red; after that it settles for years. If it fades badly, send it back and I will re-dye it once, free.

Post and payment

Rolled in muslin, inside a PVC tube, insured and tracked. Inside India the post is included; elsewhere at cost.

A third to begin, the rest on the photograph before posting.
