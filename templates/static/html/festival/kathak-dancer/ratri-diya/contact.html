What the venue needs to know

A solo programme needs a stage of five by four metres minimum, a wooden floor if possible, a live tabla player, and one lighting cue per piece. I bring my own bells and my own mat.

If you have never programmed Kathak

Say so in the message, and I will send a one-page note on what the audience will see and when to clap — which is not during the nritta, and not between the bol and its answer. It has helped more first-time presenters than any contract.

The studio is up one flight of stairs. If you need a ground-floor space for a workshop, say so and we will find one.
