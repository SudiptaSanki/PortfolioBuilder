Performances · 2019–2025

Nine evenings,
in the order
they happenedEach with the hall, the taal the evening was built on, and the thing that went wrong. There is always something.

216 performances in total. Nine that changed how I build a programme, which is the only reason to list them.

Show
2025Solo

Bharat Bhavan, Bhopal

Seventy minutes with the thumri singer Meera Joshi. The opening nritta ran to eleven minutes, longer than the programme note said, because the hall had not settled and I could hear it not settling.

70 minTeen Taallive tabla

2024Solo

Kamani Auditorium, Delhi

The Ada Chautal programme with pakhawaj — a full evening built on a fourteen-matra taal, which leaves no room for a slow opening. The hall's air conditioning was louder than the pakhawaj in the first four minutes.

65 minAda Chautalpakhawaj

2024Festival

Khajuraho Dance Festival

Open air, one lamp, and a stone stage that takes the foot differently from wood — the tatkar was almost silent until I adjusted the ankle on the second piece. After that the bells did the work.

45 minTeen Taalstone stage

2022Solo

The Aminabad room

Thirty invited people, in the room where I trained, three weeks before it was sold and turned into a phone shop. We danced on the cement one last time. The fan still turned too slowly.

40 minTeen Taal30 people

2023Lecture-demo

SPIC MACAY, Pune

Ninety minutes for four hundred students, half of whom had never seen Kathak. I taught them to count Teen Taal out loud, then danced the same sixteen matras so they could hear what they had counted.

90 minTeen Taal400 students

2023Festival

Varanasi, Sankat Mochan

A courtyard with no microphone and a lamp column for light. The audience sat on the steps, which put their eyes at the level of my feet — the best possible audience for nritta and the worst for abhinaya.

50 minJhap Taalno microphone

2025Theatre

Ranga Shankara, Bengaluru

A black box with the audience on three sides. Abhinaya has nowhere to hide when the front row is a metre away; I cut the padam that needs distance and kept the one that does not.

55 minRupakthree sides

2024Theatre

NCPA, Mumbai

A proscenium with one pool of light and a lighting operator who understood what a khali is. The third piece was danced almost in the dark on purpose, and nobody in the hall coughed.

60 minDadraone pool

2021Lecture-demo

Workshop, Lucknow

Twelve people, four hours, no mirrors. Everyone counted Teen Taal out loud until they could do it without counting, and then we put the bells on and found out who had been faking.

4 hTeen Taal12 people
