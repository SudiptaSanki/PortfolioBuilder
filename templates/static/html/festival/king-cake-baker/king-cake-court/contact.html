Before you order

How far ahead should I order?

Forty-eight hours in Carnival season, twenty-four the rest of the year. In the last week before Mardi Gras the book closes when the ovens are full, which is often the Friday before. Praline cakes need seventy-two hours.

Can I pick it up on Mardi Gras day?

Yes, from 7 am until we sell out, usually by noon. Parade routes close Magazine Street to cars, so come on foot or by bike. Bring a box strap; a Balcony ring is heavier than it looks.

Is the baby safe for children?

The baby is food-safe plastic wrapped in paper and pushed in after baking. We can leave it in a small bag on the side if you prefer, which many schools ask for. Tell us in the message.

Do you deliver?

Within five miles, by bicycle, on the morning of pickup, for fifteen dollars. Book it with the order. We do not ship: a king cake is bread, and three days in a box is not the same cake.

Can I change or cancel?

Up to twenty-four hours before pickup, yes, by phone. After the dough is mixed, we can change the icing but not the size. If we have to cancel your slot, you pay nothing.

Bringing it home

Keep the box flat on the floor of the car, not the seat, and not on your lap while someone else drives. A ring slides.

Eat within a day. If you must keep it, wrap the slices and warm them for four minutes at 150 °C; the glaze will forgive you.

Schools & offices

Six rings or more in one order: one invoice, one pickup time, and the babies bagged on the side if you ask. Call the counter rather than using the form.
