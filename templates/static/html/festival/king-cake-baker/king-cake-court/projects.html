Good to know

Ordering, pickup and the last week of Carnival.

The order book opens on 6 January and closes when the ovens are full, usually the Friday before Mardi Gras. Pickup is at the window on Magazine Street from 7 am; we hold a cake until 4 pm and then it goes to the counter, first come. Delivery by bicycle within five miles is fifteen dollars and must be booked with the order.

Allergens are listed per cake. The kitchen handles wheat, dairy, egg, pecan, soy and sesame, so we cannot promise a trace-free ring. A baby is wrapped in food-safe paper and pushed in from the bottom; tell us if you would rather have it in a little bag on the side, which some schools require.
