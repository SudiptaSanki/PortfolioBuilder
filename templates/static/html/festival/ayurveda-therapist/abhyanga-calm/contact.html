How to arrive

From the airport: 40–55 minutes by taxi; ask for Kunnumpuram, not Fort Kochi beach.

By ferry: from Ernakulam jetty to Fort Kochi, then a seven-minute walk. The 6:20 boat gets you here with time to spare.

By car: the lane is one-way from Bazaar Road. Park by the school wall and walk the last thirty metres.

When you hear back

Notes sent before 6am get an answer the same morning. After that, the next working day, usually by six in the evening. If it is urgent and clinical, call rather than writing.
