Six things I do,
and what they cost.

Every fee below includes the oil, the steam, the laundry and a written routine to take home. Nothing is added at the desk afterwards. Courses are paid by the treatment, not in advance, because a body that has had enough should be allowed to stop.

01The menu

What a session is for

Abhyanga with svedana

₹2,400 90 minutes · first visit or general

The full sequence: pulse, oil, steam, bath, twenty minutes of rest. Suits exhaustion, dry skin, poor sleep, a body that has been sitting since March. If you are unsure where to start, start here.

Mahanarayana for joints

₹2,800 75 minutes · course of 4–7

Sesame cooked with bala, ashwagandha and forty herbs, worked into the joint in small circles, then localised steam with a bolus of medicated rice. For a knee that has been x-rayed and found ordinary, an old shoulder injury, a stiff lower back.

Not on a joint that is hot, swollen or recently injured. Not in the first week after a steroid injection.

Shirodhara

₹2,600 45 minutes · course of 3–7

A thread of warm oil poured from a copper vessel onto the forehead, at a steady height and a steady rate, with the head held between sandbags. For insomnia, migraine, and the specific exhaustion of people who cannot stop thinking.

Not during a migraine attack, a fever, or a head cold with blocked sinuses.

Nasya

₹1,200 30 minutes · one-off or 3-day course

Medicated drops into the nose after a short face and neck massage, then a gargle. Classical treatment for sinus congestion, chronic headache, and the head that feels like a wet towel. Morning only, before food.

Not within two weeks of a nosebleed or nasal surgery, and not during pregnancy.

Udvartana

₹1,800 60 minutes · course of 5–10

Warm herbal powder in place of oil, rubbed upward with the grain of the skin, then steam. For heaviness, sluggish digestion and the specific stiffness of a kapha constitution in the monsoon. It is rough by design; say the word and I ease off.

Not on a body that has just had abhyanga, and not on eczema or psoriasis in flare.

Sutika — after childbirth

₹26,000 14 days · 60–90 min daily

A fixed course, booked in two blocks of seven: abhyanga with a warming oil, a tight udaravestra band around the abdomen, and food guidance written for the month. Best started between the eighth and fourteenth day after birth, with your obstetrician informed.

With a caesarean, nothing near the scar until it is fully closed and the surgeon has said so.

Consultation: first visits include twenty minutes of nadi pariksha and conversation, charged as part of the treatment rather than beside it. A 20-minute room visit with no treatment is free. Fees are in Indian rupees and have not changed since 2024; cash, UPI and bank transfer at the door.

02How a course runs

Seven days, and what changes

A course is not seven identical treatments. The oil, the pressure and the length change as the body answers. This is the usual shape of a week; yours will differ, and the notes will say how.

Typical seven-day course structure for joint treatment
DayOilPressureWhat I am watching for
1–2Mahanarayana, warmLight, broadDoes the skin accept the oil, or sit under it? Any redness that stays after twenty minutes.
3–4Mahanarayana + local bolusMedium, into the jointWhether the joint is warmer to the back of my hand than it was on day one.
5Mahanarayana, longer steamMedium, with movementRange before and after — I measure with a goniometer, not an opinion.
6Ksheerabala, coolerLightSleep and stiffness on waking. This is the day people report the change.
7Sesame, plainLight, then restWhat holds without the treatment. The written routine is adjusted here.

If nothing has changed by the fourth treatment, I will say so and we will stop. Seven good sessions in a row tell you more than fourteen indifferent ones, and the money is better in your pocket than in my ledger.

03Before you book

Small print worth reading

What to wear. Nothing under the towel, and a cotton change of clothes to go home in. Oil stays on the skin for two hours after a treatment, so bring the shirt you do not love.

What to eat. A light meal two hours before, nothing after except water. A full stomach and a steam box are a bad combination and I will send you home to come back another day.

What to tell me. Every medicine, including the ones you do not think of as medicine. Blood thinners, thyroid tablets, steroids and antidepressants all change what I do with pressure and heat.

Who I send elsewhere. Suspected fracture, unexplained weight loss, a new lump, fever, chest pain, sudden weakness or numbness, a pregnancy that has not yet been cleared by your obstetrician. This is a treatment room, not a diagnostic service.

Cancellations. A day’s notice, or half the fee. The steam box takes forty minutes to come up to temperature and the room is booked for you alone.

Late arrivals. Up to fifteen minutes, the treatment is shortened. Beyond that, it is a cancellation, because rushing abhyanga is worse than skipping it.

Languages. Malayalam, English, Tamil and enough Hindi to explain where it will hurt.

Not sure which one you need?

Tell me what has changed and when it started. I will tell you honestly whether this is an ayurvedic problem, a physiotherapy problem, or one for a physician — and I have a physiotherapist and two physicians I send people to.
