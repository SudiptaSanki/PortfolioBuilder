Walls Practice Commissions Write to me

Send me a photograph of the wall with something for scale.

A chair, a bicycle, a person standing against it. That one photograph tells me the surface, the light and half the design. The rest is four answers.

The form checks the four answers and prepares an email. Nothing is sent from this page.

What happens next

I read email on Fridays and Mondays, because the rest of the week is usually on a wall. If you have sent a photograph, I will reply with a size, a season and a rough range before we talk.

What to photograph

  • The whole wall, straight on, with a person or chair at the base.
  • One close shot of the surface, dry, in daylight.
  • The wall from across the street, so I can see who walks past it.
  • Any damp patch, crack or repaired section, photographed separately.

How the quote works

Area × a rate for the surface and finish, plus scaffold days and travel. The rate covers lime, sand, pigment, brushes and my time, not the wall's repair. If the wall needs plastering first, I will say so and quote it separately, or tell you which mason to call.

If it is a community wall

Schools, markets and village halls are quoted at cost plus a fee we agree before the first coat. I ask for a caretaker who will own the wall afterwards, and a conversation about what happens when it gets written on. Because it will.
