What happens next

Every enquiry gets a real reply, not an automated one. If the date is open, the reply includes a quote for the band size that fits the room, and the date is held for seven days without a deposit.

Response
Within two business days, usually the same evening
Quote includes
Band fee, travel inside 100 miles, one sound check, and the rehearsal of one requested tune
Not included
Hall hire, piano, stage lighting, or a second requested tune at short notice
Deposit
One third on confirmation, refundable up to 30 days before the date
Travel
Ohio, Pennsylvania and Western New York as standard; further by arrangement
If the date is sooner than two weeks: call rather than write. The phone is answered between 10:00 and 18:00, Tuesday to Saturday, and a short-notice date is sometimes possible.

m. 19

Before you write

Three things that make the first reply accurate instead of hopeful.

01

The room

Hall, barn, tent or living room, and roughly how many people will be dancing rather than sitting. A hall for three hundred with forty dancers needs a different band.

02

The shape of the day

Ceremony, dinner, first dance, and the hour the hall has to be empty. The set list is built backwards from the last polka.

03

The one tune

If there is a tune the family expects, name it. It will be learned properly and played in the right key, which is not always the key on the recording.
