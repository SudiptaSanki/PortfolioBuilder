A few nights that were worth writing down, out of roughly forty a year. The interesting ones are rarely the biggest.

2026

Fifty years of the parish hall

An anniversary dance for a hall that has hosted the same families since 1976. Three generations of dancers, a potluck that ran the length of the room, and a request for the 1952 waltz that took two months to track down.

2025

Barn wedding, no electricity

The couple wanted no amplification and no backing tracks. The trio played acoustically under a tin roof in the rain, which turned out to be the best natural reverb in the county. The ceremony marched to a waltz instead of the usual.

2025

Summer festival, main tent

Four sets across two days with a different band configuration each night: solo for the Sunday polka breakfast, five-piece for the Saturday headline. The floor never emptied, which is the only review that counts.

2024

Tuesday class, first recital

Nine students, ages fourteen to seventy-one, played a short set in the back room of a Slovenian hall. Two had never played in front of anyone. The room held forty people and the applause lasted longer than the set.

2023

Two weeks in Prekmurje

A return visit to the villages where the Cleveland repertoire came from: dance nights, a wedding, and a long afternoon with a ninety-year-old player who still kept the old bass patterns. Six tunes came home in a notebook.

2021

Porch concerts, eighteen of them

When the halls closed, the music went to the porches. Eighteen short concerts on eighteen streets, one per week through the summer, with a hat for the parish food pantry. The neighbours brought chairs and stayed for the whole thing.
