Four kinds of order, and the one we say no to

Ofrenda

Día de Muertos

Banners of calaveras, cempasúchil and papel picado in the four colours of the season, cut to the width of your arch or your altar. Orders close on 20 October; after that the stack is full.

From 7 pennants · 3 weeks

Weddings

Names and dates cut into the paper, not printed onto it. Two banners for the entrance, one long run for the patio, and a small sheet for the table. Proof by photograph before the stack is cut.

From 12 pennants · 4 weeks

Calle

Street and festival

Long runs for a whole street, in weatherproof tissue or in plastic for the rain. The taller has hung 400 metres of banner for a single feria, in six colours, in five days.

From 40 metres · 2 weeks

Taller

Workshops

Saturdays in Huixcolotla, six people to a table, chisels and paper included. You leave with a one-metre banner you cut yourself and the blister to prove it. In Spanish, and in English by arrangement.

4 hours · 6 places · $1,200

05 · The taller

Forty sheets, one design, one chisel at a time

The paper comes from a mill in San Luis Potosí, in 50 by 35 centimetre sheets, and is folded into a stack of forty. The pattern is drawn on the top sheet, then the stack is cut together with a chisel and a hammer: one strike, forty sheets, the same hole. That is why a handmade banner costs what it does and why no two stacks are quite identical.

My grandfather cut in the plaza when the town was the only place in Mexico making papel picado for sale. My mother took the orders by telephone for thirty years. I took over the table in 2014, added the plastic for the rain, and kept the chisels.
