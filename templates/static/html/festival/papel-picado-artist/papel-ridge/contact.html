How a commission runs

  1. You send the run length, the date and a photograph of the place.
  2. The taller replies with a motif, a price and the week it can be cut.
  3. A 50 per cent deposit holds the slot in the stack.
  4. A photograph of the top sheet goes out for approval before the stack is cut.
  5. The banner is packed flat, with a spare pennant, and shipped or collected.

Prices

$180 MXN a pennant, motif cut by hand, up to 88 cm wide. $450 MXN setup for a new pattern, including the drawing and one proof. Names cut into a border, $40 each. Minimum order seven pennants. Shipping is at cost; flat-packed runs under fifteen pennants go in a tube.

The season

Ofrenda orders close on 20 October, and the taller cuts in the order deposits arrive. Wedding runs need four weeks. Street runs need two, or five for anything over a hundred metres. In November and December the table is full of posadas and the answer may be no.

Visiting and workshops

The taller is the front room of the house on Calle 5 de Mayo, open Saturdays 10–14. Workshops are six people, four hours, $1,200, and you leave with a one-metre banner. Book by writing; the Saturday list fills three weeks out.
