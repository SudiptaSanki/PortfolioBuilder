Featured Film · Case File 01

Nine nights of circles, filmed from outside them

Navratri Garba Aerials · Ahmedabad, Gujarat · Oct 2024

The circles are the film. The edges are where we stood.

Three evenings over a garba ground of several thousand dancers. Launch points outside the ground’s four corners; the camera worked the roofline and the margins on a telephoto, so the circles read whole without the aircraft ever crossing them. The sortie planner below said 8 flights an evening — we flew 8, 8 and 7.

“He sent us the flight plan before he sent us a showreel. On the night, that is exactly the order you want things in.”

— Meera Krishnan, Festival Director, the organising committee (client for Case File 01)

Every booking starts with these two sheets. The planner works from the tables printed on it — the same figures we plan real flights with — and it is allowed to tell you no.

How T1 is built, in the open: planning length = stated endurance × wind factor (Table T2) × 0.80 landing reserve, rounded down. T2 — Calm under 10 km/h ×1.00 · Breeze 10–20 km/h ×0.85 · Gusty 20–30 km/h ×0.70 · Over 30 km/h sustained, or gusts over 38 km/h: no flight. Ground turnaround per battery swap: 6 minutes. Duty limit: 10 sorties per aircraft per day. This practice fields two aircraft.
