Case File 01 · Festival Aerials

Nine nights, one circle — Navratri garba aerials

Event
Navratri garba, open ground
Place
Ahmedabad, Gujarat
Dates
3 evenings · Oct 2024
Client
Festival organising committee

The Brief

Film the garba ground for the festival’s archive and sponsor film. Several thousand dancers move in concentric circles for four hours a night; the committee wanted the circles themselves — the pattern only the air can see — without a drone ever becoming part of the evening.

The Decisions

Launch points were set outside the ground’s four corners, on ground the committee fenced for us. The aircraft worked the roofline and the margins on a telephoto lens, so the circles read whole from standoff — it never crossed the crowd, at any height, on any night. Coverage was planned at 4 hours an evening on Endurance packs in a light breeze: the planner’s arithmetic (30-minute planning sorties) said 8 sorties a night, and the flight plan was written to exactly that.

The Outcome

We flew 8, 8 and 7 sorties — the third evening ended early when the programme did, and the log says so rather than rounding up. Delivered: an 11-minute festival film and a 90-second sponsor cut. The committee rebooked for the following season before the invoice was paid, which the log also says.

“He sent us the flight plan before he sent us a showreel. On the night, that is exactly the order you want things in.”

— Meera Krishnan, Festival Director, the organising committee — on Case File 01

Case File 02 · Procession Routes

Following the procession without joining it

Event
Ganesh Visarjan procession
Place
Pune, Maharashtra · 2.6 km route
Dates
1 day · Sep 2024
Client
City festival trust

The Brief

A route film of the immersion procession: the column of dhol-tasha pathaks, floats and devotees moving through the old city to the river — filmed as one journey, for the trust’s archive and the city’s festival film.

The Decisions

A procession is a moving subject crossing other people’s streets, so the route was surveyed on foot first and five launch points were agreed with the trust: side streets and two rooftops, all off the route itself. The method is leapfrog — film a stretch from standoff, land, move ahead of the column, relaunch. The camera crossed the route only high over rooftops; it never travelled along or above the column. One surveyed launch point was abandoned on the day when the crowd spilled into its side street. That shot does not exist, and this page is where that is recorded.

The Outcome

Fourteen sorties across the day, two aircraft rotating so the column was never waiting on us. Delivered: a 6-minute route film that reads as one continuous journey, plus the full sortie log — launch points, times, batteries — handed to the trust with the footage, as the terms promise.

Case File 03 · Venue Films · The Grounded Day

The day we didn’t fly — a lakeside venue film

Event
Wedding venue reveal film
Place
Lake Pichola side, Udaipur, Rajasthan
Dates
2 mornings · Feb 2025
Client
The venue, via its wedding planner

The Brief

A reveal film for a lakeside wedding venue: the approach over the water at dawn, the palace front, the grounds dressed for a wedding — booked for the morning the venue was dressed and empty, before the guests arrived.

The Decisions

At 6 a.m. the lake wind was 34 km/h sustained, gusting 41 — over the limit printed in Table T2, the same limit the planner on the home page enforces. The grounded-day clause was invoked before the aircraft left its case, and the client was told at breakfast, not after lunch. The day was not wasted: ground gimbal cameras covered the venue dressing, the floral work and the empty mandap — footage the film needed anyway. The aerial window moved to the next dawn, which dawned calm.

The Outcome

The film opens with the dawn flight over the water, exactly as briefed — one day later than planned. The pricing is printed here because it was printed on the invoice: the grounded day was billed at the half-day ground rate, the re-flown dawn at the half-day aerial rate, and the permission administration was charged once, not twice. Nobody remembers the grounded day except this page and that invoice — which is where it belongs.
