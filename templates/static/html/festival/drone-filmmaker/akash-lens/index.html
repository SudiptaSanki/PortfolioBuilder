F-001What I do

A film, not a folder of clips.

Most drone work arrives as a memory card and a shrug. I would rather deliver a cut: a minute and a half that has a first shot, a turn and an ending, with the raw files beside it. That means I ask about the story before I ask about the location, and I turn down jobs where the only brief is “make it epic”.

01 / reveal

Estate & landscape

Coffee, tea, cardamom, stone quarries, dams. A slow pull-back from a single leaf to a valley, flown at first light when the mist still holds the contours. Two flights, one cut, graded warm.

02 / orbit

Architecture

Heritage hotels, private houses, resorts on a slope. Orbits at 35 m, a vertical rise along a facade, and a 20-second hold that an architect can actually use in a pitch. Flown with prop guards indoors.

03 / track

Movement & documentary

Bridges, highways, processions, a boat at dawn. FPV where the subject moves, a stabilised long lens where it does not. Every flight has a spotter and a second battery on the ground.

SEQ 03 · equipment

The kit that leaves the bag.

primary

DJI Mavic 3 Cine

6K ProRes 422 HQ, four-thirds sensor, 46 minutes. The workhorse for anything that needs to be graded later. Three batteries, two chargers, one very careful bag.

fpv

Custom 5″ FPV

For tracking a moving subject through trees or down a corridor. Flown in manual only with a spotter; a crashed FPV costs less than a lost day.

glass

ND 4–32 & polariser

South Indian light at 07:00 is already two stops hotter than the camera wants. A neutral density set is not an accessory; it is the difference between a film and a video.

safety

Licence, logger, insurance

DGCA remote pilot certificate, Digital Sky permissions for every site, a flight logger that writes to the card, and third-party liability cover to ₹10 lakh.
