What a delivery includes

  • The cut: one graded film, 60–120 seconds, in the aspect ratio you asked for, plus a vertical version for social if the brief needs it.
  • The raw files: every usable clip, on a drive you keep, with the flight logs beside them.
  • Ten stills: pulled at full resolution from the 6K frames, graded to match.
  • The paper: permission reference, flight log, insurance certificate and the risk assessment, in one PDF.

Turnaround is seven working days for a one-day shoot and fourteen for a documentary sequence. Rush delivery is possible and costs 25% more, because it means cutting at night after a dawn flight.

What changes the price

Altitude and remoteness, a second operator, night-for-day grading, a monument permission, or a subject that will not hold still. All of it is quoted before, never after.
