Nine things on the board, and four months when they change.

The board behind the counter is chalk, rewritten every morning. Thandai and kulfi are always there; the sharbats arrive with the heat in April and leave when the rain does. Nothing is made in advance of the day it is sold.

02

For a table of forty

Weddings and festival mornings are the bulk of the summer trade. A ten-litre pot serves about fifty small glasses; we deliver in clay with a deposit, and collect the pots within three days.

Two things decide whether a bulk order works. The first is the notice: forty-eight hours for anything above ten litres, because the nuts have to soak. The second is the ice. We bring crushed ice in insulated boxes, but the last twenty minutes before serving are yours to manage, and a hot room will beat a cold drink every time.
