Batch order

How it arrives

  • Clay pots for the first chill, sealed with muslin
  • Crushed ice in an insulated box for the serving window
  • A paper slip with the batch time and the grind date
  • Pots collected within three days; ₹500 deposit for ten

What we ask

  • Forty-eight hours for ten litres or more
  • A week for Holi week and Shivratri
  • A shaded place to set the pots down on arrival
  • A phone number that answers on the morning

Payment

Half in advance for bulk orders, the rest on delivery. UPI, cash or a bank transfer. The deposit on the clay is separate and comes back with the pots.

Not sure how much

A 200 ml glass is the counter measure. Ten litres is about fifty glasses, allowing for a second pour.
