Holi & ShivratriKachori Gali

Cold milk, ground nuts, and four hours of patience.

Thandai is not a milkshake. The nuts and seeds are soaked overnight, ground to a paste on stone, and strained twice through muslin before they ever meet milk. What comes out is pale, grainy at the edges, and colder than the fridge because it has been standing in a clay pot since five in the morning.

02

What the book says, per litre

Three things decide whether a thandai tastes like thandai: the ratio of almond to melon seed, the freshness of the fennel, and the second strain. Miss the second strain and the drink is a paste.

IngredientPer litreWhy
Almonds, soaked40 gThe body. Skin removed, or the thandai turns bitter.
Melon seeds (magaz)20 gThickens without flour. The cheaper shops use cornflour here.
Poppy seeds (khus-khus)10 gA faint coolness at the back of the throat. More than this and it turns soapy.
Fennel (saunf)8 gGround the same morning. A week-old grind tastes like cardboard.
Green cardamom6 podsSeeds only. The husk goes into the milk while it simmers.
Saffron0.4 gBloomed in a spoon of warm milk, never sprinkled dry.
Black pepper1 gOne pinch per litre. It is there to make the cold feel colder.
Sugar90 gDissolved in the milk while warm; never stirred into the cold batch.
Full-fat milk1 litreBoiled, then cooled to room temperature before the paste goes in.

The shop has never served the intoxicating version. If a customer asks for it, we point them to the tea stall at the corner and change the subject.

03

Four hours, in order

The grinding is the only part that cannot be rushed. Everything else is waiting, which is why the counter opens at seven and not at five.

  1. Night before

    Soak

    Almonds, magaz and poppy in separate bowls of water. The almonds are skinned in the morning by hand, a kilo at a time.

  2. 04:30

    Grind

    Stone grinder, coarse first, then fine with a little milk to keep it moving. Twenty minutes for a ten-litre batch; the machine is faster and the paste is warmer, which changes the taste.

  3. 05:15

    Strain twice

    Muslin, wrung out by hand. The second strain is through a finer cloth and is the difference between smooth and chalky.

  4. 06:00

    Chill

    Into clay pots, covered with wet cloth, in the cold room at 4 °C. Four hours minimum; the clay takes the edge off the sweetness.

  5. 10:00

    Serve

    Crushed ice in the glass first, thandai poured over it, pistachio and one rose petal on top. Never stir after pouring.

Ordering for a festival

Holi and Shivratri take orders up to forty litres. Tell me the date and the number of glasses; the clay pots go back with the delivery.
