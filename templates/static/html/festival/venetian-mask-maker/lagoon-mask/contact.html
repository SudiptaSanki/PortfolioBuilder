What happens next

1 · I answer within two working days, usually with questions before answers.
2 · For a commission you receive a sketch date and the reckoning from the bench table — the planner’s figures, never higher.
3 · You approve the sketch, then the clay, in photographs. Only then is a deposit asked for.
