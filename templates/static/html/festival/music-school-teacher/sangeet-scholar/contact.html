The trial hour

  • First 10We find your Sa at the tanpura
  • Next 30A simple palte, sung slowly
  • Last 20You sing a line of a bandish, and we listen

Seva seat. Two places each term are free for students who cannot pay, on a simple conversation, no paperwork. If that is you, say so in the note.

The room holds six students and four tanpuras. It is not a conservatoire, and that is deliberate.

After you write

Three ordinary steps.

01

Anjali replies Tuesday or Friday

She will ask what you have sung before and offer two trial times. No placement test, no recording to send. The trial itself is the placement.

02

You come and sing one hour, free

Bring nothing. You will sit with the tanpura, sing Sa, and try a line. If it feels too slow, that is the method working.

03

You decide within the week

If the room suits you and a seat is open, you join the batch. If not, Anjali will say so honestly, or put you on the waiting list for the next term.

The tanpura is tuned at 05:45.

If you come for the morning riyaz, ring the bell twice. The door is heavy and the stairs are steep, but the room is warm.
