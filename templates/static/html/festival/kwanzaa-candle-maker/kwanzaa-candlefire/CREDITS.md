## Everything else

The kinara mark is an original inline SVG drawn for this template.
No libraries, no frameworks, no audio. The persona (Imani Brooks of
"Candlefire", West End, Atlanta), her customers and her order book are
fictional, and the site footer says so. The Kwanzaa content — the Nguzo
Saba, the candle colours and their order in the kinara, the dates of the
festival — is factual and was written to be accurate.
