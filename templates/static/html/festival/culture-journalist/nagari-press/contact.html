Sources

Documents can be sent by post or handed over in person; I do not use a drop-box service and I do not confirm receipt over a public channel. If a source needs protection, say so in the first line and nothing else.

Corrections policy

Wrong facts are corrected and dated. Wrong emphasis is argued about in the letters page, not edited quietly. If I cannot establish which it is, the edition says that too.

Response

Corrections: same day. Tips: within three days, if there is something to check. Commissions: a week, because I read the clips first.
