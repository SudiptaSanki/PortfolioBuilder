Commissions

I take three or four commissioned features a year, usually for magazines and cultural institutions that want reporting rather than a brochure. Rates are on request and depend on the reporting required, not on the word count.

I do not write catalogue copy, advertorial, or the “in conversation” pieces that are really transcripts with a byline. I will happily edit a transcript, and say so in the credit.

Republishing

Short extracts with a link are fine. Full republication needs the original publisher's permission as well as mine, and I will tell you which of the two is likely to say no.
