02 / QUESTIONS

What people ask before commissioning.

Does it really glow, or is there a UV light in the tank?

It really glows. There is no UV, no fluorescent dye and no LED inside the culture. A blue flash comes from luciferin reacting with oxygen when the cells are disturbed. In a dark room it is bright enough to read a label by, briefly.

How long does a culture last?

A sealed 250 ml flask lasts about six weeks on a windowsill with no maintenance. A reactor with monthly care runs indefinitely, because we replace a quarter of the medium each month and re-count the cells. We have lines running since 2021.

Will it glow during the day?

No. The flash is under circadian control and only appears after at least two hours of darkness. In daylight the culture is a pale, slightly green suspension, which is not unattractive but is not the point.

Can I put it in a bright room?

You can, but the glow will be weak because ambient light suppresses the flash. The best rooms are dim after dark: bars, museums, bedrooms, corridors. We survey the light before quoting.

Is it safe around children and pets?

The organism is non-toxic and the reactors are sealed. A flask that is opened and spilled is a salty puddle, not a hazard, though it will smell. We do not recommend drinking it, obviously.

Do you ship outside the UK?

To the EU and Norway, with a phytosanitary declaration and a courier that handles live cultures. It is expensive and we will say so; for most overseas projects it is better to license the culture from a local lab.
