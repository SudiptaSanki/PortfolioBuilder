Selected work · 2022–2026

Five rooms that go dark and start to glow.

Live cultures, reactor walls and teaching kits. Every project below is still running under a maintenance agreement, and every figure is from the commissioning report.

2025 · RESTAURANT · ISLE OF SKYE

The Salt House

A six-reactor wall behind a bar, 40 L of culture, lit from 08:00 and stimulated every 20 minutes after the room lights drop. The brief was a wall that changed character at service, not a screen.

2024 · MUSEUM · EDINBURGH

After Dark

A night-time exhibit in a science centre, built around a 90 L reactor wall and a hand-cranked paddle. Visitors turn the crank, the culture flashes, and a counter shows how long the cells need to recover before they will do it again.

2023 · HOTEL · GLASGOW

The plunge room

A spa with no daylight and a strict no-noise rule. Four reactors sit behind a glass wall in the plunge pool room, on a 12:12 cycle, with the stirring set at 30 rpm to keep the sound below 32 dB.

2023 · RESEARCH · SCOTTISH ASSOCIATION FOR MARINE SCIENCE

Light-response study

A 12-week collaboration measuring how flash capacity changes with light history. We supplied six cultures grown at different irradiances and the lab ran the photometry. The finding was not surprising but it was useful: dim-grown cells recover faster and flash longer.

The classroom flask

A sealed 250 ml culture, a battery LED base and a lesson pack for secondary schools. The flask lasts six weeks on a windowsill with no maintenance, and the pack covers circadian rhythm, photosynthesis and why the flash is a defence.
