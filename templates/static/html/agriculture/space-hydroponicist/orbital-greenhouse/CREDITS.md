# Credits — Orbital Greenhouse (orbital-greenhouse)

Rebuilt by hand for the Template Fix Program, Set 5 (The Professions
& The Table). Persona: Dr Mara Ellison, controlled-environment
agriculture scientist, Wageningen — **fictional**, disclosed as such
in the footer of every page. The horticulture is real: DLI bands,
cycle lengths, EC/pH working ranges and the DLI formula
(PPFD × hours × 0.0036) follow published controlled-environment
agriculture practice and are hedged on the page where practice varies.

Space framing, honestly kept: the metadata role is "Astrobiological
Agriculture & Space Greenhouse Scientist". The build frames the space
work at the level the evidence supports — ground-based analogue
habitat programmes and growth-chamber research — and prints the
boundary on the site itself: nothing designed by the persona has
flown. No real agency, programme, company or person's name, work or
likeness is used or implied.
