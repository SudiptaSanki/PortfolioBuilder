What happens next

A reply within two working days, from the scientist, not an assistant. If the enquiry is a fit, the next step is a short call and then a written proposal with the fee from the printed rate card — the price you saw is the price quoted.

If it is not a fit — the crop is outside the register, the quarter's slots are full, the physics does not close — the reply says that, and where possible names who could help instead.
