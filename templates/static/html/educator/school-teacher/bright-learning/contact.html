What happens next

  • A reply from Clara within two school days — assessment times, not a sales call
  • The free 45-minute assessment lesson, in the teaching room or online
  • A written plan within three school days of the assessment
  • Lessons begin the following week, if you decide to go ahead

Before you write

It helps to have your child's most recent school report somewhere nearby — not to send now, just for the assessment conversation. If your child has an EHCP or specific learning needs, say so in the message: it changes the plan, not the welcome.
