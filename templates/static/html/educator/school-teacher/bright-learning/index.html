One lesson free, every term

Book a full term and one lesson in it is free: a 12-lesson term is charged as 11 lessons. The planner takes it off in front of you — it is a term price, not a voucher.

Cancellation, in plain words

Cancel by 6pm the day before and nothing is charged. Later than that, the lesson is payable — and every term includes one no-questions rearrangement for the week a child is ill.

Payment, without extras

Per term in advance, or monthly by standing order at the same total. No deposits for assessments, no card fees, no admin charges, no charge for materials.

How tuition runs

Four steps, in the same order for everyone.

Step 1

Assessment

A free 45-minute lesson. Gentle checks at your child's level, a look at recent school work, and a conversation about goals.

Step 2

Written plan

Within three school days: what we will work on, in what order, and how you will be able to tell it is working.

Step 3

Weekly lessons

Same time each week, in the teaching room or online. A two-line note home after every lesson, without exception.

Step 4

Term report

One page, in the last week of term: what was covered, what improved — with evidence — and what comes next.
