## Sound ruling — FAILS the need-test, none used

The stub created an AudioContext cash-register beep on page load and
promised "cart sound effects". A resale boutique's work is visual
inspection; sound teaches nothing here and autoplay-adjacent audio is a
deal-breaker pattern. Deleted, not rebuilt. The site is silent by design,
and says so in its footer.

## Honest replacements of stub promises

- "3D parallax product cards" → the Condition & Price Explorer: pick a
  category and grade and the listing sheet updates (definition, checks,
  price band, returns) from one data model that also matches the printed
  rate-card table.
- "Cart sound effects" → deleted (see sound ruling).
- "Floating animation badges" → the rotation calendar: real Thursdays,
  intake counts whose grade splits sum to the printed month total.
- The stub's hero sold a real, named product ("RED OCTOBERS", $1,250) with
  a hotlinked image. Both are gone: prices are the fictional shop's own,
  from its printed rate card, and stock is described by construction and
  colour, never by brand or release name.
- Authentication is described as a working process at practice level
  (provenance, construction, materials, labels & codes, UV light pass,
  blind second checker) — a method, not a guarantee of any real-world
  authentication service.
