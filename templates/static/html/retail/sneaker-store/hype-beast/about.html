The bench

Checked twice. Graded once, in writing.

I am Marcus Webb. I started selling my own collection from a market stall in 2016, mostly to fund the next pair; I opened Second Rotation in 2019 because the resale market ran on confidence and I wanted to run a shop on evidence instead. The shop now has two bench checkers besides me. Neither of them — and this is policy, not personality — is allowed to grade a pair they priced.

§ 01

The six steps

  1. 1

    Provenance

    Receipts, order confirmations and release records are checked where they exist. No paperwork is not an automatic fail — plenty of honest pairs have none — but it raises the bar on every later step.

  2. 2

    Construction

    Stitching density and consistency, glue lines, panel alignment and symmetry between the left and right shoe. Production shoes are consistent; inconsistencies are measured, not shrugged at.

  3. 3

    Materials

    Leather grain and hand, mesh weave, midsole foam density, insole print. Materials are where copies most often feel wrong before they look wrong.

  4. 4

    Labels & codes

    The size tag, production dates and factory codes are cross-checked against what the release is documented to carry — including the box label's typography and stock.

  5. 5

    Under light

    A UV pass looks for what daylight hides: sole swaps, paint-overs, repairs and replaced panels. Anything found here is written on the card, and usually ends the intake.

  6. 6

    The second checker

    A second person repeats steps 2–5 without seeing the first sheet. If the two sheets disagree, the pair waits until they agree — or it goes back. Both checkers sign the stock card; the card travels with the pair for life.

What a check costs

Intake checks for selling or consigning: free, including when we reject the pair. Authentication of a pair bought elsewhere: $20, with the full written sheet. The sheet is yours either way — a rejection you cannot read is just an opinion.

Storage & shipping

Stock is kept boxed with silica in a climate-controlled stockroom — no window displays cooking in the sun. Sold pairs ship double-boxed and tracked within 2 business days; insurance is included on orders over $150. Shipping is $12 flat, free over $150.

Grades are argued about in this trade, so ours are pinned to things you can verify: tread percentage, wear maps, photographs taken before listing. If you think a pair is graded generously, bring it back and we will re-check it at the bench with you watching — the sheet, not seniority, settles it.

§ 03

Terms, plainly

Buying

Prices come from the printed rate card and the pair's stock card; there is no “DM for price” and no price that changes at the till. Reservations hold a pair for 48 hours, free, once. The authenticity guarantee is lifetime and grade-independent: a pair shown to be not authentic is refunded in full, whenever the question is settled.

Selling & consigning

Consignment runs 60 days at an 80/20 split in your favour; unsold pairs are returned or re-priced only with your written agreement. Trade-in pays 65% of agreed value in credit (which never expires) or 55% in cash. Valuations are free and the check sheet is yours to keep, including when the answer is no.

What we will not do

We do not sell pairs we cannot authenticate, do not grade from photographs alone, do not hold stock back to manufacture scarcity, and do not run countdown timers. We also do not buy from sellers who cannot say where a pair came from — provenance is step one because it works.

The waitlist

Checking is the bottleneck and we keep it that way: intake is booked about a week out in busy months. If you are selling against a deadline, say so in the enquiry and we will tell you honestly whether the rail can take the pair in time.
