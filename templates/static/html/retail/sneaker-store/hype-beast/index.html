Sneaker & streetwear resale — graded in the open

The drop is theatre. The shop is a business.

Second Rotation sells authenticated pre-owned and deadstock sneakers in Chicago. No countdown timers, no raffle theatre, no “trust me” grading: every pair is checked by two people, graded against the standard printed on this page, and priced from a printed card. If a pair fails a check, it does not reach the rail — and the rejection is part of the service.

    § 03

    Sell to the shop

    Three ways in — the splits are printed, not negotiated in a DM

    Consignment: we sell it, you receive 80% of the sale price; the shop keeps 20%. Listed for 60 days; unsold pairs are returned or re-priced only with your written agreement.

    Trade-in: 65% of the agreed resale value as store credit, or 55% as cash on the day.

    Buy outright: cash at the trade-in cash rate — 55% of agreed value. Every valuation starts from the rate card above, after the same two-checker inspection. Pairs that fail authentication are returned to you with the written check sheet, free.

    New stock reaches the rail on Thursdays — counted, not teased
