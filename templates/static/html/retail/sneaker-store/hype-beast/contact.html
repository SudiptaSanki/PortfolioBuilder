Before you write about selling

Valuation starts from the printed rate card: find your category and honest grade and you already know the band. Trade-in pays 65% credit / 55% cash of the agreed value; consignment pays 80% when the pair sells. Intake checks are free, and the check sheet is yours even if we say no.
