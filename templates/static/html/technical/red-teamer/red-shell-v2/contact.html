Start with a scoping call.

Every engagement begins the same way: a call under NDA, where we talk about what your board is actually asking, what a measurement would look like, and whether an exercise is the right instrument at all. Sometimes it isn’t — a measurement audit or a logging review is cheaper and answers the real question. We will say so on the call.
