What happens next

1. A 45-minute scoping call, no charge.
2. A draft rules-of-engagement document within five working days.
3. Both parties sign; testing dates are held.
4. A named safety officer and a stop word agreed before day one.

Fees

Operations are quoted as a fixed fee by phase, including the debrief and one retest. Emergency response is a day rate with a 48-hour minimum. Travel at cost. No success fee, ever: it would be an incentive to escalate further than the client needs.

If you have an active intrusion

Say so in the first line and include a phone number. Response is containment first, attribution later, and I will work alongside your incident team rather than around it.
