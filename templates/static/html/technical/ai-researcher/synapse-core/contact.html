Questions the lab gets often

Short answers Long answers in the notebook page.
Hardware

Can I run my model on the Loihi board?

If it fits the Lava compiler and you can bring a calibration sweep, yes. Book a week; the board is shared and the queue is usually three weeks deep.

Data

Do you share the event datasets?

The two released sets are on the faculty repository with the sensor bias logs. The rest are under an agreement with the capture partners and cannot be redistributed.

Review

Will you review a draft?

Not during a deadline week, and never anonymously. Send a two-page summary and I will say whether I have time before reading the full paper.

Energy

Why does your number differ from the paper's?

Usually the sensor or the idle power. Our protocol includes both; accelerator-only figures leave them out, which is fair for a chip paper and misleading for a system claim.
