P-01

Event-driven corner detection at 40 µW

A 5×5 learned kernel over the event stream, computed as a sparse convolution on Loihi 2. On a static scene the sensor emits almost nothing and the network idles; on a moving scene the cost tracks the event rate, which is exactly the behaviour the format promises. The project is about where that promise breaks: fast panning fills the event buffer and the energy advantage disappears.

Hardware: GenX320 + Loihi 2 · 0.9 mJ per inference, static scene 41 µW
Status: paper at NeurIPS 2026 · dataset released with the sensor bias log

P-02

Calibrating LIF parameters to Loihi 2

Per-neuron threshold and time constant are fitted from a 200-sample sweep across 16 bias settings, then validated against held-out spike trains from the same core. The fitted model removes most of the rate drift that makes simulated results fail to transfer, and it gives a per-core mismatch map that other groups can use before they trust a port.

Hardware: Loihi 2, 512 neurons across 8 cores · median rate error 3.8 %
Status: ICLR 2026 · calibration tooling in Lava 0.6

P-03

STDP under a spike budget

Unsupervised STDP learns a useful representation and then keeps firing. We add a per-neuron refractory penalty during training, so the equilibrium of the learned weights sits below an event budget instead of above it. The representation loses a little linear separability and gains a 27 % reduction in spikes at the same probe accuracy.

Hardware: Loihi 2 · −27 % spikes, −0.4 pt linear-probe accuracy
Status: Frontiers in Neuroscience, 2025

P-04

Event-based optical flow at 0.4 mJ

A plane-fitting flow estimator on the event stream, with the plane parameters read out from a spiking population rather than a dense correlation volume. On the MVSEC outdoor day sequences it is within 8 % of a dense baseline's endpoint error at a fraction of the energy — but only when the camera is not rolling, which the paper says plainly.

Hardware: DVS128 + Speck · 0.4 mJ per frame at 30 fps equivalent
Status: ECCV workshop 2025 · code released

P-05

An honest energy account for event vision

A position paper with a measurement protocol: sensor, encoding, network, readout, and the idle power of the board, reported per correctly classified sample at three event rates. Applied to four published systems, it changes the ranking in two of them. The protocol is the contribution; the ranking is a consequence.

Method: PPK2 logging, 1 kHz, whole-board · four systems re-measured
Status: under review · preprint available on request

§ 04

Student projects, finished

Archive Theses are in the faculty repository; the code is in the lab group.
MSc 2026

Sparse encoding of LiDAR returns

Converted a 64-beam LiDAR stream into events and ran a spiking segmentation head. The conversion cost more than the network saved, which is a result worth writing down.

MSc 2025

Refractory periods as a rate control

Showed that a 1 ms shift in refractory period changes the energy budget of the front end by 14 %, more than most architecture choices.

BSc 2025

Bias drift with temperature

Measured the neuron bias current against board temperature over four hours. The drift is the reason the calibration has to be repeated after warm-up.

MSc 2024

A spike-count readout for gesture recognition

Replaced a rate-decoding layer with a population count over 20 ms and matched accuracy while cutting the readout energy by a third.
