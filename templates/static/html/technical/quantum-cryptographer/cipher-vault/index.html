03 / How the work runs

Three habits that decide whether a migration lands

01

Inventory before algorithms

You cannot migrate a key you cannot find. Certificates on load balancers, pinned keys in mobile binaries, a vendor’s HSM firmware, the one cron job with a static RSA key — the inventory is the deliverable that outlives the project.

02

Hybrid until the evidence is in

X25519 plus ML-KEM-768 is safe against both a quantum adversary and a new lattice implementation bug. The hybrid is not a compromise; it is the correct engineering choice until one half has a decade of field time.

03

Agility is the real output

Algorithms change every fifteen years and implementations change every quarter. The team that can swap a signature scheme in a week, behind a configuration flag, has solved the problem permanently.

“The migration was nine hundred lines of configuration and one hard conversation about a certificate nobody owned.” That is normal.
— Closing note, payment network migration, 2026
