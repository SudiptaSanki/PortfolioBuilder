What an inventory costs

A fixed fee for up to 2,000 endpoints, delivered in two to three weeks, including a re-runnable discovery script and a one-hour readout. Migration work after that is quoted per phase, not per hour.

What I will not do

I do not audit a vendor I am also recommending, and I take no commission from HSM or QKD suppliers. If the honest answer is that you do not need this engagement yet, that is the answer you will get.

If you have a deadline in 30 days

Say so in the first line. Emergency reviews are possible when the calendar allows, but they cost more and cover less; the scope will be one protocol and one product, not the estate.
