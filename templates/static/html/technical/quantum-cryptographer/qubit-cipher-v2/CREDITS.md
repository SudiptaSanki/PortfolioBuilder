footer of every page. The practice, clients and case files are illustrative.

as standardised in FIPS 203, 204 and 205 (finalised August 2024), and the

## Sound

No audio is used anywhere on this site. The need-test fails: an audit is
read, not heard, and nothing in this trade teaches through sound. The stub's
decorative lattice canvas was removed; its one honest descendant is the
static, labelled timeline diagram (Fig. 1) drawn in SVG on the Audit page.
