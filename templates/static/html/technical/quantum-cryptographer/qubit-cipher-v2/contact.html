§4 · Enquire

Start with your X.

The useful first message names your longest-lived data and how long it must stay secret. Everything else — estate size, current algorithms, deadlines — can follow. If you do not know your X yet, say that: it is the commonest starting point, and it is what the inventory is for.

Write directly if you prefer: enquiries@fischer-pqc.example. Replies come from Dr Fischer within two working days, and the reply begins with your X — not with a product.
