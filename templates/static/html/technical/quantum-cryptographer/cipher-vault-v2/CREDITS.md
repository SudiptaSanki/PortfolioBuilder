# Credits — cipher-vault

## Persona
Dr Sofia Marek and the practice "Cipher Vault" are fictional, created for
this portfolio template and disclosed as fictional in the footer of every
page. Case files are invented composites; their physics, fee shapes and
failure modes are true to the trade. No real person, firm, link or client
facility is depicted or named.
