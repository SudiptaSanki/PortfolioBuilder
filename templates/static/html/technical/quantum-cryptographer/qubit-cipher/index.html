01 · Preparation

Four operations, and one of them is arithmetic.

  1. A
    Prepare

    Alice encodes each bit in one of two bases chosen at random: rectilinear (0°, 90°) or diagonal (45°, 135°). One photon, one bit, no amplification.

  2. B
    Measure

    Bob measures in a basis of his own choosing, also at random. When the bases match, his result is Alice's bit. When they differ, it is a coin toss.

  3. C
    Sift

    They compare bases over an authenticated classical channel and keep only the matches: about half the raw bits. The bases are public; the bits are not.

  4. D
    Estimate and amplify

    A sample of the sifted key is sacrificed to measure the error rate. If it is below threshold, privacy amplification compresses what remains into a shorter key Eve cannot know.

03 · Position

QKD is not a replacement for post-quantum cryptography.

QKD gives two parties a key with a physics-based argument about eavesdropping, but it needs a dedicated link, trusted hardware and a working classical channel to authenticate the sifting. Post-quantum cryptography runs on the internet you already have, and its security rests on a mathematical assumption that could, in principle, be wrong.

I work on both because the honest architecture uses both: ML-KEM for the general case, QKD where a fibre already exists and the threat model justifies the hardware. Anyone selling one as the answer to everything is selling something else.
