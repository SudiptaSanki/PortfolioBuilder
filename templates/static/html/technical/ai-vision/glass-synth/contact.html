Send the frame where it goes wrong.

One image, the sensor it came from and the decision the system made is a better brief than a specification document. From there I can usually name the likely cause in a call.

Collaboration enquiry

No mailing list and no CRM. Patient data is not accepted through this form; for clinical work we set up an agreement before any footage moves.

A useful brief has

  • One frame that fails, and one that works
  • The sensor, lens and resolution
  • The decision the robot makes from the output
  • The error that is actually harmful
  • The compute you can carry

If you have a calibration log, include it. Half the time the answer is in the intrinsics.

Not a fit

  • A demo for a funding review
  • Face recognition or biometric identification
  • Anything that needs a result in ten days
  • No data and no way to collect it

I would rather say this now than in week three. A short call costs nothing and usually saves a month.

What happens next

A 30-minute call, then a one-week diagnostic if the problem is not obvious: I reproduce the failure on your data, measure the optics, and write up the cause with the two cheapest fixes. The sprint only starts once the diagnosis is agreed, so nobody pays for a rebuild that a calibration would have solved.
