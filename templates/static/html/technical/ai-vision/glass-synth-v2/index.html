§01

The agreement calculator

RUNS ON THE TABLE PRINTED BELOW

Before a model is scored against human labels, the humans are scored against each other. The instrument is Cohen's kappa, printed here in full because a coefficient you cannot recompute is just another headline:

κ

κ = (po − pe) ÷ (1 − pe) — agreement corrected for the agreement chance alone would produce.

po

Observed agreement — the share of items both annotators labelled the same. The flattering number.

pe

Chance agreement — computed from the printed marginals: how often two annotators would coincide if each labelled blindly at their own rates.

The worked example is real practice arithmetic: Study EV-02's first round — two trained annotators, 200 surface photographs, one question: defect, or no defect? The marginals are printed in the table and the tool reads them from the table, so page and tool cannot disagree. Step the agreed-defect count and watch κ move — and watch how far raw agreement travels without it.

WHAT I WILL NOT DO

I will not certify a figure I did not measure, sign a vendor's benchmark, or evaluate a system built by a team I advise. I will not report accuracy without its basis — and if your current report does, that is the first finding, and it is free.
