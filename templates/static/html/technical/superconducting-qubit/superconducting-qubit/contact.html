What helps in the first message

  • The qubit frequency, substrate and metal stack, if they are fixed.
  • Median and worst-case T1, T2 star and echo, with the number of devices.
  • The fridge: base temperature, line count, and whether the wiring is yours or borrowed.
  • The decision the work has to feed, and the date it is needed by.
