## Physics as printed

- Thermal occupation: n̄ = 1 / (e^(hf/k_BT) − 1), with h and k_B
  printed on the page and read by the tool from the page itself.
- Gate error: ε ≈ t_gate / T₁, printed and labelled on the page as
  a rule of thumb that prices relaxation only.
- Error-correction overhead on the About page is stated as a
  published estimate with its assumption (≈ 0.1% physical error
  rate) attached, not as a schedule or a promise.
