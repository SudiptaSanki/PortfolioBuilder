Enquiries

A field range, a bandwidth and a room.

The three answers that decide everything are how weak the field is, how fast it changes, and what the sensor has to live inside. Send those and I can tell you within a week whether the requirement is a cell, a shield or a different kind of instrument.

What I will not promise

A sensitivity figure before I have seen the environment. Half of the fT/√Hz numbers in the literature are measured with the sensor inside a shield and nothing else running; the other half are measured the same way and described differently.

What happens after the enquiry

Three things, in this order, all of them before any glass is cut.

A conversation about the room

Half an hour with whoever will run the instrument, not just the person commissioning it. The failures I see most often are environmental: a lift shaft, a steel bench, a power supply in the same rack.

30 min

A one-page feasibility note

What is achievable, what is not, and which of the three constraints has to move. It usually ends with a smaller requirement than the one in the first email, which is a good outcome for both sides.

1 page

A cell plan and a schedule

How many cells, which coating, which buffer gas, and when the oven is free. Cell fabrication is the long pole and the only part of the programme I cannot compress.
