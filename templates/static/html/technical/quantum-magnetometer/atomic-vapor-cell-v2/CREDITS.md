## Sound ruling

**No audio anywhere on this site.** The stub's theme promised canvas
spectacle; nothing in magnetometry teaches through sound — the trade is
read from traces, tables and baselines. The need-test therefore fails and
the site is silent by ruling, recorded here and in the footer. No
AudioContext is created by script.js.

## Physics note

for ⁸⁷Rb, γ/2π = 7.0 Hz/nT, is the rounded working value of the accepted
≈6.996 Hz/nT and is used consistently by the tool, the context scale and
every worked line on the page — the rounding is disclosed on the page
itself. Sensitivity figures (11 fT/√Hz shielded vs 46 pT/√Hz unshielded
weekday) are plausible illustrative system figures for a fictional
practice, printed to demonstrate the shielded/unshielded honesty rule.
