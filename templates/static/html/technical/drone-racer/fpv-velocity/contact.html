01 — Preparation

What makes a bench session useful

  1. 01

    Bring the aircraft as it flies

    Not cleaned up, not with the new props you have been saving. The point of the session is the aircraft that loses you the lap, including the bent arm and the camera mount that rattles at 60% throttle.

  2. 02

    Bring the DVR of your worst heat

    Two minutes of footage tells me more about the tune than an hour of bench numbers. The moment before the crash is the diagnosis.

  3. 03

    Know your home circuit

    Gate layout, length of the longest straight, and the surface. A tune for a quarry road is not a tune for a polished hall, and neither is a tune for your car park.
