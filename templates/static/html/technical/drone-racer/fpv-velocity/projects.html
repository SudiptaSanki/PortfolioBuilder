Season 2026 · 28 heats flown

Six heats worth writing down.

Results only mean something with the conditions attached: air density, pack, and what broke. Each entry below has the lap, the field and the note I wrote on the drive home.

Quarry circuit, round 4 — second

Best lap 24.81, three tenths off Voss and half a second clear of third. The circuit was dry and fast, air density at 120 m with a cold morning, so the pack held voltage better than it had all season. I lost the heat at gate three on the first lap and spent four laps getting it back.

Note on the drive home: the line through three is two metres wider than I fly in practice, and it is faster because it sets up the straight.

28 heats, 6 pilots
Air 1.21 kg/m³
Pack 6S 1300, 3.72 V/cell

Halle indoor, round 2 — first

Best lap 18.42 and a win, in a hall with a polished floor and three pillars that punish a wide line. I ran a 4S pack and a lighter 5.0 prop for the tighter layout, which dropped the top speed and made the aircraft predictable in the hairpins.

Two props broken in practice, none in the heats. The 4S decision was made on the bench two days earlier, on the numbers rather than on feel.

P1 · 18.42 s
Indoor, 8 gates
4S 1500 mAh
5.0 × 4.3 × 3 prop

Quarry circuit, round 6 — retired at gate four

A front-left prop let go at the exit of gate four, four laps in, while I was second. The aircraft went in from about nine metres and bent an arm and a motor shaft. The DVR shows the prop departing cleanly with no warning vibration for the two laps before it.

Cause was a set of props from a batch that had been sitting in a hot car for a month. The lesson was not about flying; it was about storing polycarbonate.

DNF · lap 4
Prop failure, front left
Arm and motor shaft
Props binned, batch noted

Quarry circuit, round 7 — third, in rain

Best lap 26.90 in a wet heat that turned the quarry road into a skating rink. I ran a taller line into gate two and short-shifted the throttle all afternoon, which cost about a second a lap and kept the aircraft off the ground.

Third was the right result. Two pilots ahead of me in the championship both crashed trying to hold a dry line.

P3 · 26.90 s
Wet, 12 °C
Air 1.23 kg/m³
One prop broken, no damage

Night heat, round 9 — fourth

Floodlit racing at 21:40, air cooler and denser than the afternoon heats, which the bench said would give about 3% more thrust. It did. It also made the aircraft quicker into gate one than my brain had calibrated for, and I clipped the left gate on lap two.

Fourth, no damage, and a note: recalibrate on the first two laps of a night heat rather than trusting the afternoon's marks.

P4 · 25.12 s
Night, 9 °C
Air 1.25 kg/m³
Gate clip, no damage

Season final, quarry circuit — second by 0.19 s

Six pilots, four laps, and the closest finish of the year: 0.19 seconds between first and second after 90 seconds of racing. I was quickest through gates two and five and slowest out of three, which is the whole story of the season in one lap.

The championship went to Voss by nine points. I have the notebook open on the 2027 build already.
