What to send

  • What the agent is supposed to do, in one or two sentences.
  • What it actually does — the incident, the invoice, the complaint.
  • Run volume and the tools it can call.
  • Whether production traces can be shared, and under what NDA.

What happens next

Day 0–2: a reply from me — questions, a scoped price, or an honest "fix your tracing first".

Then: scope in writing, traces exchanged under NDA, work starts with step 01 of the method.

Always: two engagements at a time maximum; if the diary is full, the reply says when it is not.
