§4 — Enquiry

Start with a conversation, not a contract.

One contact path, two ways to use it: the form below, or the email beside it. Either way you get a reply within two working days and, if the work fits, a scoping call — after which the scope is written down and priced as a fixed fee before anything is agreed.

§4.1

The enquiry

Reply within 2 working days
Before you write: describe the shape of the work, never its sensitive detail. Do not send credentials, live vulnerability information, production addresses you haven't agreed to share, or personal data about your staff. There is a secure channel for that later, once an NDA and a scope exist — an enquiry form is not it.

What happens next

  • Reply within 2 working days, with honest availability — currently booking from January 2027.
  • A scoping call (45 minutes) to shape the engagement and name what's out of scope.
  • A written scope and fixed fee within three working days of the call. No obligation; the scope document is yours to keep either way.
  • Authorisation signed, dates set, contacts named — then, and only then, testing.

What to send — and what not to

  • The kind of engagement you're considering, and why now.
  • Rough size: number of applications, sites or accounts.
  • Any fixed deadline and who the report is for.
  • Not credentials, vulnerability details, or staff personal data — see the note above the form.
