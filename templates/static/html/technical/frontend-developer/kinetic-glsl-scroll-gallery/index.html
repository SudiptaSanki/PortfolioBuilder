(03) — ABOUT STOCKHOLM / REMOTE

I'm a creative developer working between design and engineering — building the shaders, systems and motion that make a screen feel physical. Ten years of shipping for studios, brands and the occasional 4am experiment.

PROFILE

I started with Flash and a cracked copy of After Effects, moved to JavaScript the day it could draw pixels, and never left. Today my work is mostly real-time: WebGL scenes, GPU image pipelines, scroll systems that stay smooth on a five-year-old phone.

I care about the seam where art direction meets performance. A shader is a design decision; a frame budget is an editorial one.
