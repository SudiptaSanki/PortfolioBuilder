How a commission runs

You send a sprite list and a deadline. I reply with a fixed price, a start date and one question I need answered before drawing anything. Week one is a sprite test: three characters in your palette, at your resolution. If the test is wrong, we stop there and you pay only for the test.

After the test, delivery is weekly, in batches you can put in the build. Source files go into a repository you can access from day one.

What to send

  • Resolutione.g. 16×16, 320×180
  • Palettecolours or a limit
  • Sprite listcount + states
