How a sprite gets made

  1. 01

    One colour, one shape

    The silhouette has to read at 16 × 16 with a single fill. If it does not, no amount of detail saves it.

  2. 02

    Four colours, no anti-aliasing

    Two for the body, one for the light, one for the eye. Dithering only where a ramp is genuinely needed.

  3. 03

    Animate the breath, not the body

    Idle cycles are two frames and 90% of the character. Run cycles are four. Anything longer had better be a boss.

  4. 04

    Test on the worst screen

    Every sprite is checked on a dim handheld at 1× and on a 4K monitor at 8×. If it fails either, it is wrong.

Got a small game that needs to look like it cost more?

I take art and code work together, or art alone. Two projects a quarter, and I will turn down a third even if the money is good — the pixels get worse when I hurry.
