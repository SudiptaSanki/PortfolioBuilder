Sixteen colours is not a limitation. It’s the art direction.

I’m Marisol Vega. I make small pixel-art games of my own, and I draw sprites, tilesets and character animation for other indie developers — at small canvases, in counted palettes, one frame at a time.

Every sprite that leaves this studio is counted against a printed rule: twelve colours or fewer, transparency excluded. The counter further down this page measures a real sprite from my own game and shows its working. Nothing here is a mockup of work — it is the work, at the size it was drawn.
