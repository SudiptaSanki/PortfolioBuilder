How a build runs

You tell me the room, the game and the power. I visit once with a tape measure, then send a fixed price and a date. Half on agreement, half on install. The cabinet is delivered working, leveled, with the coin mech set for your tariff and a spare fuse taped inside the door.

Restorations start with a bench inspection. I photograph every fault, send you the list with prices, and do nothing until you approve it. If the tube is dead and the machine is not worth a donor, I will tell you that instead of taking the job.
