Three rules of a good cabinet

01

The first ten seconds must teach the coin

No cabinet survives a bad attract mode. If a stranger cannot understand what the machine is and why it is fun without paying, the design is not finished.

02

One button, then depth

Every cabinet I have shipped plays with one stick and two buttons. Complexity that needs a third button is complexity that needs a manual, and nobody reads a manual in a bar.

03

Frame data is a promise

If the move looks like it hits on frame 4, it hits on frame 4. Arcade players learn with their hands; lying to them by even two frames teaches them not to trust the machine.
