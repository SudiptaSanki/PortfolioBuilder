# Credits — Crane Arcade Systems (retro-arcade-gamedev)

Hollis Crane / Crane Arcade Systems is a fictional persona created for
this template. Titles, clients and figures in the copy are illustrative;
the frame-budget method and trade practice are stated at
established-practice level.

## Sound

One fenced exception, stated on the page and in the footer: the Input
Test toy plays a single synthesised blip on a hit, only after the
visitor switches its SOUND control on (the switch is the prompt), at
fixed low gain (0.12), self-stopping within 0.13 s. No other audio
exists anywhere in this template. The stub's canvas demo with its
invented "SCORE: 999900" was removed, not rebuilt.

## Dry-brush edge pattern

Not used. The owner's brush-edge divider is genre-fitted to creative /
festival / food / music surfaces; this template's design system is a
ruled service manual, whose honesty language is the straight ruled
edge and the printed table. Forcing a torn edge onto a test-mode
manual would be decoration against the concept, so it was left out
deliberately.
