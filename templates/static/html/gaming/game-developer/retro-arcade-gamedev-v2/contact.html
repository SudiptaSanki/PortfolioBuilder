Log a service call

One form, one pair of hands reading it. Tell me which switch you need — an original, a port, a cabinet, an audit — and the target hardware if you know it. The hardware matters more than the pitch: the budget sheet gets written against a machine, not a mood. Replies within two working days, with the prototype week or the audit as the usual first step.
