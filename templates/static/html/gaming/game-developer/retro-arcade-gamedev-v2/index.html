Test 01 · Frame Test

What a frame costs

At 60 frames per second the whole game — input, logic, collision, drawing, sound — gets 16.67 ms. At 30 it gets 33.33 ms, and half the responsiveness with it. The table below is the real budget sheet for Bullet Garden on the stated target hardware. Flip the DIP switches and watch the sheet add itself up. This is the sheet that killed the CRT post pass on the Switch build: with it switched on, the frame does not fit.

Test 02 · Input Test

One button, honestly labelled

Every cabinet test menu has an input test: press the button, watch it register. This is that screen, rebuilt as a toy. Stop the marker inside the red zone. It exists to make one point — input is polled first in the frame because the gap between your press and the game's answer is the whole feel of an arcade game. It is a toy, not a game and not a portfolio piece. The games are in the Title Log.

Sound ruling: sound is off until you switch it on — that switch is the only way this site makes a noise. With sound on, a hit plays one short blip (synthesised, fixed low volume, stops itself). A miss stays silent: the zone is the feedback. The marker's position is read out in milliseconds of sweep because that is the unit input latency is honestly measured in.

Title Log · Summary

Three lists, kept separate

Shipped (3). Bullet Garden — score-attack arena shooter, PC March 2024, Switch November 2024; 41,300 copies across both platforms as of 31 Oct 2026, counted from the platform dashboards. One Credit Clear — an original game running on two cabinets in a Brighton arcade, 98.2% uptime over 14 months by the operator's service log. Gravity Well 84 — a £3 pocket title, 9,800 downloads as of Oct 2026.

Jam (6). Weekend sketches, built in 48–72 hours to learn one thing each. Two are written up in the log. A jam game is a sketch, not a shipped game, and it is never counted as one.

Cancelled (2). Printed, with reasons — one killed by a publisher's funding decision, one killed by me when its own budget sheet said the design could not hold 60 frames. What was salvaged from each is in the log too.
