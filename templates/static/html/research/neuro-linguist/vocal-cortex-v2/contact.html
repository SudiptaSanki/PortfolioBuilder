Enquiries · One path, no funnel

Tell me about the study, not the deadline first.

There is one email address and one form, and they reach the same desk. Enquiries are answered within two working days — with questions, usually, before answers.

Email — the direct route enquiries@vocalcortex.example

What a useful enquiry includes

  • The claimThe sentence the study is meant to support, in one line. If it takes a paragraph, the design review starts there.
  • The stageGrant draft, protocol written, pilot run, data collected — the honest one. Rescues are taken on, but they are priced as rescues.
  • The shapeModality, rough participant numbers, sessions per participant. The session planner and estimator on the protocol page will turn these into a first session length and a first budget.
  • The constraintFunder deadline, ethics window, a departing analyst — whichever is real. Constraints change the queue, not the arithmetic.

Research enquiries only. This practice cannot take clinical referrals, cannot assess or treat any individual, and cannot write reports for legal or insurance purposes. If your enquiry is about your own or a family member's health, the right door is your clinician — this one would waste your time, kindly but completely.
