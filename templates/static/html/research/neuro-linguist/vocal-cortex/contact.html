  • AnalysisPreprocessing and single-trial modelling; cluster permutation for the time windows.

Visiting

2027 places

One visiting researcher, three to six months. Two master's projects. Requests are read in January and decided by the end of February.

Speech and Cognition Lab, Department of Psychology, University of Oslo. Entrance from the north courtyard; the lab is on the second floor.

How a request is handled

Data. Published epochs are open and need no request. If you need the raw recordings, say which study and which participants; raw files leave the lab only under a data-processing agreement and with ethics approval for the new purpose.

Collaboration. Send the contrast and the predicted component. I will tell you whether the lab has the participants, the paradigm and the time, and whether the question is one EEG can answer.

Replication and correction. These go to the front of the queue. If a figure on this site cannot be reproduced from the archive, that is a bug in my work and I will fix it publicly.

Students. Master's students at Oslo apply through the department. Students elsewhere can propose a project if they can visit for at least three months; the lab does not run remote-only EEG projects.
