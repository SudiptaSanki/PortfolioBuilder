P-01

From pressure to prediction

The path from air to meaning has three measurable stages. Each one has its own instrument, and each one fails in a characteristic way when something is wrong.

01 / acoustic

Formants

The vocal tract is a filter. Its resonances — F1, F2, F3 — sit at frequencies set by tongue height, tongue frontness and lip rounding. Every vowel is a point in that three-dimensional space.

F1 270—730 Hz · F2 840—2,290 Hz

02 / phonetic

Categories

Listeners do not hear a continuum. They hear /i/ or /e/, and the boundary between them shifts with dialect and with the sounds around it. Categorical perception is measurable in reaction time and in the N400.

boundary shift 20—60 ms VOT

03 / cortical

Components

An unexpected word produces a negative deflection at about 400 ms — N400 — and a later positive one at 600 ms — P600 — when the sentence can be repaired. Their latency is stable; their amplitude is not.

N400 382 ms · P600 612 ms

Most of my work is on the mismatch between the acoustic signal and what the listener reports hearing. A vowel can be moved 30 Hz along F1 and still be heard as the same category; move it 60 Hz and the percept flips. The N400 follows the percept, not the signal, which is the strongest evidence we have that the brain is predicting a category rather than measuring a spectrum.

The experiments are simple in principle: record 32 channels of EEG while a person listens to 400 sentences, average the epochs by condition, and compare the components. In practice, half the work is artefact rejection, and the other half is convincing yourself that the difference is not an eye blink.

P-03

Three findings that survived replication

Each study is listed with its paradigm, its sample and its effect size. Pre-registrations and anonymised epochs are on the studies page.

S-01

Vowel categories shift the N400 before the percept does

A 12-step F1 continuum from /i/ to /e/, 48 participants, 1,200 trials. The N400 amplitude tracked the category boundary, not the acoustic distance, and moved with the listener's dialect.

Effect
2.9 µV
Latency
378 ms

S-02

P600 appears only when the sentence can be repaired

Garden-path sentences with and without a plausible continuation, 64 participants. A late positivity appeared only in the repairable condition; the unrepairable ones ended with a sustained negativity.

Effect
3.4 µV
Latency
608 ms

S-03

Speakers converge on F2 within 40 seconds

Dyads solving a map task, 36 pairs, tracking F2 over 8 minutes. Convergence began within the first 40 seconds and predicted the pair's later word-choice agreement, not the reverse.

P-04

Apparatus

The lab runs a 32-channel passive EEG system at 1,000 Hz, a sound-treated booth, and a synthesis and analysis stack written in Python.

EEG

32 channels, 1,000 Hz, impedance kept below 5 kΩ. Average reference, 0.1–30 Hz band-pass, epochs −200 to 1,000 ms.

Stimuli

Formant synthesis with a glottal pulse source and three resonators; 48 kHz, 16-bit. Every token is checked with a spectrogram before it enters a paradigm.

Analysis

MNE-Python for preprocessing, linear mixed models for single-trial amplitudes, and cluster-based permutation for the time windows.

Participants

Right-handed adults, 18–45, normal hearing, no neurological history. Six language backgrounds, tested in their first language.
