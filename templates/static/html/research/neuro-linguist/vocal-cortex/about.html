I study the half second in which sound becomes a word

The auditory cortex resolves a vowel in about 50 ms. The N400 tells us whether the word fits the sentence at 400 ms. Most of what I do is measure that gap and argue about what fills it.

I came to speech through music. A piano note and a vowel are both made of harmonics, and I wanted to know why one becomes a word. That question took me from a music degree in Bergen to a PhD in cognitive neuroscience in Oslo, with a detour through a speech technology group that taught me how to synthesise any vowel I liked.

The lab's core method is the event-related potential: average the EEG over hundreds of trials and small, reliable deflections appear. It has poor spatial resolution and excellent temporal resolution, which suits a question about when meaning arrives rather than where it lives.

I care about replication more than novelty. Three of the studies on this site are replications or extensions of earlier work, including one of my own that did not hold up in a larger sample. That result is on the site too, with the original and the replication side by side.

I teach a master's course on speech perception and an introductory course on EEG methods. In both, students analyse a dataset that is already published, so they can check their answer against the paper.

Teaching

Speech perception — master's course, 10 weeks. From the acoustics of a vowel to the N400, with a lab component in which students run a 12-participant pilot.

EEG methods — introductory course, 6 weeks. Preprocessing, artefact rejection, averaging and the statistics that go with a small number of trials.

Supervision — four PhD students and seven master's students since 2019.

Service and reviewing

Reviewer for Journal of Cognitive Neuroscience, Brain and Language, Neuropsychologia and Language, Cognition and Neuroscience.

Member of the Norwegian national committee on research ethics in the humanities and social sciences. I also run the lab's open-data policy, which is that every published study releases its anonymised epochs within a year.
