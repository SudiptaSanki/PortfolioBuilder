## The printed constants

The sol converter uses the standard mission-planning figures, printed on the
page beside the tool: 1 sol = 88,775 s = 24 h 39 m 35 s; 1 Earth day =
86,400 s; 1 Mars year = 668.6 sols. The estimator uses only the fee table
printed on the index page.

## Sound

None. A planetary geologist's product is the map and the report; the sound
need-test fails and the template is silent by design. (The stub's original
hover-triggered "orbital hum" was removed: hover audio is a deal-breaker
under the program rules regardless of theme.)
