Before You Write

The estimator on sheet S.01 prices a programme from the printed fee table — running it before enquiring makes the scoping call shorter and the quote faster. The field studies on sheet S.02 show the standard of proof a study is delivered to, including the ones that overturned their own brief.

What Is Not Taken On

Certification of a landing site as “safe”; ages quoted without their ranges; interpretations a client has written before the data is opened. Geology informs. It does not certify, and it does not decorate conclusions reached elsewhere.
