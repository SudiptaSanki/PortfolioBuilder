How a request is handled

Dating. Send the scene, the image product and the unit boundary. I will tell you whether the crater statistics are adequate, whether the surface is likely to be resurfaced, and what the realistic uncertainty is before anyone counts anything.

Spectra. The reduced cubes are public; custom inversions are possible if the science question is specific and the calibration is documented.

Field analogs. The summer school has space for six students and one external instrument. Proposals are read in March.

Data and code. Everything published is open. If a product is missing, that is an oversight and I will fix it.
