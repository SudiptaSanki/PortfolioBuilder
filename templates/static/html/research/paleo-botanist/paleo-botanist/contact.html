What happens to a request

Visits. I answer with two or three possible dates and a list of what is in the relevant drawers. Bring a notebook; photography is allowed for research, not for commercial use.

Scans. Reference scans of the 412 catalogued pieces are free for research. New scans are scheduled around the synchrotron beam time, usually twice a year.

Loans. Destructive sampling is the hardest request to approve, because each piece is unique. A proposal that uses a fragment, leaves the inclusion intact, and publishes the result openly is much more likely to succeed.

Identification. Send a photograph with a scale and the locality. If the piece is important and you are willing, the museum may offer to acquire it; you keep the credit for the find.
