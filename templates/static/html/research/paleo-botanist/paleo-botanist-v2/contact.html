Before you send anything

Do not post pieces yet. Enquire first: fragile amber travels by insured courier, arranged after intake, and some collections are better served by an on-site day. Pieces sent unannounced sit unopened — amber dislikes surprises as much as registers do.

The bench, at present

Identification work is booking about four weeks out; the single-piece waitlist runs to about six; one audit slot remains this quarter. Loan documentation is scheduled against exhibition dates — ask early, and the register will hold the slot.
