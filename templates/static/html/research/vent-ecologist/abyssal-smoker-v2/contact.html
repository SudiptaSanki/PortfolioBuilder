Entry 04 — Enquire

One form, one email address, no funnel

Cruise planning starts 12–24 months before sailing, so the right time to ask is when the programme is still an idea with a field attached. If you are earlier than that — a question about feasibility at a given depth is a perfectly good enquiry.

Write directly enquiries@ribeiro-vent-ecology.org

The form and the inbox reach the same desk. Replies come from Dr Ribeiro or the practice coordinator — never from a marketing sequence, because there isn't one.

What makes an enquiry answerable

  • The field — a name, a ridge segment, or coordinates as precise as you have them. Depth, if known, decides feasibility faster than anything else.
  • The vessel window — allocated, applied-for, or still hypothetical. Each needs a different first answer.
  • The question the cruise must answer — one sentence. Sampling is designed backwards from it.
  • What already exists — prior surveys, charts, sample archives. Resurveys stand on earlier work; say what there is.

Response window: enquiries are answered within five working days when the practice is ashore, and in a single batch on return when it is at sea — the sailing schedule is stated in the reply either way. If your programme has a funding deadline inside that window, say so in the first line and the enquiry jumps the queue.
