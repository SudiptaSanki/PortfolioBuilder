Tell me where you were standing, and what moved

Felt reports still calibrate intensity maps better than any instrument between M 4 and M 7. If you want data, say which channels and which dates. If you want to propose a site, say what is under it.

If there is an emergency

Call 131 for fire and rescue, 133 for police. The observatory does not operate a public warning service; the official alert comes from SENAPRED and SHOA. Do not wait for an email from a seismologist.
