## Everything else

The seismogram trace in the hero (Fig. 02) is an SVG drawn for this template and
labelled schematic on the page. All copy, the magnitude–energy explorer and the
estimator are original to this template. The energy relation printed on the page
(log₁₀ E ≈ 4.8 + 1.5 × M) is the standard Gutenberg–Richter energy approximation,
and the TNT conversion (1 tonne = 4.184 × 10⁹ J) is the printed definition the
explorer uses.
