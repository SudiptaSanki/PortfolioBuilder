Enquiries — the studio answers in the order things arrive

Tell me what the component has to survive

The useful first message names the object, the load, the fire class and the date. If you do not know those yet, say so and describe the object; that is enough to start.

The form composes a draft locally; nothing leaves the browser in this demonstration. In the studio it routes to a shared inbox with a reference number, usually answered on Tuesdays and Fridays.

The studio

Grefsen, Oslo. A workshop, a dark growing room, a kiln and a press. We take four to six projects a year, and we decline more than we accept.

The most common reason for a no is scale: the studio can grow panels up to 1.2 × 0.8 m, and anything larger has to be a joined assembly, which changes the design.
