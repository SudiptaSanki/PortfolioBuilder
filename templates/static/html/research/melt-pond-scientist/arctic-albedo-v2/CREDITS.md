## Sound ruling

No audio anywhere. Sea-ice field science is not a sound profession
and no interaction on this site teaches through audio, so sound
fails the owner's need-test. The ruling is stated in every footer.

## Honest replacements of the stub's promises

The stub (template.json description) promised a "melt pond
reflection particle canvas" and "ice floe parallax layers". Both are
decoration with no job, and the parallax would fight the touch
protocol; they were deleted, not rebuilt. In their place the site
ships the Albedo Explorer (surface-mix weighted-mean calculator
asserted against the printed Table 1) and a campaign estimator on
the printed rate card. The stub's hardcoded "SURFACE ALBEDO: 0.65"
chip — a single unsourced number presented as data — was removed;
albedo on this site is computed from printed ranges, or it is not
printed at all.
