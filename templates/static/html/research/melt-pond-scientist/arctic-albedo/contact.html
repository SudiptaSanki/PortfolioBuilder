Before you write

Field season 2027

Fram Strait spring drift (April–June) and the Qaanaaq continuation (April). Two berths for students; requests close 31 January.

The group is part of the Polar Surface Energy Group at the Arctic University of Norway. Post and visits: Framsenteret, Hjalmar Johansens gate 14, 9007 Tromsø.

How a request is handled

Data. Published products go out immediately with the DOI. Unpublished raw files are shared under a collaboration agreement if the request is specific and the instrument calibration is documented. I do not share data from a floe that another group is still analysing without their agreement.

Model evaluation. Send the model version, the pond scheme and the period. I will tell you which of our datasets can discriminate between schemes and which cannot, before you spend time on a comparison.

Field collaboration. The spring camp has room for one external instrument or one scientist. Berths are decided in February, and everyone going on the ice does the safety course first, without exception.

Talks and teaching. I give four or five talks a year, usually in Norway or Greenland. Student questions are always answered; if the answer is long, it becomes a short note on the site.
