# Credits — Deep Abyss (deep-sea oceanographer portfolio)

## Persona

**Dr Ruth Calloway** is a fictional demonstration persona, disclosed as
fictional in the footer of every page. Calloway Deep Science, the
cruises, clients, fees and figures in the log are invented for this
template. No real person, institute or production is depicted.

The oceanography is real and was kept to well-established science:
pressure rises by roughly 1 atmosphere per 10 m of depth on top of the
1 atm at the surface (the explorer shows its working on the page);
the five zones and their boundaries (sunlight 0–200 m, twilight
200–1,000 m, midnight 1,000–4,000 m, abyssal 4,000–6,000 m, hadal
below 6,000 m) are the standard scheme; deep temperatures of about
4 °C in the midnight zone and 2–3 °C in the abyssal are the standard
figures; the Titanic lies at about 3,800 m; Challenger Deep is about
10,935 m; crewed research submersibles of the 6,500 m class (Alvin,
Shinkai 6500) are real; black-smoker vent fluids can exceed 350 °C;
hadal snailfish are among the deepest-living fish and do not survive
decompression, which the museum case states plainly. The stub's
"sonar ping" audio button and particle canvas were removed: the sound
need-test fails for this trade, and the ruling is stated in the footer.
