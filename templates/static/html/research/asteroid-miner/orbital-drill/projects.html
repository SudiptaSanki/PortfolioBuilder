A-04

47 Egeria anchor trial

Anchoring · 2025

Requirement. Hold a 6 kN drill reaction on a body where the lander weighs 4.1 N. No pyrotechnic hold-down allowed; the anchor had to be reversible so a jammed bit does not end the mission.

Decision. Three 40 mm augers on a 0.8 m triangle, driven to 3 m at 0.8 kN each, with a flight-computer rule that sheds feed force when the measured displacement rate passes 0.4 mm/s. The augers are counter-rotating so the torque cancels on the frame.

Result. Capacity 6.9 kN per anchor in simulant, 0.9 kN margin at 6 kN feed, and 41 minutes of continuous hold on the shaker table before the first slip. The slip is repeatable and re-bites in 12 mm, which is why the release is a controlled reverse rather than a cutter.

A-03

Solar cavity oven

Extraction · 2023

Requirement. Liberate water at 2.7 AU, where the flux is 187 W/m², using no more than 4 kWh per kilogram of product and without a moving refrigerant loop.

Decision. A conical cavity receiver with a 96 mm aperture, a static 180 K cold trap cooled by a radiator, and batch trays of 2 kg so a single jam does not spoil the run. The aperture was cut from 140 to 96 mm after the first test lost more heat than it collected.

Result. 3.8 kWh per kilogram of water across a 40 kg analogue batch, with 38 kg of ice recovered over eleven days. The weak point is the filter: fines blind it after about 60 kg of regolith and it has to be back-flushed with product vapour.

A-02

Microgravity hopper

Extraction · 2022

Requirement. Feed regolith into a sealed oven from a hopper in vacuum, without relying on gravity, at 0.4 g/s with no more than 10% loss per batch.

Decision. A flexible screw conveyor inside a sealed tube, with a rotary gate at the oven end and a 12° cone hopper that vibrates at 40 Hz to keep fines moving. The auger pitch widens toward the outlet so material does not compact at the gate.

Result. 93% mass closure over 60 cycles at 0.4 g/s, with losses concentrated at the gate seal. Above 0.9 g/s the tube blocks within four cycles, so the flight design will run two conveyors in parallel rather than one faster one.

A-05

Optical navigation at 0.02 AU

Prospecting · 2024

Requirement. Arrive at a 1 km body with a bearing error under 0.2° and enough range knowledge to pick a landing site, using a 20 cm aperture and no ground station in the loop.

Decision. A star tracker as the inertial reference and a 1024² detector on the same bench, with the target tracked in a window that grows as range closes. Feature tracking switches from centroid to crater matching below 4 km, where the body fills the frame.

Result. Bearing error 0.06° and range error 1.8% at 2.9 km, after 6 hours without a ground update. Crater matching fails in the last 300 m because the illumination is too oblique; the terminal phase uses a laser altimeter instead.

A-06

Regolith flow rig

Extraction · 2026

Requirement. Find a gate opening that passes 1.6 kg/min of 12 wt% icy regolith without jamming or bridging, at 0.1 g and in vacuum.

Decision. A 40 mm slot with an adjustable gate, a 40 Hz vibrator on the hopper wall and a wall coating of 20 µm of PTFE. We tested the gate at 5 mm steps from 10 to 40 mm on three mixes, twelve runs each.

Result. The icy mix jams below 22 mm and flows at 1.4–1.8 kg/min between 24 and 30 mm. Above 32 mm the fine mix bridges, so the usable window is narrow and depends on the mix. This is the least settled asset in the catalogue.

A-01

Sample return canister

Return · 2021

Requirement. Return 1.4 kg of water ice from a C-type surface without losing more than 5% to sublimation over a 14-month transit.

Decision. A double-walled canister with a 6 mm vacuum gap, a spring-loaded lid with two indium seals, and a radiator that holds the core at 78 K in sunlight. The first design used MLI blankets; they settled under vibration and the leak rate rose by a factor of nine.

Result. Leak 0.2 W and 2.1% mass loss in a 14-month thermal-vacuum test, within the 5% budget. The lid seal is the single-point failure: one of six units leaked after 200 mating cycles and the design now counts the cycles in telemetry.
