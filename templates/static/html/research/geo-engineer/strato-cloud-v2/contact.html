What happens next

A reply within five working days. If the question fits the practice, the next step is a scoping conversation and a written scoping note — free, and with no obligation on either side. Conflicts of interest are declared at that point, before any quote. The written quote then fixes scope, protocol and publication date, at the rates printed on page 01.

Enquiries declined, plainly Deployment services, procurement or dispersal of aerosol material, operational cloud seeding, and research commissioned to reach a predetermined conclusion are declined — in writing, with the reason stated. The practice also declines work whose methods would have to stay secret.

Not sure the question is ready?

Send it anyway. Sharpening questions is the first step of the method, and it costs nothing but an email.
