Feasibility desk

Tell me the basin, the season and the water you are missing

A feasibility study takes four to six weeks and answers one question: does this basin have the storm climatology, the terrain and the measurement density for seeding to be worth operating? About a third of the enquiries I receive get a no, and the no is free.

This form composes a local draft in this demonstration. In operation it files with the programme office and receives a reference the same day; feasibility replies take up to two weeks outside the winter season.

Before you write

Seeding works best on winter orographic cloud in a basin with a mountain barrier upwind and a reliable snow gauge network. If your problem is a summer convective drought, the honest answer is probably that seeding will not solve it.

Bring the storm climatology if you have it. If you do not, the first two weeks of the study are spent assembling it from reanalysis, and that is billable.
