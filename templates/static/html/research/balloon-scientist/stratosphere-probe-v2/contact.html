What helps an enquiry

  • Payload mass and dimensions — or an honest “not built yet”
  • Target altitude or altitude band, and why that band
  • What the instrument does if it gets cold, wet, or lost
  • The campaign’s real deadline, not the optimistic one
  • Whether the data or the hardware matters more — they plan differently

Before you write

The fees are printed on the flight log page, the method and the honesty page are on Scientist & method, and the record — losses included — is in the flight record. If those pages talk you out of enquiring, they have done their job too.
