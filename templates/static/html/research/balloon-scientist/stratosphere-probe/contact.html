Mass, power, volume, data rate, and the altitude where the measurement has to happen. If those five numbers fit the train, the rest is scheduling. If they do not, I will tell you which one to change before you write anything longer.

How a slot is decided

  1. 1
    A reply in three working days

    Usually one question about the measurement and one about the cold soak.

  2. 2
    Bench test, six weeks

    Thermal, vibration and vacuum. About one in three prototypes fails the first cold soak.

  3. 3
    Flight slot and recovery

    Two days of preparation, one launch window, and a snowmobile if the wind turns.

What a slot costs

€11,500 per payload for a shared flight, including the bench campaign, the integration and the recovery. A dedicated flight is €38,000 and buys you the whole train and the launch day.

Academic groups pay cost. No charge for a payload that fails the bench.

Where the launches happen

Esrange Space Center, 40 km east of Kiruna. The bench and the workshop are in Kiruna town; the recovery team is on call from the launch until the beacon stops.
