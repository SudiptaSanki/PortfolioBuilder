## Sound ruling
**No audio anywhere on this site.** The sound need-test fails for this trade:
astrophysics is not a sound profession and audio teaches nothing here — the
sky is silent at these distances. The stub's hover-triggered Web Audio tones
and the "space ambient" promised in template.json were removed, not rebuilt.
