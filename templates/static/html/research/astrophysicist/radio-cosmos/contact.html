Correspondence

Write something specific and I will answer it properly

Observing proposals, data questions, visits to the site, and disagreements about calibration are all welcome. I answer in the afternoon, South African time, ordinarily within five working days.

This form prepares a draft in your browser; it does not transmit anything. In production it would post to the observatory's scheduling desk, which records every enquiry with a reference number.

Where to find me

The array sits on a farm road 42 km north of Carnarvon, Northern Cape. Visitors are welcome by arrangement; the site is radio-quiet, which means no phones, no cameras with Wi-Fi, and no petrol engines within two kilometres of the antennas.

For formal proposals, use the observatory's scheduling portal and copy me the reference. For everything else, the form is faster than finding my address in a paper.
