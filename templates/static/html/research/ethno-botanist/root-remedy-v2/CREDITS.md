## Everything else

Layout, copy, the consensus calculator, the programme estimator, the leaf-mark
and stamp devices are hand-drawn CSS/SVG made for this template. No audio is
used anywhere on this site (see the sound ruling in the build report: field
recordings belong to the communities who made them, so the practice's site
stays silent).

## Fiction disclosure

Dr June Ashworth and the Root Remedy practice are fictional, created for this
portfolio template; the disclosure also appears in the site footer. The
methods, the informant consensus factor, the fee shapes and the ethics terms
described are true to ethnobotanical documentation practice. Community
references are deliberately generic — a region and a language group, never a
named community presented as a client.
