Correspondence

A specific question about a specific cloud will always get an answer

Data requests, collaboration proposals, instrument comparisons and student enquiries are all welcome. I am in the field for six to eight weeks a year, and slower to reply during those weeks.

This form composes a draft locally for the demonstration. In the group it files a request with a reference number, and data requests are answered after the teaching semester ends.

Where I am

The group sits on the fourth floor of the science building in Bergen, which has a view of a cloud most days and a working CCN counter in the basement.

Fieldwork is usually on the coast north of Bergen, or at the Andøya station in Vesterålen. Visitors are welcome during campaign prep, which is the only time anyone has spare attention.
