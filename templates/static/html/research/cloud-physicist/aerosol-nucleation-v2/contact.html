What happens next

A reply within three working days, from the scientist, not a coordinator. Then a scoping call — the question, the platform, the calibration chain — and a written quote in which every line traces to Table F1 or to a platform cost passed through at cost.

A note on platforms

Aircraft and facility time are booked by the client, or arranged by the practice at cost with invoices attached. If your programme already holds platform time, the practice fits into it — that is the commonest arrangement, and the cheapest.
