If you are asking about the PhD

  1. 1
    Say what you have built

    A laser, a detector, a simulation — anything you made and had to debug is more useful than a grade.

  2. 2
    Name one paper you disagree with

    Not because disagreement is required, but because I want to know how you read.

  3. 3
    Come for a Thursday

    One day on the bench, unpaid, before either of us commits to four years.

What the lab can host

  • A calibrated single-photon source at 780–850 nm, characterised to g²(0) < 0.1.
  • Visibility and indistinguishability measurements on your source, with the error budget written out.
  • Shared use of the 4 K cryostat for a fibre-coupled sample, up to four weeks a year.
  • A second pair of eyes on an interferometer that will not stay locked.

What we cannot host

No cleanroom, no nanofabrication, no dilution refrigerator below 4 K, and no theory student. If the project needs any of those, I will tell you in the first reply rather than the third.
