E-05

Does a phonon bath remember?

2026 · running

Question. If a photon passes through a mechanical resonator, does the resonator retain enough information to reduce the fringe visibility after the photon has left?

Apparatus. The Mach–Zehnder is split so that one arm ends in a fibre-coupled sample inside a 4 K cryostat. Path lengths are matched to within 2 mm outside, and the phase is locked to the piezo.

Result so far. We see a 6% visibility loss that persists for 400 ms after the photon, then recovers. That is either the memory we are looking for or a thermal transient in the fibre feedthrough, and the next run is designed to tell the two apart.

E-04

Coherence after a 40 m delay

2024

Question. How much coherence survives when one path is 40 m longer than the other, and is the loss explained by detector timing jitter alone?

Apparatus. A fibre spool of graded lengths in one arm, a piezo sweep of 2π, and coincidence counting between two SNSPDs with 45 ps jitter. Each point is an eleven-minute run.

Result. Visibility falls from 99.1% at zero delay to 91.4% at 40 m. Jitter accounts for the first 3.2 points; the remaining 4.5 points are stable across three cooldowns and are the reason for E-05.

E-03

A calibrated which-path detector

2025

Question. Can we set the distinguishability D of a which-path detector without fitting it from the visibility we are trying to test?

Apparatus. A variable tap on the upper arm sends 0–22% of the light to a third detector. The tap ratio is characterised with a classical source before every run, and D is computed from the measured ratio and the detector efficiency.

Result. Seven settings from D = 0.00 to 0.62 follow the Englert bound to within 1.8%, and the bound is saturated at low D. At D above 0.5 the measured visibility sits 4% above the bound, which we think is a dark-count artefact and have not yet proven.

E-02

Active phase lock on a 4 m spool

2026

Question. Can a simple analogue loop hold the phase of a 4 m fibre imbalance long enough to average a fringe to better than 1%?

Apparatus. A piezo stretcher in one arm, a 1 kHz dither, a lock-in amplifier and a PI controller with a 12 Hz bandwidth. The error signal comes from the same detectors that measure the fringe, so the lock is not independent of the measurement.

Result. Phase stability improved from 41° rms to 3.4° over eleven minutes, and the visibility uncertainty fell from 1.9% to 0.4%. The loop adds a 1 kHz sideband that we remove by gating the counters.

E-01

Single-photon source at 810 nm

2022

Question. Does the fibre-coupled source deliver one photon at a time, and how much of the residual g²(0) is detector dark counts?

Apparatus. A Hanbury Brown–Twiss set-up with a 50:50 fibre splitter, two SNSPDs and a 6 ps resolution time tagger. The source is a quantum dot in a micropillar, pumped quasi-resonantly.

Result. g²(0) = 0.06 ± 0.01 after dark-count subtraction, with a count rate of 180 k/s at the detectors. The uncorrected value is 0.09; the difference is the whole argument, and the correction is written out in the paper.
