A useful first message contains three things: the claim, stated as plainly as you can; the document it lives in — deck, roadmap, funding case, paper; and the decision it feeds — a round, a tranche, a build, a hire. With those three, the first reply can be a real reply.

The first reply is questions, not a quote: the claim set has to be enumerated before a fee can be fixed, and that enumeration is the first piece of work. Expect it within three working days. If your deadline falls inside a funding round, say so in the first message — it changes the price, and you are entitled to know that before anything else happens.

Please do not send confidential documents with a first message. Send the claim and the decision; documents travel after an engagement letter and its confidentiality terms exist. This caution is the practice protecting your file, not declining your work.

This form is a demonstration: it transmits nothing and stores nothing. On the live practice site it files the enquiry in the notebook; here, it shows you exactly what filing looks like. To make the enquiry real, send the same three things to the email address on this page.
