Open an enquiry

One path, no funnel. Write by email or use the form — both land in the same logbook, and both are answered by me, not an assistant.

Direct email

enquiries@vossvolcano.com

Response times. Ordinary enquiries: within two working days. If a volcano is restless and the matter is time-critical, put UNREST in the subject line — those are read the same day, including weekends during an episode.

If something is erupting now: contact your national observatory or civil protection first. They hold the alert level and the legal duty. Then write to me.

What a first message should carry

  • The volcano — name, and the survey or agency responsible for it
  • The problem in two sentences: unrest, a map, a network, a training need
  • What record exists already — traces, maps, reports, even old ones
  • Your timeline, and the date a decision has to be made by
  • Who else is reading the same signals (observatory, university, another consultant)

No newsletters, no lists, no follow-up sequence. If the work is not mine to take, the reply will say whose it is.
