What to send

  • The volcano — name, or coordinates if it has no famous one.
  • The decision — the map, claim, plan or programme the work must support.
  • The fixed dates — hearings, seasons, budget years.
  • The data you hold — even a sentence: "twenty years of gas data, uncalibrated" is useful.
  • If it is unrest, say so first. Eruption-response terms exist for exactly this; the reply comes the same day.

What happens next

  • Reply within two working days (same day for unrest).
  • A scoping call, 30–45 minutes, no fee.
  • A written scope and fixed fee — or a better name.
  • Work starts on a signed scope: 30% on commission.
