# Credits — Magma Core (volcanologist portfolio template)

## Persona & content
Dr Sara Lindqvist and the Magma Core practice are **fictional**, created for this
template and disclosed as fictional in the footer of every page. The science is real:
the volcano alert-level scheme follows the USGS system (US Government, public domain),
the VEI volume bands follow Newhall & Self (1982), and the historical eruptions cited
(St. Helens 1980, Pinatubo 1991, Tambora 1815, Nevado del Ruiz 1985, Eyjafjallajökull
2010, Toba ~74 ka) are established record. Client engagements, the testimonial and all
fees are invented for the persona; prices are plausible shapes, not quotations.

## Sound
None, by ruling. The trade's product is judgement, not audio; eruption sound on a
portfolio would be spectacle. The stub's hover/click sound hooks were deleted with
the rest of the stub.

## The stub's promises, dealt with honestly
template.json describes "lava flow gradient layers, obsidian glass panels, and seismic
waveform overlays". All three were decoration, and two of them (gradients, glass) are
named slop in this program — they were replaced, not implemented: the working
instruments on this site are the Alert-Level Explorer and the VEI reference, which
teach the two scales the trade actually runs on. The stub's animated seismic canvas
and sound hooks were deleted.
