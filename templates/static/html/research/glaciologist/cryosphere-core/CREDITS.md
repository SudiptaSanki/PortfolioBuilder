# Credits — Cryosphere Core

## Persona
Freya Nilsen and "Nilsen Polar" are fictional, invented for this template
and disclosed as fictional in the footer of every page. No real person's
name, face or work is used. The science content (ice-core records, EPICA
Dome C's ~800,000-year span, volcanic marker horizons, methods, treaty
practice) is real and stated carefully; the depth–age explorer is a
clearly labelled composite teaching model, not a named site's timescale.

## Sound
None. The sound need-test fails for this trade — ice makes no teachable
sound here — so the site ships no audio at all: nothing autoplays,
nothing plays on hover, no synthesis. Ruling recorded per program rules.

## Code
Hand-written HTML/CSS/JS for this template. No libraries, no frameworks.
The stub's bubble-particle canvas and frosted-glass card were removed:
the template.json's promised "depth climate log" is delivered instead as
the working Depth–Age Explorer on the home page.
