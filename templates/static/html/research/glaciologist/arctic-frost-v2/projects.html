Programmes · Case files

Three programmes, told the way they are reported.

Every programme in the book is written up the same way it is filed to the client: the brief, the decisions taken in the field, and the outcome — including the season that failed. A monitoring practice that only publishes its good years is selling something other than measurement.

Case file 01 · The long series

Ten balance years, one honest line

Client
Water authority
Region
Mainland Norway
Glaciers
6
Period
2016 — running
Status
Running

The brief

A hydropower authority's reservoir-inflow models rested on glacier data from the 1990s. They commissioned a standing programme: six glaciers across the catchment, measured every year, reported in a form their hydrologists could use directly — not a research paper, a working series.

Decisions

Stake networks were placed by elevation band, not by convenience, and kept fixed even when two stakes became awkward to reach as the ice thinned — moving a stake quietly is how a series stops meaning anything. When the survey method improved in year five, the whole series was re-homogenised and the change logged in the report, so any reader can see exactly where method ends and glacier begins.

Outcome

Ten balance years: a cumulative −6.8 m w.e. across the catchment's glaciers, with two positive years inside the decade — +0.21 and +0.08 m w.e. — printed in the same table as the bad years. The authority revised its long-term inflow assumptions on the strength of the trend, not of any single season. That distinction is the programme's whole product.

Series figures: cumulative balance 2016–2025 −6.8 m w.e. · most negative year −1.31 m w.e. · positive years 2 of 10 · method changes 1, logged and homogenised.

Case file 02 · The baseline problem

A baseline built from a neighbour

Client
Regional authority
Region
The Alps
Glaciers
3 (1 added 2023)
Period
2023 — running
Status
Running

The brief

Mid-programme, the authority added a third glacier — the one their town's water supply actually depends on. It had never been measured: no stakes, no pits, no baseline. They wanted its first number to stand next to the other two without an asterisk-shaped embarrassment.

Decisions

Year one was built honestly from what existed: two spring snow pits and probing on the new glacier, read against the neighbouring monitored glacier's series, whose weather the two share. The report printed the transfer plainly — and printed its price: a year-one uncertainty of ±0.30 m w.e., three times the programme's usual ±0.10. A stake network went in that first autumn so the borrowed baseline would only ever be borrowed once.

Outcome

By year three the new glacier's record stands on its own stakes, and the borrowed first year remains in the series with its wider uncertainty attached — flagged, not smoothed over. The authority's engineers now quote the glacier's trend from year two and its level from year one, which is exactly the right way round.

Baseline figures: year-one uncertainty ±0.30 m w.e. (programme standard ±0.10) · stake network 14 stakes, installed autumn of year one · borrowed-baseline years in the final series 1, permanently flagged.

Case file 03 · The failed season

The autumn the mountain said no

Client
Research institute
Region
Svalbard
Glaciers
4
Period
2019 — running
Status
Running · 2024 flagged

The brief

Four coastal glaciers, measured since 2019 for an institute's sea-level work. The autumn read — the visit the whole year's number hangs on — is a narrow window between the end of melt and the return of the polar night.

Decisions

In 2024 the window closed early: a storm cycle, then avalanche conditions on the approach slopes. The read was abandoned on the second attempt — a decision made the night before, by the rule written in the programme plan, not argued with at the tent door. Rather than leave a gap, the year was reconstructed from the spring accumulation survey and the end-of-summer snowline mapped from satellite scenes, and published as what it is: an estimate.

Outcome

The 2024 balance sits in the series flagged “estimated — method note attached”, with an uncertainty of ±0.35 m w.e. against the usual ±0.10, and the flag is permanent: it will still be there in thirty years, next to the number it qualifies. The institute kept the series unbroken and renewed the programme. A gap would have been tidier. It would also have been a lie of omission.

2024 figures: field attempts 2, both abandoned on the written rule · published balance estimated, ±0.35 m w.e. · seasons lost from the series 0.
