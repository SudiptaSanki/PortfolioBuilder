Before you write

The programme estimator prices a programme's shape from the printed rates in about a minute — worth doing first, so the conversation can start at access and logistics, where the real variables are.

And if your question is about a single season's dramatic number: read the honesty page on About first. One year is weather. The practice will happily measure you a decade.

Strand Glacier Programmes is the practice of Dr Elin Strand, glaciologist, Tromsø. Elin, her clients and her glaciers are fictional; the glaciology, the arithmetic and the published rates are real practice, and any resemblance to a working programme is the resemblance of method, not of record.
