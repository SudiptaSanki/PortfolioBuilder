A community lab is a safety culture with a centrifuge in it

Bench 14 has been running since 2019 in a former print shop in Gowanus. Eighty-six members, one paid staff member, no institutional review board and therefore a great deal of self-imposed paperwork. This page is the paperwork.

What the bench is for

Founded 2019
412 sq ft
BSL-1 only

The lab exists for the experiment that is too small for a university and too strange for a company. Synthetic biology hobbyists, a furniture designer growing bacterial cellulose, two high-school teachers building a course, a nurse testing whether a cheap pH meter can be trusted. The common thread is not ambition. It is that none of these projects could justify a lab of their own.

We do not do gene drives, human samples, vertebrate animals, or anything that requires a biosafety cabinet. That is not a philosophical position. It is a lease, a ventilation system and an insurance policy, and those three things are the actual limits of the lab.

Everything that leaves the bench gets published. Protocol, result, and the parts that did not work. Forty-one protocols have gone out this year, and about a third of them were corrections to earlier ones. The corrections are the useful part.

Biosafety, plainly

Read before
your first session

  1. 01Only BSL-1 organisms. E. coli K-12, P. putida, common lab yeasts. Nothing that carries a virulence factor, nothing environmental without a kill switch.
  2. 02Antibiotic resistance markers are allowed on plasmids but never in an organism that leaves the building alive. Plates are autoclaved or bleached before they go in the bin.
  3. 03No open flame. We use a small electric incinerator for loops and a lot of 70 % ethanol. The fire department was clear about this after 2021.
  4. 04Gloves, goggles and closed shoes at the bench. Long hair tied. No food or drink, including the coffee that everybody carries in anyway.
  5. 05Every spill is logged, even the ones that were only water. The log has 214 entries and two of them were real.
  6. 06If you are unsure whether something is allowed, it is not allowed until two members and I have said otherwise in writing.

The full manual is 18 pages, mostly checklists. Reading it takes about forty minutes and is the only prerequisite for a bench slot.

Where it came from

A short, unromantic
history

  1. 2026

    Seventy-two member protocols published

    An open library with reagents, failure notes and a cost line for every entry. Two community labs in Europe have adopted it wholesale.

  2. 2024

    Gel rig v3 released

    A laser-cut acrylic design that takes standard platinum wire. Twenty-three builds so far, including one in a school in Nairobi.

  3. 2022

    The CRISPR workshop starts

    Six weeks, twelve people, biosafety first. It has run nine times and produced one published protocol and four people who now teach it.

  4. 2020

    Closed for eleven months

    The pandemic. We lent the thermal cycler to a public health lab and spent the year writing the manual we should have had from the start.

  5. 2018—19

    A print shop with a sink

    Signed a five-year lease on 412 sq ft, installed a second-hand sink and a fume hood rated for nothing in particular. Opened with six members.
