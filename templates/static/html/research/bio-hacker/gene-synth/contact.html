Access / membership / workshops

Book a bench. Read the manual. Bring closed shoes

Bench slots are two hours, two people at a time, Tuesday and Thursday from 18:00. Membership includes consumables at cost, the protocol library, and one induction session that nobody has ever enjoyed and everybody has needed.

The form composes a local draft for this demonstration. In the lab it opens a ticket in the shared inbox, which is checked on Tuesday and Thursday afternoons.

Practical notes

The door is the unmarked grey one between the woodshop and the bike repair shop. Buzz twice; the intercom is old. If it fails, call and someone will come down.

Membership is $60 a month. Waived for students, and for anyone who teaches a protocol back to the group. Consumables are billed at cost, usually under $15 for a session.
