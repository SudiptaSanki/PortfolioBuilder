No audio is used anywhere on this site. The stub's "gene sequencing"
Web Audio synth (a beep on a button) was removed, not rebuilt: screening
records and yield arithmetic are read, not heard, and no interaction here
teaches through audio. The sound need-test fails; the site is silent by
ruling, and the ruling is printed in the site footer.

## Content note

Dr Naomi Osei is a fictional persona; the practice, clients and case files
are illustrative. The site deliberately contains no DNA sequences, no
protocols and no wet-lab instructions. The yield arithmetic
(full-length fraction ≈ c^(n−1)), the screening practice against
regulated-agent lists, and the refusal categories are true to the trade.
