The Sun writes in X-rays. We read it nine minutes late and four days early

A flare is visible eight minutes after it happens, because that is how long light takes. The plasma it throws arrives two to four days later, if it arrives at all. Everything between those two facts is forecasting: a physics problem with a deadline and a public.

Three scales

What the letters mean, and who feels them

The NOAA scales are the only part of space weather that most people ever meet, usually in a headline. Each one runs from one to five and each one describes a different physical channel arriving at a different time.

R

Radio blackout

X-rays arrive in eight minutes and ionise the dayside D-layer, which absorbs high-frequency radio. R1 is a brief fade at the subsolar point; R5 is a blackout over most of the daylit hemisphere lasting hours. This is the fastest effect and the only one that is essentially immediate.

R1 M1 · R3 X1 · R5 X20

S

Radiation storm

Protons arrive within an hour to a day and matter to satellites, aircrew on polar routes and anyone outside low Earth orbit. The flux is small in absolute terms and enormous relative to the background, and it can last for days as the same region keeps flaring.

S1 10 pfu · S3 1,000 pfu · S5 100,000 pfu

G

Geomagnetic storm

The CME arrives two to four days later and couples to the magnetosphere if its field points south. G1 is aurora at high latitude; G5, in May 2024, put aurora over Mexico and stressed transformers in two continents. This is the channel that reaches the ground.

G1 Kp5 · G3 Kp7 · G5 Kp9

Campaigns and forecasts

  1. AR 3664

    The May 2024 storm

    Five X-class flares in a week from one region and a G5 storm on 10 May. I issued four forecasts in that window; three were right, and the one that was wrong called the arrival twelve hours early because the drag model was fitted to a faster earlier event.

    2024
  2. LOOP-7

    Kink oscillations in a fan loop

    Eleven loops in one active region observed with AIA 171 for four hours, with periods from 140 to 380 seconds. The periods imply a field strength that agrees with the magnetogram to within 20 %, which for this method is a result.

    2023
