Note

What makes a solar request answerable

A date and a region. "AR 3664, 9–11 May 2024" is a request. "The big storm in 2024" is a conversation. The archive is indexed by region and time, and a request with both gets answered from the data rather than from memory.

The channel. XRS, EUV imaging, magnetogram, coronagraph and in-situ each answer different questions and none substitutes for another. Asking for "the flare energy" without saying which channel usually means the asker wants the X-ray class and does not yet know that it is not the same thing.

The use. A benchmark needs a reproducible table; a paper needs error bars; a press enquiry needs a sentence. I answer all three, and the sentence takes the longest because every simplification has to be chosen deliberately.
