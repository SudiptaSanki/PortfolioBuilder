What happens next

  • A reply within two working days — questions first, always.
  • If the study is sound: a reconnaissance proposal at the fixed €450, credited against the study fee.
  • If it is not sound, or not mine: I say so, and name who does it better.

Useful things to mention

  • The spring or stream, and the nearest settlement.
  • What the water is doing — falling, flooding, discolouring, vanishing.
  • The decision riding on the answer, and its deadline if it has one.
  • Any existing records: gauge boards, old reports, a grandfather's notebook. All of them count.
Kovač Karst Hydrogeology

Karst groundwater studies — spring monitoring, dye-tracer testing, catchment delineation — from a field base in Postojna, Slovenia, across the Dinaric karst and other limestone terrains.

Science support only: abstraction licences and source-protection decisions are made by the competent regulator. Reports from this practice are evidence for those decisions, never the decision itself.

About this site

Dr Ana Kovač is a fictional demonstration persona created for this portfolio template. The studies described are illustrative composites written to real practice standards — they are not records of real commissions, and no real spring, client or catchment is depicted.

Photography is used under the Pexels licence and credited in CREDITS.md inside this template. Every photograph is a reference image of karst terrain, captioned as such — never a study site.
