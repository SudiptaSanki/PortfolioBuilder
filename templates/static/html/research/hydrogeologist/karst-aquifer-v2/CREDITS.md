## Persona & content

Dr Ana Kovač is a fictional demonstration persona (disclosed in the footer of
every page). The three studies are illustrative composites written to real
karst-hydrogeology practice — Maillet recession analysis, dye-tracer testing
with uranine and charcoal receptors, segmented recession fitting — and depict
no real commission, spring, client or catchment.
