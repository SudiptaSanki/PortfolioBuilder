01

Limestone is a plumbing system that nobody drew

Karst covers roughly a fifth of Europe's ice-free land and supplies drinking water to about a quarter of its population. It is also the hardest terrain in which to say where water goes. A sandstone aquifer is a sponge: flow is slow, spread out and reasonably predictable. A karst aquifer is a sponge with pipes dissolved through it.

Those pipes form along bedding planes and faults, widened by carbonic acid over hundreds of thousands of years. A conduit two metres across can carry most of a catchment's discharge while the surrounding rock contributes a trickle. Models built on the rock matrix alone miss the flood wave by days.

The practical consequence is that a pollutant dropped into a swallow hole can reach a spring in hours, not the months a porous-medium model would predict. And a spring can look clean while its catchment is not: turbidity arrives with the first flush, long before the chemistry changes.

My work sits between the tracer test and the water-supply decision. I map the connections, measure how fast they move water, and put uncertainty around the answer so that a utility knows how much treatment it actually needs.

Dye-tracer breakthrough bench

Set a conduit and a gradient, release a mass of dye, and watch what a spring monitor downstream would record. The model couples pipe-flow velocity with one-dimensional advection–dispersion and a slow matrix-exchange tail, the two features that make karst curves look nothing like a Gaussian.

04

What I bring to a catchment

Tracer tests

Uranine, rhodamine WT, eosin and sodium chloride, with field fluorometers at up to nine springs simultaneously. Injection design, mass balance and breakthrough inversion in one pass.

38 tests · 2016—2025

Continuous monitoring

Pressure transducers, conductivity and turbidity loggers on springs and in caves, at 5-minute resolution. Rating curves for spring discharge, including the storm events that are usually missed.

14 stations · 6 catchments

Numerical models

MODFLOW 6 with the Conduit Flow Process (CFP) for conduit–matrix exchange, and a lumped reservoir model for utilities that need a fast scenario tool rather than a full grid.

MODFLOW 6 · CFP · Python

Advising

Source-protection zoning, spill-response maps and monitoring plans for municipal utilities, parks and a regional landfill operator. Reports in Slovene, Croatian or English.

9 utilities · 3 national parks
