What to send

  • Detector & run — which instrument, which observing period.
  • Data span — how many weeks, and whether witness channels are included.
  • The problem — the glitch family, the trigger, or the cohort to train.
  • Deadline — run starts and breaks are fixed dates; say yours early.

Response & availability

Replies within two working days, with either a scoped quote from the published fees or a plain statement that the engagement isn't a fit — and, where I can, the name of the team to ask instead. Current position: investigations booking for the next run break; audit slots, two per run cycle; one sprint place on the waitlist.
