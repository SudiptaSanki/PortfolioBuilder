# Credits & licensing — ligo-interferometer ("Castellano Detector Characterisation")

## Photography: none used — drawing-led by design

This template ships with **no photographs**. Its design system is the detector log:
every visual is an original SVG figure drawn for this template (the strain-trace
header rule, the Fig. 2 noise-budget chart drawn by the page script from a stated
model, the Fig. 3 time–frequency chirp track, and the Fig. 4 interferometer
schematic on the About page). All figures are labelled "schematic" on the page
itself and are never presented as detector data or as photographs of real
facilities.

Why no stock photography: for this trade a generic laboratory or observatory
photograph would imply a real site and a real affiliation, which the fictional
persona must not claim. The drawn-figure route is also the direction sheet's
sanctioned option ("pure CSS/SVG/canvas where the theme is stronger without
photos"). A Pexels photo pass was attempted during the build; the environment's
search route returned no verifiable Pexels photo pages in the build window, and
no unverified URLs were used. If a later pass adds photography, it must come
from Pexels with photographer name + photo-page URL recorded here, captioned
as a reference photograph.

## Science sources (public facts, stated with approximations marked)

- GW150914: sweep ≈35→150 Hz, ~0.2 s in band, peak strain ≈1.0×10⁻²¹,
  chirp mass ≈28 M☉, distance ≈410 Mpc (LIGO/Virgo published values).
- GW170817: ~100 s in band from 24 Hz, chirp mass ≈1.19 M☉.
- Noise budget bands: seismic dominates low frequencies (wall below ~10 Hz),
  thermal (suspension/coating) the mid band, quantum (radiation pressure low,
  shot noise high) the high band — the standard published picture; Fig. 2's
  depths are a schematic model, stated as such on the page.
