Work, described at process level

Casework is confidential and stays that way. What can be published is how the work was done — the brief as received, the decisions taken, and what the work product changed. No case facts, no locations, no outcomes of proceedings appear on this page, and none ever will.

CA-2019-114

The amended report

Audit closed 2024

The brief

No client brief began this file. The archive audits its own retained specimens on a rolling programme, and the 2024 audit reached a 2019 instruction in which this practice had served a post-mortem interval estimate. The audit's instruction to itself was the standing one: re-identify the retained specimens as if they had arrived that morning, and compare.

The decisions

Re-examination under the microscope found that a portion of the retained material had been recorded as one blow-fly species where the prepared specimens keyed to a sibling species — a pair separable reliably only on fine structural characters, which the original examination had not prepared. The two species develop at different rates. The decision taken the same week: the estimate in the 2019 statement could no longer be relied on as written, so the file was reopened, the development analysis re-run against the correct species' data, and a formal amendment drafted before any party asked for one.

The outcome

A formal amendment was served on the instructing parties, before the proceedings in which the statement was to be used. The estimated window moved by 26 hours and — because the corrected species' data carried wider tolerances — was honestly widened. Nothing in the amendment was softened: it states what was wrong, why, and what changed. The practice rule that came out of this file is now standing: one identification in ten, chosen at random, is independently re-checked before any statement is served. I would rather amend a report than defend a mistake.

CA-2023-041

Cold-case archive review

2023

The brief

A police cold-case unit instructed the archive to review insect material retained from a historic investigation, and to state — narrowly, and only if the material allowed it — whether the original estimated window could be improved with modern keys and development data. The brief was explicit that a conclusion of “the material cannot say” would be a useful answer.

The decisions

The first decision was an audit, not an analysis: every retained item was accession-checked against the original exhibit list and condition-graded before any identification was attempted. Roughly half the material had degraded past reliable determination and was set aside, on the record. The usable evidence sat in the empty puparial cases — the hardened shells that persist long after the insect has gone — which were re-identified against modern keys. Temperature reconstruction used the nearest station record of the period, corrected by a stated margin and reported with that correction visible rather than absorbed silently into the figures.

The outcome

The window the unit was working to narrowed from “up to three weeks” to a stated six-day range, with its assumptions printed in the statement. Two lines of enquiry that depended on the wider window were closed by the unit; the file records that decision as theirs, not the archive's. The review also recorded, in terms, what the surviving material could never establish — so that no future reader of the statement mistakes its silence for evidence.

CA-2021-078

A regional succession dataset

2021 – 2024

The brief

Post-mortem interval work leans on succession data — which insects arrive, in what order, under what conditions — and the published data for this region was thin and old. A university partner and this practice set out to build a regional reference dataset properly: replicated, seasonal, and recorded to a standard a court could be shown.

The decisions

The study ran at a licensed research facility over three years, across all seasons, with temperature logged at the study site itself rather than borrowed from a distant station — the single most common weakness in the older literature, designed out from the start. Sampling followed a fixed protocol, identifications were verified against the archive's reference collection, and the dataset was structured so that every record carries its conditions with it: a succession record without its temperatures is an anecdote.

The outcome

The dataset — 38 sampling rounds across three years — was published openly for regional practitioners and is now the reference this practice cites in its own statements for regional work, named in the statement so any reader can inspect it. It does not make estimates exact; it makes their basis checkable, which is the most a dataset can honestly do.
