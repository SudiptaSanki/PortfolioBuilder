Casework 2023–2026

Eight cases, and the correction in the third one

Case numbers are the laboratory's own, not the court's, and details that could identify a person have been removed. Each entry gives the material, the method and the limit of what the insects could carry.

Showing 8 of 8 cases

  1. 2026-041

    A flat above a bakery

    Third-instar Calliphora vicina recovered from beneath a lifted kitchen floor, with the scene logger showing 26 °C for six hours of every day and 17 °C at night. The prosecution's expert had used the constant-temperature table and proposed four days. I reared a subsample of the same cohort at 20 °C and found the local strain needed about 15 % more degree hours to reach the same stage, which moved the minimum to six days. The defendant's alibi covered the first of the two candidate days and no longer helped.

  2. 2025-217

    A car in a nature reserve

    No larvae remained, only empty puparia in the footwell and a beetle fauna in the boot. The succession list — Dermestes, Necrobia, a late-arriving staphylinid — was consistent with a body exposed through a warm autumn and into winter, not the two weeks the defendant claimed. No single species carried this one; the community did.

    succession onlysix monthsno development clock
  3. 2024-309

    The interval I withdrew

    I reported 8–11 days from third-instar material and a weather-station series corrected for an unheated garage. Three weeks later the file disclosed that the body had been wrapped in plastic for part of that period, which raised humidity inside the wrap and slowed the larvae. I issued a second report withdrawing the range and explaining why, and the court heard both reports on the same day. The corrected interval was 10–17 days.

    withdrawnreissuedhumidity
  4. 2025-088

    A care home, one room, two summers

    Larvae of Lucilia sericata in a pressure sore of a resident who had been bed-bound for months. The question was not the time of death but the duration of neglect: whether the wound had been colonised once, weeks earlier, or repeatedly. Puparia in three size classes said repeatedly. This is the case I think about most, because the entomology was easy and the outcome was not.

    three cohortsL. sericataliving patient
  5. 2024-176

    Poached deer, two sites

    Two red deer carcasses found 40 km apart, both with calliphorid fauna that included a species absent from the surrounding woodland but present at a livestock farm between the sites. That is not proof of transport on its own, and the report said so. It became useful when a vehicle was searched and the same species turned up in the load bed.

    faunal comparisonnot proof alone
  6. 2023-402

    Puparia in a medieval churchyard

    Eight soil samples from a burial excavated before a development, each containing empty puparia. The species assemblage — Hydrotaea, Fannia, and a single Calliphora — suggested bodies that had been exposed for some days before burial rather than buried immediately, which is a small but real fact about the burial rite. The puparia survived 700 years; the interpretation depends on the soil pH and the drainage, both of which were measured.

    empty pupariaburial practicesoil pH
  7. 2025-133

    Larvae as a toxicology sample

    A suspected overdose where blood was unavailable and the larvae had fed on the tissue for several days. Midazolam and its metabolite were detected in pooled third-instar material by LC-MS/MS, with a concentration an order of magnitude above the rearing substrate. The result supported the toxicology picture but could not give a dose: larval concentration depends on stage, feeding site and time on the body, and the report said all three.

    LC-MS/MSqualitative only
  8. 2024-265

    Moths in a food facility

    Not a death at all. A prosecution under food hygiene regulations needed to show a persistent infestation rather than a single contaminated pallet. Pheromone trap catches, larval head capsules in the dust and the age structure of the pupae together described a population that had been breeding on site for at least four generations.

    age structureregulatory
