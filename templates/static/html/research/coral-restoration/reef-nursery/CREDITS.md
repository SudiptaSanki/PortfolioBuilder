# Credits — Reef Nursery (coral restoration portfolio)

## Persona

**Sofia Reyes** is a fictional demonstration persona, disclosed as
fictional in the footer of every page. Reef Nursery, Casa Brisa Resort,
its sustainability manager, the partner programmes, fees and every
figure in the log are invented for this template. No real person,
resort or programme is depicted.

The restoration science is real and was kept to well-established
practice: *Acropora cervicornis* (staghorn) is the fast-growing
branching coral raised on underwater "coral tree" nurseries throughout
the Caribbean; *Acropora palmata* (elkhorn) is the slower, heavier
breakwater species; massive corals (*Orbicella faveolata*,
*Diploria labyrinthiformis*) are raised by micro-fragmentation —
one-centimetre fragments that race to cover a plug and fuse with
same-genotype neighbours. Survival after outplanting is honestly
variable, so the log prints ranges (63–81% across cohorts and survey
ages), never a single miracle figure. Coral bleaching is
temperature-driven, and the Florida/Caribbean risk peaks in the
late-summer months (August–October), when this practice plants
nothing. Broodstock ethics (licensed fragments of opportunity only,
no cutting of healthy wild colonies), minimum-genotype planting,
reserve stock held back against storms, and photo-point monitoring
are all standard, real practice in the field.

## Sound

None, by ruling. The sound need-test fails for this trade: the work
is measured in surviving colonies, not heard, and underwater ambience
would be decoration. The site ships no audio files and no Web Audio.
The ruling is stated in every page footer.
