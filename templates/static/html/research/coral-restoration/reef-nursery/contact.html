What to send

  • Where: the site, nearest dock or marina, rough depth.
  • What happened: bleaching, storm, anchors, construction — and whether the cause is still active.
  • How much: the area you're picturing, even roughly — a house reef, a hectare, a coastline.
  • Who decides: resort, park authority, NGO, private owner — permits follow the answer.
  • When: any season or reporting deadline the work has to meet.

The honest first read

Some sites shouldn't be planted yet — if the cause of loss is still running, we'll say so and say what has to change first, free of charge. Planting into an ongoing insult is billing, not restoration, and the log doesn't carry that kind of entry.
