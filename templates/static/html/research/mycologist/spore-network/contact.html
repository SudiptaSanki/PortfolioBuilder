What to include

  • Where the site is — nearest town is enough at this stage.
  • Rough acreage, and the main tree species if you know them.
  • What prompted it: a felling or planting plan, trees declining, a course to run.
  • Any deadline — planning conditions and felling licences set real ones.

What happens next

A reply within three working days with either dates, a short list of questions, or — where the enquiry is outside the practice's competence — the name of someone better placed. No obligation attaches to asking.
