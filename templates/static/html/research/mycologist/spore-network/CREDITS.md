## Fiction disclosure

Dr Helen Marlowe and Spore Network are fictional, created for this portfolio
template; the footer of every page states this. All species data (taxonomy,
substrate, season, spore print, toxicity and protection status) is real and was
checked against standard references: British Mycological Society checklist,
Index Fungorum nomenclature, and Schedule 8 of the Wildlife and Countryside
Act 1981 for *Hericium erinaceus*. Fees and case-study details are illustrative.
