## Fiction disclosure

Dr Rachel Donovan and Supercell Radar are fictional, created for this
portfolio template; the footer of every page states this. The meteorology
is real and was kept to established references: the reflectivity bands
follow the NOAA/NWS radar legend (dBZ), and the wind ranges and damage
descriptions follow the Enhanced Fujita scale as published by the Storm
Prediction Center / NWS (EF0 65–85 mph … EF5 over 200 mph). Fees and
case-study details are illustrative.

## Sound

None. The stub's hover-triggered thunder synth was deleted: sound fails
the need-test for this profession (thunder ambience is decoration, and
hover audio is a deal-breaker under the program rules). The ruling is
stated in every page footer.
