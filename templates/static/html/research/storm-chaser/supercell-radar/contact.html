One form. One inbox. A person answers.

Tell the station the place, the date or window, and what is at stake. In season the reply comes within one working day; a storm-day enquiry marked urgent in the message is answered the same day.

Demonstration form — on this template nothing is transmitted; a valid submission confirms here, on the page, with a reference. On the live practice site the same form posts to the office inbox.

The same inbox the form posts to. If your date is inside 72 hours, put the date in the subject line — short-notice bookings carry the +40% shown in the fees, and the reply will say so plainly rather than surprise you with it.

What speeds up a reply

  • The place — venue or county, not just the state. Radar coverage and travel both turn on it.
  • The window — date and gate hours for events; the storm date for surveys.
  • The decision — what will be decided from the forecast or the report. It changes what gets written.
  • For surveys — who else is relying on the finding. Joint instructions, like Case 02, are welcome.
