## Artwork
The event displays and the four-lepton mass spectrum are hand-drawn SVG
schematics written for this template, captioned as schematics wherever they
appear. Detector-layer behaviour (electrons stopping in the ECAL, muons
reaching the outer chambers, jets, missing transverse energy, pileup
vertices along the beam line) is true to a general-purpose collider
detector; the events are teaching examples, not recorded data.

## Sound ruling
**No audio anywhere on this site.** The sound need-test fails for this
trade: particle physics is not a sound profession and audio teaches
nothing here — a collision is silent at the point of interaction, and the
stub's Geiger-counter synth (promised in template.json) was a decoration
that made noise about a different instrument entirely. Removed, not rebuilt.

## Honest replacement note
template.json promises a "particle collision splash canvas" and a "Geiger
counter audio synth", under the theme "LHC Collision Amber & Violet". The
canvas splash and the synth were slop and were replaced by the Event
Explorer (hand-drawn schematic event displays with readouts) and the
luminosity panel (N = σ × ℒ, with a printed check). Amber was kept and
given its real job — in event displays amber/yellow is the charged-track
colour; the violet was dropped, because violet is not a data colour in
this field and purple-gradient chrome is precisely the tell this rebuild
exists to remove.
