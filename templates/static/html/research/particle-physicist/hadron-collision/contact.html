What happens next

  • Day 0–2 — a reply with two scoping-call times, or a referral if the work isn't mine.
  • The call — 45 minutes on the problem, the dataset and the deadline. No slides.
  • Within a week — a written scope: question, deliverable, acceptance test, fixed fee or day count, start date.
  • Current opening — March 2026 for studies and reviews; Q2 2026 for one simulation slot.

Useful things to send

  • The dataset's rough size — events, format, where it lives.
  • The deadline's reason — a run, a review, a funding gate.
  • What has already been tried. Especially what has already been tried.
