## Honest replacements

The stub's Unsplash hero image, Lorem ipsum copy and "Project
Alpha / Beta" placeholders were deleted, not rebuilt. Fees, durations
and practice figures printed on the site are the fictional practice's
own published figures, labelled as such on the page; they are
illustrative of how a real practice publishes its numbers, not
medical advice.
