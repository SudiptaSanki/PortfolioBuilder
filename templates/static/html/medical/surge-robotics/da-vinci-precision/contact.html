INSTRUCT THE PRACTICE

One form, one reply, in writing.

Enquiries come from hospitals, departments and boards. Every enquiry gets a reply within two working days — including the ones the honest answer to is "you don't need an assessment yet", which will say what to measure first, free of charge.

Before you write: this practice advises hospitals and surgical teams. It does not accept patient referrals and cannot comment on any individual's care — please don't send clinical details through this form.

Please give your name — reports are addressed to a person, not a department.

That email doesn't read as complete — check it and try again.

A few lines on the situation, please — the reply is only as good as this.

Demonstration form: on this template it transmits nothing and stores nothing. It validates your entries and issues a reference so you can see the full flow.

Enquiry logged. Your reference is CA-2026-000.

A reply follows within two working days. If the situation is a board deadline, say so in the message and it will be answered first.

Or write directly

colin@avery-robotic.example

The .example domain is deliberate: this persona is fictional (see the footer), so the address is a reserved one that cannot receive real mail. In a live deployment this is the practice's real inbox, answered by Dr Avery, not an assistant.

What to send

  • Where the programme stands: system(s), specialties, approximate annual volume.
  • What is being decided, and by when.
  • What you've already measured — logs and figures welcome, impressions expected.
  • Nothing patient-identifiable. Ever.

What happens next

  • A written reply within two working days, naming the fee from the printed table.
  • If the honest answer is "not yet", the reply says what to measure first — free.
  • Scope agreed in writing before any day is booked; two assessments run at a time, no more.
