## Honest replacements (template.json promises)

The stub promised a "sonar ping sweep canvas" and "AUV telemetry"
decoration. Both were slop — ambient animation wearing the trade's
clothes — and were replaced by tools that do the trade's actual work:
the **Dive Planner** (task × depth × current → vehicle class, working
window, deliverables) and the **day-cost estimator**, which reads the
printed rate card and shows its arithmetic, including the weather
contingency and the honest vessel line.

## Sound ruling

**Fails the need-test — no audio anywhere.** A sonar ping would be
decoration, and hover-triggered audio is a deal-breaker regardless.
The ruling is stated in every footer.

## Domain notes (accuracy)

- Vehicle classes: observation-class = eyes only; work-class =
  manipulators/tooling; AUV = untethered route survey. Air diving
  practical limit ~50 m; beyond ~300 m no diver option.
- Current limits: observation-class struggles above ~1 kn; 1.5 kn
  stated as the hard line for fine work — standard working practice.
- Rates are indicative North Sea shapes (£, ex-VAT): the vessel line
  exceeding the vehicle line is the trade's real cost structure, and
  the site says so in print.
