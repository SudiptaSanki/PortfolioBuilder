## Honest replacements of the stub's promises

template.json describes "EEG wave canvas visualizer, neural impulse
audio synth". Both were slop for this trade and were replaced, not
rebuilt:

- The animated canvas wave (decoration over every page) was replaced by
  the **Band Explorer** and the **Artefact Panel**: static, labelled
  schematic traces that teach the actual bands and the actual artefacts.
- The **hover-triggered audio synth** was deleted outright. Sound ruling:
  **fails the need-test** — EEG has no soundtrack, sonification here
  would be decoration, and hover-triggered audio is a deal-breaker in
  this program regardless. There is no audio anywhere in this template.
- The stub's framing (intracortical arrays, prosthetic control, "neural
  decoding") contradicted the trade's honest scope and was replaced by
  the non-invasive, research-support practice the site describes,
  including its printed refusal of "mind reading" claims.
