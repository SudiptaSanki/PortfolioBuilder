The .example domain is deliberate: this persona and practice are fictional, created for a portfolio template, so the address is a reserved demonstration domain that cannot receive mail. The form beside it follows the same rule — it demonstrates, and transmits nothing.

What to send

  • Channel count and sampling rate
  • Dataset hours, and the file format
  • What the study or product is trying to show
  • The specific worry — a smell, a gap, a too-clean result
  • Your ethics board's approval status, for study data

The reply you will get

A personal reply within two working days: whether the work fits, what it would cost in the shape printed on the Index sheet, and — for audits — the fixed fee once dataset hours are known. If it does not fit, the reply says why, and suggests who it might fit instead.
