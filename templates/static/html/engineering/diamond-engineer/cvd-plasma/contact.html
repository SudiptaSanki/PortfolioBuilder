Lead times and prices

        
A standard thermal run is 8–10 weeks from purchase order, including lapping. Single crystal is 14–20 weeks because the growth alone is 400 hours. Optical coatings are the fastest at five weeks.

What ships with a film

        
A Raman spectrum at three points with FWHM, a thickness map, a photograph of the witness coupon, and the thermal or optical measurement if the grade is specified by that number. Certificates are traceable to the run number.

      


      

        
What we will not do

        
We do not promise single crystal above 15 mm, we do not ship without metrology to hit a deadline, and we do not quote a thermal number we have not measured on a coupon from the same film.
