G-207 · 2025

Spreader 100

100 mm polycrystalline disc
for a radar power module

Requirement

A 100 mm heat spreader under a GaN module running at 4 kW: κ above 1,700 W/m·K, thickness 0.6 mm ±0.02, and a flatness that lets a 30 µm indium foil do the rest. Quantity 40, with delivery in eleven weeks.

Recipe

940 °C, 2.1% CH₄, 4.2 kW, 110 Torr, 96 hours. Seeded at 1010 cm⁻² with a 5 nm suspension. The high power keeps the plasma ball wide over a 100 mm holder, which is the whole reason this grade needs a 6 kW reactor.

Result

Measured κ 1,860 W/m·K on the witness coupon, thickness 0.61 mm, Raman FWHM 3.1 cm⁻¹. Thirty-eight of forty shipped; two failed flatness after lapping and were remade. The customer's junction temperature fell 23 K against their aluminium nitride reference.

G-198 · 2024

Plate 12

12 × 12 mm single crystal
quantum substrate

Requirement

A low-nitrogen single-crystal plate for a nitrogen-vacancy magnetometer group: dislocation density below 104 cm⁻², Raman FWHM under 2.2 cm⁻¹, surface Ra under 1 nm, and no polishing damage in the top 2 µm.

Recipe

1,050 °C, 0.8% CH₄, 2.4 kW, 130 Torr, 420 hours on a 4° off-axis HPHT seed. Deliberately slow: the step-flow mode needs a low supersaturation, and pushing the methane here buys growth at the cost of the one property the customer is paying for.

Result

FWHM 1.9 cm⁻¹, dislocation density 6 × 103 cm⁻² at the centre and 2.4 × 104 at the corners. The plate shipped with the corner figure in the certificate rather than the centre one, which is the honest way to sell it.

G-211 · 2025

Window coat

2 µm nanocrystalline
on germanium

Requirement

A hard, transparent coating on a 50 mm germanium window for a thermal imager used in sand: abrasion resistance better than DLC, absorption below 0.15 cm⁻¹ from 8 to 12 µm, and no delamination over 200 thermal cycles.

Recipe

760 °C, 3.4% CH₄, 3.6 kW, 80 Torr, 9 hours. Low temperature keeps the germanium from softening and the nanocrystalline regime gives the smooth surface the optical figure needs.

Result

2.1 µm thick, Ra 3.4 nm, absorption 0.11 cm⁻¹ at 10 µm. Passed 200 cycles and a 500 g Taber test. This is now the standard optical recipe; the only complaint is that 760 °C makes the run sensitive to holder contact.

G-203 · 2024

Electrode B

Boron-doped film
for water treatment

Requirement

A conductive diamond electrode for electrochemical oxidation of wastewater: 1020 cm⁻³ boron, 5 µm thick on niobium, and a service life of 2,000 hours at 30 mA/cm² in chloride.

Recipe

880 °C, 1.6% CH₄, 3.1 kW, 95 Torr, 14 hours, with trimethylboron at 400 ppm in the gas feed. Boron changes the plasma chemistry as well as the film: the growth rate drops 30% and the Raman line broadens.

Result

Resistivity 0.008 Ω·cm, 2,400 hours in an accelerated cell before the coating failed at a pinhole. The failure was a substrate defect, not the film, but the customer now specifies an electropolished niobium surface.

G-214 · 2026

Fast window

High-rate trial
rejected at metrology

Requirement

An internal experiment: can the optical recipe be pushed to 6 µm/h by raising methane to 6% and power to 5.4 kW? If it worked, coating cost would fall by half.

Recipe

820 °C, 6.0% CH₄, 5.4 kW, 120 Torr, 5 hours. It grew at 6.8 µm/h, which is the fastest film this lab has produced.

Result

Rejected. Raman FWHM 7.9 cm⁻¹, sp² content 4.1%, absorption 1.8 cm⁻¹ — a grey film, not a window. The run is kept in the log because it maps the edge of the process window and because the next person who suggests "just add methane" can be shown it.

G-189 · 2023

Spread 150

150 mm coating on
silicon carbide

Requirement

A 40 µm diamond layer directly on an SiC tile for an inverter module — not a free-standing film, but a coating that survives 3,000 thermal cycles between −40 and 150 °C without delaminating.

Recipe

900 °C, 2.4% CH₄, 4.8 kW, 105 Torr, 11 hours, with a graded nucleation step (2 hours at 0.9% CH₄) to reduce interfacial stress.

Result

38 µm, κ 1,620 W/m·K in-plane, and 3,000 cycles passed with no delamination. The graded nucleation step added 20 minutes and removed the interfacial cracking that had failed the previous three attempts.
