What a useful first message contains

The part: material if you know it, dimensions, quantity.

The budget: the temperature drop, transmission or impurity figure the part must meet — and where that figure came from.

The date: when the part is needed. Growth is days per fraction of a millimetre; a honest date gets an honest answer.

What happens next: a reply that either books a specification call, quotes from the printed rates on Sheet 01, or tells you plainly that a cheaper material — or another grower — fits better.
