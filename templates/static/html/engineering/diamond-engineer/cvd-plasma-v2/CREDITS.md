## Persona

Dr Marta Kowalczyk / Kowalczyk CVD is a fictional persona created for
this template; no real person's name, face or work is used. The
materials science is stated at established-practice level: Table T1's
conductivities are through-plane values at 25 °C with thickness and
method attached (single-crystal CVD ≈ 2,100 W/m·K; polycrystalline
film falls as it thins — ≈1,500 at 500 µm, ≈700 at 50 µm; copper OFHC
398; 4H-SiC ≈370; silicon ≈148), and the calculator's relation
R_th = L / (k · A) is the standard one-dimensional conduction
resistance. Fees, runs and clients are illustrative.
