Before you write

Three things make a first reply useful rather than polite: the site (a chart position or a lease-area name), the species or sound of concern, and the decision the work supports — a consent, a construction window, a research question. If one of the three is unknown, say so; "unknown" is a workable starting point, and grading it is part of the first reply.

Current availability

Baseline surveys: booking for the autumn window. Real-time monitoring: one spring campaign slot. Detector-validation audits: waitlist until winter. If your programme is urgent because a regulator has asked a question, say that in the first line — the queue bends for examinations, not for tenders.
