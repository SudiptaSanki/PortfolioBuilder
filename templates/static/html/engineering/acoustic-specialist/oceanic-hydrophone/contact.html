Lead times

        
A design review takes a week. A new mooring needs 10–14 weeks from signature: instrument build and calibration, vessel booking, and a weather window that does not exist in the Southern Ocean between May and August.

What to include

        
Coordinates or a chart snippet, water depth, the species or noise source in question, the decision the report feeds into, and your deadline. If there is an existing monitoring programme, send the last report — I would rather extend a baseline than duplicate one.

      


      

        
Where I work

        
Based in Lisbon, mobilising from Portugal, the UK and South Africa. Fieldwork is quoted with travel and mobilisation separated, so you can see what the boat costs before you commit to it.
