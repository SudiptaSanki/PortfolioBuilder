## Honest replacements (template.json promises vs. what shipped)

- The stub's "sunburst ray canvas" was decoration and was replaced by
  the Yield Explorer: a working 12-month generation model (bars,
  printed table and payback estimator all computed from one data
  model in script.js, so the page cannot disagree with itself).
- The stub's hover-triggered "energy grid hum" audio was deleted, not
  rebuilt. Sound ruling: FAILS the need-test — a solar designer's
  portfolio has nothing that needs to make a noise, and hover audio is
  a deal-breaker pattern regardless.
- The stub persona "HELIOS VANCE" was discarded; the yield model uses
  a stated reference profile (south-facing, ~35°, unshaded, central
  England, 992 kWh/kWp/yr — PVGIS-class) with its assumptions printed
  on the page beside the numbers.
