What to send

  • Your postcode — the yield sheet is drawn for your location, not a national average.
  • A year of electricity bills, or a smart-meter export (CSV) if you have one.
  • Roof direction and any obvious shading, if you know them. Photos help; they're not required.
  • For a quote check: the installer's quote itself, exactly as issued.

What happens next

  • A reply within two working days with a first straight answer.
  • If it stacks up: a fixed fee from the published card, agreed in writing before any work.
  • If it doesn't: a short letter saying why, charged at the assessment fee at most — and no design sold.
