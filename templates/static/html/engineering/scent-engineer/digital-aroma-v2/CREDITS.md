## Sound ruling — NONE

The stub promised a "scent frequency audio synth" and implemented a
hover-triggered hum. It was removed, not rebuilt, for three reasons:
scent is not sound and a smell has no frequency to synthesise; nothing
in this trade teaches through audio; and hover-triggered audio is a
program deal-breaker regardless. The site states the ruling in its
footer. No audio files, no Web Audio, no autoplay anything.
