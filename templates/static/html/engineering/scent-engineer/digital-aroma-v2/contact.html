What happens next

1. A reply within two working days, with survey dates and the fixed £1,400 fee confirmed in writing.

2. The survey day: measurements, background odour, the noses' positions. You get the report either way.

3. A written quote against a written scope — or a written recommendation not to build, which is also a deliverable.

Availability

Surveys and channel audits: booking inside a fortnight. Build slots (S.03): one at a time, currently a six-week waitlist. Calibration visits for existing systems: by arrangement, usually within the month.
