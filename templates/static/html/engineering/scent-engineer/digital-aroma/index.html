    What we do

    01

    Capture

    A room, a fruit, a memory with a name. SPME and dynamic headspace onto Tenax, then GC-O so a trained nose marks which of the two hundred peaks actually matters. Usually eleven of them do.

    02

    Formulate

    A formula is a weight table, not a poem. Every material carries a dose, a supplier lot, an IFRA limit and a reason. We build the accord, then we take materials out until it breaks, then we put one back.

    03

    Deliver

    Piezo micro-pumps, heated nozzles, sealed cartridges. The device decides what the formula can be: onset time, chamber count, whether the base notes survive a hundred hours of idle.

    04

    Verify

    Dose by mass, repeatability across a batch, sensory panel against a reference, and an honest shelf-life figure. If a claim cannot be measured, it does not go on the page.
