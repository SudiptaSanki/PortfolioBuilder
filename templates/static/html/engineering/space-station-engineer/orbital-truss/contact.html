What we need to quote

      
The launch vehicle and its coupled loads, or at least the quasi-static levels. The interface control drawing, even a rough one. The mass cap. And the number that actually matters: how far the far end may move.


      

        
Minimum engagement
A two-week concept study, two geometries and a mass estimate.

        
Lead time
Ten weeks to a design review, seven months to a tested qualification unit.

        
Not a fit
Structure without an interface, and anything that has to be qualified without a test.

      


      
Where the work happens

      
Design and analysis sit in Bremen next to the test cell, which is deliberate: the person who signs the stress report can walk forty metres and watch the article being struck with a hammer. That distance has settled more arguments than any review meeting.
