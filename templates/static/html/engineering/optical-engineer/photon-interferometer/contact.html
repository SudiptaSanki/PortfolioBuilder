What the report contains

        
The phase map, the Zernike coefficients to 36 terms, the residual PV and RMS, a repeatability figure from two rotations, and the reference flat's certificate number. Everything needed for someone else to doubt the number properly.

      


      

        
What we will not do

        
We do not measure a surface too rough to unwrap, we do not certify a part tested in a different orientation from its service orientation, and we do not report a figure without the repeatability that goes with it.
