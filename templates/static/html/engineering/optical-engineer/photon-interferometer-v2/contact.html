A FIRST MESSAGE THAT GETS A USEFUL REPLY
  • The measurand — displacement, figure, vibration — and the range it moves over.
  • The limit it must be judged against — a specification, a drawing tolerance, a reviewer.
  • The room — floor type, storey, neighbours with machinery, and the hours the building is awake.
  • The deadline, if one is real. Instruments can be hurried; buildings cannot.
WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

A reply within two working days. If the measurand fits the register, the reply carries the survey shape and its fixed fee. If it does not fit — if a dial gauge would do it — the reply says that instead, and names who to call.
