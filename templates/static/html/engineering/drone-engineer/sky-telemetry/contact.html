What to send

  • The aircraft — type, autopilot, firmware family if you know it
  • The links fitted — bands and radios, or a photo of the label
  • The symptom — what the logs show, or fail to show
  • The deadline — season start, audit, demo day, whenever it bites

The reply you’ll get

A plain read of the problem, whether it is a link problem, a logging problem or a routine problem — and, if it is mine to fix, a scoped next step with a figure. If it isn’t mine, I will say who it belongs to. Replies within two working days; the bench is in Delft and the kettle is on.
