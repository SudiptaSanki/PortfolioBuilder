Enquiry

REPLY WITHIN 2 WORKING DAYS

One contact path, two ways to use it: the form below, or the email beside it. Either way the first reply is a scoped price or an honest refusal — with the reason — never a sales call. Reviews are cheapest six weeks before the stand window; say when yours is.

Please give your name — reports are addressed to people, not inboxes.

That email does not read as valid — the reply has to reach you.

A few lines are needed — engine, question and date are enough to scope a reply.

Demonstration form: this portfolio is a design demonstration, so the form validates and issues a reference on this page but transmits nothing. On the live practice site it posts to the address beside it — which is also where real enquiries should go.

Direct email

enquiries@kessler-propulsion.example

The .example domain is reserved and undeliverable — it is used because Dr Kessler is fictional and this is a demonstration site. The shape of the address is the shape a real enquiry would use.

What to send

  • The engine: propellant combination, cycle, thrust class
  • The question: review, investigation, audit or trade study
  • The date that matters: stand window, CDR, board review
  • What data exists: models, drawings, prior test records
  • What you do not need — it sharpens the scope

What happens next

  • Reply within two working days, from Dr Kessler personally
  • A short call only if the scope genuinely needs one
  • Written scope and fixed or capped price before any work
  • If the answer is no — capacity, conflict, wrong specialism — it is said plainly, with a reason
