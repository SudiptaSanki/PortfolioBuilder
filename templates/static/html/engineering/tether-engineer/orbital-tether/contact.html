We answer within five working days. If the mass penalty does not work for your mission, we will tell you in the first reply rather than the third.


      
What we need to give a number

      
Mass, orbit, drag area, and the disposal rule that applies. With those four we can return a Δv budget, a line length, and the mass your satellite has to give up for it.


      
What happens after the note

      
We run the deorbit model against your orbit and send back three numbers: the line length, the mass penalty, and the time to re-entry. If the penalty is over 8 % of your dry mass we say so plainly, because at that point a small propulsion module is usually the better engineering answer.


      
Not a fit

      
Missions where the tether is the only disposal mechanism and there is no backup. Crewed platforms. Anything that needs the line to be retracted and reused, which we have never made work.
