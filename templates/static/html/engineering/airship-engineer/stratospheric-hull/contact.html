Programme timeline

        
A feasibility study takes three weeks. A first hull takes 14–18 months from signature: laminate qualification, layup, proof inflation, flight test, then the certification file. A repeat hull is about nine months.

What we need first

        
Payload mass and power, station altitude, required endurance, launch and recovery site, and the season. If you have a thermal or wind profile for the site, send it — it changes the fin and ballonet sizing more than people expect.

      


      

        
What we will not do

        
We do not quote a hull from a payload mass alone, and we do not accept a programme where the mass budget only closes with a 5% margin. Both of those produce a beautiful drawing and a failed flight.
