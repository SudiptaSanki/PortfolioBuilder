## Numbers

Table 1 densities are the ISA (ISO 2533) standard atmosphere,
rounded to 4 decimal places. The helium ratio 0.138 is the
molar-mass ratio 4.00 / 28.97. Deduction fractions (46 / 24 / 10%)
are printed on the page as this fictional practice's planning
allowances, and the calculator reads all of them from the printed
DOM — table, relation and tool cannot disagree.
