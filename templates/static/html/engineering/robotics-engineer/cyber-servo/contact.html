What to send

  • Machine type and axis count
  • The symptom in plain words — buzz, overshoot, creep, fault code, heat
  • What changed just before it started (tooling, payload, a swapped drive)
  • A trace or error log, if you have one
  • Your cycle-time target, if the machine has one to miss
