## Sound ruling

**None.** Motion control is not a sound profession and a servo noise teaches
nothing. The stub's click/hover Web Audio servo synth and template.json's promised
"servo motor Web Audio feedback" were decoration and have been removed, not
rebuilt; the ruling is stated in every footer.

## Honest replacements

template.json's "cybernetic HUD" theme and the stub's fake telemetry readouts
(SERVO_STATUS / BUS_VOLTAGE chrome) were replaced by the trade's real material:
the tuning bench (step-response explorer), the torque-sizing calculator, the
engagement register and fees in print. The stub's orange accent survives — as the
