A useful first messageFour things

  • The application — vehicle, stationary or marine, and what it actually does all day.
  • The power you think you need — and where that number came from. “The brochure” is an acceptable answer; it's where most numbers come from.
  • Whatever data exists — a week of logs beats a year of estimates. No data is fine too; logging becomes step one.
  • The hydrogen — where it comes from today, or “no idea yet”. Both are workable; pretending it's settled when it isn't is the only bad answer.
Before you write If the question is “how efficient is it?”, the honest first answer is “at which point on the curve, doing what?” — bring the duty cycle and the question answers itself.
