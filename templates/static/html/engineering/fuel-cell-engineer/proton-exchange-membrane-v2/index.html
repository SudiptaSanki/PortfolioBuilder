    What moves the price

    • Stack size. Cell count and area drive tooling, test time and report length — in that order.
    • Test programme depth. A polarisation sweep is a day; a 1,000-hour durability run is a season of bench days.
    • Hydrogen scope. If supply and storage are in the brief, the study grows — because that is where projects actually fail.

    What never moves the price: the conclusion. A study that ends “not yet” is billed at the same rate as one that ends “build it”.

      From the case files · CF-01

      The 40-second peak

      A vehicle client arrived with a finished specification: a 120 kW stack, sized — as stacks usually are — on the vehicle's peak power. Six weeks of logged duty cycles told a different story. The peak was real, but it was one climb, lasting about 40 seconds. Cruise demand sat at 22–38 kW and the 95th-percentile demand was 54 kW.

      The stack was resized to 65 kW at the 0.8 A/cm² sizing point, with a battery buffer carrying the 40-second event. Smaller stack, smaller cooling, smaller bill — and a vehicle that spends its life at an efficient point on the curve instead of idling at the bottom of an oversized one.
