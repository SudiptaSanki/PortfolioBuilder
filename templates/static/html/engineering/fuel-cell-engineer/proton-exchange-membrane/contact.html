What a first reply contains

        
An estimated cell count and active area, the polarization point at your duty, a first-order thermal balance, and the two or three risks that would change the design. Not a price — a price needs the second meeting.

      


      

        
What we will not do

        
We do not quote from a rated power alone, we do not warranty an efficiency taken at a current density you will never use, and we do not ship a stack without a measured curve.
