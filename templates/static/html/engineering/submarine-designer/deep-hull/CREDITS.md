## Honest replacements
template.json's description promises a "sonar ping ripple canvas"
and "depth gauge gradient layers". Both were slop — decoration with
no job — and were **deleted, not rebuilt**. In their place: a working
depth/pressure explorer whose figures the visitor can check with a
pencil, and a plate-renewal estimator tied to the printed rate card.
The stub's headline claims ("CRUSH DEPTH: 2,400m", "HY-130",
"120 dB acoustic signature") were invented specifications and were
removed; the rebuilt site states only standard, checkable engineering.
