## Stub promises — honest replacements

The quarantined stub ("ALCHYMIA") promised claymorphic cards, a
liquid-nitrogen bubble canvas and recipe-card popups, carried a
fabricated "MICHELIN ★★★ 2026" claim, and played a pop sound on
hover from an AudioContext created at page load. All of it was
addressed on its merits:

- **Bubble canvas → replaced by the Temperature Log explorer.**
  The canvas was decoration. The explorer teaches the real
  processes — band, physics, governing rule, time, price — and its
  figures are asserted against the printed reference table on the
  same page by the verification probe.
- **Recipe-card popups → replaced by the bench worksheet.** The
  popups held placeholder text. The worksheet does the trade's
  real arithmetic (nitrogen requirement for a freeze) with every
  assumption printed and the working shown line by line.
- **Claymorphism and the fake star claim → deleted.** The design
  system is the temperature log: flat slate, hairlines, mono data,
  cold/heat colours used only as data. No invented honours appear
  anywhere in the rebuild.
- **Hover pop synth → deleted.** Sound need-test: FAILED. A chef's
  profession is taste, smell and temperature, no interaction here
  teaches through audio, and hover-triggered audio is a
  deal-breaker regardless. The site has no audio anywhere; the
  ruling is stated in every page footer.

Persona: Nadia Ferro is fictional and disclosed as such in every
footer; the clients in the case entries are fictional composites.
All temperatures and safety statements are at established
professional-practice level (LN2 −196 °C and ≈700× expansion,
food-grade nitrogen E941, dry ice −78.5 °C, blast chilling
+70 °C → +3 °C inside 90 minutes, Maillard onset ≈140 °C), hedged
where practice varies. The worksheet is labelled an estimate with
its assumptions printed; it is never presented as a measurement
or a firm quote.
