## Drawn lines

Every written line on the site (the hero line, the four bench samples and
the three case-file plates) is an original SVG drawn for this template —
variable-width strokes generated from the bench's own stated sample data.
They depict no real person's handwriting.

## Type

Cormorant Garamond and EB Garamond — Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License.

## Sound

None. No audio files, no audio code. The ruling is printed in every footer:
a nib on paper is nearly silent, and the page keeps the faith. (The stub's
hover-triggered scratch sound was removed with the stub.)
