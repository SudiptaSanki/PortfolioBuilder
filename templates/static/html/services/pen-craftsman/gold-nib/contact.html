The five answers that decide the nib

A fitting is free and takes about ten minutes to complete. Send your answers and a writing sample on the paper you use; the sample can be a phone photograph of two or three sentences. The bench replies within two working days with a grind, a flow setting and a price.

How to pack it

Empty the pen, wrap the nib in a tissue, then in a rigid tube. A padded envelope is not enough: nibs bend at the shoulders in the post. Send it tracked and insured for its value.

Can you grind a nib I did not buy from you?

Yes, any 14k, 18k or steel nib that has enough tipping to work with, which is judged when it arrives. Steel nibs cannot take a flex grind; the metal does not spring back.

What if I do not know my pen angle?

Send a photograph of your hand writing, taken from the side, with the pen and paper both visible. Three sentences is enough to estimate the angle within five degrees.

Do you work on 18k or 21k nibs?

For regrinds, yes. For flex work, no: softer alloys do not spring back reliably, and a nib that stays splayed is worse than a stiff one.

Can you match a grind I had before?

If you still have the pen, send it and we will measure the tipping. If not, a writing sample at your normal pressure tells us most of what we need.

Do you sell gift vouchers?

Yes, for nib work or towards a pen, posted as a card with a hand-written note. They do not expire and can be used against a commission in any year.
