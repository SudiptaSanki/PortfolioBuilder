What comes with a pen

The nib is ground to your angle and flow, written with for a page, and set in an ebonite section. The pen is delivered in a cotton sleeve with a written card naming the grind, the tipping width and the date it was tested, plus a 2 ml sample of the ink it was tested with.

  • Nib workincluded in the model price
  • Writing testone A5 page, your paper if sent
  • Warrantytwo years, parts and labour
  • Servicefree in the first two years, £25 after
  • Shippingtracked and insured, UK £9

If the pen does not suit after a fortnight, send it back with the card and we will regrind the nib once at no charge. The nib is the part that is fitted to you; the body is the part that has to be right first time.
