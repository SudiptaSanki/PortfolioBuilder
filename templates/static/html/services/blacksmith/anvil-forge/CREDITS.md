## Notes

- No audio is used anywhere in this template. The sound need-test was
  applied: blacksmithing is not a sound-based profession, a hammer-ring
  effect would be a gimmick, and hover-triggered audio is a deal-breaker.
  The site is silent by design.
- The smith, clients, jobs and prices shown are a fictional
  demonstration portfolio; the craft content (steels, temperatures,
  joinery, restoration practice) is true to the trade.
