Start an enquiry

One route in, whichever suits you: email the forge directly, or use the form. Either way you get a reply within two working days, and every quote is in writing before a deposit is mentioned.

What to send

  • For gates and railingsRough width and height of the opening, a photograph of it straight-on, and whether the posts or pillars are sound.
  • For a knife or toolWhat it is for, how it will be used, and any size that matters — a hand span for a handle, a blade length for a knife.
  • For restorationPhotographs of the whole piece and close-ups of the damage, plus where it is — some ironwork can be assessed from pictures, some needs a visit.
  • Any deadlineSay so at the start. The book is honest: if your date cannot be met, you will be told before anything else happens.
