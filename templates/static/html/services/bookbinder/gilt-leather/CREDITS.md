# Credits — Gilt Leather (Marlowe Bindery)

## Persona

Hester Marlowe and the Marlowe Bindery are fictional, as are the
clients, books and prices shown. The binding methods, materials,
timelines and care guidance are real practice. The site footer says
the same.

materials and the condition of old books; they are not photographs
of the fictional bindery's own work, and captions say what each
photograph actually shows. The before/after slider on the home page
pairs two different books — one as books arrive, one as they leave —
and says so on the page, because clients' books are photographed
for their owners' records only.

## Type

Cormorant Garamond, Spectral and IBM Plex Mono, from Google Fonts,
all SIL Open Font License.

## Sound

None. Bookbinding is not a sound profession, so the site is silent;
the stub's hover-triggered "leather snap" audio was removed under
the programme's sound need-test (hover audio is a deal-breaker in
any case).
