Enquire

Begin with photographs.

No book travels until it has been looked at. Send the five photographs below with your enquiry, and the first answer — whether the book should come to Oxford, and roughly what its work will cost — arrives within five working days.

bindery@marlowe-bindery.example

The bindery is in Oxford; visits are by appointment, so a book is never left at a door. Enquiries are answered within five working days.

The five photographs

Phone photographs are fine. Plain daylight, no flash, and do not force the book open wider than it opens on its own.

  • The spine — straight on, showing the bands, the title and any splits at the joints.
  • Both boards — front and back covers flat, corners included.
  • The title page — so the edition can be identified and the assessment can name the right materials.
  • The damage, close up — the worst of it: detached boards, torn leaves, a failing headcap.
  • The book beside a ruler — height and thickness set the price more than age does.

What happens next

  1. A first answer

    From the photographs: whether the book should travel, what work it likely needs, and a price range — or an honest "leave it alone".

  2. Assessment in the hand

    If it travels, the written assessment follows within two weeks: fixed price, materials, and your place in the queue. £40, credited in full against the work.

  3. The bench

    Deposit of 40% books the work. Photographs at every stage, and the treatment record returns with the book.
