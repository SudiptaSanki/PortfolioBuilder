The craft

Forwarding first, finishing last

A binding is built in two crafts in sequence. Forwarding makes the book work — it is the sewing, rounding and board attachment nobody sees. Finishing is the part everybody sees: the covering, the tooling, the title. A book that is only finished well is a costume; the forwarding is the book.

1

Assessment & collation

The book is examined and collated — every section checked against the register of signatures — before anything is taken apart. What will be kept, repaired or replaced is agreed in writing at this stage, not discovered later.

2

Forwarding

Sections are folded, pressed and sewn on cords or tapes; the spine is rounded and backed so the book opens without fighting; boards are laced on through the sewing supports, not glued to a case afterwards.

3

Covering

Leather is pared thin at the edges and turns-ins, pasted, and drawn over the boards by hand; cloth bindings are cased or bound in the same sequence. Headbands are sewn, not stuck on from a roll.

4

Finishing & tooling

Blind tooling is impressed with heated hand tools; gold tooling lays 23.5-carat leaf into the impression over glaire. Titles are set in type and tooled letter by letter. Then the book rests in the press before it goes home.

Commissions

How a book comes to the bench

Nothing is bound, repaired or boxed on a first impression. The book is assessed first — in photographs, then in the hand — and the assessment is a document you keep whether or not the work goes ahead.

  1. Photographs first

    Send the five photographs listed on the enquiry page — spine, both boards, the title page, the damage close up, with a ruler for scale. Half of all enquiries are answered from these alone.

  2. Assessment in writing

    If the book should travel, it comes to Oxford — in person or by insured post. You receive a written assessment: condition, what repair can and cannot do, the materials proposed, a fixed price, and your place in the queue. The £40 fee is credited in full against the work.

  3. The work

    A deposit of 40% confirms the booking. The book is photographed at every stage, and the treatment record — what was kept, what was replaced, and with what — returns with it.

  4. Return

    Balance on completion, before return. Books travel in a fitted box, wrapped against damp, fully insured, signature on delivery. Boxes and slipcases are made after the book, to its finished measurements.
