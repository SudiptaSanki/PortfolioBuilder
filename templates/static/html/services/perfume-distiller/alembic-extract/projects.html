The 2026 batch book

Thirty-four batches from the season so far. Each line is a real charge: a field, a date, a method and a measured yield. Batches marked trial did not meet the reference profile and are sold only with the deviation recorded.

R-26-07Rose absolute

Rose absolute, second cut

Fresh Damask petals from the upper plot, extracted the same morning, the concrete washed twice and chilled to drop the waxes. Deep and honeyed, with the green stem note still in the top. Sold to two perfumers and a soap house; 1.4 kg left at the time of writing.

Charge
312 kg flowers
Yield
0.21% absolute
Method
Concrete → ethanol
Matured
6 weeks

O-26-02Orris butter

Orris butter, three-year rhizome

Iris pallida from the Tuscan cooperative, dried three years, ground and steam distilled. A cream-coloured butter with 14% irone by GC. Bought by a leather house in Millau for a single perfume and held for them for two years.

Charge
3 kg rhizome
Yield
0.05% butter
Method
Steam, dried rhizome
Matured
12 months

J-26-11Jasmine concrete

Jasmine concrete, dawn pick

Jasminum grandiflorum from one family in Grasse, picked before dawn and extracted by nine. Indolic and tea-like. Half went on to absolute; the rest is sold as concrete to a perfumer who wants the wax in the formula.

Charge
48 kg flowers
Yield
0.34% concrete
Method
Solvent, fresh flower
Matured
4 weeks

V-26-04Vetiver oil

Vetiver, first and second oil

Two-year Haitian roots, hydrodistilled in two passes; the first oil and the cohobated second oil are kept apart in glass and blended to order. Earth, smoke, grapefruit peel. Six months of maturation before release.

Charge
180 kg roots
Yield
1.12% oil
Method
Hydrodistillation
Matured
6 months

L-26-03Labdanum resinoid

Labdanum resinoid, washed twice

Andalusian rock-rose cut in June, dried in the field and extracted in September. Washed twice to take the dust out. Sticky, leathery, amber; sold by the kilo to two houses that build amber accords with it.

Charge
240 kg leaf
Yield
2.4% resinoid
Method
Solvent, dried leaf
Matured
8 weeks

N-26-05Neroli

Neroli, four hours from the tree

Bitter orange blossom from Vallauris, distilled within four hours of picking. Green, honeyed, with the soapy note just held back. A small batch: 60 kg of blossom, and the hydrosol sold alongside it as orange flower water.

Charge
60 kg blossom
Yield
0.10% oil
Method
Steam, fresh blossom
Matured
4 weeks

R-26-09Trial · rose oil

Rose oil, late-season trial

A late charge from the lower plot, distilled as an experiment. The yield was acceptable at 0.041% but the profile ran short on citronellol and the batch is marked trial. Sold at a reduced price with the deviation written on the certificate.

Charge
210 kg flowers
Yield
0.041% oil
Method
Hydrodistillation
Status
Trial, deviation noted
