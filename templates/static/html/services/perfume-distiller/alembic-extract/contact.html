Practicalities

Samples

Two millilitres of any batch in the book, free within the EU, sent with the batch record and certificate. Up to six samples per request; beyond that we ask for a small charge to cover courier.

Minimum order

10 g for absolutes and concretes, 50 ml for oils, 100 ml for hydrosols. White label and private distillation start at 5 kg of finished material.

Lead times

Stock materials: 5 working days.
A batch to brief: 4–8 weeks, season dependent.
Private distillation: booked six months ahead.

Documentation

Batch record, certificate of analysis, gas chromatograph, IFRA conformity statement, safety data sheet and allergen declaration come with every shipment.

Do you sell to hobbyists?

Yes, above the minimum order. The documentation is the same, and we will explain any term on the certificate you do not recognise.

Can you make a material that is not in the list?

If the plant grows within reach of Grasse and the season is right, often yes. Private distillation is booked six months ahead and starts at 5 kg of finished material.

Are your materials organic?

Some batches are certified and some are not. We will not call a material organic without a current certificate, and the certificate number goes on the batch record.

Do you supply IFRA statements for a specific end use?

Yes. Tell us the product type and the concentration and we will issue the statement for that use, not a generic one.

Can I visit in winter?

The still is cold, but the store and the batch book are here. Visits by appointment all year; in season we will run a small charge for you if the weather allows.
