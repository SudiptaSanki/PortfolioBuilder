03

The pyramid

A composition is a structure in time. Choose a tier to read the materials and the job each one is doing.

Top, heart and base are not three smells in a bottle; they are three rates of evaporation. The top is what leaves the blotter in ten minutes, the heart is what the room remembers after an hour, the base is what is left on the skin at the end of the day. Standards

The paperwork is part of the material.

Terms that mean something

ISO 9235 defines every material by the process that made it. An essential oil comes from steam, water or dry distillation, or from cold-pressing citrus peel. A concrete comes from solvent extraction of fresh material. An absolute is washed out of a concrete with ethanol — there is no absolute made directly from a plant. A tincture is a maceration, and it is the weakest thing in the palette, not the strongest.

Every batch leaves with

  • Batch record: charge, method, yield, still, operator
  • Certificate of analysis and gas chromatograph
  • IFRA conformity statement for the intended use
  • Safety data sheet and allergen declaration
  • Harvest note: grower, plot, date, weather
