The date, the site and the music

Three facts decide whether a show is possible: how far the spectators are from the mortars, what the ground is, and when the show has to end. Send those and we will send back a feasibility note, a site radius and a budget band within five working days.

What the show is for

Practicalities

Lead time

Six weeks minimum for a new show with a permit application. Larger public events need three months; New Year and Bonfire night are booked by September.

Permits

We apply to the local fire authority and prepare the risk assessment, site plan and insurance certificate. The site owner's permission is yours to secure.

Insurance

£10m public liability, certificate supplied with the quote. Additional insured parties can be named for a fee.

What we need from you

A site contact, a power supply or a generator, vehicle access to the firing point, and a decision on music at least three weeks before the show.

Can you fire a show in a town centre?

Only if the site meets the separation distances, which usually means a 3-inch ceiling and angled racks. We will tell you at the brief if the geometry does not work.

Do you do low-noise or silent displays?

Yes: low-noise product, quieter breaks and a shorter show. Silent displays with headphones are a different trade and we do not offer them.

Can we use our own fireworks?

No. Product has to be bought, stored and logged by a licensed operator; we cannot fire customer-supplied shells.

What happens if the weather turns?

Wind and visibility are monitored from the day before. We can reduce the largest shell, shorten the show or postpone; the decision is made with the fire officer and is never taken for the look of it.

Do you film the show?

We can bring a camera operator and a second unit for a broadcast-quality recording. Drone filming needs its own permit and a separate pilot.

Before you write The numbers

70 ft per inch of shell, and no exceptions

The site radius follows the largest shell in the show, not the average. The safety section sets out the table and the cases where the distance doubles.
