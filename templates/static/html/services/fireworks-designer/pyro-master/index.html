02 Site and safety

The largest shell decides the whole site

NFPA 1123 sets the minimum radius of a display site at 70 ft per inch of internal mortar diameter, measured from the mortars to the spectators. Mix a 3-inch show with a 6-inch finale and the site is a 6-inch site: 420 ft radius, 840 ft across. The fire authority can require more, and often does.

We write the site plan before we write the cue sheet. Angle the mortars away from the crowd and the distance on that side can come down, but it is paid for downrange and it must be drawn on the plan.
