## Sound ruling

**One sound, user-started, and it teaches.** The acoustic engineer's
profession is sound itself, and the tone bench (Sheet 03, home page)
is an interaction that genuinely teaches through audio — hearing
what 50 Hz, 100 Hz and 1 kHz actually are is the lesson the page
exists to give. It therefore passes the programme's sound need-test,
under its strictest conditions: tones are pure Web Audio sines
generated only when a visitor presses a tone button — never on load,
never on hover, never as decoration; the level is fixed low in code
(gain 0.12); every tone stops itself after two seconds; a Stop
button is always present; a hearing-safety line is printed beside
the buttons. The stub's signature — an animated equalizer canvas
pretending to display a live signal — was deleted, not rebuilt: a
fake measurement is the one thing a measurement practice cannot
ship. The footer states the ruling on every page.
