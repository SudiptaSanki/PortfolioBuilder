Visits by appointment only — the bench does not keep shop hours, and the door is answered when the work allows.

Sending shoes for resole

Post the pair with your name and what you would like done inside the box. On arrival I confirm receipt, examine the welt, and give you a fixed price before any work starts. If a pair cannot be repaired properly, it comes back to you at my cost with the reason written down.

The order book, at present

First pairs: about seven months before the making begins, then the making itself. Further pairs on an existing last: about five months end to end. Resoles and refurbishments: three to four weeks from the agreed price.
