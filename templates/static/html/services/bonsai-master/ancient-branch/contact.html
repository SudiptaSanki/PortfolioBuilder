Contact

Start with photographs

Send four photographs of your tree — front, back, both sides — plus its base and anything that worries you. You will hear back within two working days, and the first answer is often simply: feed it for a season, then we'll talk.

Write directly

nursery@ancientbranch.example

Email is read every evening. Photographs attached to an email are as good as the form — better, if you have the tree's history to send with them.

  • VisitsBy appointment only, Thursday to Saturday. A working nursery is not a showroom; an appointment means the benches get walked with you, tree by tree.
  • BoardingDrop-off and collection by appointment. $85 a month, $45 a fortnight (two-week minimum). Winter protection included; a written note of all care comes home with the tree.
  • Assessments$40, credited against any work that follows. The tree stays a week; you receive a written health report and a staged plan before committing to anything.
  • Reply timeWithin two working days. In repotting season (February to April) replies slow down — the trees cannot wait, and the email, respectfully, can.
