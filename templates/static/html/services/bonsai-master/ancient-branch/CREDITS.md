## Honesty notes

The photographs are stock images of real bonsai, captioned on the
site for what they actually show. The persona (Daichi Mori), the
nursery, the named trees, their records and their owners are
fictional, created for this demonstration portfolio — the site's
footer says so. No real grower, nursery or client is depicted or
impersonated. The craft content — seasonal work, soil mixes,
repotting cycles, care practice — is real bonsai practice, and the
prices are a realistic shape for a small specialist nursery.
