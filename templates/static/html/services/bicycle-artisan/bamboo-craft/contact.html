How to measure inseam

The one measurement people get wrong. It matters more than height.

Stand against a wall without shoes, feet 150 mm apart. Pull a hardback book up between your legs, spine against the wall, as high as it will go. Mark the wall at the top edge of the book. Measure from the floor to the mark.

Common error: measuring to the crotch rather than the book. That reads 20–30 mm short and lands you on a frame that is a size too small.

Questions

Before you write

If the answer is not here, the answer is probably "come and see the bench".

Is bamboo safe?

A well-made bamboo frame is as strong as a steel frame in the directions that matter and has passed the same ISO 4210 fatigue test. The failure mode is different: a steel frame bends before it breaks, a bamboo tube can split at a joint. That is why every joint has a 45 mm lug overlap and why the frame comes back to the bench for an annual inspection.

How much does the whole bike weigh?

A complete gravel build with mechanical gears and 45 mm tyres lands around 9.8–10.4 kg. The frame is about 2.1 kg; the bike is not a weight-weenie build and is not sold as one.

Can you fit a belt drive or an e-bike motor?

Belt drive yes, with a split in the seat stay. Mid-drive motors no: the torque load at the bottom bracket is outside what the lug is designed for, and I would rather say no than build something I cannot stand behind.

What happens if the frame breaks?

It comes back to the bench. I cut out the damaged culm, scarf in a replacement, and re-wrap the lug. A repair typically costs £180–£320 plus carriage, and the frame keeps its number and its log book.

Do you ship outside the UK?

Yes, to the EU and North America. Carriage is at cost, typically £90–£180, and the buyer handles import duty. The frame travels in a wooden crate because cardboard and cane do not get on.
