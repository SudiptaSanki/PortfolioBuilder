## Stub promises — honest replacements

The quarantined stub promised a "fragrance pyramid notes selector"
and a "crystal mist Web Audio synth". Both were addressed on their
merits:

- **Pyramid selector → rebuilt as the Formula Explorer.** The stub's
  selector was decorative; the rebuild's explorer teaches the real
  structure — one material per tier, each tier's role and rough
  evaporation window, and a character sentence composed from the
  chosen facets — plus a concentration panel with the conventional
  percentage bands hedged as conventions, not law.
- **"Crystal mist" hover synth → deleted.** Sound need-test: FAILED.
  Perfumery is not a sound profession, no interaction on this site
  teaches through audio, and hover-triggered audio is a deal-breaker
  regardless. The site has no audio anywhere; the ruling is stated
  in every page footer.

Persona: Sylvie Marchand is fictional and disclosed as such in every
footer. All material names are real perfumery materials used
generically (bergamot, cedarwood, orris and the like); no real brand
fragrance is named as the persona's work, and the method page states
the practice refuses "inspired-by" briefs. Concentration bands and
the IFRA discussion are stated at practice level, hedged as
conventions — no regulatory advice is given.
