Evaporation windows are rough guides on skin, in the order materials leave. Fabric, weather, dose and your own skin chemistry move them — sometimes by hours. Any perfumer who quotes you an exact hour is guessing politely.

THEN, THE STRENGTH

Concentration is a decision, not a compliment.

15–20% perfume concentrate, by convention

More base weight in the blend, so the dry-down lasts — typically six to eight hours. The structure you built in the explorer is most legible at this strength.

The bands are industry conventions, not law — no regulator fixes them, and houses interpret them loosely. Longevity figures are typical, never promised: the same formula wears differently by material, skin and climate.
