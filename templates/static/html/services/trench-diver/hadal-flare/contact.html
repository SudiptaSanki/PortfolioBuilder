Before the vessel sails

Four things I check first

The gas. Not the mix on the drawing, the analysis certificate from the cylinder. A heliox blend that is 0.4% richer in oxygen than specified can put a 190 m dive over the ceiling before anyone has left the deck.

The chamber. Where it is, who can operate it at 3 a.m., and how long the transfer takes in the actual weather. A chamber that exists on a neighbouring field is not a chamber.

The abort. Who says stop, and what happens to the task when they do. If the answer is "the client will decide", the client is the supervisor and the spread has a problem.

The handover. Whether the previous shift's diver came up with everything the next one needs to know. Most incidents start in a handover, not in the water.
