# Credits & honesty notes — Savile Thread

## Persona
Daniel Whitcombe, his workshop, his clients and his garments are
**fictional**, created for this demonstration portfolio and disclosed
as such in the footer of every page. No real tailor, client or garment
is depicted or described. The methods, cloth weights, construction
details, timelines and price shapes are true to the bespoke tailoring
trade.

## Sound
None. Tailoring is not a sound profession, so the site is silent by
the owner's sound need-test. The original stub's hover "snip" sound
effect (a Web Audio blip on mouseenter) was removed: hover-triggered
audio is a deal-breaker regardless of theme.
