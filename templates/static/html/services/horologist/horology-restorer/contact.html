Enquire — one path, kept clear

Send the watch's particulars.

An assessment begins with particulars, not with the watch itself — do not post a watch anywhere on the strength of a web page, this one included. Write first. If the bench can help, you will receive the assessment terms and the insured-transit instructions; if it cannot, you will receive a written reason and, where possible, the name of the kind of bench that can.

Enquiry particulars and form

Write to severin@atelieraubert.example
The bench Atelier Aubert, Geneva, Switzerland — visits by appointment only, and only after an assessment is agreed
The queue Assessments are currently being booked for February 2027; the bench takes six to eight watches a year
What to include The watch's type and approximate year; its complication, if known; what is wrong, as far as you know; how it came to you; and photographs, if you have them — dial, movement if it has ever been photographed, and any marks. Honest uncertainty is useful. “I do not know” is a perfectly good entry.
What happens next A reply within five working days: either an invitation to send the watch for assessment under the printed terms, or a written refusal with its reasons.

A name is needed — the report is written to a person, not to an address.
