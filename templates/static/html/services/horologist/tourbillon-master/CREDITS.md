## Everything else

- The favicon is a self-drawn inline SVG (a watch dial with blued
  hands). No icon libraries, no third-party CSS or JS frameworks.
- No sound is used anywhere in this template, by design: the
  trade's tick is not the product, and ambient audio fails the
  programme's sound need-test.
- The workshop, its watchmaker (Amos Whitaker), its clients and
  its bench register are a fictional persona created for this
  portfolio template, and the site footer says so. The horology —
  service stages, timing practice, parts policy, prices and care
  advice — is true to real independent watchmaking practice.
