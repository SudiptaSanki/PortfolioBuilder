Book a fitting, and have the horse ready

A fitting takes about two hours, in the stable and in the school. We need the horse, any saddle you currently use, and your own measurements. If you are more than sixty miles from Ledbury, travel is charged at cost and arranged in advance.

Fitting request

The form assembles an email; nothing is sent from the page. We answer within three working days and give you the next two fitting dates.

What you are asking for

A photograph of the back, taken from the side with the horse standing square, saves a lot of guessing.

Your details are used for this booking only.

What to have ready

The horse

Clean, dry and standing square on a level surface. No saddle pad if we are checking an existing saddle. Bring the bridle you ride in as well.

The rider

Boots and breeches, and your normal stirrup length. We measure seat, thigh and lower leg in the school, not on the yard.

Your current saddle

Even if it is for sale or borrowed. It tells us what you are used to and what the horse has been carrying.

Timing

Allow two hours. The horse should be in work and not just back from a long break; a fit on a soft horse will change within a month.

Do you fit saddles you did not make?

Yes, if the tree is sound and the panels can be adjusted. We will not re-flock a foam panel or straighten a broken tree.

How long is the waiting list?

Nine months for a new saddle, three to four weeks for a re-fit or a repair. A deposit of £500 holds a place on the list.

Can you fit at a competition?

Sometimes, if it is within an hour of Ledbury and the horse is stabled overnight. It is not the best place to see a horse move, so we prefer the yard.

What if the saddle does not fit after the first check?

We adjust the flocking and, if the tree is wrong, we will say so and rebuild at cost of materials. We would rather remake one than have it hurt a horse.

Do you take card payments?

Bank transfer or card. Half on order, half on delivery; the fitting visit is free within sixty miles and charged at cost beyond.

Before you book

Look at the three things that fail first

The tree, the billets and the flocking decide whether a saddle lasts. The workshop page sets out what we use and why, so you know what you are paying for.
