Six saddles and a bridle, with the horses they were made for

Each commission below is a real build from the last three years. The measurements are the finished ones, taken on the bench; the price is what the customer paid, including the fitting and the first check at forty hours.

The Much Marcle dressage

Havana bridle butt · beech tree · wool flock

For a 16.2 hh mare with a high wither and a short back, ridden by a tall rider who needed a longer seat without a longer panel. The tree was ordered with a medium-wide gullet at 83° and gusseted panels front and rear, and the flap is 16½ in with a forward cut of three inches.

The Woolhope event saddle

Havana bridle butt · forward flap · calf-lined

A compact 15.3 hh gelding with a flat back and a rider who events at BE100. The flap is cut forward for a shorter stirrup and the seat is shallow so the rider can move; the panel is wide to spread the load over a flat back that would otherwise take the pressure in one place.

The Colwall showing saddle

Havana · cut-back head · straight flap

For a show hunter with a big shoulder and a pronounced wither; the head is cut back so the saddle sits behind the shoulder without a pad. The flap is straight and the seat deep, because the class is judged in walk, trot and canter with the rider still.

The Bosbury bridle

Bridle butt · brass · raised and stitched

A cob-sized double bridle with a raised browband and a lightly padded noseband. The cheek pieces are cut from the same butt as the saddle so the leather ages at the same rate; the buckles are solid brass, hand-finished so no edge catches a rein.

What is included

The price is the saddle, fitted twice

A commission includes the fitting visit, the trial tree, the finished saddle, and the check at forty hours with any flocking adjustment. Repairs in the first two years are free unless the damage is from a fall or a dog; after that, work is charged at the bench rate.
