Before you write

  • Buying: the bin number is enough — the sheet you read is the description you will be held to.
  • Selling: a rough list — region, vintage, count — starts the conversation. Photographs of levels come later; the first review of a list is free.
  • Restaurants: say how many covers and what the list looks like now. The first call is free and usually settles whether a retainer makes sense.
  • A single bottle for an occasion: give the date. Old wine travels badly in a hurry and not at all in a heatwave; I will say so if the timing is wrong.

The cellar's hours

Enquiries are read on weekdays and answered within two working days. The bonded store dispatches Monday to Wednesday. There is no shop to visit — the book is the shop.

Grand Vintage

Grand Vintage and Clara Whitfield are a fictional practice, created for this portfolio template.
