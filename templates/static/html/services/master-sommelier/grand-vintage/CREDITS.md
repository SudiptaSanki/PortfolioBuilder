## Sound ruling

**No audio anywhere.** The stub's signature was a crystal-glass clink
played on hover. It failed the programme's sound need-test on two
counts: a sommelier's and wine merchant's profession is taste and
smell, not sound, so no interaction here teaches through audio; and
hover-triggered audio is a deal-breaker regardless of profession. The
clink was deleted, not rebuilt. The footer states the ruling on every
page: "This site makes no sound."

## Fiction & originality notes

Clara Whitfield and Grand Vintage are fictional; the disclosure runs
in every page footer. No real château, estate, domaine or wine brand
is named anywhere: wines are described by region and vintage only
("left-bank Bordeaux, 2005"), which are facts of geography. The
Master Sommelier title follows the template's own metadata
(template.json role: "Master Sommelier & International Wine
Director"); the persona's career is invented and no real institution
is claimed as an employer.
