## Sound ruling

**No audio anywhere.** The stub's signature — and this template's
own catalogue description — promised a crystal-glass clink played by
Web Audio, in the stub on hover and on load. It failed the
programme's sound need-test: a cellar director's profession is taste
and smell, not sound, and no interaction on this site teaches through
audio; hover-triggered audio is a deal-breaker regardless. The clink
was deleted, not rebuilt, and replaced by the signature interaction
the trade actually earns — the Cellar Allocation Planner, which
judges chosen cases against the printed drinking-window table. The
footer states the ruling on every page: "This site makes no sound."

## Fiction & originality notes

Elena Aguirre and Viña Aldama are fictional; the disclosure runs in
every page footer. No real estate, château, bodega or wine brand is
named anywhere: the wines are the fictional estate's own, described
by Rioja's real, factual ageing categories (Crianza, Reserva, Gran
Reserva). The role follows the template's own metadata
(template.json: "Master Sommelier & Wine Estate Director"); the
persona's career is invented and no real institution is claimed as
an employer. This build is deliberately distinct from the programme's
earlier wine template (grand-vintage, a collector's showcase): this
one is a working cellar book — allocations, windows and storage
truth — with a different persona, region and frame.
