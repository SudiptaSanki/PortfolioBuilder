Every bottle in this book has a year to be opened. My work is saying when — and when not yet.

I am Elena Aguirre, cellar director of Viña Aldama in Rioja Alta. I keep the estate's reserve cellar the way it has been kept for forty years: as a book. Two hundred and fourteen bins, each one tasted twice a year, each with its drinking window written in ink. Member households drink from this book, restaurants build their cellars from it, and no bottle leaves it before its time — or after it.

A bottle kept as an investment is a book bought to appreciate, unread. This is a cellar, not a vault.

§ 4

How the book is kept

Three rules, kept without exception. Members are served before restaurants, and restaurants before the waitlist. On any bin holding fewer than six cases, a household may take one case only — scarcity is shared, not auctioned. And nothing is sold past its window: a bin whose window closes is poured at the estate table or written down in the book, never discounted into somebody's cellar to become their disappointment.
