Write to the Cellar · p. 4 of the book

One letter, entered in the book

Write to cellar@vinaaldama.example, or use the form below — both arrive on the same desk. Letters are answered within two working days, in the order they arrive, which is also the order of the waitlist.

No account, no newsletter, no list sold on. Your letter is read by the keeper, not a machine.

The estate

Viña Aldama, Camino de las Bodegas, Haro, Rioja Alta, Spain. The cellar is under the old house; the book is on the desk by the door.

Visits & tastings

By appointment, Thursday to Saturday. Six wines at the cellar table, €35 a person; members bring a guest free twice a year. Harvest visits (late September to October) are booked a season ahead.

The spring release

Allocations open 2 March 2027. Membership is €60 a year and is the only way onto the first page of the book; the waitlist is free and honest about its length — currently eleven households for library stock.

A bottle question

If you hold a bottle and want its window read, write with the wine, the vintage and where it has been kept. The answer may be “not yet” or “drink it this winter” — both are given free, and both are final.
