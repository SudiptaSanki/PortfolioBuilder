# Credits & licences — Iridium Point

## Persona & fiction

Hannah Whitlock and "Iridium Point" are fictional, created for this template
and disclosed as fictional in the footer of every page. The pens in the bench
register and bench cases (Bench № 246–251), their owners and the testimonial
are invented; the craft, line-width figures, materials facts and stated limits
are real nib-work practice.

## Drawn specimens

The line specimens in the hero, the bench register and the grind catalogue
are SVG drawn by script.js to scale (30 px = 1 mm) from the figures printed
beside them. They are specimens of line width and variation — the pages say
so in their captions — and are not photographs or scans of handwriting.

## Sound

None, by decision: nib work fails the sound need-test (the profession is
silent, and no audio could honestly represent a grind the client hasn't
felt). The original stub's canvas ink animation was removed with the rest
of its decoration.
