Posting a pen safely

  • Flush it first — plain cool water until it runs clear — then let it dry a full day, nib-down on tissue. A pen posted with ink in it is a pen posted with a leak in it.
  • Empty, not half-full: converter out or rinsed, cartridge removed.
  • Tissue around the nib, cap on, then the pen in a rigid tube or box — never loose in a padded envelope alone.
  • Tracked and signed for, always. Insure for what the pen is worth to replace, not what you paid decades ago.
  • Declare honestly on international post: “fountain pen for repair” — and note it is being returned after repair, which keeps customs simple.
  • Put your name and booking reference inside the parcel as well as outside it.
