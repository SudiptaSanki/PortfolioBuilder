The last fraction of a millimetre.

A fountain pen writes well or badly in the last fraction of a millimetre of its nib — the shape of the tipping where metal meets paper. I tune, grind and repair nibs by hand, under the loupe, for writers who post their pens from everywhere: scratchy nibs made smooth, broad nibs ground to italics with real line variation, worn tipping replaced and ground back to its original profile. Every line width below is measured, not guessed — and your pen comes home with its bench card, showing the lines it wrote before it left.

Book this grind

A grind can only take material away. A 0.9 mm italic needs a broad nib's tipping to start from; a fine nib cannot be ground wider than the metal it has. If your nib is too narrow for the grind you want, the written assessment says so before any money moves — and says what the nib can honestly become.

What can change a quote

  • How much tipping is left. A nib ground once before has less metal to give.
  • Bent or sprung tines — straightening is quoted separately, from photographs.
  • A cracked feed or a nib cracked at the breather hole: repair may be refused, and the assessment fee is the whole cost of finding out.
  • Steel and gold nibs are priced the same. Steel grinds faster; your bill doesn't care.
  • There is no rush service. The queue is the queue, and it is published at the top of this page.

How a pen comes to the bench

  1. 01

    Photographs first

    Send the booking form and photographs of the nib — point, both sides, the tipping under any bright light. Most faults can be named from these.

  2. 02

    Quote in writing

    A fixed price and a queue date, in writing, before the pen travels. The £15 assessment is credited against the work.

  3. 03

    The bench work

    Ground, lapped and polished in stages, checked under the loupe between each. Line widths measured before and after, and written on the bench card.

  4. 04

    The writing check

    The pen writes a full page in my hand before it is passed: every direction of stroke, at writing speed, on ordinary paper — not just the bench's best stock.

  5. 05

    Return post

    Tracked and signed for, in a rigid box, with the bench card in the parcel. If it doesn't write as the card says, it comes back to the bench free.
