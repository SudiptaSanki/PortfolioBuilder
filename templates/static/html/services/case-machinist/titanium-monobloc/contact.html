Questions · before the first cut

    
The five that come up every week

    

      

        Do you sign an NDA?
        
Yes, on request, before drawings are sent. We also work to a mutual NDA for development projects where the design is the customer’s. What we will not do is pretend a part is not export-controlled; ITAR and EAR work goes through the review first.

      

      

        Can you hold ±0.005 mm on a watch case?
        
On a bezel or a caseband, yes, with the right set-up and a stable temperature. On a deep lug feature, ±0.010 mm is more honest. The inspector on the home page shows where the limit sits for each face; if a drawing asks for more than the process can hold, we say so at quote rather than at delivery.

      

      

        What does a prototype cost?
        
More than a production part and less than you might fear. A one-off case in Grade 5 is typically £1,400–2,800 including finish and inspection, depending on how much five-axis time the pockets need. The quote is fixed after the DFM review, not before it.

      

      

        Do you supply the material?
        
Normally yes, with a mill certificate. Customer-supplied material is accepted but the certificate has to come with it, and we will not sign a conformance report against stock we did not buy unless it has been tested.

      

      

        What happens if a part is out of tolerance?
        
It is quarantined, measured again after a soak, and you get a call with the numbers before anything is re-cut. If it is our error, it is remade at our cost. If the drawing was ambiguous, we split the cost of the rework and fix the drawing together.
