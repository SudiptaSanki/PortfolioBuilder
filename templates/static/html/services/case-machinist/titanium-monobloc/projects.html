TM-039 · REV B

Monobloc dive case

2026 · Grade 5 titanium · 42 mm

Case and lugs from a single billet, no caseback joint. The crystal seat was the difficult feature: a 0.004 mm roundness over a 0.6 mm land, cut in one continuous five-axis pass so there is no witness mark to seal against.

Roughing took 26 hours, finishing 9. Two billets were consumed finding the right cutter engagement; both were logged as development, not billed.

TM-044 · REV A

Survey instrument housing

2025 · Ti-6Al-4V · 180 × 120 mm

A sealed housing machined from solid with fourteen pockets, six tapped holes and a gasket groove held to ±0.012 mm. The groove depth was the callout that mattered: too deep and the seal leaks, too shallow and the lid will not seat.

First article showed the groove 0.006 mm deep on the far side, caused by thermal drift over a long cycle. The program now pauses for a probe check at 60 %.

TM-047 · REV C

Satellite frame bracket

2025 · Grade 5 titanium · 96 g finished

A weight-relieved bracket with a 0.03 mm profile tolerance across the mating face and a wall thickness that had to stay above 1.2 mm everywhere. Eleven parts, all within 0.006 mm, all with full FAIR documentation.

The pocket pattern was re-sequenced to keep the part stiff while cutting. The original order moved 0.02 mm and was caught on the first article.

TM-051 · REV A

904L bezel, mirror chamfer

2024 · 904L stainless · 40 mm

A bezel in 904L with a mirror chamfer at Ra 0.025 µm, hand-lapped after machining because no cutter leaves a true mirror. The chamfer width was held to ±0.02 mm around the full circumference.

904L work-hardens under a dull tool. Tooling was changed every 40 minutes by the clock, not by ear, and the finish held across 24 parts.

TM-053 · REV B

Tantalum case, limited run

2024 · tantalum · 38 mm

A limited edition in tantalum, chosen for the dull satin it takes and the weight in the hand. The material is gummy to cut and the stock cost means there is no room for a scrapped billet, so the first part was cut in aluminium to prove the program.

All six parts passed on the first CMM run. The finish is as-machined at Ra 0.8, with no blasting at all.

TM-056 · REV A

Optical bench plate

2026 · 904L · 300 × 200 mm

A breadboard for an optical bench: 96 tapped holes on a 25 mm grid, all true position Ø 0.02 mm to datum A, with the top face flat to 0.01 mm over 300 mm. Stress-relieved before final machining, or it would move after the first bolt.

Flatness was measured on the CMM in 25 points. The worst reading was 0.007 mm, at the corner furthest from the clamping.
