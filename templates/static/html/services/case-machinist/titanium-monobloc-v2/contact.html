Sheet 04 — Enquiry

One path, whichever way you take it.

Write on the sheet below, or email cases@feldmann-caseworks.example directly. Both reach the same bench and the same pair of hands. What helps most: the case class, the quantity, and the tolerance you actually need — the planner on Sheet 01 will show you the shape of the price before you write a word.

A name is needed — the reply is written to a person.
An email address in full is needed, so the reply can reach you.
A few lines on the commission are needed — class, quantity and tolerance are enough to start.

After the enquiry

What happens next

3steps · no surprises
  1. 1
    A reply within two working daysIt states first whether the tolerances you asked for sit inside the Table T1 floors. If they do not, the reply prints the floor and what it would take to go further — the same answer the planner gives.
  2. 2
    A written quoteBuilt from the printed tables, with the agreed tolerances and the measurement method stated in the quote itself — instruments, temperature, sampling. No method in the quote, no order.
  3. 3
    The work, then the sheetPrototypes in 3–5 weeks; runs against the queue. Every delivery travels with its measurement sheet, written to survive your own micrometer.
