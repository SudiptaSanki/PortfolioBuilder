Orders & enquiries

Tell us what's going in the cask

The fastest route is email — it lands on the bench, not in an office:

orders@charredoak.example

The yard is in Louisville, Kentucky. Visits by appointment — come and see the seasoning stacks and the fire before you commit to forty casks.

What to include

  • What you're filling — bourbon, malt, wine, cider…
  • When you need to fill — the date drives everything
  • How many casks, and what size
  • The char or toast, if you have one in mind
  • For repairs: the vessel, its size, and what's wrong with it

Lead times, honestly

Standard casks: 8–12 weeks from deposit. Distillery runs of ten or more: booked by season. Seasoned butts: fourteen months in Jerez before the clock even reaches shipping — if your fill date is fixed, write early.
