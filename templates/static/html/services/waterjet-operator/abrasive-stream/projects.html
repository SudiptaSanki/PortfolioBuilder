SELECTED JOBS · 2023–2026

Five materials, five reasons the jet was the right tool.

Each job below ran on this machine. Dimensions are as-cut unless the note says machined, and every one passed first-off inspection before the run continued.

JOB 2214 · 2026 · AEROSPACE

Titanium ground-station bracket

A 6 mm Ti 6Al-4V mounting bracket with four ⌀26 bores and a 30 mm corner radius. Laser would have left a heat-affected edge that needed a second op; the waterjet left none. Tolerance held at ±0.05 mm on the bore centres.
