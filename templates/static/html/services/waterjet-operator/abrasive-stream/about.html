The pump is the heart of the shop and the reason the machine is serviced quarterly whether it needs it or not. A seal that is starting to weep shows up as a pressure drop of 1,000 psi, which shows up as a kerf that is 0.05 mm wide of nominal on a part nobody has time to remake.

Garnet is bought by the tonne and stored dry. Damp abrasive bridges in the hopper and starves the cutting head, which is the second-most-common cause of a scrapped part after a bad nest.

The water is chilled and recirculated: a 60,000 psi jet puts a lot of heat into the pump even though the cut itself stays cold.

SHEET 03 · QUALITY

How a part is proved

ISO 9001:2015
CERT 44182

Every job has a first-off inspection before the run continues. Critical dimensions are measured on a coordinate measuring machine to 0.001 mm; the rest are checked with calipers and a shadowgraph. Records go in the job folder, which you can have with the parts.

  • First-off: every dimension on the drawing, not just the tight ones.
  • In-process: kerf and taper checked every 20 parts on small runs.
  • Final: visual and fingertip check for burr, lag and embedded abrasive.
  • Certificates: material certs kept with the job; we can supply mill certs if the plate came through us.
  • Non-conformance: photographed and reported the same day, with a re-cut date.

SHEET 04 · LIMITS

What we will not cut

SAFETY AND PROCESS
NO EXCEPTIONS

The process is indifferent to hardness, which makes people assume it is indifferent to everything. It is not.

  • Tempered glass. It is under internal stress and shatters, sometimes in the operator's face.
  • Sealed hollow sections and pressure vessels. Water and abrasive go in; they do not come out.
  • Materials that react with water. Unstabilised lime, some cements, water-soluble salts.
  • Asbestos-containing board, and anything we cannot identify. Bring the data sheet.
  • Explosive or pyrotechnic assemblies. No.

We will cut composites with a low-pressure pierce, but we will not guarantee a structural edge without a sealing operation, and we will say so in the quote.
