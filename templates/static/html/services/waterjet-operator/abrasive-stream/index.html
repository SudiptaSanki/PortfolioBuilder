SHEET 04 · TOLERANCE

Where the ±0.002 comes from

CMM INSPECTION
FIRST-OFF + SAMPLE

Waterjet is an erosion process, not a thermal one, so the tolerance story is different from laser or plasma: no heat distortion, no recast, but a slight V-taper and a lag line at the exit. Both are managed by speed, standoff and the tilting head.
