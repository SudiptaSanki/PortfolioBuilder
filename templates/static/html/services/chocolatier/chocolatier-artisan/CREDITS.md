## Stub promises — honest replacements

The quarantined stub promised "tempering mastery" as decoration:
an animated canvas wave, a glass HUD card and gold-leaf chips.
Addressed on their merits:

- **Canvas wave + glass HUD → deleted.** Pure decoration with no
  job; replaced by the batch-ticket design system and content that
  carries weight: a printed tempering table, a rate card, a register.
- **"Tempering artistry" as a slogan → rebuilt as the Bar & Batch
  Explorer.** The explorer teaches what a cacao percentage actually
  counts, quotes each product's tempering line from the printed
  Table 1 (the touch probe asserts the figures agree), and prices
  every combination from the printed rate card (also asserted).
- **Sound → none.** Sound need-test: FAILED. Chocolate-making is
  not a sound profession and no interaction here teaches through
  audio. The site has no audio anywhere; the ruling is stated in
  every page footer.

Persona: Ana Beltrán is fictional and disclosed as such in every
footer. The Larkspur Café and all clients are fictional. Origin
names (Kilombero Valley, Manabí, Sambirano-region Madagascar) are
geography, used at region level; no real brand, estate or
chocolatier is named as the persona's work. No health claims are
made for chocolate anywhere on the site — the About page states
that refusal in print. Tempering ranges, shelf lives and process
steps are stated at established practice level, hedged as working
ranges where the trade itself hedges them.
