# Credits & honest replacements — lawyer-legal-trust

## Persona & content
**Ashworth Solicitors** is a fictional practice and **Clare Ashworth** a
fictional solicitor, disclosed as fictional in the footer of every page.
The three matters on the projects page are illustrative composites
written at **service level** — stages, fee bases and timescales only:
no real client, matter or legal detail is described, no outcome is
promised, and nothing on the site is legal advice (the "Information,
not advice" panel on the about page says so in terms). The professional
framework is real practice for solicitors in England & Wales, presented
as real firms present it: conflicts check, fixed £60 first meeting,
identity checks required by anti-money-laundering regulation, an
engagement letter before work begins, complaints to the practice first
and then the Legal Ombudsman, regulation by the Solicitors Regulation
Authority. Fees are a plausible published rate card for a high-street
practice, and every figure in the Matter Planner is the figure printed
in the fee table on the same page — the touch probe asserts they match.

## Honest replacements (stub content removed, not rebuilt)
The stub was a generic corporate-litigation page ("Defending Your
Rights with Unyielding Resolve") filled with Lorem ipsum, an Unsplash
hotlinked hero, "Item 1/2/3" attorney cards and Project Alpha/Beta
subpages. All of it was deleted:

- Lorem ipsum everywhere → real practice content across all four
  pages (planner, register, full fee table, three service-level
  matters, instruction steps, terms, complaints route).
- The Unsplash hero → Pexels photographs only, credited below, each
  captioned literally as a reference photograph.
- The litigation posture → a high-street practice for individuals and
  small businesses, with litigation explicitly *not* taken on.
- The dead consultation form → a validated demonstration enquiry form,
  honestly labelled as transmitting nothing, beside a visible email.

## Sound ruling
**Fails the need-test — no audio anywhere.** A solicitor's product is
careful writing; any sound would be costume. Verified in code: no
`<audio>`, no Web Audio, no audio files. The ruling is stated in every
page footer.
