        §3 · FEES IN FULLThe whole table, not the headline

        Standard matters carry fixed fees because their stages are predictable — they are the same stages the planner shows. Estates vary too much for that honesty, so probate administration is hourly, with the estimate in writing first and time recorded in six-minute units, itemised on every bill. All fees exclude VAT at the prevailing rate; disbursements — search fees, court and registry fees — are charged at cost and itemised, never marked up.

        The first meeting — £60 fixed

        Forty-five minutes with the solicitor who would run your matter, not a salesperson. Bring whatever papers you have; you will leave knowing whether we can act, what the stages are, what it will cost, and what it will not include. A written summary and fee estimate follows within two working days. There is no obligation to instruct, and if the matter is not ours, we will say so at the meeting rather than after it.
