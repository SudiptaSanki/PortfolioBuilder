Ashworth Solicitors

Ashworth Solicitors is a fictional practice, and Clare Ashworth a fictional solicitor, created for this portfolio template. The matters described are illustrative composites written at service level — no real client or matter is described, and nothing on this site is legal advice. The professional framework, however, is real practice presented as real firms present it: an engagement letter before work begins, identity checks required by regulation, fixed fees in print, and a complaints route if we fall short. This site is silent by design — sound fails the need-test for a profession whose product is careful writing.
