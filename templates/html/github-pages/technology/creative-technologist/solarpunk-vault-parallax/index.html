About

Solarpunk Summit is Innovation Campus's premier hackathon. We fly out the world's most talented builders to Innovation Campus, where they create products that tackle humanity's toughest challenges in just 36 hours.

Our hackers have food and travel covered, and get to compete for incredible prizes while networking with recruiters from top companies.

If you want to bring your ideas to life, even if you've never attended a hackathon, come to Solarpunk Summit!

BEFORE YOU ASK

FAQ

A hackathon is where you turn your crazy ideas into real projects. At Solarpunk Summit, for 36 hours (February 12th to 14th) on Innovation Campus's campus, you'll be learning and building in an energetic environment with people as passionate as you are. This year is our 13th anniversary, and we're going ALL OUT — flights (regional caps), food and swag for all hackers, designated sleeping spaces, epic prizes, world-renowned speakers, mentors and deeply technical workshops. You take care of hacking, we take care of you.

Solarpunk Summit applications are open to any enrolled college student (undergrad or grad) from all over the world. Due to venue capacity we can't offer automatic acceptance to Innovation Campus students, so hackers from everywhere get a fair shot. Innovation Campus students who apply by the priority deadline do get priority consideration.

Solarpunk Summit is free for all admitted hackers. Workshops, food, swag and prizes cost you nothing — travel is covered within regional caps. We're committed to making Solarpunk Summit accessible.

Solarpunk Summit abides by the Hack Code of Conduct. Every applicant must agree to it to be considered for admission. Solarpunk Summit is a safe place where everyone is welcome ❤️.
