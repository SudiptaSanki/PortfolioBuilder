+ 01 — Ground insight

Clarity beneath the surface

The most effective way to control project safety, cost and schedule starts with fully characterising foundation soil uncertainty — and a thorough understanding of ground stratigraphy and engineering properties.

Our multidisciplinary team combines advanced geoscience exploration with integrated research techniques to turn ground data into engineering insight, so projects can be delivered safely, cost-effectively and on programme. Every investigation is built around the conditions that actually decide the outcome.

Sound ground parameters come from advanced site characterisation and deep expertise

We work on high-stakes offshore developments where data quality and traceability are critical. Ground conditions are assessed offshore, investigation methods are reviewed in real time, and the workflow stays transparent from acquisition through to interpretation.

The result is information project teams can rely on as the work progresses.
