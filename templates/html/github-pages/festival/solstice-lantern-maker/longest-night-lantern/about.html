About Rafael

Hello! I'm Rafael Laurent, a solstice lantern maker based in Haarlem. Gather the season — gratitude, warmth and shared tables.

My journey began around family, festivals and community celebrations — the places where this craft lives and breathes. Over the years those beginnings grew into a practice that blends traditional technique with a modern sense of presentation, serving families, festivals and cultural organisations alike.

Whether it is the biggest night of the year or a quiet personal milestone, my promise is the same: work made with patience, respect for tradition, and a little extra glow.
